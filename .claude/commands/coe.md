description: Open a Correction of Error (usage: /coe <what went wrong>)
---
Incident: $ARGUMENTS

Run `npm run company -- ritual coe`. Fill in the facts first, then ask why until the answers stop being about people. Every action must change a mechanism (a gate, a check, a pattern, the kit) and goes on the board with an owner. Use `grep -r` to find claims and decisions that rested on what failed, and send them back to review. Ask the pattern librarian subagent for the pattern update.
