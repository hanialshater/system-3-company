### Notes on the thresholds
- **HI-2, why keep 10 GB and not lower it to fit today.** `/` showed 9.9 GB free at 13:28Z and 11 GB at 13:35Z (`df -B1G /`). A production `.next` dir is about 0.5 GB (507 MB for `/home/user/s3c-qa/.next-review4`, 497 MB for `.next-lr`). A builder worktree with hard-linked node_modules adds little more. At the C2 cap of 3 builders that is roughly 1.5 to 2 GB, plus git objects, logs and Playwright traces. 10 GB is a margin of about 5 times one round's build output. On 10-14 the host reached 15 MB free, and git could not write refs. Lowering the floor because the host sits near it would move the alarm, not the risk. Raising it would block builds on a host that only the founder may prune (worktree removal is founder-approved). The fix for being near the floor is pruning (W-525) and the second machine (W-531), not a new number. The SubagentStop event under 5 GB is new: C4 sees the disk only at dispatch, and a run can fill it in between.
- **HI-1 fails `check` too**, unlike C4, because git and every shell fail with a broken /dev/null. A check run on that host would be noise.
- **HI-1(c) is the root-cause fix and HI-1(a)/(b) are detection.** The hook is not live while W-1280 (H1) is open. Until then the generic brief rule ("Never create, link or write to /dev/null except as a shell redirect target") is the control.
- **HI-4 baseline:** the main checkout already has two stash entries (`stash@{2026-10-16 13:11:10}` saved by cm-history and the 10:55 autostash). The test compares against the count at dispatch, so old entries do not fail every run. Resolving those two is COE-B's work.
