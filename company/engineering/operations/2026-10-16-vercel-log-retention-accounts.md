They are kept for about 1 hour on Hobby (1 day on Pro, about 30 days with Observability Plus). That figure is **from memory**. Our deletion cannot reach them. Mark it `pending: read from Vercel`, not `pass`.
- **Our prints:** no email, token or body **once gap A-6 is closed**. Until then, an email can reach the log on a database error.
- **Plan coupling:** F-18 (Pro) lengthens log retention from about 1 hour to about 1 day and makes drains possible. Whoever brings F-18 to the founder (run 6) should know that the inventory and the policy change with it.
