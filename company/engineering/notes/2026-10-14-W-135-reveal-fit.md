# W-135: the reveal on a phone (clipped text, thumbnail stage)

owner: platform-engineer · date: 2026-10-14 · tier: 1 (shared CSS + the card component, one-commit
revert). It touches every lesson's reveal, so the principal engineer may rule it tier 2; this note is
written to serve as the EDD-lite and rollback plan if so.

## Symptom

About 12 wave-A learner reviews (2026-10-13, adult beginner) said that on a phone the reveal's last
paragraph is cut off under "Why trust this?" (paint-mix-shared-secret: fatal), and/or that the
stage shrinks to a thumbnail at the reveal (whose-cutoff-wins, dilute-until-none-left, ...).
Published lessons are affected too.

## Root cause (reproduced at 390×844 with Playwright, `tools/reveal-fit.mjs`)

The kit change of 2026-10-13 (54ae817) made the stage content-sized at the reveal and the card body
(`.card-body`) a scroll container. Two defects followed:

1. **Reveal below the fold, no affordance.** The reveal panel is appended under a natural-height
   stage, but the card body stays at scrollTop 0. The reveal starts near the card's bottom edge and
   the rest sits inside an inner scroller with no visible scrollbar on mobile. It is not truly
   clipped (it can be scrolled to), but the learner and every walkthrough screenshot see the text
   end mid-word at the footer. 245 of 509 lessons hid part of the reveal this way (by 19-170px).
   The footer is a flex sibling and never overlaps; nothing is fixed-height in the kit.
2. **Thumbnail stage.** About 200 lessons size their picture to fill the stage:
   `.wrap :global(.stage-svg) { flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 140px }`. That works while the stage has a
   definite height (predict/explore). At the reveal the stage is content-sized, so a zero
   flex-basis gives the picture nothing but its min-height: the SVG is letterboxed to 120-140px
   (drawn at 45-70% of the scale the stage width allows). 141 of 509 lessons.

Neither cause is in `src/kit/Lesson.tsx`.

## Fix (no amendment needed)

- `src/components/DemoCard.tsx`: `useRevealFit` watches the lesson's `data-phase` (MutationObserver,
  no kit change). On the reveal it scrolls the card body just enough to put the reveal's bottom on
  screen, or its top if the reveal is taller than the card. Instant, not smooth, so screenshots and
  walkthroughs taken right after the tap show the settled state; re-checked once at 400 ms for
  reveals that grow.
- `src/app/globals.css`: at the reveal, `.stage .stage-svg { flex: none; height: auto }`, so the
  picture takes its natural viewBox height (any lesson max-height still applies).

Trade-off: when the reveal is long, the top of the stage scrolls off above (it is not shrunk; one
swipe down shows it). Reading the why was judged more important than seeing the whole stage at once.

## Per-lesson fixes still needed (lesson owners)

Thumbnail at the reveal, caused by the lesson's own viewport-height caps on its picture:
- waves-return: `.svg { max-height: 14dvh }`, `.chart { max-height: 10dvh }`
- dish-runs-out: `.svg { max-height: 19dvh }`
- hidden-carriers: `.svg { max-height: 21dvh }`
- random-dots-cluster: `.svg { max-height: 21dvh }`
- in-order-is-sorted: picture drawn at 59% scale (190px); check by eye
- the-fast-ones-leave: probably a false positive (the chart is HTML; the largest SVG is the thermometer); check by eye

Not measurable by the QA-contract path (reveal never unlocks after every control once, or sliders at
an end): exponential-growth, merge-sort-payoff, odd-numbers-make-squares, pendulum-period,
push-the-swing-in-time, puck-on-ice-no-push, ramp-same-work. Measure by hand or extend the tool.

## Rollback

Revert the commit. Both changes are additive and only touch the reveal phase.

## Possible amendment (not required)

If lessons are ever hosted outside DemoCard (e.g. the EDD-003 sandbox iframe), the scroll-into-view
belongs in the kit: `showReveal` in `src/kit/Lesson.tsx` would call
`requestAnimationFrame(() => revealRef.current?.scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" }))`. Until then
the wrapper is sufficient; the spike host should copy `useRevealFit`.
