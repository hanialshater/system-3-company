These are observations for the reviewer who owns the verdict (W-1506).

1. **The engine changed after both engineering approvals.** The principal-engineer final review (`company/engineering/reviews/2026-10-16-books-100-final-review.md`) inspected the engine "through `cded0cd9`" and says "PR completion, readiness and merge approval are not asserted", with conditions 1–5 open. The ops readiness (`company/engineering/readiness/2026-10-16-books-100-ops-approval.md`) also reviewed `cded0cd9` and says "an engine change after O1 invalidates the affected family/browser evidence". `fe7fc95` then changed `src/components/books/BookIdeaLesson.tsx` (4 lines) and `src/lib/books/model.ts` (+7 lines: `formatCompoundOperation`, lines 132–138), plus 3 payloads. **No engineering record covers the shipped engine, and no record gives merge approval.** That is why W-1506 is a merge gate.
2. `src/lib/books/model.ts:104`: the spec validator checks that `predict.answer` is a valid index, not that the option it points to equals the model's output. Correctness of the 100 answers rests on the fact-checker's out-of-band audit (`company/reviews/books-100/checks/visible-answer-results.json`: 100/100 prediction and recall checks, 0 failures). A test that recomputes each `idea.json` would keep that true after edits.
3. `src/lib/books/model.ts:38`: the `flow` arity is `values.length`, so the arity check is a no-op for flow; line 45 does the real bound (2–4). Harmless, but reads like a check.
4. `src/components/books/BookIdeaLesson.tsx:18`: `validateBookIdeaSpec` runs on every render and throws on a bad payload, which would crash the lesson page rather than fail the build. Every payload is valid today (100/100 in the engineering review); a build-time test over all `idea.json` files would catch a bad edit earlier.
5. `src/components/books/BookIdeaLesson.tsx:56`: the Reset control has no `aria-label` beyond its text "Reset"; fine for a single stage, noted for the accessibility scan (W-1507).
6. `src/components/books/BookIdeaLesson.tsx:89`: in `flow`, every station equal to the minimum is highlighted as the constraint; ties show two constraints. Probably intended.
7. Sample lesson `src/demos/books/bk-brooks-ramp-up-cost/`: `Demo.tsx` is the shared 8-line wrapper; `idea.json` intervals [60,20,0] → 60 and [90,20,20] → 40, matching the predict answer index 1 ("40.00") and claim `books.bk-brooks-ramp-up-cost.toy`; `meta.json` has all 5 claims `unverified` with honest sources.
