**Proposed order: milestone → C2 ad slot → C4 rule-based personalization → C3 challenges → C1 prediction swap.** The tech lead confirms or changes the order (run 10), and F-44 gives the order to both of us.

- **The ad slot first.** In the tech lead's words it has "no stored data, no accounts dependency and no `src/kit/` change" (full-scope note §5), so it is the cheapest release to put through its own gates right after the milestone. That answers the founder's "personalization or ads soon after".

Writing its rules after a few weeks of real-learner data (R-011) is better than writing them blind, so second costs the founder about one release slot of wait.

- **The swap last.** It has the most outside conditions (F-26, the "light social" question, the M5 reading, three privacy runs, P4.3), and it is the only follow-on with stored data.
