# EXP-002: Do real learners remember what the lessons claim to teach?

owner: founder (sessions), analytics-steward (analysis) · status: designed, not run · date: 2026-10-12

## Hypothesis
Lessons that pass our gates and borrowed-mind reviews are remembered by real people. Borrowed-mind
reviewers predict where real learners get confused.

## Committed prediction
5 adults × 5 published lessons each, think-aloud, then a recall question 3 days later.
- First-revisit recall ≥ 60% correct.
- At least half of the confusion points real learners voice were flagged in the lesson's borrowed-mind review.

would_be_wrong_if: recall ≤ 45% (near chance for 2–3 options plus 10 points), or fewer than a quarter of real confusion points were predicted by borrowed minds.

## Design
Participants choose lessons from the map. The founder runs sessions and records confusion points
verbatim before reading the lesson's reviews. The analytics steward matches confusion points to
review findings blind to which lesson they came from.
