# System 3 Lessons: art direction

This company's art follows the city's shared style, `system3-city ART.md` (true isometric, light from the upper left,
three tones per face from the city's palette, labels on surfaces, original logos, subtle motion that is safe under
reduced motion). This file keeps what is particular to System 3 Lessons. The city's illustration draws it in system3-meta
`hq/city-scene.js` (`hq/ART.md`).

## The metaphor

**A school whose windows are lesson cards.** It makes 60-second lessons (predict, explore, reveal); its windows alternate a ? card (predict) and a ! card (reveal).

## Its building in the city

- **The building:** a long stone schoolhouse with a red pitched roof; two rows of card-windows on its face (violet ? and yellow !), a door in its violet accent.
- **Live data it shows:** a queue of cards at its door, one card for every 120 lessons in flight in its pipeline (at most 12); a crane while a project is active; people at the door for the agents active in the last hour.
- **The roof sign:** its logo, on a light board, lit at night.

## Accent colours

- **Accent:** violet `#8a74d4`; yellow (the city's crane yellow) for the ! card.
- **Status colours:** the city's reserved status colours only (running, ended, onboarding, blocked).

## The logo

`company/logo.svg`: a card mid-flip from ? (predict) to ! (reveal). Original; readable at 24 px.

## Its HQ pages and products

Its HQ pages use the HQ's own tokens (light and dark, phone width 390) and the city's page templates; nothing on a page imitates the illustration. **Its lesson cards** keep their own design system (the product's), but share the city's light direction and the predict-and-reveal pairing of violet and yellow, so a lesson card and the school's window read as the same thing.
