# The System 3 city: Meta is company zero, every company migrates to its own repository

owner: vp-meta · date: 2026-10-25 · status: decided (direction) · class: product strategy · route: founder → vp-meta

## Ask

Founder, 2026-10-25: "our purpose was org is scalable and allows for fallible agents, going to 100% is good idea, also
I want to work on UI (the HQ with linear like side bar, clear tracking, meeting records, etc), at the end we want to
launch the meta company and be able to bootstrap new companies"; "if we are successful it will migrate (new repo) using
the meta company and we will have s3 city/universe (of companies)"; "the same for meta company, it should migrate to
meta company".

## Decision

- The goal is a **city of companies** on one platform ([../product/VISION.md](../product/VISION.md)).
- **Meta is company zero**: it migrates first to its own repository and runs on its own starter kit; its product is the
  platform (kernel, starter kit, generator, city registry).
- **The lesson company migrates next** to its own repository on the starter kit, keeping its custom features.
- **Then a new company is bootstrapped from a spec** (milestone M6).
- Order of work: product docs (done) → fold the benchmarks' org mechanics into the kernel → the starter HQ (Linear-like
  sidebar, tracking, meeting records, from the event log, extension points) → Meta migrates → the lesson company
  migrates → a new company from a spec. Benchmark 13 (scale to 8 members with fallible agents, toward 100% of SQLite's
  tests) runs alongside, as the org's proof and the HQ's first live company, at the founder's approval of its budget.
