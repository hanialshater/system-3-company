# Company onboarding: archetypes, the role and skill catalog, HQ developers in every company

owner: vp-meta · date: 2026-10-25 · status: decided (direction); **draft** (ONBOARDING.md details, for review) ·
class: product architecture · route: founder → vp-meta

## Ask

Founder, 2026-10-25: "to complete the picture we need company onboarding plans (we have amazon/corp like, science like,
committee, dynamic workflow, etc.) we can add different company archetypes to allow for org design, we always have
catalog of roles and skills that we can use to bootstrap a company org and HQ developers (also in current s3 company)".

## Decision

- A new company is bootstrapped from **an archetype** (per lane, mixable), **the role and skill catalog**, and **the
  starter kit**, through an eight-step onboarding plan ([../product/ONBOARDING.md](../product/ONBOARDING.md)).
- Archetypes recorded now, each with its structure, decision mechanism, rituals, referee, when to use it and its
  evidence: corporate (Amazon-like), science (lab), committee (deliberative), dynamic workflow (autonomous),
  population (evolutionary), single strong agent, open source (maintainers).
- The generic roles of System 3 Company's catalog (111 roles today) become the starter catalog; domain roles stay with
  their company. **Every company gets HQ developers** (`hq-engineer`) from day one.
