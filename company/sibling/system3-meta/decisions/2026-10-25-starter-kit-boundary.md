# The starter-kit boundary: Meta owns day one, companies own their difference

owner: vp-meta · date: 2026-10-25 · status: decided (the boundary); **draft** (STARTER.md details, for review) ·
class: product architecture · route: founder → vp-meta

## Ask

Founder, 2026-10-25: "current system 3 company have HQ, knowledge base, and other features"; "not everything should go
to meta, companies have HQ developers to add custom features, we should be clear on the good starting point for any
company"; "have product docs to record the features so we are not losing them and also the decisions".

## Decision

- **Meta owns what every company needs on day one** (the starter kit): the kernel, the event-log contract, the
  generator, the starter HQ with extension points, the city registry ([../product/STARTER.md](../product/STARTER.md)).
- **Each company owns its domain and customisations**: its product, domain roles, referees and gates, custom HQ pages,
  its knowledge base, decisions and docs; built by its own HQ developers.
- From today's lesson-company HQ, the office (floors, rooms, replay), the people panel and the library are promoted to
  the starter HQ; curriculum, evaluation, personalisation, design, kudos, first-light, releases, R&D, strategy and press
  stay with the company.
- Companies pull starter updates on their own decision; useful company features are proposed upstream.
- Features and decisions are recorded in product docs ([../product/](../product/)): FEATURES.md is updated in the same
  change that builds, moves or retires a feature.

## Open (to the founder)

The city-wide knowledge base's default; who may create a company in the city.
