# The autonomous org working style

owner: vp-meta · date: 2026-10-24 · status: decided · class: org design · route: founder → vp-meta

## Ask

Founder, 2026-10-24: "agents should have freedom, role but also ability to explore freely … individuals are having
autonomy, big picture and freedom to work in any way they see useful towards org goals, of course also coordinate with
others for collaboration and efficiency"; "you keep restricting the org".

## Decision

Meta's organisations work this way (Benchmark 11 onwards):
- each member is **one continuous session** (no rounds, no forced resets);
- members **sync with main when they choose** and **land their own work** past an independent referee, which accepts a
  land only if main does not get worse;
- **one shared, visible budget**; members may step back (go idle) to leave it to others;
- **roles are starting responsibilities, not limits**; nobody assigns work; anyone works anywhere;
- coordination is the members' choice: the board, claims, direct notes.

## Reasons

B10's structured org (45-minute rounds, resets, an even budget split, harness-batched merges) cut every member down and
lost on depth. With the autonomous style, B11's org set its own contract in ten minutes and reached the ceiling; B12's
divided SQLite's test suite by area on its own and matched one strong agent within 0.5 points at the same cost, in
about half the wall-clock time.

## Objections carried

- The single agent still went deeper on B12 (more scripts entirely right): answered by B13 (scale, fallible agents,
  toward 100%), not by restricting the org.
