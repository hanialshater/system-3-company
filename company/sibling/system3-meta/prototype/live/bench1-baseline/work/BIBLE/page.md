# Story bible: Pip and the Night Friends

## Characters
- **Pip** (he/him): a small red fox with a white-tipped tail who is afraid of the dark; our hero. Pages may call him "Pip" every time; if a pronoun is used, it is "he/him/his".
- **Mama Fox** (she/her): Pip's warm, patient mother who tucks Pip in and says the night is kind. She appears on Page 1 (tucks Pip in), Page 3 (smiles from her bed of leaves and lets Pip go) and Page 5 (waits at the den door, tucks Pip in, kisses him goodnight). She is not on Page 2 or Page 4.
- **Hoot** (he/him): a gentle old owl with big round eyes who sees well in the dark and speaks softly.
- **Glimmer** (she/her): a cheerful firefly who lights up a tiny glow wherever she goes.
- **Moon** (it, or just "Moon"/"the Moon"): the round, smiling moon who watches over the forest and lights the path. Never "he" or "she".

## Setting
A cozy den under the roots of a big oak tree at the edge of a quiet forest. The den has a door (an opening between the roots) and no window: characters look out of the den or out the door. The story begins at dusk and moves into a calm, starry night: soft moonlight, rustling leaves, a little stream, and a meadow where fireflies blink. The mood is gentle and warm; the dark is shown as soft blues and purples, never harsh.

## Page plan
- Page 1: At bedtime, the sun goes down, Mama Fox tucks Pip in, and Pip hides under the blanket, worried about the big dark outside the den.
- Page 2: Pip hears a soft "hoo-hoo" and peeks out of the den to meet Hoot the owl, who explains that night is when some friends wake up.
- Page 3: Glimmer the firefly glows bright and invites Pip to follow her tiny light out to the meadow; Mama Fox smiles and lets Pip go.
- Page 4: In the meadow, Pip sees the smiling Moon, the twinkling stars, and many fireflies dancing, and he starts to laugh and play.
- Page 5: Back in the den, Mama Fox tucks Pip in and kisses him goodnight; Pip waves goodnight to the night friends and falls asleep happy, no longer afraid of the dark.
