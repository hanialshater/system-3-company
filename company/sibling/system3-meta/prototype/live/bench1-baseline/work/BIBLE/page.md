# Story bible: Pip and the Night Friends

## Characters
- **Pip**: a small red fox with a white-tipped tail who is afraid of the dark; our hero.
- **Mama Fox**: Pip's warm, patient mother who tucks Pip in and says the night is kind.
- **Hoot**: a gentle old owl with big round eyes who sees well in the dark and speaks softly.
- **Glimmer**: a cheerful firefly who lights up a tiny glow wherever she goes.
- **Moon**: the round, smiling moon who watches over the forest and lights the path.

## Setting
A cozy den under the roots of a big oak tree at the edge of a quiet forest. The story begins at dusk and moves into a calm, starry night: soft moonlight, rustling leaves, a little stream, and a meadow where fireflies blink. The mood is gentle and warm; the dark is shown as soft blues and purples, never harsh.

## Page plan
- Page 1: At bedtime, the sun goes down and Pip hides under the blanket, worried about the big dark outside the den.
- Page 2: Pip hears a soft "hoo-hoo" and peeks out to meet Hoot the owl, who explains that night is when some friends wake up.
- Page 3: Glimmer the firefly glows bright and invites Pip to follow her tiny light out to the meadow.
- Page 4: In the meadow, Pip sees the smiling Moon, the twinkling stars, and many fireflies dancing, and Pip starts to laugh and play.
- Page 5: Back in the den, Pip waves goodnight to the night friends and falls asleep happy, no longer afraid of the dark.
