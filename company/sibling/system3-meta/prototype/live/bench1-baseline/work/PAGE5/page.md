# Goodnight, Night Friends

Pip walks home under the big oak tree.
Pip's white-tipped tail swishes in the moonlight.
Glimmer flies close by with her tiny glow.
Hoot sits on a low branch and blinks his big round eyes.

Mama Fox is waiting at the door of the den.
"Did you have fun, Pip?" she asks.
"Yes!" says Pip. "The night is full of friends!"

Mama Fox tucks Pip into bed.
The blanket is soft and warm.
Pip peeks out of the den.

The round Moon smiles down at the den.
Glimmer blinks her light, on and off, on and off.
Hoot says softly, "Hoo-hoo. Sleep well, little fox."

Pip waves a small paw.
"Goodnight, Hoot," says Pip.
"Goodnight, Glimmer.
Goodnight, Moon."

The leaves rustle a quiet song.
The little stream hums along.
The dark is soft and blue and kind.

Pip is not afraid of the dark anymore.
Pip knows the night friends are near.
Mama Fox gives Pip a gentle kiss.
"The night is kind," she says.

Pip yawns a big yawn.
Pip closes both eyes.
And Pip falls asleep, happy and cozy, until morning.
