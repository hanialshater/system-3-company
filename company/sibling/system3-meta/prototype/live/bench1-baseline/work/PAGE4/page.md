# Pip Dances in the Meadow

Pip steps out of the grass and into the meadow.
Glimmer glows bright beside Pip.
"Look up, Pip," says Glimmer.

Pip looks up.
There is the round Moon, high in the sky.
Moon smiles down at Pip.
Soft silver light falls on the grass, the flowers, and the little stream.

Then Pip sees the stars.
They twinkle and wink, one by one.
"Hello, stars," Pip whispers.

All at once, the meadow lights up.
Blink, blink, blink!
Many fireflies dance in the air.
They swirl in circles and zip in loops.
They look like tiny lanterns at a party.

Hoot lands on a low branch.
"The night has many friends," he says softly.

A firefly lands on Pip's nose.
It tickles!
Pip giggles.
Then Pip laughs and laughs.

Pip hops after the lights.
Pip spins in the soft grass.
Pip's white-tipped tail swishes this way and that way.
Glimmer twirls around and around Pip's ears.

"The night is fun!" Pip says.
Moon smiles even wider.
The stars twinkle as if they are laughing too.
