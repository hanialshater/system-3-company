# Hoo-Hoo in the Night

Pip lies very still under the blanket.
The den is quiet. The leaves go swish, swish.

Then Pip hears a sound.
"Hoo-hoo. Hoo-hoo."
It is soft and low, like a sleepy song.

Pip lifts the blanket just a little.
Pip peeks out of the den.
Up in the big oak tree sits an owl.
He has big round eyes and fluffy gray feathers.

"Hello, little fox," says the owl.
His voice is soft and kind.
"My name is Hoot."

"Hello," says Pip in a tiny voice.
"Why are you awake? It is night."

Hoot fluffs his wings.
"Night is when some friends wake up," he says.
"The sun goes to bed, and we come out to play.
My big eyes help me see in the dark."

Pip blinks.
"Friends wake up at night?"

"Yes," says Hoot. "Lots of friends.
The night is not empty, little Pip.
The night is full of friends."

Pip's white-tipped tail gives a small wiggle.
Maybe the dark is not so big after all.
