# Pip and the Big Dark

The sun is going down. The sky turns pink, then purple, then soft blue.

Pip is a small red fox with a white-tipped tail. Pip lives in a cozy den under the roots of a big oak tree.

"Time for bed, little one," says Mama Fox. She tucks Pip in with a warm blanket.

Pip looks out of the den. The trees are getting dark. The path is getting dark. Soon the whole forest is dark.

"Mama," says Pip, "the dark is so big. And I am so small."

Pip pulls the blanket up. Pip pulls it over two ears. Pip pulls it over one nose. Now only the white tip of Pip's tail pokes out.

Mama Fox gives Pip a soft kiss. "The night is kind," she says. "You will see."

But Pip is not so sure.

Outside, the leaves go swish, swish. The little stream goes trickle, trickle.

Pip stays under the blanket. Pip holds very still. Pip listens and listens.

What is out there in the big dark?
