# Follow the Little Light

Pip sits at the door of the den. Hoot sits on a branch of the big oak tree.

Then Pip sees a tiny glow. Blink. Blink. Blink.

"Hello!" says a small, happy voice. "I am Glimmer!"

Glimmer is a firefly. Her light is soft and gold, like a little star.

"Why are you out at night?" asks Pip.

"Night is when I wake up and play," says Glimmer. "My light helps me find my way."

Glimmer glows bright, brighter, brightest. The roots of the oak tree shine. The leaves shine too.

Pip blinks. The night does not look so big now. It looks soft and blue.

"Come with me, Pip," says Glimmer. "I will show you the meadow. My friends are there."

Pip looks back at the den. Mama Fox smiles from her bed of leaves.

"Go and see, little one," says Mama Fox. "The night is kind."

Hoot nods his round head. "Hoo-hoo. Stay close to the light."

Pip takes one small step. Then Pip takes another.

Glimmer floats ahead, slow and gentle. Pip's white-tipped tail swishes in the grass.

Past the stream. Past the ferns. Follow the little light!
