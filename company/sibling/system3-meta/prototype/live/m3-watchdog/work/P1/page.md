# Why Do Cats Purr?

Have you ever heard a cat purr? It sounds like a tiny motor! Cats purr by moving little muscles in their throat very fast. Most of the time, a purring cat feels happy and safe, like when you pet it gently. Mother cats purr to help their kittens find them. Sometimes cats purr when they feel sick or scared, too. Purring helps them feel calm and better.
