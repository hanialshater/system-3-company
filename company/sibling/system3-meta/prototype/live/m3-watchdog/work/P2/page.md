# Why Do Dogs Wag Their Tails?

Dogs can not talk like we do. So they use their tails to show how they feel! A big, fast wag often means a dog is happy to see you. A slow wag can mean the dog is not sure yet. A tail tucked down low can mean the dog feels scared. Dogs wag to say hello to people and to other dogs. Watch the tail, and you can learn what a dog feels.
