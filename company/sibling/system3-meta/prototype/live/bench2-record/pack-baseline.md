# Solar System Quiz Pack

# Round 1: The Sun

**Q1.** The Sun is not a planet. What kind of object is it?
**Answer:** Star
**Why:** The Sun is a star, a huge ball of hot, glowing gas that gives us light and warmth.

**Q2.** In which direction does the Sun come up each morning?
**Answer:** East
**Why:** Earth spins toward the east, so each morning the Sun seems to rise in the east and later set in the west.

**Q3.** Which gas makes up most of the Sun?
**Answer:** Hydrogen
**Why:** About three quarters of the Sun is hydrogen, which is the lightest gas there is.

**Q4.** About how many minutes does light from the Sun take to reach Earth?
**Answer:** 8
**Why:** Light is the fastest thing there is, but the Sun is so far away that its light still takes about 8 minutes to get here.


# Round 2: The rocky planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars)

**Q1.** Which planet is often called the Red Planet?
**Answer:** Mars
**Why:** Mars looks red because its rocks and dust have lots of rust in them.

**Q2.** Which rocky planet has big oceans of water on its surface?
**Answer:** Earth
**Why:** Oceans cover most of Earth, which is why our home looks so blue in photos taken from space.

**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest of all the planets?
**Answer:** Venus
**Why:** Venus has a very thick blanket of air that traps the Sun's heat, so it is even hotter than Mercury.

**Q4.** What is the name of the giant volcano on Mars, the biggest in the solar system?
**Answer:** Olympus Mons
**Why:** Olympus Mons is more than twice as tall as Mount Everest, the highest mountain on Earth.


# Round 3: The giant planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune)

**Q1.** Which planet is the biggest one of all?
**Answer:** Jupiter
**Why:** Jupiter is so big that it holds more than twice as much stuff as all the other planets put together.

**Q2.** Which planet is famous for its wide, bright rings that you can see in a small telescope?
**Answer:** Saturn
**Why:** Saturn's rings are made of lots and lots of bits of ice and rock that go around and around the planet.

**Q3.** Which giant planet is tipped right over, so it spins on its side?
**Answer:** Uranus
**Why:** Uranus leans over so far that it looks like it rolls along its path, like a ball on its side.

**Q4.** Which planet has the fastest winds of any planet we know?
**Answer:** Neptune
**Why:** Neptune is far away and very cold, yet its winds blow faster than the winds on any other planet.


# Round 4: Earth's Moon

**Q1.** What do we call the Moon when it looks like a whole bright circle?
**Answer:** Full
**Why:** On the night of a full moon, we can see all of the Moon's bright side at once.

**Q2.** What are the round, bowl-shaped holes all over the Moon called?
**Answer:** Craters
**Why:** Craters formed when space rocks crashed into the Moon long ago, and most still look the same today.

**Q3.** About how many days does the Moon take to go once around Earth?
**Answer:** 27
**Why:** That is one trip around Earth, but from one full moon to the next takes about a month.

**Q4.** What name do we give the big, dark, flat patches we can see on the Moon?
**Answer:** Maria
**Why:** Maria means "seas" in Latin, because people long ago thought these dark plains of old, cooled lava were oceans.


# Round 5: Space exploration (rockets, astronauts, rovers)

**Q1.** Who was the first person to walk on the Moon?
**Answer:** Neil Armstrong
**Why:** Neil Armstrong stepped onto the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission, and Buzz Aldrin joined him soon after.

**Q2.** What is the name of the big space station where astronauts live while it circles Earth?
**Answer:** International Space Station
**Why:** Many countries built the International Space Station together, and people have lived on it without a break for more than twenty years.

**Q3.** Who was the very first person to travel into space?
**Answer:** Yuri Gagarin
**Why:** Yuri Gagarin flew all the way around the Earth one time in a small spacecraft called Vostok 1.

**Q4.** Which NASA rover took a tiny helicopter called Ingenuity with it to Mars?
**Answer:** Perseverance
**Why:** Perseverance carried Ingenuity under its belly, and Ingenuity became the first helicopter to fly on another planet.
