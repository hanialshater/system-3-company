# Why Do Dogs Wag Their Tails?

Dogs wag their tails to talk! They cannot use words like you. So they use their tails to show how they feel. A big, fast wag often means, "I am so happy to see you!" A slow wag can mean, "I am not sure yet." A tail held low may mean a dog feels scared. Dogs also wag to say hello to other dogs. Watch a dog's tail, and you can learn what it is telling you.
