# Why Do Cats Purr?

Have you ever heard a cat purr? It sounds like a soft, buzzy motor. Cats purr when they feel happy and safe, like when you pet them gently. Mother cats purr to tell their kittens, "I am here." Kittens purr back to say, "I am okay." Sometimes cats purr when they are sick or hurt, too. Purring helps them feel calm and may even help their bodies heal. A purr is a cat's way of talking!
