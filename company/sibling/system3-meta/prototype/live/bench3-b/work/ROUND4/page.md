# Round 4: Moons

**Q1.** In what year did astronauts first walk on our Moon?

**Answer:** 1969

**Why:** Two astronauts from the Apollo 11 trip stepped onto the dusty ground of the Moon in July 1969.

**Q2.** What is the name of the biggest moon in the whole solar system?

**Answer:** Ganymede

**Why:** Ganymede goes around Jupiter, and it is about one and a half times as wide as our own Moon.

**Q3.** Which moon of Jupiter has more volcanoes that erupt than any other world we know?

**Answer:** Io

**Why:** Io gets squeezed and stretched as it moves on its path, and this makes it hot inside, so lava shoots out of hundreds of volcanoes.

**Q4.** Which moon of Jupiter is covered in a thick shell of ice with a big salty ocean under it?

**Answer:** Europa

**Why:** Scientists think the ocean under the ice on Europa may hold more water than all the seas on Earth.
