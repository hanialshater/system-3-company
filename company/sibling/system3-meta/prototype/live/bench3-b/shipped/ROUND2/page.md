# Round 2: The Rocky Planets

**Q1.** Which planet is closest to the Sun?

**Answer:** Mercury

**Why:** Mercury goes around the Sun on the smallest path of all the planets, so no planet is nearer to the Sun.

**Q2.** Which planet is the only one we know of that has living things on it?

**Answer:** Earth

**Why:** Earth has air to breathe, liquid water and just the right warmth, and so far no other planet is known to have life.

**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest of all, even though it is not the closest one to the Sun?

**Answer:** Venus

**Why:** Venus has thick clouds and heavy air that trap the heat like a blanket, so it stays hotter than any other planet.

**Q4.** What is the name of the giant volcano on Mars that is the tallest in the whole solar system?

**Answer:** Olympus Mons

**Why:** Olympus Mons is more than twice as tall as Mount Everest, the tallest mountain on Earth.
