# Judge 1: Solar System Quiz Packs (ages 8-10)

| Criterion | Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Accuracy | Tie | I found no false statements in either pack, and both simplify fairly (A: Olympus Mons as wide as Arizona, methane lakes on Titan; B: Ganymede about 1.5 times as wide as the Moon, Halley's Comet due back in 2061); A's "almost all of it" and "wider than a small planet" are a little vague, and B's "tallest volcano in the whole solar system" is slightly disputed, so the small flaws even out. |
| 2. Fun / wow | B (narrowly) | B has more jaw-drop moments a child could repeat at dinner: Io's hundreds of erupting volcanoes, Europa holding more water than all Earth's seas, a storm wider than Earth, a comet they could see in 2061. A's best images (heat that melts lead, a planet rolling on its side, spacecraft flying through the asteroid belt) come close. |
| 3. Difficulty | A | A's rounds climb cleanly (Mars, Mercury, Venus, Olympus Mons; meteor, Pluto, asteroid belt, then the comet-tail puzzle), while B undercuts its own climb: Round 1 Q4 hands over "one is helium" in the hardest slot, Round 4 opens with a hard history date (1969), and Round 2 Q4 names Mars in the question. |
| 4. Why lines | A | Almost every A line adds a new idea (rust, 88-day year, 165-year orbit, reflected moonlight, how a comet's tail works), while several B lines mostly repeat the answer (Mercury "no planet is nearer", the 8 minutes, asteroids "in this band", comets as ice and dust). |
| 5. Coverage | A | A covers 7 of the 8 planets plus the Moon and phases, Ganymede, Titan, meteors, Pluto, the asteroid belt and comets with little overlap, while B leaves out Mars and Neptune as answers, makes Jupiter or one of its moons the answer five times (Jupiter, Great Red Spot, Ganymede, Io, Europa) and asks about comets twice. |
| 6. Publish | A | A is ready to publish as is (the only fix is a cosmetic one: its Round 5 title says "Space Travel" but the round has no space-travel question), while B needs its giveaway hint and its Jupiter-heavy overlap fixed first. |

**Publish at all?**
- A: **Yes**
- B: **Yes** (it is accurate and fun; it is just the weaker of the two on difficulty and coverage)

**Overall pick: A.** It is just as accurate as B, its difficulty climbs better, its Why lines add more, and it covers more of the solar system. B's stronger wow moments do not make up for the gap.
