# Ocean Animals Quiz

# Round 1: Ocean Mammals

**Q1.** Which animal is the largest animal that has ever lived on Earth?

**Answer:** A blue whale

**Why:** The blue whale is even bigger than the biggest dinosaurs were, so it is the largest animal of all time.

**Q2.** Which smart sea mammal finds its food by making clicks and listening for the echo?

**Answer:** A dolphin

**Why:** A dolphin sends out clicks that bounce off fish and come back as echoes, and this trick is called echolocation.

**Q3.** Which furry sea mammal uses a rock as a tool to crack open shells?

**Answer:** A sea otter

**Why:** A sea otter floats on its back, puts a rock on its belly, and bangs a shell on it until it opens.

**Q4.** Which slow, gentle sea mammal eats plants and is also called a sea cow?

**Answer:** A manatee

**Why:** A manatee munches sea grass for many hours each day, much like a cow grazing on a field, so people call it a sea cow.

# Round 2: Fish and Sharks

**Q1.** Which small orange and white fish lives safely among the stinging arms of a sea anemone?

**Answer:** A clownfish

**Why:** A clownfish has a slimy coat that keeps the anemone from stinging it, so it can hide there safely.

**Q2.** Which fast fish with a tall fin on its back has a skeleton made of cartilage, not bone?

**Answer:** A shark

**Why:** A shark's skeleton is made of cartilage, the bendy stuff in your ears, which is lighter than bone.

**Q3.** Which fish is the largest fish in the whole ocean?

**Answer:** A whale shark

**Why:** The whale shark is the largest fish of all, yet it eats tiny food like plankton and small fish.

**Q4.** Which fish has a father that carries the eggs in a pouch until they hatch?

**Answer:** A seahorse

**Why:** A seahorse mom puts her eggs in the dad's belly pouch, and he carries them until the babies hatch and swim out.

# Round 3: Animals Without Backbones

**Q1.** Which sea animal with a soft belly moves into an empty shell and carries it like a home?

**Answer:** A hermit crab

**Why:** A hermit crab has a soft belly, so it lives in an empty shell and swaps it for a bigger one as it grows.

**Q2.** Which animal is not a fish, even though many people call it a starfish?

**Answer:** A sea star

**Why:** A sea star has no backbone and no fins like a fish, and it can grow back an arm that it has lost.

**Q3.** Which soft, see-through sea animal floats in the water and has no brain?

**Answer:** A jellyfish

**Why:** A jellyfish has no brain, so it uses a simple net of nerves to feel what is around it.

**Q4.** Which sea animal with 8 arms has 3 hearts inside its body?

**Answer:** An octopus

**Why:** An octopus has 3 hearts, with 2 that pump to its gills and 1 that pumps to the rest of its body.

## Changes

- Round 3, Q1 Why line: "soft body" changed to "soft belly". Only a hermit crab's belly is soft (its front and claws have a hard shell), and this now matches the question.
- Round 3, Q2 Why line: "no fins or gills like a fish" changed to "no backbone and no fins like a fish". Sea stars do breathe through tiny skin gills, so "no gills" was not quite true; the backbone fact is true and belongs to Round 3.
