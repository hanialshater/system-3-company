# Round 3: Animals Without Backbones

All four animals in this round have no backbone at all. Can you name them?

**Q1.** Which sea animal with a soft belly moves into an empty shell and carries it like a home?

**Answer:** A hermit crab

**Why:** A hermit crab has a soft body, so it lives in an empty shell and swaps it for a bigger one as it grows.

**Q2.** Which animal is not a fish, even though many people call it a starfish?

**Answer:** A sea star

**Why:** A sea star has no fins or gills like a fish, and it can grow back an arm that it has lost.

**Q3.** Which soft, see-through sea animal floats in the water and has no brain?

**Answer:** A jellyfish

**Why:** A jellyfish has no brain, so it uses a simple net of nerves to feel what is around it.

**Q4.** Which sea animal with eight arms has three hearts inside its body?

**Answer:** An octopus

**Why:** An octopus has three hearts, with two that pump to its gills and one that pumps to the rest of its body.
