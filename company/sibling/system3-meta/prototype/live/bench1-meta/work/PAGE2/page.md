# Pip Meets Old Hoo

Hoo-hoo. Hoo-hoo.
A soft sound floats into the den.
It is slow. It is low.
It does not sound cross at all.

Pip lifts her tail from her eyes.
She creeps to the den door.
She peeks out, just her nose and one ear.

On a branch of the big oak sits an owl.
She is round and brown, with big gold eyes.

"Hoo is there?" says the owl. "Only me."
She blinks one slow blink.
"I am Old Hoo."

"Hello, Old Hoo," Pip says in a small voice.
"The dark is so big."

Old Hoo does not laugh.
She does not say, "Go to bed."
She just fluffs her feathers.
"Wait," she says. "And look."

So Pip waits.
And Pip looks.

Up, up, past the top of the big oak,
one tiny light comes out.
Twinkle.
Then two.
Then three.
Then more and more, all across the sky.

"Stars," Pip whispers.
"They were there all along," says Old Hoo softly.

The sky is not so empty now.
The dark looks soft, like a blanket with sparkles.
Pip stays right by the den door, warm and safe.
Old Hoo sits close by in the oak.

Then, just past the den,
something tiny glows.
Pip tips her head.

What is that in the night?
