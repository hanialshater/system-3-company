# Pip and the Big Dark

It is bedtime in the den.
The sun has gone down.
The light is going, little by little.

Pip is a small fox.
She has soft red fur and a long, fluffy tail.
Her bed is warm. Her blanket is snug.
But Pip is not sleepy.

She looks at the wall.
The shadows look huge.
One shadow is tall and thin.
One shadow is wide and round.
They stretch and sway.

Outside, the wood is very quiet.
The dark is big. It is so big!
It fills the den door.
It fills the whole clearing.
It fills the space by the big oak.

Pip holds her blanket tight.
Her ears twitch.
Her nose wiggles.
Her heart goes thump, thump, thump.

"The dark is too big," Pip whispers.
"It is too big for a small fox like me."

So Pip pulls her tail over her eyes.
Now she cannot see the shadows.
Now she cannot see the dark.
She is a small, warm ball of fox.

Her bed is still warm.
Her den is still home.
Pip feels a little better, safe and snug.
But one small question stays with her.

What is that in the night?
