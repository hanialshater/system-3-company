# Three Brave Steps

The tiny glow blinks on.
It blinks off.
It blinks on again.

It is a firefly!
She giggles a tiny giggle.
"I am Glim," she says. "Hello, Pip!"

Then more lights come.
One, two, ten little fireflies.
They hum and they giggle.
They make a little path of light,
just outside the den door.

"Blink, wink, follow the light!" they sing.

Pip looks at the path.
It is short. It glows soft and gold.
It goes from the den door to the big oak.
Old Hoo sits in the oak and nods her slow nod.

Pip takes a deep breath.

One brave step.
Her paw is on the cool grass.

Two brave steps.
The fireflies blink and wink around her.

Three brave steps.
Now Pip is by the big oak.
She stops right there.
That is far enough.

Pip looks back.
There is her den, just behind her.
She can see her warm bed.
She can see her snug blanket.
Home is right there.

"I did it," Pip whispers.
"Yes, you did!" giggles Glim.
"Blink, wink, follow the light!"

Pip smiles a small smile.
The night does not feel so big now.

Then, in the grass, something goes rustle, rustle.
And somewhere close, something sings.

What is that in the night?
