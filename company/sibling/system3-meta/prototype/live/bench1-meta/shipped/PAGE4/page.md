# Who Is in the Grass?

Pip stands by the big oak.
Her den door is right there.
The stars blink up high.

Rustle, rustle.
Something moves in the leaves.
Pip holds her breath.
"What is that in the night?"

Two small eyes peek out.
A round little face.
Lots of soft spikes.
Snuffle, snuffle.

"Hello," says a tiny voice.
"I am Bristle."
Bristle the hedgehog shakes a little.
"Are you a fox? I was a bit afraid of you."

Pip blinks.
"I was a bit afraid of you too," she says.
Bristle snuffles.
Pip smiles.
Now the rustle has a name.

Then a new sound comes from the grass.
Chirr-chirr. Chirr-chirr.
It goes up and down, like a song.

"Who sings?" asks Pip.
Bristle points with a snuffly nose.
In the grass sit lots of crickets.
It is the cricket choir.
"Chirr-chirr," they sing.
"Chirr-chirr, good night."

Pip sways to the song.
Bristle sways too.
Old Hoo sits in the big oak.
Glim and the fireflies blink, wink, all around.

The night is full of sounds.
And every sound is a friend.

Pip looks out at the dark.
She tips her head.
She is not shaking now.
She asks it with a smile.
"What is that in the night?"
