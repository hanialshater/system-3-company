# Goodnight, Night

Pip goes back into her den.
She curls up in her soft bed.
She leaves the den door open a crack.
Just a crack, so she can see out.

The stars shine in the dark.
The big oak stands by the door.
Pip pulls up her tail.
But not over her eyes.
She tucks it under her chin.

"Goodnight, Old Hoo," says Pip.
From the big oak comes a low, kind sound.
"Hoo is there? Only me."

"Goodnight, Glim," says Pip.
Glim and the fireflies glow by the door.
"Blink, wink, follow the light," they giggle.

"Goodnight, Bristle," says Pip.
Out in the leaves comes a small snuffle.
Snuffle, snuffle. Goodnight.

"Goodnight, cricket choir," says Pip.
The grass sings back, soft and slow.
Chirr-chirr. Chirr-chirr.

Pip yawns a big yawn.
Her eyes feel heavy.
The shadows are just shadows now.
The dark is not too big.
It is full of friends.

Pip is not afraid.
She is warm.
She is home.
She is sleepy.

The night hums a quiet song.
Pip closes her eyes.
She smiles one last sleepy smile.

"Goodnight, night."
