# Why Do Dogs Wag Their Tails?

Dogs cannot talk like we do, so they use their tails to tell us how they feel. A big, fast wag often means a dog is happy and excited to see you. A slow wag can mean the dog is not sure yet. If the tail is tucked down low, the dog may feel scared. Dogs also wag to say hello to other dogs. Watching a tail helps you understand a dog!
