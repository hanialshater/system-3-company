# Why Do Cats Purr?

Have you ever heard a cat purr? It sounds like a tiny, soft motor. Cats often purr when they feel happy and safe, like when you pet them gently. Mother cats purr to their kittens, and the kittens can feel it even before they can see. Sometimes cats purr when they are hurt or scared, too. Purring helps them feel calm and may even help their bodies heal. So a purr is a cat's way of saying many things!
