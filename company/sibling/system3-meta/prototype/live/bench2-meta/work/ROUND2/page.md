# Round 2: Rocky planets

**Q1.** Which planet is closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Mercury
**Why:** It zips around the Sun on the smallest path, so one of its years is only about three Earth months.

**Q2.** Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
**Answer:** Mars
**Why:** Its ground is covered in rusty dust, like the red rust on an old bike left out in the rain.

**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest, even though it is not the closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Venus
**Why:** Its thick clouds trap heat like a blanket, so it is even hotter than Mercury.

**Q4.** What is the name of the giant volcano on Mars, the tallest in the solar system?
**Answer:** Olympus Mons
**Why:** It is about two and a half times taller than Mount Everest, and it is wider than many countries.
