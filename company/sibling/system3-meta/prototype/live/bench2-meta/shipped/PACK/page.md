# Solar System Quiz Pack

# Round 1: The Sun and the solar system

**Q1.** What kind of object is the Sun?
**Answer:** A star
**Why:** The Sun is a huge, hot ball of glowing gas, like the stars you see at night, only much closer.

**Q2.** How many planets go around the Sun?
**Answer:** Eight
**Why:** Pluto used to be on the list, but now it is called a dwarf planet, so eight planets are left.

**Q3.** What gas is the Sun mostly made of?
**Answer:** Hydrogen
**Why:** Deep inside the Sun, tiny bits of this gas squeeze together and give off the light and heat we feel.

**Q4.** How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?
**Answer:** About 8 minutes
**Why:** Light is super fast, but the Sun is so far away that the trip still takes a while.

# Round 2: Rocky planets

**Q1.** Which planet is closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Mercury
**Why:** It zips around the Sun on the smallest path, so one of its years is only about three Earth months.

**Q2.** Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
**Answer:** Mars
**Why:** Its ground is covered in rusty dust, like the red rust on an old bike left out in the rain.

**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest, even though it is not the closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Venus
**Why:** Its thick clouds trap heat like a blanket, so it is even hotter than Mercury.

**Q4.** What is the name of the giant volcano on Mars, the tallest in the solar system?
**Answer:** Olympus Mons
**Why:** It is about two and a half times taller than Mount Everest, and it is wider than many countries.

# Round 3: Giant planets

**Q1.** Which planet is the biggest in our solar system?
**Answer:** Jupiter
**Why:** It is so huge that about 1,300 Earths could fit inside it, like marbles in a giant ball.

**Q2.** Which planet is famous for its bright, wide rings?
**Answer:** Saturn
**Why:** Its rings are made of billions of chunks of ice and rock, and the ice shines when sunlight hits it.

**Q3.** What is the name of the giant storm on Jupiter that people have watched for more than 150 years?
**Answer:** Great Red Spot
**Why:** This swirling storm is like a huge hurricane, and it is wider than our whole Earth.

**Q4.** Which planet spins tipped over on its side, like a rolling ball?
**Answer:** Uranus
**Why:** Because it is tipped over, each end takes turns pointing at the Sun for many years at a time.

# Round 4: Moons

**Q1.** What do we call the changing shapes of the Moon that we see each month?
**Answer:** Phases
**Why:** The Sun lights half the Moon, and as it circles us we see more or less of that bright half.

**Q2.** In what year did people first walk on the Moon?
**Answer:** 1969
**Why:** Two astronauts stepped out, and their footprints are still there because the Moon has no wind or rain.

**Q3.** Which moon of Jupiter has the most volcanoes?
**Answer:** Io
**Why:** Jupiter pulls and squeezes Io like a stress ball, and that rubbing makes enough heat to melt rock.

**Q4.** What is the biggest moon in the whole solar system?
**Answer:** Ganymede
**Why:** This moon of Jupiter is so big that it is even wider than the planet Mercury.

# Round 5: Small worlds

**Q1.** What is the real name for a shooting star?
**Answer:** A meteor
**Why:** It is a tiny space rock that glows as it burns up in our air, not a star.

**Q2.** Which small, icy world far past Neptune has been called a dwarf planet since 2006?
**Answer:** Pluto
**Why:** It is round like a planet, but it shares its path around the Sun with lots of other icy bits.

**Q3.** What do we call the wide ring of space rocks between Mars and Jupiter?
**Answer:** The asteroid belt
**Why:** Millions of rocks circle the Sun there, but they are so spread out that spacecraft fly through safely.

**Q4.** What is the biggest object in the asteroid belt?
**Answer:** Ceres
**Why:** It is so big and round that it counts as a dwarf planet, just like Pluto.
