The product: "Solar System Quiz Pack", a quiz pack for ages 8-10. A style sheet (STYLE) sets the format and five round topics; five rounds (ROUND1-ROUND5) of 4 questions each, every question with `**Qn.**`, `**Answer:**` and a one-sentence `**Why:**`; then PACK assembles the shipped rounds and fixes anything that clashes across rounds. Every fact must be true; no answer may appear in two rounds. The kickoff decisions are in meeting MTG-1 (`node meta.mjs meeting show --id=MTG-1`, minutes in the lane:e channel). Shipped work is in live/bench2-meta/shipped/<TASK>/page.md.
