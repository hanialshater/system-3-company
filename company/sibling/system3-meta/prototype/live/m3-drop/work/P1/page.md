# Why Do Cats Purr?

Have you ever heard a cat purr? It sounds like a soft, happy motor. Cats purr when they feel safe and cozy, like when you pet them gently. Mother cats purr to help their tiny kittens find them. Cats can also purr when they feel sick or scared, because purring helps them feel calm. So a purr is a cat's way of saying, "I feel good," or "Please help me feel better."
