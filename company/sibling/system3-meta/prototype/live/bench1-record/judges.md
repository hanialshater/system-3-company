| Criterion | Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Consistency | B | B keeps pronouns, short-line layout, present tense and settings on every page; A switches between paragraph and verse pages, never gives Pip a pronoun, and has Pip "peek out of the den" while tucked into bed. |
