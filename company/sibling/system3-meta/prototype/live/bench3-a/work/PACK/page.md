# Solar System Quiz Pack
# Round 1: The Sun
**Q1.** The Sun looks like a ball of fire, but what kind of object is it really?
**Answer:** A star
**Why:** The Sun is the closest star to us, so it looks much bigger and brighter than the stars at night.
**Q2.** How much of all the stuff in our solar system is packed into the Sun?
**Answer:** Almost all of it
**Why:** The Sun is so heavy that all the planets put together weigh only a tiny bit next to it.
**Q3.** About how long does light from the Sun take to reach us?
**Answer:** About 8 minutes
**Why:** Light is the fastest thing there is, but the Sun is so far away the trip takes about 8 minutes.
**Q4.** Which gas makes up most of the Sun?
**Answer:** Hydrogen
**Why:** Deep inside, the Sun squeezes hydrogen into helium, and that is what makes its light and heat.
# Round 2: Rocky Planets
**Q1.** Which planet is often called the Red Planet?
**Answer:** Mars
**Why:** Its dust and rocks are full of rust, so the whole ground looks rusty red.
**Q2.** Which planet is closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Mercury
**Why:** Mercury zooms around the Sun faster than any other planet, taking only about 88 days per trip.
**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest, even though it is not the closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Venus
**Why:** Thick clouds trap the heat like a heavy blanket, so it gets hot enough to melt lead.
**Q4.** What is the name of the biggest volcano found on any planet?
**Answer:** Olympus Mons
**Why:** This giant volcano is about as wide as the whole state of Arizona, and much taller than Mount Everest.
# Round 3: Giant Planets
**Q1.** Which planet is the biggest in our solar system?
**Answer:** Jupiter
**Why:** It is so big that more than 1,000 Earths could fit inside it.
**Q2.** Which planet is best known for its bright, wide rings?
**Answer:** Saturn
**Why:** Its rings are made of lots of chunks of ice and rock, some as tiny as dust.
**Q3.** Which planet is the farthest from the Sun?
**Answer:** Neptune
**Why:** It is so far away that a single trip around the Sun takes it about 165 years.
**Q4.** Which planet spins on its side, rolling along like a ball?
**Answer:** Uranus
**Why:** Scientists think a huge crash long ago knocked it over, so it rolls as it circles the Sun.
# Round 4: Moons
**Q1.** What bright ball of rock lights our night sky and seems to change shape each month?
**Answer:** The Moon
**Why:** The Moon makes no light of its own, so it glows when light bounces off its gray rock.
**Q2.** What do we call the shapes the Moon seems to have, like full moon?
**Answer:** Phases
**Why:** As the Moon goes around us, we see more or less of its bright side each night.
**Q3.** Which moon is the largest moon of all the moons in our solar system?
**Answer:** Ganymede
**Why:** Ganymede is so big that it is wider than a small planet, and it is covered in ice and rock.
**Q4.** Which moon has thick orange air and lakes that are not made of water?
**Answer:** Titan
**Why:** Titan is so cold that its lakes hold liquid methane, a gas on Earth, instead of water.
# Round 5: Small Worlds and Space Travel
**Q1.** What do we call a quick streak of light from a space rock in our air?
**Answer:** A meteor
**Why:** Many people call them shooting stars, but most are tiny bits of rock and dust that glow as they fall.
**Q2.** Which small, icy world was called a planet until 2006?
**Answer:** Pluto
**Why:** In 2006, scientists named Pluto a dwarf planet, which means a small round world that circles the Sun.
**Q3.** What is the wide ring of space rocks between the rocky and giant planets?
**Answer:** The asteroid belt
**Why:** Its rocks are so spread out that spacecraft have flown right through it without hitting any.
**Q4.** Near the Sun, a comet grows a long tail. Which way does that tail point?
**Answer:** Away from the Sun
**Why:** Sunlight and a wind from the Sun push its gas and dust away, even as the comet leaves.
