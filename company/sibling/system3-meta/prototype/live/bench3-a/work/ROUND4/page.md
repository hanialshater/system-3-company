# Round 4: Moons
**Q1.** What big, bright ball of rock lights up our night sky and changes shape each month?
**Answer:** The Moon
**Why:** The Moon makes no light of its own, so it glows when light bounces off its gray rock.
**Q2.** What do we call the changing shapes of the Moon, like full moon and half moon?
**Answer:** Phases
**Why:** As the Moon goes around us, we see more or less of its bright side each night.
**Q3.** Which moon is the largest moon of all the moons in our solar system?
**Answer:** Ganymede
**Why:** Ganymede is so big that it is wider than a small planet, and it even has its own magnetic field.
**Q4.** Which moon has thick orange air and lakes that are not made of water?
**Answer:** Titan
**Why:** Titan is so cold that its lakes are filled with liquid gas instead of water.
