# Round 5: Small Worlds and Space Travel
**Q1.** What do we call a quick streak of light when a space rock zips into our air?
**Answer:** A meteor
**Why:** Many people call them shooting stars, but most are tiny bits of rock and dust that glow as they fall.
**Q2.** Which small, icy world was called a planet until 2006?
**Answer:** Pluto
**Why:** In 2006, scientists named Pluto a dwarf planet, which means a small round world that circles the Sun.
**Q3.** What do we call the wide ring of space rocks between the rocky and giant planets?
**Answer:** The asteroid belt
**Why:** Its rocks are so spread out that spacecraft have flown right through it without hitting any.
**Q4.** A comet has a long tail. Which way does that tail point?
**Answer:** Away from the Sun
**Why:** Sunlight and a wind of tiny bits from the Sun push the comet's ice and dust out behind it.
