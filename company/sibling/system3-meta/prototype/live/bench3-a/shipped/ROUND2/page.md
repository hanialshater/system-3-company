# Round 2: Rocky Planets
**Q1.** Which planet is often called the Red Planet?
**Answer:** Mars
**Why:** Its dust and rocks are full of rust, so the whole ground looks rusty red.
**Q2.** Which planet is closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Mercury
**Why:** Mercury zooms around the Sun faster than any other planet, taking only about 88 days per trip.
**Q3.** Which planet is the hottest, even though it is not the closest to the Sun?
**Answer:** Venus
**Why:** Thick clouds trap the heat like a heavy blanket, so it gets hot enough to melt lead.
**Q4.** What is the name of the biggest volcano found on any planet?
**Answer:** Olympus Mons
**Why:** This giant volcano is about as wide as the whole state of Arizona, and much taller than Mount Everest.
