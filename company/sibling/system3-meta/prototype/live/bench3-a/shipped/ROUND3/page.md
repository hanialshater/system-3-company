# Round 3: Giant Planets
**Q1.** Which planet is the biggest in our solar system?
**Answer:** Jupiter
**Why:** It is so big that more than 1,000 Earths could fit inside it.
**Q2.** Which planet is best known for its bright, wide rings?
**Answer:** Saturn
**Why:** Its rings are made of lots of chunks of ice and rock, some as tiny as dust.
**Q3.** Which planet is the farthest from the Sun?
**Answer:** Neptune
**Why:** It is so far away that a single trip around the Sun takes it about 165 years.
**Q4.** Which planet spins on its side, rolling along like a ball?
**Answer:** Uranus
**Why:** Scientists think a huge crash long ago knocked it over, so it rolls as it circles the Sun.
