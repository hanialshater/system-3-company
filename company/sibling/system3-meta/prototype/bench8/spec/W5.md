# Wave 5: the rest of common C

Read CONTRACT.md first. W1-W4 stay in scope (regressions). This is the largest wave: most of the gcc torture tests
first fit here.

## In scope

- **switch.** `switch`, `case`, `default`, fall-through, `break`, and nesting inside loops; case values are integer
  constant expressions.
- **goto.** `goto` and labels, forward and backward.
- **Casts.** Explicit casts between all the scalar types and pointers, `(void)` casts, and the conversions they
  imply.
- **Integer types:**
  - `unsigned`, `long`, `long long`, `short`, `signed char`, `unsigned char`, `_Bool`, in every combination;
  - integer promotions and the usual arithmetic conversions;
  - unsigned wraparound;
  - sign and zero extension;
  - literal suffixes `u`, `l`, `ul`, `ll`, `ull`, and literals too big for `int` that take the next type up.
- **Bitwise.** `& | ^ ~ << >>` and `&= |= ^= <<= >>=`; `>>` on signed values is arithmetic.
- **Function pointers.** Declare, assign, compare and call them; use them as parameters and in arrays and structs;
  pass them to `qsort`; call through `(*fp)(…)` and `fp(…)`.
- **Storage.** `static` locals and file-scope `static` variables and functions, and `extern` declarations.
- **Floating:**
  - `float` and `double`: arithmetic, comparisons, literals;
  - conversions to and from integers;
  - arguments and returns in SSE registers per the ABI, and variadic calls (`printf("%f")`, which promotes float to
    double, with `%al` = the SSE count);
  - `va_arg(ap, double)`.
- **Misc:**
  - compound literals (`(struct P){1, 2}`) and bit-fields;
  - flexible array members;
  - `volatile`, `restrict` and `inline` (accepted; `inline` may be ignored);
  - `_Static_assert` and `__func__`.

## Floats: the decision

**Floats are included**, as their own category (`W5 floating`). The reasons:

- They are part of everyday C, and the gcc torture suite uses them a lot.
- The SSE code generation and the float part of the SysV ABI are small and well documented.
- glibc's printf handles the formatting, so a compiler does not need its own float-to-text code.

Because they are a separate category, a compiler that skips them loses only that category's tests, and W6's
benchmarks use no floats. `long double` (x87), `_Complex` and decimal floats stay out.

## Categories

`W5 floating`, `W5 function-pointers`, `W5 goto`, `W5 switch`, `W5 misc`, `W5 storage`, `W5 bitwise`, `W5 casts`,
`W5 integer-types`. A test's category is the first of these it uses.

## Out of scope in every wave

- `long double`, `_Complex`, variable-length arrays, `_Generic`, `_Atomic`, `_Thread_local`, `_Alignas`;
- wide and Unicode string or character literals;
- `#pragma` and `#line`;
- K&R-style definitions with parameter lists;
- GNU extensions: statement expressions, `typeof`, `__attribute__`, builtins other than the va intrinsics, labels as
  values, nested functions, zero-length arrays, case ranges.

No test uses any of these.
