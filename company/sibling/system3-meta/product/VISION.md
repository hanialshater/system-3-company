## The picture

A **city of companies**, each a self-running organisation of agents with its own repository, its own product and its
own HQ, all built on one shared platform.

- **Meta is company zero.** Its product is the platform: the kernel, the starter kit, the generator and the city
  registry. It runs on its own platform (it dogfoods every feature before another company gets it).
- **Every other company** is started from a spec by Meta's generator, gets the starter kit on day one, and then grows
  its own domain: its product, roles, referees and HQ pages, built by its own HQ developers.
- **The city** is a social network for company owners and a public repository of the knowledge they choose to share;
  any owner creates a company; a company's knowledge stays private unless its owner publishes it.

## Why it can work (the evidence so far)

- The autonomous org style matches one strong agent at the same budget and finishes in about half the wall-clock time
  on broad work (Benchmark 12: 88.5% against 88.97% of SQLite's own tests, $295 each); members divided the work by
  themselves and every one of 46 lands kept or raised the score.
- Mixed models work: cheaper members earned 46% of the gains at about 60% of the price.
- Off-boarding into a persistent knowledge base works: 158 entries with evidence and beliefs, read by every member of
  the next run.
- What is still open: an org that beats one agent outright (Benchmark 13: scale and fallible agents, toward 100%), and
  a company bootstrapped from a spec by someone other than the founder (milestone M6).

## The migration path

1. **Write down the boundary** between the starter kit and each company ([STARTER.md](STARTER.md)) and the catalogue of
   features ([FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md)), so nothing is lost when code moves.
2. **Fold the proven org mechanics into the kernel** (from bench11/bench12 into the kernel's tracker, channels,
   meetings and learning), one platform and one event log.
3. **The starter HQ**: the generic parts of today's HQ (office view, people, replay, library as knowledge browser) plus
   a tracker sidebar, progress and spend, and meeting records, reading any company's event log, with extension points.
4. **Meta migrates first** to its own repository, running on the starter kit (company zero).
5. **The lesson company migrates** to its own repository on the starter kit, keeping its custom features.
6. **A new company is bootstrapped from a spec** (milestone M6), and the city registry shows all three.
