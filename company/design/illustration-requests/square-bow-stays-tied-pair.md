## What the images must show
Two shoes from directly above, same shoe, same lace, same light, framed identically:
1. A square (reef) bow: loops lie across the shoe, left and right.
2. A granny bow: the bow has twisted so its loops point along the shoe, toward the toe and the heel.

## What they must NOT show
No ticks, crosses or colour coding for right and wrong (the stage adds those). No text, logos or brands. No feet in socks with patterns that read as letters.

## Where they go on the card
The two panels of the side-by-side comparison, 4:3, 1024 × 768 each.

## Alt text (written by the interaction designer; the illustrator may suggest edits)
1. "A shoe from above with a shoelace bow whose loops lie across the shoe."
2. "The same shoe with a twisted bow whose loops point toward the toe and the heel."

## Accuracy notes for the illustrator
The only difference between the two images is the bow's orientation (across vs along) and the slight twist of the granny's knot centre. Same lace length, same tightness, same loop size. The fact-checker checks both against a knot reference before acceptance.
