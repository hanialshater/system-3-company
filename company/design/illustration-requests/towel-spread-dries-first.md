## What the image must show
A damp bath towel hanging spread flat over a simple indoor drying rack, in soft daylight. The cloth looks heavy and slightly darker with water, with a faint sense of air moving past it (a curtain stirring at the window edge is enough).

## What it must NOT show
No heater, radiator, iron or tumble dryer near the towel. No steam. No text, labels, brands or patterns with letters. No faces.

## Where it goes on the card
The hero above the prediction question, 1:1, 1024 × 1024.

## Alt text (written by the interaction designer; the illustrator may suggest edits)
"A damp towel spread flat over an indoor drying rack, next to a window with a curtain stirring."

## Accuracy notes for the illustrator
The towel hangs in a single layer over the rack bars, so both faces are open to the air. Water darkens the cloth evenly; no drips pooling on the floor. Room temperature: no steam.
