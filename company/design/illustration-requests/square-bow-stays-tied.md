## What the image must show
Two hands finishing a shoelace bow on a plain, unbranded running shoe, seen from slightly above. The bow is a square (reef) bow: its two loops lie across the shoe, left and right, not pointing toward the toe and heel.

## What it must NOT show
No logos, stripes or swooshes that read as a brand. No text. No double knot (it would muddy the lesson). No face.

## Where it goes on the card
The hero above the prediction question, 1:1, 1024 × 1024.

## Alt text (written by the interaction designer; the illustrator may suggest edits)
"Two hands pulling a shoelace bow tight on a running shoe; the bow's loops lie across the shoe."

## Accuracy notes for the illustrator
This is accuracy-critical: the fact-checker will check the knot. In a square bow the loops point sideways across the instep, roughly at right angles to the length of the shoe, and the knot centre is flat. Use one lace colour so the crossing can be read. If you cannot draw the crossing reliably, show the finished bow from above with the loops clearly across, and say so in provenance notes.
