## What the image must show
One or two sentences, concrete. Example: "Two glasses of water with ice; the left one has a spoonful of salt being stirred in."

## What it must NOT show
For example: no thermometer readings (the stage shows the numbers), no brand labels, no text.

For example: the hero above the prediction question, 1:1.
