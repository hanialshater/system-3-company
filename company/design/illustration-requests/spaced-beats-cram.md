## What the image must show
A study desk by a window in daylight: a small stack of blank flashcards, one card held in a hand, an open notebook with wavy pencil marks that are not letters, and a cup of water. The mood is calm and unhurried.

## What it must NOT show
No readable writing, numbers, calendar dates or clock faces (no text in images). No coffee or energy drinks, no night scene, no tired or stressed person (this lesson makes no sleep or caffeine claims). No brands, no faces.

## Where it goes on the card
The hero above the prediction question, 1:1, 1024 × 1024.

## Alt text (written by the interaction designer; the illustrator may suggest edits)
"A hand holding a blank flashcard over a tidy desk with an open notebook and a glass of water, in daylight."

## Accuracy notes for the illustrator
Daylight, not a late-night lamp scene: the lesson is about spreading study over days, not studying late. Marks in the notebook are abstract squiggles, never letters.
