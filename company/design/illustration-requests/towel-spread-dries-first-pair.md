## What the images must show
Two step images of the same rack, same towel, same light:
1. Before: the wet towel folded in quarters and draped over one rack bar, a thick, compact stack.
2. After: the same towel opened out and spread in a single layer across the rack.

## What they must NOT show
No clock, timer or numbers (the stage shows the times). No heater or dryer. No text, labels or brands. The two images must not suggest that one towel has more water than the other.

## Where they go on the card
The before/after toggle on the stage, 4:3, 1024 × 768 each. They sit beside the stage's own diagram; keep the rack in the same position in both so the toggle reads as one change.

## Alt text (written by the interaction designer; the illustrator may suggest edits)
1. "A wet towel folded in quarters into a thick stack over one bar of a drying rack."
2. "The same wet towel spread out in a single layer across the drying rack."

## Accuracy notes for the illustrator
Folded in quarters means four layers thick, about a quarter of the spread size. Same towel colour, same wetness and same rack in both. The camera does not move between the two.
