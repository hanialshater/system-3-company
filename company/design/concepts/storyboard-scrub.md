# Storyboard / scrub

A process over time: a timeline scrubber plus key frames.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from company/design/formats.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- Orbits, epidemics, algorithms: anything that unfolds over time.

## When not to
- Order matters but time does not (use the multi-card sequence).

## Anatomy
- A timeline scrubber.
- Key frames marked on the track.
- The reveal names the frames the learner visited.

## Rules
- Scrubbing backwards works.
- Key frames are reachable by the QA path.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion if it auto-plays.

- What does the proposed kit <Timeline> own: the track, the key frames, or the playback too?
