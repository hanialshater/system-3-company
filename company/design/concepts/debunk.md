# Debunk (Life Hacks)

A popular claim tested on the stage: the claim, the test, the result, then the mechanism.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from company/design/formats.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- Life Hacks lessons that test a popular claim.

## When not to
- The claim is not popular enough to be worth testing.
- The test would show a dangerous attempt (show the result instead).

## Anatomy
- The claim, quoted.
- The test, run by the learner.
- The result.
- The mechanism.
- The safety class, shown.

## Rules
- The safety class is visible.
- A 'doesn't work' picture shows the failed result, never a dangerous attempt.
