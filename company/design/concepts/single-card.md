# Single card

The default format: one stage, one mechanism, one picture and a few controls.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from company/design/formats.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- One mechanism that fits in one picture.
- The key state can be reached with up to six controls.

## When not to
- The idea needs two or more pictures in order (use the multi-card sequence).
- The idea is a comparison of two methods (use side-by-side).

## Anatomy
- Hook line above the stage.
- One stage SVG.
- Up to 6 controls in one or two rows.
- Readouts under the stage.
- Milestones on the key state.

## Rules
- Six controls at most.
- Milestones mark the key state, and the reveal is gated on them.
- Stage text stays within 25 words.
