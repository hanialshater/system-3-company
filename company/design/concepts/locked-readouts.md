# Locked readouts ('?')

Answer readouts show '?' until the learner has predicted.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from company/design/system.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- Every readout that would give the answer away.

## When not to
- Readouts that only echo a control's value.

## Anatomy
- Readout label.
- Value '?' while locked.

## Rules
- The answer is never visible before the prediction.
