# Chart style

Charts and diagrams: axes labelled on the stage, the model named, answers shown as '?' while locked.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from company/design/system.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- Every stage chart or diagram.

## When not to
- Decorative pictures (illustration hero).

## Anatomy
- stage-svg, capped at 46dvh.
- Labelled axes.
- A model label ('Toy model', the projection).
- Readouts under the chart.

## Rules
- Label the axes on the stage.
- Name the model.
- No overlapping labels.
- Round floats for display.
