# Preset buttons

Buttons that jump a parameter to named values (23 people, 183 people).

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from real lessons in src/demos (pattern seen in Demo.tsx); design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- A few values carry the idea; the question names them.

## When not to
- The learner should discover the value (use a slider).

## Anatomy
- A row of Controls with the values as labels.
- Often paired with a slider.

## Rules
- Every value named in the question or reveal has a preset (QA reachability).
- The active preset is marked.

## Open questions
- Pair with a slider always, or only when the range matters?
