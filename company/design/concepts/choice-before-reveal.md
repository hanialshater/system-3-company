# Choice before reveal

The learner commits to one of 2 to 4 options before the stage unlocks.

_Seeded by design-engineer on 2026-10-13 from src/kit/Lesson.tsx and company/product/demo-standard.md; design lead to confirm._

## When to use
- Every lesson: it is the lesson contract.

## When not to
- Never skipped.

## Anatomy
- Question.
- 2 to 4 options.
- A commit button.
- Locked stage visible behind.

## Rules
- Commit before anything moves.
- The question's numbers match the stage exactly.
