## Selection from rendered artifacts

Inspected the actual rendered PNGs first: `company/reviews/number-island/mockups/desktop.png`, `phone-a-continuous.png`, `phone-a-continuation.png`, `phone-b-regions-village.png`, and `phone-b-regions-mountain.png`. Then read the interaction and owner boundaries. Notes/README were initially absent, then supplied and read after the images, together with `number-island-visual.md`. Mockup measurements describe depicted geometry, not runtime target sizes or behavior.

**Choose phone B: region-focused map, with details immediately below; choose the desktop full island beside details.** Number Village, Fraction Harbour and Scaling Mountain are three selectable areas of the same twelve-lesson collection. The illustration and all available lesson stops remain usable without locks or any learner-state layer. List offers the entire collection in one action.

Phone A's continuous layout has pleasing geographic continuity, but a stop near the top is far from the single Open action below all three scenes. Its continuation image acknowledges rather than resolves that gap. Do not implement A or a fixed floating detail overlay. B keeps selected title and explicit Open action close to the selected scene, fits the five mountain stops, and makes all three areas visible before the scene.

The desktop composition successfully preserves a single coast, a continuous winding trail and the three landmarks at a glance. Keep the broad land shape and side-panel relationship. It is a layout hypothesis, not a requirement to reproduce every coordinate exactly.

## Concrete builder conditions

1. **Keep the immediate action relationship.** On phone, area choices → active region scene → selected full title/status/duration/Open action. No panel overlay, fixed-height clipping or remote bottom-of-page action. Allow ordinary vertical page growth when titles or text resize. The three region controls remain before the scene; desktop may repeat area controls in the sidebar only if duplicate focus stops add no confusion.
2. **Rendered size, not SVG size.** Use native HTML stop buttons with at least 44×44 CSS-pixel hit areas, preferably 48×48, at 320, 390 and desktop widths. Never scale the entire desktop control layer down with the illustration. Mobile scene coordinates/height must leave five mountain hit areas disjoint at 320; increase scene height or rearrange stop positions rather than shrink them. Keep a visible focus ring clear of the coast, scene clip and adjacent controls.
3. **Keep map/list and region controls unambiguous.** Follow the interaction spec's native-button/`aria-pressed` behavior. Map/List is presentation, area buttons choose geography, and numbered stops choose a lesson. Selection stays available and never becomes a disabled control. Distinguish keyboard focus from selected gold/double outline. Color alone is insufficient.
4. **Use real data in HTML.** The map uses stable editorial numbers; selected details and List show full actual titles and canonical publication labels from the public projection. Do not copy the mockup's title/status/duration literals into runtime collection metadata. Keep all twelve available lessons in List, not only the active area. Missing/empty visibility cases remain those in the interaction/engineering contracts.
5. **Refine original illustration without clutter.** Retain the quiet sea, warm coast, mint lowlands and moss mountain planes. Production may add layered shoreline, a few shrubs/rocks, simple roof/eave details, jetty planks, a small bridge and restrained summit contours. Keep a clear halo around every control and path. Scenery must not introduce text that competes with titles, tiny navigable objects, factual teaching diagrams, game rewards or continuous motion. Terrain remains decorative SVG; meaningful labels/controls remain HTML. No copied game assets.
6. **Retain legibility at the edges.** In desktop mockup, the mountain controls approach the scene's top/right; preserve margin for full focus rings and avoid placing a node over the summit flag. In phone B, the lower mountain stops approach the left/bottom coast; give them breathing room at narrow widths. Place optional scene captions outside the clipped SVG so text does not shrink or become a decorative-only source of meaning.
7. **Use the accepted copy and navigation boundaries.** The page is an optional collection, not a progress report. Preserve the existing feed; catalog supplies discovery. Use the current-URL-only allowlisted stop/view contract and explicit player return in the interaction spec/EDD. No storage, progress/history/account reads, completion/revisit markers, inferred learning, new tracking, rewards, checkpoints or automatic advancement.
8. **Use the visual handoff; accessibility remains separate evidence.** Adopt the page-local Paper/Card/Ink/Muted ink roles and decorative palette in `number-island-visual.md`, with explicit foreground/background in both system color preferences; do not change global tokens. Its measured candidate pairs support the handoff, not a built-artifact verdict. Keep ordinary text off Raised land/Sea except the handoff's permitted contrasting pairs. Use its paired Card/Ink focus outline and ensure full rings clear clipping and neighbors. Independent accessibility review must verify actual rendered contrast, keyboard operation, reflow and focus on the built artifact. This direction does not approve them.
9. **Remove mockup-only language.** No “Your next exploration,” “Level 01,” internal review annotation or fixed-frame dimension caption in the product. Use **Mathematics · {available count} lessons**, **Choose any stop to explore a lesson**, and **Selected stop** per the growth/visual handoffs. The depicted 176 px phone detail-card height is not an implementation constraint. Header links also need real 44 px hit areas; the drawn text bounds do not establish them.
