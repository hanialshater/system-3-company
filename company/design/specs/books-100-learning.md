## Learning boundary and the non-negotiable unit

Default learner: an adult who can compare small quantities and follow a labelled picture, but has not met this particular relationship. Each brief must replace that default with what its question actually needs. The objective is a transferable distinction: what changed, why it changed in this model, and when the distinction could matter. Recognising an author, remembering a slogan, pressing two preset buttons, or retrieving the toy's final number is not the objective.

Each brief supplies its own prediction question and 2–4 equally precise options, a plausible misconception, the input change that tests it, the required observed comparison, and a recall in a new situation. A book title alone supplies none of these. Stage controls may share code; the misconception, causal question and transfer decision must remain specific.

## Seven-family acceptance

These are implementations of the existing single-card format. **Approved conditionally for draft authoring only**, within the stated mathematical meaning and engineering's validation ranges. The independent artifact review still decides whether the picture actually teaches the mechanism. Match these names to EDD-010; do not reinterpret a convenient formula as an unrelated social claim.

| Family | Predict / act / notice | Acceptance condition and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `flow` | Predict which station improvement changes completed output; change a station; follow work through the serial line. | Draw the limiting station and constrained output. Show baseline and intervention, including whether the limiting station moves. `min(capacities)` is a steady serial-capacity model; it does not establish that a real company, city or person is a serial factory. |
| `payoff` | Predict which stated lottery/rule has the larger mean payoff; change a probability or outcome; inspect the two weighted branches. | The probability and both outcome contributions are visible. Mean payoff is not certainty, utility or suitability. A lesson about downside risk cannot be reduced to mean payoff if it needs a distribution the engine does not show. |
| `compound` | Predict the effect of a changed rate, horizon, initial stock or addition; inspect the recurrence path. | Show at least two updates and the changed term. Distinguish growth from repeated additions. Rates are stipulated; financial, learning or historical growth forecasts do not follow. Different starting amounts on the identical recurrence do not by themselves create different ideas. |
| `selection` | Predict the combined proportion after a changed sampling weight; alter counts or rates; inspect included groups and their denominators. | The weighted numerator and denominator change together and group rates remain inspectable. The engine shows aggregation/selection, not causal treatment effects, discrimination, diagnostic validity or a full Simpson reversal unless their necessary states are actually represented. |
| `network` | Predict reachable count under a changed branching rule; inspect paths through the bounded tree. | Every counted node/path is represented within design limits. This is collision-free branching until the cap, not a general social graph. No claims about weak ties, clustering, contagion thresholds, collective rationality or independent evidence without those mechanisms being represented. Route a non-tree need back to CM/design. |
| `feedback` | Predict stock after stated inflows/outflows; change one flow; inspect stock updates and bounds. | The container, inflow and outflow visibly account for the result. This formula has **fixed flows, not state-dependent feedback**. Use it for accumulation, net flow and depletion; reject a brief promising reinforcing/balancing feedback or oscillation unless engineering/design adds an approved causal representation. |
| `intervals` | Predict usable work under a changed chunk/overhead rule; inspect work and overhead segments inside the finite budget. | Draw whole chunks, overhead and unused remainder. Each chunk pays the stated overhead. This can show accounting/packing consequences, not empirically measured concentration, productivity, learning retention or the superiority of a named work method. |

No empirical evidence-comparison family is approved by this plan. Actual studies, new representations, research verdict comparisons, drag-only stages, random/agent simulations and non-default formats require the per-lesson learning/interaction route in `books-100-pr.md`.

## Brief distinctness and release to builders

For every brief, CM owns a short difference statement: **nearest existing/new lesson → shared element → new misconception or causal contrast → what this learner must decide differently**. Two entries that differ only by author, labels, units, coefficients, population, or the answer's magnitude fail this test. Shared family membership is allowed when the learner encounters a different checkable relationship: for example increasing a non-limiting station versus moving the bottleneck requires different comparisons. A cosmetic relabel of the same two-case calculation does not.

Builders may claim a brief only when its `knows`, `doesNotKnow`, misconception, objective, exact prediction case, counterfactual, recall and source holds are present. The inputs must produce the prediction answer under the **actual family formula**. The mechanism drawn must explain the stated gap. The default engine must never silently replace a brief's meaning to make a numeric answer fit. Missing or duplicated teaching goals return to cm-books; causal/representation gaps return through learning-designer to interaction-designer.

## Reveal and recall guardrails

The reveal normally uses three short beats, ≤90 words total: the saved comparison and visible cause; ≤20 original words attributing the book idea; the exact model/evidence limit. All counts are computed from the learner's two required cases and stay on screen with their case labels after Reset. Do not let a generic source caveat consume the causal explanation. A copied disclaimer cannot repair an inappropriate model.

Only call the kit's milestone interaction for meaningful progress toward the two required cases. The DOM-order path must reach those cases within the existing timing contract. Keep answer-bearing outputs hidden before commit; scenario labels name interventions, not winners. A prewritten verdict appearing after “Test” is not exploration. Stage ≤25 words, readable phone picture, no tiny labels to rescue fit.

Recall removes the original scene and changes the surface situation while preserving the relationship. Ask which intervention matters, which denominator to inspect, which missing assumption changes the conclusion, or which model is applicable. Do not ask the author/book name, quote completion, toy-number recall, or a universal recommendation. Recall and its answer/explanation are claims too; unverified attributions and empirical generalisations stay held there as well.

## Approved bounded groups and handoff

Use the existing **demo-builder** role through the normal claim lock. No more than ten slugs per claimed internal group, no concurrent ownership of a slug. One builder may process several groups sequentially. CoS records exact slugs from the frozen manifest when dispatching; a 34-book strand is four groups (10/10/10/4), never one 34-item build claim. This file does not create a second queue or remove host/build limits.

1. **Representatives:** the first eligible brief in each actually used family, at most seven slugs total, excluding any brief needing specialist design. Build these first, stop for the required independent family/phone/learner checks, and resolve shared findings before wide consumption.
2. **Remaining groups:** partition the remaining frozen-manifest entries in strand order into disjoint groups of ≤10. Root may give one builder several explicitly named groups, but the builder closes/releases each group before claiming the next. Reconcile the original 100 once, so representatives are not counted twice.
3. **Specialist group:** briefs requiring a non-default representation are excluded from ordinary groups until their per-lesson learning and interaction note is approved. Do not fill missing slots by forcing their content into a convenient formula.

Shared use above ten consumers remains contingent on EDD-010's engineering and ops-readiness route. Builders write per-lesson `build.md` and the seven pre-flight answers; the model's math passing is not evidence of learner understanding. Root coordinates the integrated check/build/server; independent QA walks every new route and independent learning/design review samples the required representatives and groups. Every reported count names its scope. No publication, source verification, finished-lesson judgement or deploy is performed by this role.
