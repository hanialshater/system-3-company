## The honest summary

- **At launch the ad slot is a cost. It earns nothing.** Stage 1 runs house cards only (our own messages), so revenue is **$0**.
- It costs about **0.53M tokens of engineering and about 1.4M of quality runs** (head-of-product's figures), plus four decisions from you and a privacy ruling.
- Paid cards (stage 2) come later, if ever. My revenue figures are assumptions, not data: about $33 to $200 a month at 10,000 monthly learners. We have 0 real learners today.
- What the money buys: a slot built to your rules, plus a registered test of whether a card between lessons hurts the next lesson, all before any money is involved.

## The rules already in force (Ruling 3)

Between lessons only, never inside one and never before a reveal. 1 per 6 lessons is a ceiling, never raised for revenue. None on lesson pages, ever. Teen mode (13 to 15): house cards only. The slot ships only if the registered experiment's guardrails hold, and the no-card arm is allowed to win. No personalised ads.

## F-17: pursue ads, on these terms?

- **My recommendation:** yes, stages 0 and 1 only, on Ruling 3. Stage 2 (paid) comes back to you as a separate item.
- **Unblocks:** W-893 (principal-engineer reviews page weight, CSP and the beacon) and W-894 (analytics-steward registers the experiment).
- **If no:** the slot leaves release 1, about 1.9M of planned work is freed, and the date odds improve.
- **Cheaper option:** donations for adults and a school-licence discovery, with no slot.
