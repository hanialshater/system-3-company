## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: the prediction. I doubled 9% to about 19%, which is the trap; 50% felt implausible. Honest and well chosen.
- Explore: 1,000 dots, 10 red; "Test everyone" rings 98, of which 9 are red; "Test the positives again" leaves 16 rings, 8 red. The tiles (POSITIVE / OF THOSE, SICK / SICK : HEALTHY) make 9 : 89 → 8 : 8 concrete. The healthy rings vanishing while the red ones stay is the picture of the idea. Strong.
- Readability: on phone the dots are tiny; a ringed red dot and a ringed grey dot are hard to tell apart in the 98-ring frame. The tiles carry it.
- Told-not-shown: "Each positive multiplies it by about 10 (90% ÷ 9%)" is told; the stage shows the two ratios, which is enough to check it.
- Layout: reveal clipped on phone (W-135): the independence caveat "A test that is fooled the same way twice ... helps less" is cut. It is a caveat, not the explanation, so not blocking, but it matters for real-world use. After "Start over" the reveal sits over the untested 10 : 990 grid rather than the 8 : 8 result.
