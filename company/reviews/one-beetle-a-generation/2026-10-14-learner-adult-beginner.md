# Learner review: one-beetle-a-generation
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows drift: chance changes trait shares in a small group; has not thought about migration between groups).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 5 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: reading the stage. It is a scatter of group 1 share red against group 2 share red, with shaded off-diagonal corners labelled "differ by over half". I had to work out that the diagonal means "the groups match" and that each dot is one whole 200-generation run. Nothing says so except the grey axis labels. Big dots in the corners (many runs stacked at all-red/none) are not explained.
- The stem is long and abstract ("red shares end up over half apart in about half of runs"); the intuition "one beetle out of 50 can't matter" is there, so the prediction does its job.
- What landed: the captions "100 of 200 runs (50.0%)" vs "4 of 200 (2.0%)", and the cloud visibly pulling from the corners onto the diagonal. The numbers carry the lesson even if the plot doesn't.
- Told-not-shown: "a newcomer every generation keeps bringing the other colour back" is told; nothing shows a beetle moving. Fine for 60 s.
- Layout: on the predict frame the third button ("5 swaps each way") is cut off at the bottom of the stage box. Reveal fits.

## 2. After reading the brief (version only)

- Version 1, 4 claims shown as checked.

## Fix suggested

Label the diagonal "groups match" and say "each dot = one run; big dot = many runs"; fix the clipped predict-frame button.

Verdict: launch
