## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. "Keeps climbing" is the natural reading of "gravity never lets go"; the "5 m/s" option is easy to dismiss, and "43 m/s (155 km/h)" is the only specific number, which makes it look like the designed answer. Drag area "0.7 m²" is a term I skip.
- Explore: the time slider draws a speed curve that flattens, with a weight arrow (down) and a drag arrow (up) that grow to equal length. The caption "Drag now matches weight: speed has levelled off." appears at the first move (15 s), so the answer is announced rather than found. The curve itself is a good picture of "levels off".
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal's "Air pushes back harder the faster she goes (as speed squared)". I accept "harder the faster", but "speed squared" is jargon, and nothing on screen shows drag growing with speed except an arrow length I would have to compare by eye.
- Reveal earned? Mostly for "levels off". Not for the numbers: the reveal says "At 10 s you saw her at 42.3 m/s", but the recorded run visited only 15 s and 30 s, so that number was not seen. The limit "43.2 m/s" (80 kg, belly-down) was seen only as 43.1 m/s at 15 s before the pose and mass changed.
- Problems:
  - The final screen (belly-down, 120 kg, 52.9 m/s) sits beside text about 80 kg and 43.2 m/s; the picture and the reveal text disagree because the learner has been moving the sliders.
  - Heavier body gives a higher top speed (74 m/s at 110 kg head-down versus 64.5 m/s at 80 kg); this is a surprise and is never explained in the reveal, which never mentions mass.
  - A caption said "Speed still climbing; drag growing" at 15 s head-down 110 kg, then "levelled off" at 30 s, a nice touch, but the same caption wording at 15 s belly-down said "levelled off" at 43.1 m/s, so "levelled off" appears to arrive at different times with no hint why.
  - The dashed dark curve in the graph (a ghost of the earlier setting?) and the green dashed line are unlabelled.
  - The two controls (Pose, Body mass) plus Time are three things to change at once; the prompt only asks to move time.
  - "Within 3% of her limit" is a statistic I did not ask for.
- FATAL: none found from the artifact.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The objective (drag grows until it equals weight, net force zero) is shown by the two arrows and carried by the reveal; this is the lesson's strongest match between intent and experience.
- The mass slider's counterintuitive effect is outside the stated objective but dominates what the learner can play with; it is a distraction or an unplanned lesson.

Verdict: revise
