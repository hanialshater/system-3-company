# Build note: skydiver-speed-limit

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: v-t graph revealed by a time slider (manipulation, not playback), RK4 quadratic drag, with weight and drag arrows beside it; pose toggle and mass slider leave a dashed ghost of the previous curve.
- Earned reveal: milestone 'levelled' when v >= 97% of v_t (belly-down 80 kg reaches it at 10 s). Reveal quotes the run the learner saw. Model labelled on stage ('sea-level air').
- Note: the toy C_d A = 0.7 m^2 gives 43 m/s, below commonly quoted belly terminal speeds (about 50-55 m/s); flagged in claim c5 for the fact-checker.
- After screenshots: 'm/s' axis label collided with '80'; moved. Pose button now reads 'Pose: ...'.
