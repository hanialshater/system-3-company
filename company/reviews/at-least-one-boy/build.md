# Build note: at-least-one-boy (demo-builder, 2026-10-13)
- Intent: 1,000 seeded families shown as dots in a 2 x 2 grid (older child by row, younger by column). A clue greys out the cells it rules out. 'At least one boy' leaves 3 cells (240/744 = 32%); 'the older is a boy' leaves 2 (240/504 = 48%).
- Gate: 2 milestones, the at-least-one clue, then the older-boy clue for contrast.
- The information rule ('you learn only the clue') is on the stage under the grid and in the question (Gardner 1959; c4).
