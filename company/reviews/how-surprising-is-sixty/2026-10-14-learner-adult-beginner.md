## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction felt real: I had no feel for whether 60/100 is rare; three orders of magnitude (0.3 / 3 / 30%) is a good spread.
- Explore: one tap runs 250 batches into a histogram centred on 50 with a dashed marker at 60. The bell shape and a thin shaded tail make "luck rarely goes this far" visible. Sliding to 62 shrinks the tail to 1.
- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: "p-value" appears in the stage caption ("Their share is the p-value") before I know what it is. It is defined properly only in the reveal. Not blocking, but the word arrives early.
- Run noise: my run gave 2.0% against the exact 2.8%. With 250 batches a run could give 0.8% or 1.2%, which sits between my options and might make "about 0.3%" look right. More batches (or a second run button) would steady it.
- Shaded tail bars are tiny on a phone; the yellow is hard to see at 60+.
- Good: the reveal's caveat ("It is not the chance that the coin is fair") is exactly the misreading I would bring.
- Layout: empty top band in explore; no clipping.
- Fix suggested (minor): caption "Shaded share: how often luck goes this far" and name it a p-value only in the reveal; run 1,000 batches; make the tail colour stronger.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Objective: p-value = how often a fair coin goes at least this far (~3%), and not the chance the coin is fair. Both halves are present; the first is shown, the second told in the reveal, which is appropriate.
