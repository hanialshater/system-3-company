## 1. From the artifact (written before reading build.md or anything else; meta.json read only for version and learner fields)

- **Where it loses me first (main finding): the title and hook point at the answer.** "Drop the loser, change the winner?" and "C came last. Take C off the ballot and recount. Could the winner change?" Together they tell me the lesson exists because the winner changes. The belief the lesson targets ("removing a last-placed option cannot change the winner") is exactly the "B again" option, and the card has argued me out of it before the commit. What is left is a coin flip between "A" and "A tie". Under "No answer before the commit" ("point to" includes phrases), this is a revise. I still picked "B again", but a learner who reads titles as hints will not, and then the reveal answers a question they never really held.
- Predict otherwise: the three options are worded alike, none numbered. The locked stage shows five ballots (three A > B > C, two B > C > A), "Borda: 2 points for 1st, 1 for 2nd, 0 for 3rd", and empty tallies with "?". No count is shown. A learner could count it themselves, which is fair.
- Step 1 (Count points): points appear next to each ranking, bars show A 6, B 7 wins, C 2. "With C: B wins 7–6 (C 2)." Clear.
- Step 2 ("C on the ballot", which removes C): C becomes a dashed slot on every ballot, the rule changes to "1 point for 1st, 0 for 2nd", bars show A 3 wins, B 2. "Without C: A wins 3–2." The flip is on the stage.
- Step 3 (A vs B on each ballot): "+1 A" under ballots 1–3, "+1 B" with "was +2" under ballots 4 and 5. This is the cause, shown: C sitting between B and A on two ballots doubled B's lead there, and removing C halves it. Excellent.
- Reveal: "voters 1–3 put A above B, voters 4–5 put B above A, before and after", "2-point lead", "4 beats 3: B won 7–6", "every lead is 1: A wins 3–2". All of it is on the stage when it opens (the +1 / was +2 chips, both result lines). About 75 words. Ends on the idea.
- Minor: the toggle is labelled with the current state, not the action. Before pressing, it reads "C on the ballot", and pressing it removes C; afterwards it reads "C removed". A learner looking for "Remove C" may not see that this is the button.
- Minor: "4 beats 3" in the reveal is the sum of leads (2 + 2 vs 1 + 1 + 1); the stage shows the chips but not that sum. A learner can add it, but a "B +4, A +3" line would make it a reading, not a sum.
