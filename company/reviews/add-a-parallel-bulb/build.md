# Build note: add-a-parallel-bulb

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: one circuit diagram. Ideal 6 V battery with its own meter on the top rail; three branches with a meter tag each; bulbs 2 and 3 have switches (open switch and dashed wire when off). Glow is the same for every lit bulb (6 W each).
- Earned reveal: milestone 'two-branches' (a second bulb switched in). All meters read '?' and bulbs stay unlit until the prediction.
- Added an optional third bulb so the learner can see the pattern 1, 2, 3 A rather than one pair of numbers; reveal mentions 3.0 A only if they reached it.
- After screenshots: battery meter text '3.0 A' overflowed its circle; circle enlarged.
- Fixed 6 ohm bulbs are an idealisation stated on stage and in c5.
