The question is about one extra device with the heater, but the stage adds devices cumulatively, so what I saw trip was TV + blender + heater, not blender + heater. - Suggested fix (minor): make plug buttons swap the one extra device instead of stacking (or prompt "unplug the TV and try the blender alone"); show each device's amps on its tile; make the reset path obvious.
