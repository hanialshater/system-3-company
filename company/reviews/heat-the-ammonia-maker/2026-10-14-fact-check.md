# Fact-check: heat-the-ammonia-maker (2026-10-14)

Board: W-639. Lesson: src/demos/chemistry/heat-the-ammonia-maker/ (status draft, version 1).

Checker: fact-checker (I did not write these claims). Method: I read Demo.tsx (and model.ts where there is one) on every reachable path, including the dynamic strings, locked state, toggles and the order of presses, before reading the claims. Then I re-ran every number in node from my own copies of the lesson logic. The scripts are kept in artifacts/fact-check-wave-j/ (chem.mjs, nasa.mjs). Web: only PyPI, npm and raw.githubusercontent.com were reachable, so no web page, paper or book could be opened. The sources I did open: the CRC 'Standard Thermodynamic Properties of Chemical Substances' table, JANAF 1998, ATcT 1.112 and the Perry/DIPPR-105 density fits, all as transcribed in the PyPI package chemicals 1.5.2; and the NASA TM-4513 polynomials (McBride, Gordon & Reno 1993) from Cantera's data/nasa_gas.yaml and nasa_condensed.yaml. Where a verdict rests on a derivation instead of a source, the claim's note says so. No verdict rests on a source I could not open. No wave-J chemistry check script exists, so I re-ran the brief's inline Check (#11). The departures the build note lists got special attention. **c3** (the new ammonia-share claim): the code is right under its model, but under full temperature-dependent data the shares at 200 °C and 450 °C are off (14.8% and 0.23%, against the stage's 17% and 0.45%). **c6** (the factories sentence): no source could be opened, so it is held. **c2**: the brief asked for a stage caption on the constant-dH/dS assumption, and the stage does not carry it. With full NASA data, K at 450 °C is 4.9e-5, 4 times below the stage's 1.9e-4.

Unrecorded claims: I compared every learner-visible sentence (hook, question, options, stage labels, reveal, recall) with the claims list and found none unrecorded.

| c1 | verified | Opened: NASA TM-4513 polynomials (McBride, Gordon & Reno 1993; Cantera data/nasa_gas.yaml) give exactly the CODATA values at 298.15 K: NH3 -45.94 kJ/mol and 192.77, N2 191.61, H2 130.68 J/(mol K). | c2 | disputed | The arithmetic is right under the stated assumption: 5.61e5, 0.624 and 1.94e-4. But the stage and the reveal show these Ks as the values at 200 °C and 450 °C, with no caveat (the brief asked for a stage caption). With full temperature-dependent data (NASA TM-4513 polynomials, opened, integrated in node) K is 5.60e5 at 25 °C, 0.39 at 200 °C and 4.9e-5 at 450 °C, because dH reaches -106 kJ/mol at 450 °C. The stage's 450 °C value is 4 times too high. 'A factor of billions' still holds (1.1e10). Fix, either one: (a) add the caption 'estimate: 25 °C dH and dS held constant' on the stage, as limestone-crossover does with '(25 °C data)'; or (b) switch to the full-data values 5.6 x 10^5, 0.39 and 4.9 x 10^-5, citing NASA TM-4513. |
| c3 | disputed | Derivation re-checked with two solvers: from 1 N2 : 3 H2 at 1 bar the shares are 93.8%, 17.5% and 0.449% under the constant-dH/dS K of c2, so the code matches c2. But the claim text states these shares as fact. With the full-data K they are 93.8%, 14.8% and 0.227% (at 450 °C half the stage's 0.45%). Same fix as c2: either state the assumption on the stage and in this claim's text, or use the full-data K. |
| c4 | verified | Derivation: in the lesson's model ln K = -dH/RT + dS/R, so d ln K/dT = dH/RT^2 < 0 when dH < 0 (van 't Hoff). The full NASA data agree (K falls at every temperature). 'Large K: mostly ammonia' is shown by c3's calculation (K = 5.6e5 gives 94% NH3 at 1 bar from a 1:3 feed). |
| c5 | unverified | Held: I could not open the cited Atkins kinetics chapter, or any other source on the rate of ammonia synthesis against temperature (web blocked). Arrhenius behaviour with a positive activation energy is an empirical fact about this reaction and cannot be derived from the thermodynamic data I did open. To verify it, give me an openable source for the temperature dependence of the ammonia-synthesis rate (or a general Arrhenius statement). |
| c6 | unverified | Held: Greenwood & Earnshaw (or any source on industrial Haber-Bosch conditions) could not be opened. Only the 'high pressure wins yield back' part can be derived (4 mol of gas -> 2 mol, so at fixed K the ammonia mole fraction rises with P); 'factories run hot with an iron catalyst' cannot. The brief says to cut the factories sentence if no source can be checked. Cut it (cm-chemistry's call), or supply an openable source. |
| c7 | verified | Derivation: K = exp(-dG°/RT), and dG° for a fixed reaction and standard state depends only on T. Pressure and concentration enter Q, not K. A catalyst changes neither the reactant nor the product states, so dG° and K are unchanged (thermodynamic K, in fugacities). |
