## 1. From the artifact

- Hook and predict: the title "60% or 50%: does a little error matter?" and the hook set a real tension, and "About equal" is the natural guess. The stem is short and clear. The locked stage shows an empty chart with a dashed 50% line and "?" readouts, so nothing leaks.
- Explore: "+50 races" (a step) and "Run to 500" grow the line of the 60% bettor's share. Five seeded runs can be overlaid and highlighted. Every run ends near 100% (98.8%, over 99.9%, 99.2%). Run 2 visibly dives far below the dashed line before recovering. The core idea, that the more accurate belief ends up with nearly all the money even though luck can lead for a long time, is on stage and comes through.
- **Where I wobble: the mechanism is a caption, not something I see happen.** "Win: money ratio ×1.2. Loss: ×0.8." appears as stage text after the first tap. I never see a single race move the money, and "money ratio" (whose money over whose?) is not defined. The reveal's "three wins and two losses multiply it by 1.11: a small edge that compounds" is arithmetic told in words. Nothing on stage shows 5 races, or ×1.11 per 5 races, building up. A beginner gets "accurate wins" from the picture, but "why a 10-point error is fatal" from trust.
- "Pool's win odds" appears as a readout (57.3% → 60.0%) with no gloss. A beginner does not know what the pool is or why its odds matter until the last reveal sentence.
- Reveal: "in run 2 the 50% bettor held 88.6% at race 200" is a number the stage never displayed. The dive is visible, but the readouts only show race 50 and race 500. The learner cannot check the 88.6% against anything they saw.
- Minor: the chart's axis text ("race 0", "line: 60% bettor's share") is very small on a phone. Five overlaid grey runs plus a highlighted one is busy, but readable.
- Minor: "Why trust this? (0/6 claims checked)", so nothing is verified yet (fact-checker item, lesson is DRAFT).

## 3. After reading the brief

- The doesNotKnow item is shown. Wealth goes to the accurate bettor (every run near 100%), pool odds drift to 60% (readout 57.3% → 60.0%), and luck can lead (run 2's dive). For this boundary, which already knows pool odds, the "pool's win odds" gloss matters less than I first thought, so it is minor.
- **Top finding: the cause the objective asks the learner to explain is told, not shown.** The objective is "each race multiplies the ratio of two bettors' money by how well each belief fit the result, so a small edge compounds". On stage that is a static caption ("Win: money ratio ×1.2. Loss: ×0.8."). The learner never sees a race change the ratio, or the ×1.11 per five races build up. Under "Show the why you promised" that is a revise. Something on the existing chart or readouts must stand for the per-race multiply, and the reveal must point at it. For example, a single-race step with a money-ratio readout that visibly goes ×1.2 / ×0.8. How to do it is the builder's call.
