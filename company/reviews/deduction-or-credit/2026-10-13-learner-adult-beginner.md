lesson version: 1

boundary: an adult beginner who thinks a $1,000 deduction saves $1,000.

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: why a deduction cuts only $200. The stage shows just the tax bill bar ($2,000 -> $1,800). The income ($10,000 -> $9,000) that the deduction acts on is never drawn, so "taken off income first" stays a caption. The step I need, 20% of $1,000 = $200 (or 20% of $9,000 = $1,800), is never shown or said.
- Credit is clear: bar drops to $1,000, "BILL CUT BY $1,000".
- Rate slider: the walkthrough moved it to 40% with the credit selected (cut stays $1,000). Nothing prompts me to try the deduction at 40%, and the reveal never says a deduction is worth more at a higher rate. A learner can leave without that idea.
- Layout: lots of empty space above the bar; reveal fits on phone.
- Reveal answered my question ("why only $200?"): partly; it restates the numbers and the definitions.

## 2. After reading the brief

Objective includes "a higher rate makes the deduction worth more": not prompted on stage and not stated in the reveal. Claims: 5 verified.

## Suggested fix
Draw the income bar shrinking by $1,000 with the 20% slice taken from it; change the panel to "Try the deduction at 20%, then at 40%"; add a reveal line: a deduction saves rate x amount.
