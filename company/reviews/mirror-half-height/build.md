# Build note: mirror-half-height

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: side view at one scale on both axes. The person (1.80 m, eye 1.70 m) stands d from the glass; her reflection (dashed, filled in where she can see it) stands d behind. Two sight lines run from the eye to the reflection's head and toes and cross the glass at 1.75 m and 0.85 m; they are green if the crossing is on the mirror, red dashed if not. Heights are cm integers so the edges are exact.
- Retitled: "How tall a mirror do you need?" (the slug says half-height; the title does not).
- Departure from the brief: the brief asked for no text; the stage has a short caption and the crossing heights, because a recurring finding is pictures nobody explains. The caption names the idealisation (flat mirror, eye as a point) and what the dashed figure is.
- Earned reveal: milestones 'half' (mirror exactly 0.85 to 1.75 m with the whole body seen) and 'back' (distance changed while that holds). QA path: the top edge starts at its maximum, 1.85 m, and two presses take it down to 1.75 m; bottom 0.75 -> 0.85 m; distance 1.00 -> 2.00 m. The reveal quotes the distances at which the learner saw head to toe with the 0.90 m mirror.
- Numbers (node): (1.70 + 0)/2 = 0.85 m, (1.70 + 1.80)/2 = 1.75 m, length 0.90 m.
- After screenshots: added "you" and "reflection" labels above the two figures.
