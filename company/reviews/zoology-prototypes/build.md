# Zoology prototypes — 2026-10-28

Two initial draft lessons toward a requested 500. W-2162 tracks the remaining 498, including the subject plan, independently sourced claims, content and QA. These are prototypes, not a complete batch or a publication recommendation. No generated images; the diagrams are original schematic SVGs.

## Fossil tracks and bones — pre-flight v1

1. Title and hook do not identify which item records activity.
2. Options are verbal and cover bone, footprint and both.
3. Locked stage labels bone and footprint but hides the evidence descriptions.
4. Reveal describes the body part and surface mark that appear when Compare the evidence is pressed; the general inference limitation is a sourced claim, not a measured value.
5. The first and only stage control shows both descriptions and enables reveal.
6. No stage reset control is present. Reload starts the lesson over; this has not been checked in a browser.
7. Phone fit, legibility, keyboard and full application flow have not been checked. QA must inspect a 390 × 844 reveal screenshot before any publication.

## Birds among dinosaurs — pre-flight v1

1. Title and hook do not answer whether birds are inside Dinosauria.
2. Options use verbal group relationships.
3. The locked stage lists the taxa but hides the branches.
4. Reveal names the green dinosaur branch and taxa visible after Trace the branch. The tree is a simplified branching diagram and does not imply that either fossil species is the direct ancestor of modern birds.
5. The first and only stage control shows the tree and enables reveal.
6. No stage reset control is present. Reload starts the lesson over; this has not been checked in a browser.
7. Phone fit, legibility, keyboard and full application flow have not been checked. QA must inspect a 390 × 844 reveal screenshot before any publication.

## Open issues

The proposed sources are reference leads, not independently verified citations. Ask a fact-checker to check each factual statement and the exact references; ask a learning designer to test whether the stages show rather than simply assert the concepts. The phylogeny is deliberately simplified and needs taxon and diagram review. The dinosaur request is broader than these two prototypes; 498 still need scoping, design, implementation and review. No learner evidence has been collected.
