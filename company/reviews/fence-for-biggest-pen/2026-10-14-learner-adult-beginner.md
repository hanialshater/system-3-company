## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: 1 × 9 / 5 × 5 / 4 × 6. Fine, though the hook's real misconception ("every pen holds the same") is not an option, so the learner who holds it has to pick something else.
- Explore: nine buttons (1 × 9 ... 9 × 1) redraw the pen on a 1 m grid with the area inside and "Fence: 1 + 9 + 1 + 9 = 20 m". Walking through them, area rises 9, 16, 21, 24, 25 then falls back; 5 × 5 turns green. The rise-and-fall is shown well.
- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: why the square wins. The stage shows *that* 25 is the peak, not *why*.
- Told-not-shown: the why ("Width plus height is always 10 ... area is 25 minus the square of the distance from 5") is prose only.
- Layout: phone reveal is clipped (W-135) after the first sentence. Everything after "up to 25 m² at 5 × 5" is lost, which is exactly the explanation (sum is 10, each metre off the square costs d²). Here the clip carries the explanation. Also in the 1 × 9 frame the area label "9 m²" collides with the side label "9 m".
- Suggested fix: (1) shorten the reveal so the "25 minus distance squared" line survives the fold (or show it on the stage: e.g. 4 × 6 drawn as the 5 × 5 square with a 1 × 1 corner missing); (2) add "they all hold the same" as a prediction option; (3) move the area label off the side label for thin pens.

Verdict: revise
