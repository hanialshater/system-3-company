## Findings (written from the walkthrough and probe before reading build.md, design.md, meta.json claims or the earlier reviews)

1. **Where understanding first broke: the figure-eight "failed" while showing four green ticks.** At reveal time on every path I tried, the stage shows 4₁ with all arcs A, "Crossings OK: 4 of 4" and four green ticks, and the reveal says "The figure-eight (4₁) failed". With my boundary, the rule I was given ("all one colour or all different") is obeyed here, so my first reading is "it passed". What the test actually asks (more than one colour, or all three) is never said on screen as the pass mark: the predict question asks "Can the trefoil use all three colours?", and the reveal says "whether it can be coloured this way", where "this way" has to be read back to "took all three colours". The bold stage line "4₁: 3 of 81 obey: all one colour" and the reveal's "only the three one-colour ones obey the rule" let me repair it within a sentence, so I did not lose the main argument; but this is the spot a first-time learner is most likely to stall. Same gap, smaller: the plain loop also obeys the rule (one arc, no crossings), so "can be coloured this way" is true of everything if "this way" means only the rule. Not blocking; a one-phrase pass mark ("coloured with more than one colour" / "with all three") in the reveal or the 4₁ result line would close it.
2. **What the prediction felt like.** A fair guess, not a coin toss: the locked stage shows 3₁ already at "3 of 3" with all A, so the question reads as "can you do better than all one colour?", which is a real question. Step 1 of the QA path is informative: one tap (B A A) breaks all three crossings at once, which tells me each trefoil crossing touches all three arcs. Two taps reach B C A with 3 of 3, and the "Seen on 3₁: 3 colours, 3 of 3 OK" line keeps the success after I break it again (QA step 3, probe A), which I liked: I could keep poking without losing what I found.
3. **Whether the reveal answered the question I actually had.** The hook ("Can three colours tell a knot from a plain loop?") is answered: loop one arc so one colour, trefoil three colours, wiggling keeps that, so they differ; and the 4₁ paragraph honestly says the test has limits. The question it leaves open is the one my boundary raises at once: "if I wiggle the plain loop so it has crossings, couldn't I colour it with three?" The reveal answers that only by assertion ("Wiggling ... never changes"); I cannot test it on the stage. I accept that as told context, but it is the weakest link of the argument for this mind.
4. **The "Only with two colours" predictor gets no answer.** Probe B: choosing it gives "Not quite: it was 'Yes'", and nothing in the reveal says why two colours never work. The only place it is shown is "3₁: 9 of 27 obey: 3 one colour, 6 all three", which appears only if the learner presses Check all on 3₁, and the panel tells them to press it on ○ and 4₁. Not blocking.
5. **Tap cycles differ per arc.** Arc 1 and arc 3 go A, B, C, A; arc 2 goes A to C first (QA step 2). Harmless (any arc reaches any colour in at most two taps), but a learner trying to plan a colouring cannot predict a tap. Note only.
6. **The reveal gate is earned.** Probe A: after making three colours and pressing Check all on 3₁ the button still says "Try it first"; it opens only after ○ and 4₁ are visited and 4₁ checked. Probe B: Check all on 4₁ without ever making three colours on 3₁ keeps it locked; making B C A opens it. So "The trefoil took all three colours" is true of every learner who reaches the reveal. The panel hint stays the same sentence throughout and never names what is still missing (kit behaviour, seen in other lessons).
7. **Reset** returns 3₁ to A A A with "Colours reset to A." and keeps the Seen line. Fine.

Verdict reason: no finding stops a first-time learner with this boundary from following the main argument; finding 1 (4₁ "failed" beside four green ticks, pass mark never named on screen) is the strongest note and the first thing to fix if the lesson is revised.

## After reading meta.json

Read after the findings above: meta.json `objective`, `misconception`, `recall`, `evidence` and the eight claims (as shown in the trust sheet). build.md, design.md and the QA / fact-check records were not read for this review.

- **Objective, first half** ("the trefoil is not a plain loop because it allows a three-colouring that a plain loop never allows"): matches what I experienced; the gate guarantees every learner at the reveal has made the three-colouring themselves (finding 6). The recall question uses "coloured with all three colours", which is the pass mark finding 1 says the reveal and the 4₁ line never state; aligning the reveal wording with the recall wording would close finding 1 cheaply.
- **Objective, second half** ("settling 'can't be coloured' takes checking every colouring, not one failed try") and the **misconception** ("if I couldn't colour it, it can't be coloured"): weaker in experience than in intent. The panel sends the learner straight to Check all on 4₁, so on the QA path there is no failed try of their own for the 81-way check to be contrasted with; the reveal says "all 81 ways were checked" but never says why one failed try would not have been enough. A learner who taps 4₁ arcs first (probe B: 2 of 4) gets the contrast by accident. Hypothesis for the builder, not a blocking finding: ask for one 4₁ attempt before Check all, or add a clause such as "one failed try would not have settled it". Only human learners can show whether the second half lands.
- **Claim c4 / c5 vs stage:** "only the 3 single-colour ones obey" is on the stage at reveal time on every path I ran ("4₁: 3 of 81 obey: all one colour"). c5 (wiggling invariance) is labelled told context in both meta.json and the trust sheet, consistent with finding 3.
- **Claim c1** defines tricolourable as "more than one colour"; the learner-facing panel and reveal only ever say "all three colours". For the trefoil and the figure-eight these agree (c3: none of the trefoil's colourings use exactly two), so nothing false is shown, but it is the same unstated pass mark as finding 1.
- **evidence.simulatedLearner** is empty; this record should be linked there by the lesson owner (I did not edit the lesson).
