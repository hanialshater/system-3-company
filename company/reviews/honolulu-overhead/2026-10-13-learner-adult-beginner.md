## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess for a beginner; "Miami" is the tempting answer (hot, southern US). The hook ("Summer noon in Miami feels like the Sun is right on top of you. Is it?") leans toward "no", which tips the sceptical reader off, but does not say which city. The options carry their latitudes (25.8, 30.0, 21.3 °N), which a beginner has no scale for until the stage supplies one. The locked stage shows a world map with three hollow city rings, a dashed line at the equator (20 Mar) and "FARTHEST NORTH SO FAR ?".
- Where understanding first broke: the term "overhead" is fine, but "°N" latitudes are used before they are tied to the map lines; I only worked out that the dashed line's height is the number in "NOON SUN OVERHEAD AT" once I tapped 21 Jun. After that it is clear.
- What was SHOWN is strong: tapping 21 Jun moves the dashed line up to 23.4°N, Honolulu's ring fills green, Miami and Cairo stay hollow, and "FARTHEST NORTH SO FAR 23.4°N" stays put when I go to 21 Dec and 20 Mar. A beginner can read the answer straight off the map: the line never reaches Miami or Cairo. The thin solid lines drawn at about 23°N and 23°S after exploring are unlabelled on the stage (the reveal calls them the Tropics).
- Reveal vs stage: 23.4°N matches. NOT shown: "crossed Honolulu on about 27 May and again about 16 Jul" (I jumped to 21 Jun, so I never saw when Honolulu filled), "Tropic of Cancer / Capricorn" naming, and the tilt explanation ("That limit is the Earth's tilt, about 23.4°") which is told with nothing on the stage about tilt.
- Instruction mismatch: the panel says "Drag the date through June", but the control offers three date buttons plus a slider across the whole year; no month markers on the slider.
- Phone legibility: the map is clear, city names legible; the dashed line is easy to follow. The reveal is clipped at the bottom in the screenshot.
- Reveal earned: yes for "only Honolulu"; the "because of tilt" half is asserted.
- Problems: (1) hook leans toward "no"; (2) latitude used before tied to the map; (3) 27 May / 16 Jul and tilt told, not shown; (4) unlabelled tropic lines; (5) "drag through June" does not match the controls.
