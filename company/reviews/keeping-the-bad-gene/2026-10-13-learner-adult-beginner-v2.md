## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess; "disappears" is the natural answer for a gene that kills in pairs. The hook ("Two copies of this gene kill. One copy helps. What does selection do with it?") hands over the key fact (one copy helps) before the question. The question defines allele in brackets and gives survival numbers (0%, 100%, 85%), which is fair. The locked stage is an empty chart and the controls are cut off below the stage panel.
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal. "Rare w sits mostly in carriers, who survive best, so it rises; common w makes more two-copy babies, who die, so it falls. The balance is 0.15 / 1.15 = 13%." The causal story is told in two sentences, and "0.15 / 1.15" has no source on the stage (0.15 is the 85% no-w survival's gap to 100%; this is never shown). The learner is not given the step "what is a carrier / a baby with two copies" visually: the stage shows only share curves.
- What was SHOWN is strong: two curves, one starting at 1% and one at 50%, both bending to the same dashed line, "balance 13%". That kills "disappears" and "takes over" on sight. Moving survival to 95% moves the line to 5%.
- The "balance 13%" label and readout appear already at generation 10, before the curves arrive, so the answer is on the screen as soon as the first button is tapped.
- The reveal card shows the stage after the QA path ended on Restart: generation 0, two dots and a dashed line at 5%, while the reveal text speaks of the curves having met at 13%. A learner who restarts before revealing sees none of the convergence on the card they are reading.
- Reveal earned: the converge-to-13% fact yes (seen); why, only by assertion; the 13% formula not at all.
- Problems: (1) hook leaks "one copy helps"; (2) "0.15 / 1.15" unexplained; (3) mechanism told not shown; (4) balance line visible before the curves reach it; (5) reveal card can sit on a reset stage.
- No FATAL.
