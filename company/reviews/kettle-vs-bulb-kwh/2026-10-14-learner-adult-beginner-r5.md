# Learner review (re-review r5): kettle-vs-bulb-kwh
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner; reads watts off labels and knows bills charge kWh; treats watts as "how much it costs".
Screenshots seen: yes (artifacts/walkthroughs/kettle-vs-bulb-kwh/sheet.png)
Verdict: launch

## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: the hook states the misconception ("So the kettle always costs more to run. Right?"). The question asks which uses more energy: a 2000 W kettle for 3 min or a 60 W bulb for 2 h. The locked stage shows two empty bars and two sliders (bulb 1 h, kettle 3 min). Nothing leaks.
- Explore: moving the bulb to 2 h puts the green bar (0.12 kWh) above the orange one (0.10 kWh). Pushing the kettle to 5 min flips it back (0.17 vs 0.12). Seeing the taller bar swap with time, while the watts stay fixed, is the idea: energy depends on time too. I got it from the stage.
- Where the picture misled me: the bars are striped, and the caption says "Lines: kettle 1 min, bulb 10 min". So the stripes are not the same unit of time. A kettle stripe looks about 3× as tall as a bulb stripe, but per minute the kettle is about 33× the bulb. The reveal then says "each kettle minute was a thick slice, each bulb minute a thin one". Read literally, that sentence makes me count the bulb's 12 stripes as 12 minutes. The "thin slices" story is right, but the drawing does not show a bulb minute at all.
- Reveal: 0.12 vs 0.10 kWh, both seen. "power × time, in kilowatt-hours" names the unit after I have watched it accumulate. The reveal-time stage is left at kettle 5 min (kettle taller), but the text is written for 3 min; "In the question's case" covers it.

## 3. New findings

- Moderate: the slice metaphor and the drawing disagree. The stripes are 1 kettle-minute vs 10 bulb-minutes, while the reveal says "each bulb minute a thin one". Either draw both in 1-minute slices (the bulb's would be very thin, which is the point) or say "each 10 bulb minutes a thin one".
- Minor: the locked bulb slider starts at 1 h, while the question says 2 h. That is fine as a starting point, but a learner who looks at the stage before reading may think the question is about 1 h.
- Minor: at reveal the stage shows the learner's last state (kettle 5 min, kettle ahead) next to text saying the bulb won.
- No FATAL.
