## Fast presses, mid-flip, chip switch, double taps

- Presses are accepted mid-flip (both toggles show pressed while the readout still reads the old state); the state then settles on the last combination. Mirror (50 ms) Turn over, Turn over (50 ms) Mirror, Turn over (200 ms) Mirror and Mirror (150 ms) Turn over all settled on mirrored-and-turned, Sum +3, reveal unlocked, and showed the **branch** opener. So a learner who pressed Turn over then Mirror quickly gets the branch opener, since the plain turned state never rested. Defensible (the sentence matches what they saw), but "Turn over then Mirror always shows the standard opener" is true only when the card is allowed to settle.
- Fast Turn over x2, Mirror x2 and Turn over, Mirror, Turn over, Mirror (30-40 ms) return to the front with nothing recorded and the reveal locked ("Try it first"). Double-click on Turn over and on Mirror: same, no state change visible at rest, no error. Not a defect; a double tap on a toggle is two toggles.
- Reveal button timing: it unlocked about 400 ms after Mirror then Turn over, about 300 ms after Turn over then Mirror (after the flip settles, not at the press). Pressing "Show me why" 60 ms into a flip that was about to reach plain turned showed the standard opener and the stage ended on the plain turned face, consistent.
- Chip switch mid-flip (Turn over or Mirror then Figure-eight at 10 or 150 ms): figure-eight at rest, front, Sum 0, seen line empty (nothing had settled), no crash; back to Trefoil gives a front trefoil; Turn over then works normally and records. Mirror, Turn over on the figure-eight only leaves the reveal locked (as designed: trefoil milestones). Figure-eight Mirror, Turn over, then trefoil Mirror, Turn over gives the branch opener and the figure-eight branch "The figure-eight's marks summed to 0 both ways, and it is its own mirror image" shows (the eightMirror milestone fired).
- Double-click on "Show me why": one reveal, no error. Reload mid-lesson: back to the predict phase.
