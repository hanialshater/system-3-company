## C1 (principal-engineer): after Turn over the picture is the turned-over knot, not the mirror

Method (independent of the builder's `c1-check.mjs`; script `qa-2026-10-16/r4.mjs`, results `qa-2026-10-16/r4-results.json`). On every rest state of both knots, reached by real clicks in two different orders, the script (1) coloured the front face red and the back face blue and counted pixels in a screenshot to see which face actually paints; (2) injected probe circles into the face on show at every crossing and at the last two control points of both strands that arrive there, and read their screen positions with `getBoundingClientRect` (the browser composes the card's rotateY, the wrapper's scaleX and the back face's own reflection); (3) computed each crossing's sign from those screen vectors (cross product of under and over directions), calibrated by one constant from the front state; (4) read the badge glyphs and the readout from the page.

Result, at 390x844 and 360x640, light and dark (4 runs, 630 assertions passed, 0 failed on this check):

- Only the expected face paints: front and mirrored show the front face, turned and mirroredTurned the back face; never both.
- Screen x of every crossing equals "stage width minus its front x" on turned and mirrored, and equals its front x on mirroredTurned. Example, trefoil at 390 wide (marks box 294.8 px): crossing 0 front 228.7, turned 66.1 (228.7 + 66.1 = 294.8); figure-eight crossing 0 front 265.3, turned 29.5 (sum 294.8). The crossings are off-centre (more than 20 % of the width from the middle) in all four states, so a reflection could not hide.
- The turned picture is therefore the turned-over knot (the same reflected outline as Mirror, but with the original handedness), and the mirror is only reached by Mirror.
- Signs read from the screen geometry equal the badge glyphs on all 4 states of both knots: trefoil front −−−, turned −−−, mirrored +++, mirroredTurned +++; figure-eight front −+−+, turned −+−+, mirrored +−+−, mirroredTurned +−+−. The readout sum equals the badge sum and the expected value (trefoil −3 / −3 / +3 / +3; figure-eight 0 on all four). Each badge sits about 13 view-box units from its own drawn crossing, so no mark is on the wrong crossing after a reflection.

## Motion

- **Mirror never squeezes.** Sampling every animation frame, the wrapper's computed transform was only ever `none` or `matrix(-1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0)`; the widest face box varied by under 0.5 px; the transform swapped only while the wrapper's opacity was under 0.05 (minimum 0); the card did not rotate (angle under 0.5 deg). Both knots, all four runs.
- **Marks hide mid-flip.** Turn over: marks opacity falls linearly over 100 ms from the press (about 0.17 left at 29 deg, 0 from about 42 deg), and stays 0 on every frame from 45 deg to 180 deg and from 110 ms until the card rests, then fades back in 150 ms. The card really passes 80-100 deg (a rendered turn, not a cut). Mirror: marks are hidden for the whole dip. Note for design: the marks are not gone at the instant of the press, they take 100 ms, as the spec says (L-3).
- **Readout never blank.** A requestAnimationFrame recorder ran over each whole session (rest pass, flips, double taps, mid-flip presses, chip switches): 0 blank frames in all four runs; the readout was steady (one text) through every Turn over.
- Wrong-order and stress: double-tap on Turn over ends in front; Mirror pressed mid-flip settles on mirroredTurned with Sum +3 and marks back; switching chip from mirroredTurned or mid-flip resets to front with the right readout and no stuck angle; reduced motion lands on the new state at once, shows the "Turned over" label, and Mirror also lands at once; keyboard (Space on Turn over, Tab, Enter on Mirror) reaches mirroredTurned; reload returns to the predict step. No page or console errors in any pass.

## Findings (what happened)

1. **At 360x640 the controls start below the fold and the readout leaves the screen when you scroll to them.** After committing the prediction the stage is 279 px tall and 372 px of content: the readout is visible, Turn over and Mirror show only their top 1 px (top 452, stage bottom 453), the chips are out of sight. Tapping a control scrolls the stage by 43 px; then the readout (top 141) is above the stage's top edge (174) and the seen line is clipped, so the learner can never see the readout and the controls at once on this size. Screenshot `qa-2026-10-16/360x640-fresh-controls-below-fold.png`. The CSS comment says short phones "scroll the stage a little" on purpose; I record the effect. Light and dark identical.
2. **Mirror then Turn over leaves the reveal locked.** The "turned" milestone fires only on the plain turned state. Mirror, then Turn over (state mirroredTurned, both controls used) still shows "Try it first" with only "mirror +3" in the seen line; the reveal unlocked only after Mirror was pressed again (state turned: "turned over −3 · mirror +3", button "Show me why"). Nothing tells the learner why. (probe: `qa-2026-10-16/probe4.mjs`.) The contract passes because its order is Turn over then Mirror.
3. **Reveal at 360x640 is scrolled past the picture.** The kit scrolls the card body by 240 px so the reveal text shows; the picture's top 18 px and the readout are above the visible area, and one wheel step up brings them back (screenshot `qa-2026-10-16/360x640-light-reveal.png`). Same behaviour as earlier lessons on this size (see memory), so it is the kit's, not a defect specific to this lesson; it is why my one "picture top on screen at reveal" check failed (2 of 630 assertions, light and dark, 360x640 only). At 390x844 the whole picture, readout and text fit (`qa-2026-10-16/390x844-light-reveal.png`).
4. My first run of the motion thresholds failed 16 checks because I asked for marks to be fully hidden from 20 deg; the spec is a 100 ms fade, so that was my threshold, not the lesson. I corrected the criterion (fully hidden from 110 ms and from 45 deg) and reran everything once; the numbers above are from that rerun. The first run's log was overwritten.
