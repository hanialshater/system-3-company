# left-or-right v3: accessibility review

reviewer: accessibility-reviewer · date: 2026-10-17 · tree: main `d238ac7b` (src/demos/math/left-or-right: Demo.tsx, card.ts, styles.module.css) · status of lesson: as in meta.json at that commit

## Verdict: pass with conditions. No lock-out failure found.

A keyboard-only learner completes predict, explore and reveal. Every mark has a text twin (the badges are drawn "+" and "−" glyphs and are also spelled out in the picture's label), so colour carries no meaning. The turned and mirrored states are in the picture's label and in `aria-pressed`. The one lesson-level gap that matters to a screen-reader learner is that **Turn over is silent**: nothing is announced when it is pressed (L1). That is a missing cue, not a barrier to doing the task, so it does not block.

## How it was run

Real Chromium (Playwright), `next build --webpack` with a private `NEXT_DIST_DIR` (`.next-a11y-lor`), own `next start` on 47411 (killed by PID afterwards), node_modules symlinked, `tsconfig.json` restored, build dir removed. `npm run walkthrough -- left-or-right --url …` first; then `tools/a11y-scan.mjs left-or-right` (lesson-only fail 0, fix 0; the 2 fail and 4 fix it prints are app/kit, below); then own probes (`artifacts/a11y-lor/probe.mjs`, `probe2.mjs`, `probe3.mjs`, logs `probe.log`, `probe2.log`, not committed). Contexts: 390x844 dark, light, dark with reduced motion; 360x640 and 320x568 for fit; colour-blind runs by feColorMatrix (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, greyscale) on the mirrored-and-turned state in both themes, of which I looked at light greyscale and dark protanopia. **No real screen reader was run**; screen-reader findings come from the accessibility tree, the live-region mutation log and markup.

## What works (checked)

1. **Keyboard path.** Tab reaches the predict options, commit, then in explore Turn over, Mirror, Figure-eight (the selected chip is disabled and skipped), "Show me why". Enter and Space both toggle. One full run by keyboard alone (Turn over, Mirror, Turn over, Figure-eight, Mirror, then the reveal) reached the reveal; I did not repeat QA's press-order matrix. Focus stays on Turn over and Mirror after pressing them.
2. **Names and states.** `Turn over` and `Mirror` are `button[aria-pressed]`; the chips carry `aria-pressed`. The picture is `role="img"` with a label that changes with the state: "Trefoil, front. Marks minus, minus, minus. Sum minus 3, left-handed." → "…turned over…" → "…mirrored and turned over. Marks plus, plus, plus. Sum plus 3, right-handed." The "+" and "−" badges and both faces are `aria-hidden`, and their meaning is in that label, so nothing is read twice or missed. Hand labels ("Left-handed" / "Right-handed") are real text in the readout; at Sum 0 (figure-eight) the hand label is blank by design and the label says "Sum 0."
3. **Is the turned or mirrored state announced?** Partly. The picture's label always names it ("turned over", "mirrored", "mirrored and turned over"), and a screen reader announces "pressed" on the toggle. The live region (`role=status`, polite) fires only when the sum or hand changes (see L1).
4. **Contrast, measured from computed colours, both themes** (stage background `--bg-3`): text `--fg` 13.72 dark / 15.38 light; seen line `--muted` 6.12 / 5.07; badge glyph on its disc 15.40 / 17.71; strands are `--fg`. Pressed-button text 9.85 / 14.25. All pass AA. The badge disc against the stage is only 1.12 / 1.15, but the disc is not what carries the sign: the glyph and the thin `--fg` ring are.
5. **Colour-blind safety.** The strands, badges and readout are monochrome `--fg`; the signs are glyph shapes (+ against −), and the Sum line gives the total in words. In greyscale and protanopia nothing is lost. The only hue-carried thing is the pressed-button tint (L3), and it is backed by `aria-pressed` and the readout.
6. **Reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: `getAnimations()` is 0 after each press, the card, mirror wrapper and badge overlay all report `transition-duration: 0s`, the card jumps to `rotateY(180deg)` (computed `matrix3d(-1,…)`), the wrapper to `scaleX(-1)`, and the opacity dip never runs (opacity stays 1). The lesson reads the media query itself (`useReducedMotion`, Demo.tsx) and settles at once, and the global rule also zeroes the transitions. A 1 s "Turned over" text label appears instead of the flip (visual only; see L1). Without reduced motion the flip is 300 ms and the Mirror dip is 100 ms out and 100 ms in (opacity sampled: 1 → 0 → 1 in about 250 ms). That is one dim-and-return per press, far under three flashes a second, and the marks are hidden during it, so no flash hazard. Not run: a mid-session change of the OS setting (the hook subscribes to `change`, so it should follow).
7. **Fit.** No horizontal scroll at 320, 360 or 390; the seen line (`white-space: nowrap`) does not overflow at any of them; stage controls are 44 px tall (Turn over 91x44, Mirror 68x44, chips 69x44 and 110x44). The lesson's own `min-height: 44px` rule means W-1650 does not apply to this stage's controls.
8. **Focus ring.** On these buttons it is Chromium's default auto ring (white on dark, near-black on light), which I saw on a screenshot in both themes: clearly visible. The accent-ring finding (W-1649) is therefore not what Chromium draws here; Firefox and Safari were not run.

## Conditions (route; none is a lock-out)

- **L1 (lesson, fix): Turn over is not announced.** Pressing it changes the picture, `aria-pressed` and the label, but neither the sum nor the hand, so the `role=status` region does not change (mutation log empty for Turn over; same for un-turning with Mirror on, which stays "+3 Right-handed"). A screen-reader learner who presses Turn over hears "pressed" and nothing else; the label with "turned over" is only read if they navigate to the picture. The reduced-motion "Turned over" label is a visible-only `<span>` with no live role and disappears after 1 s, so it does not help either. Suggested change: add the card state to the status text (for example "Turned over. Sum −3, left-handed.") on every settle, or make the picture a live label. Any new learner-visible text is a claim for `meta.json`. Owner: demo-builder.
- **L2 (lesson, fix): pressing a chip drops focus to `<body>`.** The selected chip is `disabled={locked || chip === c.id}`, so after Enter on Figure-eight `document.activeElement` is `BODY` (observed; Trefoil is the same code). A keyboard learner must Tab from the top again. Same pattern as Books B1 and unknot-game U2. Use `aria-disabled` and ignore the press, or move focus. Owner: demo-builder.
- **L3 (kit, fix): the pressed cue is weak in light.** `.active` is a tint (pressed against unpressed background 1.56:1 dark, 1.24:1 light) plus a 1 px accent border (5.95:1 dark; **2.26:1 light**, under the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11 for a state indicator). In greyscale the pressed buttons are only just darker. State is also carried by `aria-pressed`, the readout and the seen line, so this is not a lock-out. A selected-and-disabled chip also looks the same as a pressed toggle. Owner: design-lead (style), platform-engineer (`src/kit/controls.tsx` is protected).
- **L4 (lesson, low): badge glyph is 11.5 px at 360x640 and 320x568** (7 units at 1.64 px per unit; 19.3 px at 390x844). WCAG sets no minimum text size and the sign is also in the label and readout, so informational. Owner: demo-builder if the design lead wants it larger (cf. W-289).
- **Known kit/app items, confirmed, not re-routed:** W-1618 focus drops to `<body>` at "Lock it in" and at the reveal (scan `focus-management`, `focusAfterReveal: body`; the reveal paragraphs are not announced); the scan also reports stage-before-question reading order (kit). App: "Why trust this?" 221x23 and Map 30x17 are under the 24 px floor (W-1650 territory).

## Not checked

A real screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA, TalkBack); text zoom and 400% reflow; forced-colors mode; Firefox and Safari focus rings; viewports wider than 390; the figure-eight chip by touch. `meta.json` has no accessibility evidence field, so none was edited. No lesson code, board.json or progress.md was changed.
