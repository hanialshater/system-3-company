Built by demo-builder (sonnet build, EXP-003). Kept from evaluators until they report.

- Key state: six halves shaded, white sliver 1/64. Milestone `six`. QA path: "Shade the next half" (1), "Shade 5 more" (6), "Start over".
- The reveal quotes the largest k the learner reached (>= 6 because of the gate), from the same shaded()/white() functions that print the readouts.
- Angle per brief: the picture (pieces fit inside the unit square), not 0.999... The claim that the infinite sum is exactly 1 rests on the limit definition (c2).
- QA's final screenshot shows the reset state ("Start over" is the last control), so I took my own at k = 6: shaded 63/64, white 1/64 (red outline), labels 1/2 to 1/16 on the pieces. Labels for 1/32 and smaller were dropped (they overflowed their piece).
