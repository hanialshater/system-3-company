lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows logistic growth levels off at a limit; has not met delayed feedback).

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: "grows at 0.5 a year". I don't know what 0.5 means (50% a year? half a unit?). It matters later, because the rule is "growth × delay passes about 1.6".
- The prediction is good: "settles" is my default, and the predict stage even draws the 1-year-delay line settling, which strengthens the trap.
- Explore shows it clearly: delay 2 settles; delay 4 swings to 2.9x and 0.07x, in growing-then-steady waves; slowing growth to 0.2 settles again. The two tiles (highest/lowest) make the swing measurable. Strong.
- Told-not-shown: "it overshoots before the signal arrives" is the mechanism; the stage never shows the late food signal (e.g. a lagged ghost line). It is told.
- "here 0.5 × 4 = 2, against 0.8" is cryptic: "0.8" is 0.2 × 4, but the sentence doesn't say so.
- Small display issues: "Delay 1 years"; "1.0x limit" next to "1.00x limit" (mixed precision).
- Layout: reveal fits on phone; no clipping.

## 2. After reading the brief (version only)

- Version 1, 5 claims shown as checked.

## Fix suggested

Gloss the growth rate ("50% a year when food is plentiful"); write "against 0.2 × 4 = 0.8, which settles"; optionally draw the food signal the population is reacting to, lagged.
