# Learner review: pesticide-paradox
lesson version: v2
boundary: an adult with school-level science, new to the topic (reviewed after the lesson was revised; the lesson's own learner field is quoted in section 2).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess; "falls" is the obvious answer for a spray, and the question builds a model (pests breed freely, predators grow only by eating pests, same share killed) which a beginner has to hold in mind with no picture. The hook ("What happens to the pests?") does not leak. The locked stage already draws two wavy lines, pests and predators, with "?" in the average boxes.
- Where understanding first broke: the one explore caption, "Same extra death rate for both. Dashed lines are averages; the pest average is where predators break even." "Break even" is never defined: the beginner has no idea why predators would have a break-even pest number, and that idea is the whole mechanism. It is explained only in the reveal's second paragraph, in words.
- What was SHOWN: raising the slider to 20% moves the dashed green average up to 1,200 and the dashed red down to 800, against a dotted reference "pests, no spray" at 1,000. The contrast is real, but small: a 20% rise in the average of two wavy lines is hard to see by eye; the learner mostly reads the two numbers. Only one spray strength (20%) is on the main path, so "more spray, more pests" is not seen as a trend. The "pests, no spray" label overlaps the pest curve and is hard to read.
- Told not shown: why predators suffer more ("so the same spray costs them more"), which is the paradox's reason; nothing on the stage shows predators eating pests or predators' food supply.
- The reveal card sits on the stage after Spray off: 0%, 1,000 and 1,000, while the reveal text says "At spray strength 20%... rose from 1,000 to 1,200". The learner reading the reveal sees numbers that contradict it.
- Reveal earned: the "rises" yes by the two numbers; the mechanism no (told).
- Problems: (1) "break even" undefined; (2) mechanism told; (3) one spray level shown, small effect; (4) reveal card on the reset 0% stage; (5) overlapping chart label.
- No FATAL.
