# Build note: one-boss-moves-the-mean (demo-builder, 2026-10-12)

- Intent: five salary bars (30, 35, 40, 45, boss) with a solid mean line and a dashed median line. The brief asked for dots on a number line; at a 0–500 scale the four low dots overlap into one blob on a phone, so I used bars (same data, readable).

- After screenshots: the value labels on top of the short bars collided with both lines; moved the values under the axis and moved the mean label off the median bar.
