## Verdict: approve with changes

The shorter panels, with the nets inside the blocks and the Total on each title row, make the comparison the reveal asks for ("Look at the two Totals: −$100 both times") visible at a glance. At 390×844 both panels, the shaded band, $200, $100, $300 and both Totals are on screen when the reveal opens (stage top 174 px, reveal bottom 792 of 844). One label is clipped.

## Against the four criteria

| Criterion | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Pass | The model chip, then each panel's title and Total on one row, blocks with nets, the ruler with offer marks, and that panel's buttons under it. Totals sit in the same place in both panels, so the eye compares them. |
| Labels ≥ 10 px | Pass on size, **fail on clipping** | 12.2 px in explore, and 10 px at the phone reveal after the kit fit (the floor, not the 11 px target); 15.7 px on desktop. The refusal flash ("−$350: worse than smoke") is aria-hidden at 8.5 px. That is acceptable, because the red $350 and ⊗ mark stay. But in panel 2 the "$100" label on the filter arrow starts **7 px (phone, explore) / 9 px (desktop, every phase)** left of the svg and shows as "ı100". |
| Grouping, tap targets ≥ 24 px | Pass | Offers 101×44, liability buttons 156×44 (phone). Each panel's controls sit directly under it. The gap between "Offer $350" and the "Right to clean air" title is tight (about 9 px), but the order still makes the grouping clear. |
| Reveal evidence kept | Pass | The $200 inside the band, $100 vs $300, both Totals −$100 and the band itself are visible. The clipped "$100" is repeated under "Factory −$100", so no reveal evidence is lost. |

## Required changes (board)

1. **Un-clip panel 2's "$100" arrow label.** Move it inside the viewBox (for example, put it right of the arrow or above the chimney, or widen the viewBox left margin). Done when no svg `<text>` box extends past its svg at 390 or 1280 px, in explore or at the reveal.

## Recommended (not blocking)

- The Total bar is an empty white box with no scale, so it reads as an input field. Fill it with `--bad-fill` (it is always a cost) or drop it and keep the value.
- Add 6 to 8 px between panel 1's offer row and panel 2's title.
- In the predict frame at 390×844 the stage is cut at the bottom: the tops of "Pay $300 damages" and "Fit $100 filter" show under the panel 2 ruler. Nothing a learner needs to predict is lost, but it looks broken. Check it after the fix above.
