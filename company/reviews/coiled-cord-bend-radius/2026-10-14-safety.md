## Reasons
Curvature = 1/radius on a plain cord. The stage is a plain cord drawing with no plug or power, so the coiled-powered-lead hazard in the safety lens is not shown. Risk is transfer: a learner may apply it to an extension lead, and a coiled lead in use can overheat. The card disclaims any electrical limit.

## Required stage warnings
Existing: "Plain cord; storage only." Stage caption "Plain cord; storage only" (HomeLesson.tsx caption for view=cord) and the reveal limit "The drawing gives no electrical safety limit or product-specific bend radius." Both must stay.
