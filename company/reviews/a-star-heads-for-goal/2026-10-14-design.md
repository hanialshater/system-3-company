## Verdict: approve with changes

The compact stage works. With the controls in a column beside the grid, the reveal opens with the stage top on screen (stage top 174 px, reveal bottom 788 of 844), and everything the reveal points at is visible: the table (7 cells · route 6, 46, 16, both routes 10) and the line under it ("Guess at S: 6. Real route with the wall: 10."). One required change is about legibility of meaning-carrying labels.

## Against the four criteria

| Criterion | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Pass | Rule line, then grid with controls beside it, then legend, table and summary line. The live rule line switches with the toggle ("Score = steps so far." / "+ guess of steps left."), which is a good use of the space. |
| Labels ≥ 10 px | Pass on size, **fail on overlap** | Settle numbers are 11.3 px in explore and 10 px at the reveal (phone). Desktop is 12.5 px. But the route polyline is drawn over the settle numbers on route cells (5, 10, 16, 20, 24, 28, 34 are struck through), and "1" and "46" sit on top of the S and G markers. |
| Grouping, tap targets ≥ 24 px | Pass | One column of four controls, each 96×44 px (Settle to goal 96×46). Action buttons first, toggles second, in DOM order. The column ends above the grid's bottom. |
| Reveal evidence kept | Pass | Every number the reveal quotes is in the table or the summary line at the reveal, on the QA path and after a second guess/wall run. |

## Required changes (board)

1. **Make the settle-order numbers on the route readable.** Today the route line runs through them, and the start and goal numbers collide with S and G. The legend says "Numbers: settle order", so these labels carry meaning. Draw the route under the labels and give the labels a halo (`paint-order: stroke` in `--bg-2`), or offset the route. Also move the S/G order numbers clear of the markers (for example, drop them, since S is always 1 and G is the last count, which the table already shows). Done when, at 390×844, no label on the route is crossed by the line.

## Recommended (not blocking)

- **Toggle look.** "Wall: on" (aria-pressed true) and "Guess: off" (false) look identical, so the state is carried only by the text. Elsewhere in the product a pressed toggle is shaded (rotation-untangles-the-chain's "Rotating"). Give aria-pressed="true" the kit active style so the two toggles read as switches, not actions.
- At the reveal, the grid grows from 218 to 240 px wide and the row ends 1 px past the stage's right edge. The cause is the kit, not this lesson (see the platform item below). Re-check this after the kit fix.

## Kit observation (routed to platform-engineer)

At the reveal, `.lesson[data-phase="reveal"] .stage .stage-svg { flex: none }` (src/app/globals.css) applies to a picture that is a flex item in a horizontal row, and the picture jumps to its natural width. Here that costs 1 px. In every-second-seat-out it costs 6 px on each side. The fit then measures the grown picture, so the "labels at 10 px" Pre-flight numbers describe the reveal, not explore.

## Builder's account, read after the findings

Pre-flight v7 reports crop 0 px and grid labels at 10 px at the reveal, and I agree with both. It does not mention the route overprinting the labels, or the S/G overlap, which may predate v7. Merging "No guess"/"With guess" into one toggle matches the brief's wording and costs nothing on screen.
