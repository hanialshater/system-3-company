lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (school science; knows populations level off when food runs out; has not met a minimum-group threshold).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 7 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: nowhere. The question stem itself states the rule ("below that, numbers fall"), so with 40 < 50 < 60 the answer is reading comprehension, not prediction. The surprise the lesson wants ("small groups don't always recover") is defused before the learner commits.
- Explore is clear: 40 and 20 curve down to an X, 60 and 80 rise to the 1,000 line; the dashed "50: mates found" line visibly splits fates. Good.
- Told-not-shown: "Below it each animal finds fewer mates, so the group shrinks faster" is told; the steepening fall on the log scale does show it, but nothing points the learner at it.
- Layout: the "60"/"80" start labels overlap ("8̶0") in step 4. After Clear, the reveal sits under an empty chart, so the learner reads "two fates" with no curves in view. No reveal clipping seen.

## 2. After reading the brief (version only)

- Version 1, 3 claims marked verified in the trust sheet.

## Fix suggested

Take the rule out of the stem (say only "these animals need mates to breed; groups of 40 and 60 share room for 1,000") so the learner must guess whether small groups recover; reveal the threshold on the stage. Keep curves drawn under the reveal; separate the overlapping start labels.
