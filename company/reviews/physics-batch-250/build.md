# Physics expansion — draft review record

The founder requested 250 physics lessons in one PR, with SVG and 3D when useful. PR #28 contains exactly 250 draft lessons: an inclined-orbit projection, 17 other curated cases, and 232 interactive scaling scenarios across eight areas. The orbit uses an orthographic projection drawn in SVG; some other scenes show shaded spheres. No WebGL engine or new dependency was introduced.

The scaling scenarios use one shared slider/chart representation and 137 distinct equation strings. Ninety-five additional scenarios vary a different input to an equation already used in the batch. They are bounded mathematical illustrations and require overlap and learner review; the count does not establish 250 distinct laws or 250 independently designed simulations. Inputs with integer constraints use whole-step multipliers. The doubled comparison remains visible while the learner explores other inputs. All claims remain unverified and all lessons remain draft.
