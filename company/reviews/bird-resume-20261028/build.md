## Pre-flight v1, common to these drafts

- Every stage initially shows only “Evidence hidden”; the title and hook are intended as a tension without identifying the keyed option. Reviewers should evaluate each for inadvertent clues and overly easy distractors.
- Three options use balanced keyed positions (four each). The question case is encoded in each lesson's copy; the first button shows its context, the second exposes the visual evidence and earns reveal. Buttons set a minimum 44 px height, although actual computed targets and wrapping are not browser-tested.
- Each reveal points to the currently retained schematic and is 180–218 characters. Predicted claims or example values are not changed by the learner; these are two-step visual explanations, not 12 independently calibrated simulations.
- The component has no reset control. A reload would remount the state, but reload behavior has not been checked. More detailed recall cases are stored in metadata, with a changed context or observation where possible; they need a pedagogical review for genuine transfer.
- No full application, browser interaction, 390×844 screenshot, keyboard, screen reader, light/dark contrast, learner or independent science review. SVG stage labels, shapes, colors, and the retained picture/reveal fit require visual QA. No claim of human learning or publication readiness.
