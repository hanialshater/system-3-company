## Pre-flight v1 — common to all thirteen

1. Titles and hooks do not name the right option; the prediction is made while the stage says only “Evidence hidden”.
2. All questions use three verbal options. Distractor plausibility, parallel precision and usefulness remain open to an independent reviewer; several are intentionally obvious and may need redesign.
3. Both controls are locked at prediction, and no picture or label indicates the correct option before prediction.
4. The first control reveals the case; the second overlays the part, route or relation named in the reveal. The wetland and sun/shade conditions are explicit assumptions; the axolotl reproduction scene is illustrative. No measurement or recorded observation is asserted.
5. Inspect the subject and Trace the evidence appear first in DOM order; both modify the stage and call `interact`; reveal requires both. The path has not been exercised in a browser.
6. There is no stage Reset control. Reload should reset local React state; this has not been verified in a browser.
7. No 390 × 844 reveal screenshot, readability measurement, contrast check, keyboard check or desktop flow has been performed. The two controls may need responsive changes after visual inspection.

## Integration and review

All metadata status is `draft`, every claim is unverified, and all evidence arrays are empty. OpenStax and Smithsonian links are broad research starting points only; no source has been matched to a specific claim, taxon, image, date or question. A fact-checker must locate and inspect exact evidence. Each schematic needs biological accuracy, overlap, accessibility, visual pedagogy and learner review. In particular, toggling information into view may not give learners enough agency or show a mechanism; several distractors are weak, and a redesign may be needed.
