The learner is invited to tap three smaller dishes and gets no number back, so the scaling idea (1 billion gives 10, 100 million gives 1) is never seen, and "0.1 of a cell" is hard to picture. Payoff told, not shown.
- The three smaller-dish taps do not require Add drug, so the learner sees an unchanged dot lawn with "Resistant ones look like the rest": nothing to read.
- Terms before defined: "mutation" (a school-biology adult has "gene" but may not link it to a random change), "culture", "toy dish", "ringed". The reveal says "the drug did not create resistance" without having named the belief it rebuts.
