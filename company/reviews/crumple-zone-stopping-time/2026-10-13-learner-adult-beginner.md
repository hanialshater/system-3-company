## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. "Same / half / twice" is easy to read; I would say "same" (the car and speed are the same) or "half". Nothing gives the answer away except the bar already labelled "15,000 N·s" and "MOMENTUM REMOVED", words I do not know.
- Explore: dragging the stopping time makes a dashed tall bar become a long flat bar with the same area, and AVERAGE FORCE drops (150 -> 100 -> 50 kN). That is a clear, visible effect.
- Where understanding first broke: the caption "Bar area = momentum removed." I have no idea what momentum is, and "area of a bar" as something conserved is a leap; I would watch the number fall and take the area talk on trust. The reveal then defines it ("mass times speed") only after the fact.
- Reveal earned? The force halves with doubled time is earned by the readouts at 0.15, 0.25, 0.30 s (the pattern 100 -> 60 -> 50 is visible). But the recorded stops (0.15, 0.25, 0.30) never include 0.20 s, the exact value the prompt tells me to set ("Stretch the stopping time to 0.20 s"), so the reveal's "in 0.20 s, 75 kN" is a number I never saw. This may be a slider-step limit rather than a driver limit; the slider's marks and steps are not visible in the screenshots.
- Problems:
  - The prompt asks for 0.20 s but the slider jumped 0.10 -> 0.15 -> 0.25 -> 0.30 in the run; if 0.20 s is hard to land on, the instruction cannot be followed exactly.
  - At 0.30 s the force label at the right of the bar is cut off ("50 k") in the explore screenshot; the reveal view shows it fully but in a smaller figure.
  - Units kN, N·s and kg·m/s are used with no gloss; an adult beginner has no scale for "150 kN" (is that a lot? how many car weights?).
  - The hook says the force changes "by how much", but nothing connects "less force on the car" to "less harm to the people inside"; the lesson is about the car's average force, not about passengers, and it never says so.
  - The last paragraph says crushing "buys time" using "about 1.5 m"; the 1.5 m figure and the idea that slowing the stop needs distance are never shown, only asserted.
  - "Average force, illustrative numbers" in small grey text weakens the preceding figures without saying what is illustrative.
- FATAL: none found from the artifact.

- The brief's boundary says the learner does not know momentum or impulse, and the stage leads with both ("MOMENTUM REMOVED", "N·s"); the lesson carries its own unknown vocabulary on the very first screen.
- The objective (fixed momentum, double time halves force) is what the readouts show; the reveal states it. The misconception (a stiff car is safer) is addressed only in the last paragraph, not by anything the learner manipulates.
