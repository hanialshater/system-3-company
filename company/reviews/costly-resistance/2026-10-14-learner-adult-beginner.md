# Learner review: costly-resistance
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows resistant bacteria survive a drug; assumes that once the drug stops, slower resistant cells vanish quickly).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 8 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: the prediction. "5% slower each generation" invites "they'll be gone"; 0% vs 4% vs 10% is a real choice. Good.
- Explore shows it: a 400-dot dish, red dots thinning from 32 (gen 5) to 15 (gen 20) to 2 (gen 60). The slow fade is visible; the 1% vs 10% comparison makes cost size matter.
- Told-not-shown: "shrinks the resistant-to-normal ratio by only 5% a generation" is the mechanism and is told; nothing on stage shows a ratio. Acceptable for 60 s.
- Contradiction: the reveal says the decline "never quite reaches zero", but the stage under it (10% slower, gen 60) shows "0 of 400" and an all-green dish. A beginner sees zero and reads "never reaches zero". Say "in a big population it never quite reaches zero; in this dish of 400 it can hit 0".
- "Show me why" stays disabled until all four generations are pressed; fine but slightly slow.
- Layout: fine; reveal fits on phone.
