The unit shift "100 friendships = 1 friend each" is stated in the question and fine.

**Told vs shown.** The cause (small groups join into a giant) now has a stand-in on the existing picture: the second group in green beside the gold giant and a "Second" number that collapses. The reveal names the green group and the swallowing, pointing at it. That meets the decision. The 1.25 chip adds resolution in the jump region.

**Reveal quotes only reached states?** Yes: it lists exactly the chips pressed (the gate requires 1.5 and 2.0; the question supplied 36 and 148).

**Answered the learner's question?** Yes for "why not 36%": the numbers are on screen and the green-group story explains it.

**Verdict: launch with notes.**

**Top remaining finding:** the word "suddenly" (the brief's gap) is told, while six chips read as a gradual S-curve and the 1.0-1.5 region where the jump lives has only one extra stop; the hairball hides the merging in the picture.
