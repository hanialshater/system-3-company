**Illustrative heat values, checked closely as asked:** h = 10 W/m2K comes out at 10.6 for the ice and 9.8 for the person when computed from free convection plus radiation (ht + CoolProp). Wool k = 0.04 W/mK sits at the light end of the opened fibrous-insulation values (0.035-0.06). Latent heat 333.6 kJ/kg and ice density 916.7 kg/m3 come from CRC and IAPWS-06. Sensitivity: across k 0.035-0.06 and h 8-12, the bare block melts in 4.7-7.1 h and the coated one lasts 14-23 h, so the answer and the 'about 3.5x' story are robust. The person's 522 W bare is a flow rate with the skin held at 34 C, not a sustainable state (noted in c3).
