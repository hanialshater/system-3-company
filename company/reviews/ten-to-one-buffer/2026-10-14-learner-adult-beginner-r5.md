## 1. From the artifact (written before reading the earlier review)

- Predict: the stem now defines a buffer (a jar of weak acid HA as hollow dots and its partner base A− as filled dots) and defines pKa operationally ("with equal amounts its pH is its pKa: 4.76"). A beginner can read the question.
- Predict leak (top finding): the locked ruler hides the pH numbers ("?") but keeps the labels under each tick: "acid ×100, acid ×10, pKa 4.76 equal, base ×10, base ×100". The question is "10 times as much acid as base", and the tick labelled "acid ×10" sits one step to the left of 4.76. Since the options are 3.76 / 4.76 / 5.76, one tick apart, the ruler tells the learner both the direction and the size. The prediction becomes reading a label.
- Locked frame: the BASE : ACID and PH readouts are clipped at the bottom of the stage ("1 : 1", "4.76" half cut off).
- Explore: raising acid to 100 mmol fills the jar with hollow dots, the marker moves one tick left, and the readouts say 1 : 10 and pH 3.76. Raising base to 100 mmol puts the marker back at 4.76 with a jar full of both. That second move is the best moment in the lesson: the jar has ten times the stuff and the pH has not moved, so "the ratio, not the amount" is seen, not told.
- Gate: "Show me why" is active after the first slider move, so a learner can skip the "100 of each" step that carries the ratio point. The reveal then says "Then 100 of each sat back at 4.76". If that sentence is not conditional, it would describe something the learner never saw. (Not tested in this run.)
- Reveal: the formula is given with a gloss for the log ("the number of tens in the ratio") and tied to the ruler ("one tick, one pH unit"). The learner saw only one tenfold step (1 : 10) and one 1 : 1, so "each tenfold change moves pH by 1" is shown once and then generalised. "More buffer only makes that pH harder to push" is told; nothing on stage pushes.
- Why more acid lowers pH is not explained, but at this boundary the learner already expects acid to lower pH, so that does not block.
