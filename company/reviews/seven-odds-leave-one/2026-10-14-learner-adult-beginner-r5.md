## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: "Add 7 odd numbers, any odd numbers you like. Is the total even or odd?" Even / Odd / Depends. The locked stage shows seven columns of dots (1, 3, 5 … 13), each with one brown "leftover" dot on top, and "Total ?". Nothing leaks; the leftover-dot picture is a fair hint at most.
- Explore: each tap adds a pair of blue dots to one number ("Tap a number to add a pair. Each stays odd."). The total goes 51 → 53 → 55 → 57 → 59 → 61, always moving by 2. Tapping 13 wraps it to 1, and the total drops to 49. "Pair up the leftovers" pulls the seven brown dots down into three pairs plus one ringed in red. That single frame is the whole argument, and it is on stage. Trying to make it even and failing, then seeing the lone dot, did the work before the reveal.
- Reveal: "The 7 leftovers make 3 pairs and one dot alone" matches what I saw. But "You made 7 different totals (53, 55, 57, 59, 61)" lists five numbers. The ones I saw were 51, 53, 55, 57, 59, 61 and 49. A learner who reads carefully will notice that the list does not match its own count.
- The last line ("an even count of odd numbers pairs off completely") is told. The stage always has exactly seven numbers, so I cannot try six. That is fine as an extension.
