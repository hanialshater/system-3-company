# Build note: silent-letter-swaps (demo-builder, sonnet build, EXP-003, 2026-10-13)

- Retitled "Spelling mistakes in DNA" (the brief title gives away "silent").
- Design: a codon (CTT Leu first, then TAT Tyr, GGA Gly via "Next codon") with nine tiles, one per single-letter change. Each tile reads "1st → T" until tapped, then shows the new codon and its amino acid, coloured same / different / stop. "Try all 549 changes" shows the whole-code tally. The genetic code table is typed once; every count (549, 134, 392, 23, 8/0/126 of 183) is computed from it and matches artifacts/scratch/w5life/codons.mjs.
- Terms defined in the question: codon = three-letter DNA word naming an amino acid; amino acid = protein building block. DNA letters on the coding strand (T, not U).
- Gate: two milestones, all nine tiles of one codon tapped and "Try all 549". Locked stage shows only the "toy: standard genetic code" line and untapped tiles; the tally is hidden.
- Reveal quotes the learner's fully tapped codon (CTT on the QA path: 3 of 9) and the whole-code share the stage showed.
