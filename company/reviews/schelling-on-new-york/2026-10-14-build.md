## What the learner does

1. **Predict.** The stage is locked and shows the random NYC-shaped grid. The question is "how unevenly spread, on 2 x 2-cell blocks, before anyone moves?" with three options: "About 0 to 0.1", "About 0.4 to 0.5" (key) and "About 0.9 to 1".
2. **Explore.** The controls, in this DOM order:
   - Step, Run, Reset and New mix (the seed is shown).
   - Four block-size buttons (1x1, 2x2, 4x4, 8x8). A lattice clipped to the land marks the blocks, and each button the learner has pressed shows D "start → now".
   - The threshold slider (0 to 50% in steps of 5).
   - Readouts: tick and "would stay %".
   - After the first finished run, the line "Left out of this model: ..." appears.
   The milestones are `settled-30` (a run at 30% that settles) and `other-unit` (any block size other than 2x2 pressed).
3. **Reveal.** The stage pins the learner's last settled 30% run at 2x2. Controls become static readouts with the same values. The reveal quotes the start D (2x2), the start D on the largest other block size measured, and the settled D.

## Decisions

1. **R8, the real-city figure is dropped.** The Brown University source cannot be reached from this container, so nobody can read the value, the group pair, the year or the unit from the page. Following R8 ("otherwise drop the number and keep only the unit lesson"), the lesson has no toggle, no real D, no real group names and no Brown citation. Claim 3 is not created. Only the unit-contrast sentence is kept, verbatim: "A tract holds thousands of people. A block in this grid holds a handful of squares." It is claim c15, citing the Census tract glossary (unverified). R8(d) and R8(e), the near-number check and the learner probe, therefore have nothing to test. If a later version restores the figure, all of R8(a) to (e) apply again.
2. **The grid is N = 40, from the real DCP mask.** Only the 200-byte `hex` bitmask is inlined, as `engine.ts MASK_HEX`. There is no runtime fetch. The `labels` never enter the lesson; `check.mjs` reads them from the brief's JSON for the R2 region check only. `check.mjs` asserts that MASK_HEX equals the JSON's land cells (576).
3. **Engine.**
   - `engine.ts` copies the rule of `abm.ts` (mulberry32 verbatim) and adds walls. `abm.ts` is not edited.
   - It is self-contained (no imports) so that both node and Next can load it without `.ts`-extension import issues.
   - Proof: `node src/demos/geography/schelling-on-new-york/check.mjs`. With an all-land 40x40 mask, every frame equals `abm.ts run()` (seeds 1 to 3: 15, 13 and 18 ticks). On the real mask, the start frame, end frame and tick count equal the brief's `schelling-nyc-check.mjs` for seeds 1 to 50 at 30%. Seed 1 gives 0.47 → 0.76 in 7 ticks. All checks passed (output in the report below).
4. **Seed policy.** The default is seed 1, which is simply the first seed. New mix cycles through seeds 1 to 50 in order, skips 3 and 36 (R2 edge artefact at 30%), and wraps after 50. Over these 48 seeds:
   - 2x2 start: 0.42 to 0.53 (mean 0.46).
   - Settled at 30%: 0.67 to 0.83 (mean 0.75).
   - Ticks: 7 to 24.
   No seed was chosen for how any region looks.
5. **Slider range (superseded in v2).** v1 ran 0 to 50%. v2 caps it at 0 to 30% (cultural review 2, ruling (a)); see the v2 section below.
6. **No hollow, ring or tint layers.** The lesson-one mechanism glyphs (hollow for would-move, red ring, tint) are left out. This lesson's gap is the unit, not the cascade. Leaving them out also keeps `--bad` away from the group colours entirely.
7. **Told vs shown (R3 item 5, demo-standard "Show the why").**
   - Unit dependence, the doesNotKnow item, is **shown**. The block buttons and lattice change D on the same grid (1.00 on 1x1 down to 0.12 on 8x8 at the start), and the reveal quotes those values.
   - "What the model cannot say" is **shown as a stage line**: "Left out of this model: ...", which appears after the first finished run. The reveal points at it: "Lacking what the stage lists, it does not show why any real city looks as it does."
   - The structural history (discrimination including the 1938 maps, then income) is **told** in one claimed sentence per source (c10a, c10b, c10c). I judge that this is context outside the brief's gap and does not need an animation. The evaluators rule on it.
8. **Controls give way to readouts at the reveal.** The shell's reveal fit scales the stage down to as little as 0.4. At that scale the 44 px controls measured under 24 px, and QA failed. In the reveal phase the stage now shows the same values as static readouts, and the slider becomes a text line with the same wording. Exploration ends at the reveal.
9. **Group colours are lesson-local hex values.** `colour-roles` says to use tokens, but the token set has no neutral pair other than `--accent`/`--domain`, and R5 bans `--warn`, `--bad` and `--good`. So `styles.module.css` defines `--ga` and `--gb` per theme. Open item for the visual designer.
10. **Category manager field.** `owner.categoryManager` is `cm-geography`, because `company check` requires it for the geography domain. The brief's owner is cm-geography-people-places.
