# Build note: rate-up-one-point

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13

- Intent: two bars on a 0–10% scale, cut into 1-point slices. The extra slice is the change in points; counted against the "before" bar's slices it is the percent change.
- Milestones: 4% to 5%, then a 1-point rise from another start. "Start at" tiles set the start and put the new rate one point above it (1→2 is +100%).
- The new-rate slider starts equal to the old rate (no gap), so the locked stage gives nothing away; QA's two half-point presses reach 5%.
- No model chip: this is arithmetic, not a model. c4 (style-guide wording) needs a source check.
