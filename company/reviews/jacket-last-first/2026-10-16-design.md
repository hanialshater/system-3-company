## R2 ruling (W-1339, closes W-1323)

R2 said "drawn in A's arrangement". It assumed that A's arrangement leaks nothing. jacket-last-first asks "Which goes into the bag first?", and A's arrangement (jacket at the bottom, shoes on top, arrows over the items above the jacket) is the predict option "The jacket" acted out on the stage. Drawn at predict, that picture shows a mode in effect while `mode = null`, and it suggests an answer. The served pre-merge build shows exactly that.

The amended R2 is in company/design/specs/lifehacks-engines-v2.md §2. When the question asks which arrangement, order or placement to use, the neutral picture draws the shared setup in a layout that is neither mode's answer. That layout may show what the question states, and nothing only one mode shows. The a2f14c3 neutral branch meets this. The bag is empty, and the items lie beside it, unstacked, with no arrows and no highlight, in the use order the question itself gives. Pressing A still changes the picture visibly (the stack and arrows appear), so the QA path holds.

## §8 two-option ruling (W-1248)

The ruling is "confirmed under a rule". The rule and per-lesson table are in the spec §8. Two options are enough when four things hold. The question is two-way. The options use the same precision and neither is the only exact one. No in-between outcome is left out. Neither option repeats a mode label.

- sock-pairing-by-pattern, shoes-opposite-ends, tote-balance-sides: two options are enough.
- fitted-sheet-corners-nest: **third option required.** The right option is the only number, and the hook "how much flatter" invites a smaller-saving belief that has no option.
- socks-use-shoe-space: **third option required.** The model gives "some, but less than beside" above 600 cm³. Two options force a false either/or.

In both cases, only a third option is added, last, and the answer and its index are unchanged. The stop-if does not fire.
