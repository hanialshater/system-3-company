# Build note: thales-corner (demo-builder, 2026-10-13)

- Intent: a half circle with the diameter AB through the centre O. A slider moves P through 17 places (10 to 170 degrees round from B). A green right-angle mark and the readout "Angle at P" are measured from the drawn coordinates (angleAt), not typed. They read 90° everywhere.
- "Draw the radius OP" adds the dashed radius, labels the equal angles x, x (at A and at P) and y, y (at B and at P), and turns the readout into "x + y = 90°", measured at P.
- Key states (two milestones): P has been in 3 different places (the starting place counts); the radius has been drawn. QA path: two slider presses (places 3 -> 4 -> 5), then the radius toggle.
- The reveal quotes the readings the learner actually saw ("90°, 90°, 90°"), taken from the set of places visited.
- Locked: there is no right-angle mark and the readout shows "?".
- Deviation from brief: the brief asked for "no number". I show the measured angle, because the learner's question is "does it change?" and a number answers that honestly. "Isosceles" is not used; the stage says "two equal sides and two equal angles".
- After screenshots: the layout reads cleanly on the phone. No change.
- All claims unverified, for the fact-checker.
