# Learner review: thales-corner
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows triangle angles sum to 180° and what a radius is; has not seen Thales' theorem).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 4 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: that the angle doesn't change as P moves. The hook ("does the corner get sharper?") and the prediction set this up well; "it depends" is the natural guess.
- But I never saw it stay the same. I made one slider drag from place 3 to place 5 and saw a single reading, "ANGLE AT P 90°". "Show me why" then unlocked, and the reveal says "You put P in 3 places and the angle read 90°, 90°, 90°". Those were slider pass-through positions next to each other near B, not three places I looked at. The invariance ("wherever P is") is the claim of the lesson, and on this path it is told, with a recap that overstates what I did. A one-drag gate is too easy.
- Drawing radius OP is good: two isosceles triangles with x, x and y, y marks, and the tile "25° + 65° = 90°". The proof in the reveal (2x + 2y = 180°) is clear and earned. The tile doesn't say which of 25/65 is x and which is y; the x/y labels on the figure are small.
- Layout: clean, reveal fits on phone.

## 2. After reading the brief (version only)

- Version 1, 3 claims shown as checked.

## Fix suggested

Count a "place" only where the learner releases the slider (or offer 3 spread-out preset positions, e.g. near A, top, near B), show a running list "90°, 90°, 90°" on the stage, and label the tile "x = 25°, y = 65°".

Verdict: revise
