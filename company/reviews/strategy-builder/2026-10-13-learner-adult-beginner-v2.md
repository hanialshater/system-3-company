# Learner review: strategy-builder
lesson version: v2
boundary: an adult with school-level science, new to the topic (reviewed after the lesson was revised; the lesson's own learner field is quoted in section 2).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. The question packs a lot (payoffs 3 / 0 vs 5 / 1, ten rounds, tit-for-tat defined, copy-the-winner), but it is all stated. 6% is the counter-intuitive correct choice; 25 / 50 / 95% are the natural overestimates. The hook ("Cheats win every single meeting with a helper. Can helping still take over?") does not leak the number. The locked stage already shows the payoff table (30, 9, 14, 10) and 3 green dots among 100.
- Where understanding first broke: the payoff table. Its columns "vs tit-for-tat" and "vs cheat" and rows tit-for-tat / cheat have no stated reading direction (does 9 mean what tit-for-tat scores against a cheat, or what a cheat scores?). The numbers 30, 9, 14, 10 are never derived from the 3 / 0 / 5 / 1 and ten rounds in the question; the reveal gives one line of it (9 vs 14).
- What was SHOWN: from 3%, the green dots die out (2 of 100 at gen 10, 0 at gen 60, "Cheats score more"); from 25% every dot turns green (100 of 100). The "average now" column shows why: 9.4 vs 10.1 at 3%, 14.7 vs 11.1 at 27%. That is a good picture of the tipping.
- Told not shown: "The tipping point is 1 in 17, about 6%". The panel asks the learner to "find where it flips" with the slider, but moving the slider only redraws generation 0 with a caption (27%: "Tit-for-tat scores more"); the main path never brackets 6% (it goes 3%, 25%, 27%) and never runs a start near 6% or states how 1 in 17 arises. The learner who chose 6% is told "Your prediction held" without having seen 6%, and one who chose 25% sees a run from 25% that works and may feel confirmed.
- Reveal earned: the direction yes; the 6% figure no (stated). Cooperation takeaway "when helpers keep meeting again" is a told sentence.
- Problems: (1) payoff table orientation and derivation; (2) 6% / 1 in 17 told, never shown; (3) "Your prediction held" for a number the stage never displayed.
- No FATAL.
