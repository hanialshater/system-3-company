### Reset, Seen and reveal relationship

Across all seven, the observed sequence is case A → case B → Reset → reveal. Reset returns the current picture to **completed case A**, while the Seen row retains both actually observed results. Its meaningful visible effect is therefore returning from B's comparison to A's comparison; it does not erase observation history. The reveal quotes the same two recorded results, using the same displayed rounding. The question's case has appeared before the reveal, and its result survives Reset in Seen. The reveal affordance is unavailable after only case A and available after case B in the recorded sheets. This is evidence for the shown path only, not a substitute for interaction QA of arbitrary control orders or repeat presses.
