## M3 strictness: OK

The rule is `\burl\(\s*["']?\s*(?:[a-z][a-z0-9+.-]*:|\/\/)/i`, applied to every lab file, CSS and TSX. It flags any scheme (`https:`, `http:`, `data:`, `javascript:`), protocol-relative `//`, quoted or spaced forms, and inline styles. The wider net than asked is right for this page:

- The lab has no `url(` at all today (grep of src/app/hq/personalization: none), so zero false positives now; the clean scan stays empty.
- A `data:` URL cannot leak, but a data URL is also the usual way to smuggle in a remote reference (SVG with an `href`), and the lab needs no images. Banning it costs nothing and removes a case a reviewer would have to reason about.
- Relative `url(./x.png)` and fragment `url(#id)` still pass (the rest of HQ uses `url(#tile)`; if the lab ever copies the office map's SVG patterns, they stay legal). `url(/api/...)` is caught by the existing /api rule.
- Four planted rows (three CSS, one inline style) are required to fail under the named rule.

Nit (does not block): the rule only sees `url(`. A CSS `@import "https://..."` (string form, no `url(`) is not caught, and neither is `image-set("https://...")`. Neither is used. Add `@import` and `image-set(` rows to FORBIDDEN and BREAKS when the lab first gets a stylesheet change that touches either; owner personalization-engineer. Not worth blocking W-1719.
