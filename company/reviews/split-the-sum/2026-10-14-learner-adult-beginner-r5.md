# Learner review (re-review r5): split-the-sum
lesson version: v2
boundary: adult beginner; knows single-digit products and area = width × height; does not use splitting as a strategy.
Screenshots seen: yes (artifacts/walkthroughs/split-the-sum/sheet.png, 3 frames, phone)
Verdict: revise

## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: cut a 13 × 7 rectangle in two and add the areas. Which cuts give the true 13 × 7: only at 10 / any cut / only near the middle? The locked stage shows the 13 × 7 grid cut at 8 | 5 with "?" readouts. Nothing leaks. "Only a cut at 10" is a believable misconception, since the hook primes "somewhere clever".
- Explore: I drag the cut to 10: 10×7 = 70, 3×7 = 21, together 91. The grid and the per-piece readouts are clear.
- **Where it loses me (blocking):** "Show me why" lit after this single move to 10. The panel asks me to drag "then to any other column", but nothing makes me do it. I never read the areas at any cut except 10. The reveal then says "Your cut at 9 gave 63 + 28 = 91 too". I never stopped at 9 or read 63 + 28; at most the slider passed through 9 on its way from 8 to 10. For a learner who picked "Only a cut at 10", the one thing that refutes the prediction (a second cut that also gives 91) was never seen. It is told, and it is attributed to me.
- Finding (minor): the reveal frame leaves a large empty band under the text. Harmless.
- If the gate required a second cut, and the reveal cited the cut the learner actually read last, the stage would carry "any cut works" on its own. The grid makes it obvious once two cuts are compared.

## 2. Earlier findings (v1 review)

1. The prediction was an arithmetic test ("What is 13 × 7?"): **fixed**. It now asks which cuts work, which is about the idea.
2. "Show me why" unlocked after one drag, and the reveal cites "Your cut at 9", which the learner never read: **not fixed**. This is the same failure, and it now matters more: the new prediction ("Only a cut at 10") is exactly what a second cut would refute, and the stage never makes me make one.
3. "The pieces always fill the same rectangle" is told: **partly**. Acceptable once two cuts are compared on the stage.

## 3. New findings

- **Revise:** require a second cut position (≠ 10) to be read before "Show me why" lights, and have the reveal quote the cut the learner actually stopped at, not one the slider passed through.
