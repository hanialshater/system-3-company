# Build note: double-the-size (demo-builder, 2026-10-12)

- Intent: the 6-inch box keeps its size on screen; widening the pizza (6, 12, 18, 24 in) grows the box to k × k small boxes. A toggle swaps the one big pizza for k² six-inch pizzas in the same box, so "a pizza fills the same share of its box at any size" is something you see, not a formula.
- Reveal unlocks once the 12-inch pizza has been on stage. Locked stage shows one 6-inch pizza; the readout is "?".
- After screenshots: the first version drew faint small-pizza outlines over the big pizza at all times, which read as clutter; replaced with the explicit toggle. Moved the width label above the box (it overflowed the right edge at 24 in).
