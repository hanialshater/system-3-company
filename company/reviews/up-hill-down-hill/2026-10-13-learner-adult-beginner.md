## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: that the time at each speed matters. The stage shows it as blocks on a speed-vs-hours chart: "up 2 h" is a long low block, "down 1 h" a short tall one, and a dashed "average" line at 40. Seeing the uphill block twice as wide is the point, and it lands.
- "Each block's area is 60 km" asks me to read area as distance; a beginner may not, but the caption "Average speed = 120 km ÷ total hours" carries it anyway.
- Explore is a single slider move to 60 km/h; trying other downhill speeds is possible but not prompted.
- Locked frame shows only the up block, with downhill at 30 km/h but no down block drawn; slightly odd.
- Layout: reveal fits on phone.
- Reveal answered my question (why not 45?): yes, "(30 + 30 + 60) ÷ 3" is clear.
