# Learner review: digits-are-logs
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult who remembers basic school arithmetic and everyday life; has never studied the topic (has not met logarithms; knows what powers of 2 are roughly).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess is possible only by gut. 21 / 31 / 100 with no handle on how big 2^100 is. A beginner would likely say "100" or "31" at random; 21 felt like a compromise. The walkthrough picked 21 (a wrong answer), so the panel says "You said 21", which is fine, but the prediction gives no anchor (e.g. what 2^10 is) so it is a lottery rather than a guess. Fine as a hook, weak as a prediction.
- Where understanding first broke: step 2, "Every 10 doublings: ×1024, about ×1000, so about 3 more digits." Nothing I had seen told me why x1000 means 3 more digits; the digit strip grows, but the link "multiply by 1000 = add 3 digits" is stated, not shown. Quote: "ticks: strip length after 10, 20, 30 … doublings". I could not tell what the ticks were measuring; they sit under the strip as labels 10, 20, 30, and the strip itself is a row of random-looking digits.
- The digit boxes (e.g. "2 9 5 1 4 7 9 ...") are noise to a beginner; the eye is invited to read them, not to count them. The "DIGITS" counter box is the useful part and sits off to the side next to the buttons.
- Slider value: "moved slider right (now 3)" then "+10 doublings" gives 13: fine. Jumping from 31 to 39 etc. shows 12 digits at 39 and 15 at 49: the +3 per ten is visible in the counter if I compare numbers, but I have to remember them myself.
- Large dead space on the stage (about a third of the screenshot is empty).
- Reveal: never unlocked in the transcript; the final screenshot shows "Run to 100" caught mid-run ("Doubling…") at 2^68 with "Try it first" greyed. Driver limit, not a lesson flaw as far as I can tell, since the gate looks reachable by running to 100.
- Reveal earned? Cannot judge; none seen.
- Problems: (1) prediction without an anchor; (2) "×1000 means +3 digits" asserted not demonstrated; (3) ticks unexplained; (4) digit strip is visually noisy and the number that matters is in a small box.
- No FATAL from the artifact.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Brief's boundary includes place value ("each extra digit is ten times bigger"); that is exactly the missing bridge I flagged, and the lesson relies on it without drawing it. Intent and experience diverge there.
- Misconception targeted (about 100 digits / unpredictable) is well matched to the 100 option, but the 21-vs-31 split gives no way to guess.
- Claims all verified; counts spot-checked (2^67 and 2^68 both 21 digits) are right.
- The final "logarithm" naming is only in the reveal, which I could not see.

Verdict: revise
