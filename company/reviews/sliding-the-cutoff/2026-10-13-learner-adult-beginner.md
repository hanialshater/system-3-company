- Shown: "Raise to 1.5" grows the red area under the sick curve left of the line (30.9% missed) and shrinks false alarms to 6.7%; "Lower to 0.5" flips it. The trade-off is visible.
- Shading has no key on the stage: red = missed sick, green = healthy false alarms is only inferable from the counters. The locked frame shows both colours unlabeled.
- Stage contradicts the reveal (main finding): the reveal says the better test "shrinks both at once". On the stage, switching to the better test at 0.5 changes missed from 6.7% to 0.6% but false alarms stay exactly 30.9%; only the sick curve moved. To see both fall I have to also move the cutoff (at 0.7: 1.1% and 24.2%), and nothing tells me to compare against the old test at 0.7. A careful beginner sees one error shrink, not both.
- The better-test curves move only slightly apart; easy to miss that anything changed besides the numbers.
- Layout: reveal fits on phone.
- Reveal answered my question (which mistake does the line decide?): yes; the better-test claim is not backed by what I saw.
