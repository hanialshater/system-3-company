## 1. From the artifact

- Explore: "Measure man → woman" draws the arrow and shows (0, 1). "Carry it to king" lands on queen, readout "queen 0.00 · crown 0.60 · prince 1.50". The landing ring and green crown are visible. The gate opens only after the messy map.

A sceptical learner may feel the miss was rigged by moving queen away, not by small nudges.
