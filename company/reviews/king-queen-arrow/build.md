# Build note: king-queen-arrow

- Intent: the brief's six-word toy map ("positions placed by hand" on stage). Buttons: measure man → woman, carry it to king, carry man → king to woman (the parallelogram read the other way). Readouts give the arrow and the nearest word to its tip.
- Key state: the arrow carried to king (milestone "applied-to-king").
- The brief proposed learners dragging words into place; I used fixed positions and arrow buttons, so the question's coordinates match the stage exactly. The learning designer may want the build-the-map version later.
- After screenshots: man and woman labels sat on top of the arrows; they are now drawn to the left of their dots.
