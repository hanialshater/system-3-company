## What was built

- **Format: Map explorer, without new kit code.** Today's department outlines (frdep), drawn with `geoConicEqualArea().parallels([44, 49]).rotate([-3, 0])` and `geoPath`, both re-exported by `@/kit/geo` (d3-geo). The kit's `WorldMap` is not used because it draws world countries. The B2 stop condition (the kit cannot draw the frdep GeoJSON with a named conic projection) is not met. The projection is named on the stage: "Conic equal-area projection; today's outlines".
- **Data files:** `departments.json` (84 KB), generated by `gen.mjs` (committed alongside; run `node src/demos/history/literacy-split-france/gen.mjs` from the repo root after the data pins pass). It holds Guerry's 86 rows copied verbatim (dept, Region, Department, Literacy), the 96 outlines, and the two region edges.
  - The outlines are simplified so neighbours share edges: Douglas-Peucker at 0.025 degrees, run once per shared chain with junction vertices kept, so no gaps open. That takes 14,335 vertices down to 3,876. Total area is 547.6 thousand km² after and 547 before (the check script).
  - The stand-in mapping is the brief's: Seine = 75, 92, 93, 94; Seine-et-Oise = 78, 91, 95; Haut-Rhin with 90; Corse = 2A, 2B. 06, 73 and 74 are hatched and labelled "no value in Guerry's table".
  - Every number is computed in Demo.tsx from the rows. No number on the card is typed in.
- **Shading:** 5 steps (12–19, 20–34, 35–49, 50–64, 65–74). They mix the domain colour toward the background at the low end and toward the text colour at the high end, so the order reads in both themes (checked in light and dark screenshots). The locked stage draws the outlines in plain grey with no legend and no values, so nothing points at the answer.
- **Presets in DOM order (brief):** North and east (milestone `ne`: a thick outline around Guerry's N + E), South, west and centre (`swc`: the S + W + C outline), Paris (Seine) (outlines the 4 stand-in departments, label "Seine 71%" with a leader), Most and least literate (`ext`: outlines Meuse and Corrèze, labels "Meuse 74%" and "Corrèze 12%"). There is no Reset (the brief lists none).
- **Readout:** one row of three cells. "North and east" shows 55% once `ne` is reached, "South, west, centre" shows 28% once `swc` is reached, and "All 86" shows 39% from the unlock. This departs from the brief's single readout "{area}: {x}% of conscripts": the three cells keep every mean the reveal quotes on the stage when the reveal opens (demo-standard: "the quoted state is still visible"). The unit sits in the legend title: "Conscripts who could read and write, %".
- **Deviation 1, a third milestone `ext`.** The brief unlocks the reveal after `ne` and `swc`. The reveal also names Meuse 74% and Corrèze 12%, and demo-standard requires each quoted value to be computed from the learner's state or gated by a milestone, so `ext` is a milestone too (minInteractions 3). Once reached, the Meuse and Corrèze labels stay on the map. For cm-history to confirm or overrule.
- **Deviation 2, the S+W+C mean is 28%** (see the stop flag above).
- **The optional caption is cut.** "Guerry's 1833 shaded maps were among the first maps of statistics" is not on the card: the maps were not opened, so it is not checkable (the brief says "Cut if not checkable"). So meta.json has c1-c3 only. A claim that is not on the card would be a claim with no address.
- **Title and hook are mine** (the brief gives none): "Who could read in 1830s France" / "Can one average describe all of France?". Each fits on one line at 390 px, which buys the reveal room. Neither names a region. For cm-history to confirm.
