# Build note: press-the-string

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: one string, nut to bridge (0.65 m). A finger presses at a stop (open, a quarter along, the middle, three quarters along); the free part vibrates as one loop, drawn as a shaded envelope with the string swinging inside it at f / 55 per second (slowed 55x, labelled). The open string's loop stays dashed behind, so "same shape, half as wide" is visible at the middle.
- Hz is defined on the stage ("Hz: vibrations per second") and in the question ("vibrates 110 times a second").
- Title "Press a guitar string at its middle" asks without answering.
- Earned reveal: milestone 'middle'. QA path: the finger slider starts open; two presses go to a quarter (147 Hz), then the middle (220 Hz). The reveal quotes 0.325 m and 220 Hz, and 440 Hz only if the learner visited three quarters along.
- Numbers (node): f = 110 x 0.65 / L: 0.4875 m -> 146.7 Hz, 0.325 m -> 220.0 Hz, 0.1625 m -> 440.0 Hz.
- Departure from the brief: the brief had the learner drag the finger freely; stops keep the question's state exact and reachable in two presses. The ratio readout ("x2.00 vs open string") carries the proportion.
- After screenshots: reads on phone; the readout label wraps to two lines, acceptable.
