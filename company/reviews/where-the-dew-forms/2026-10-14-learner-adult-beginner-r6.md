## 1. From the artifact

- Hook and predict: "An evening cools; the air keeps its water. When does the grass get wet?" is a real question. The predict stem gives 30 °C and 50%. The locked stage shows the Water readout (2.12 kPa), "Max here 4.24 kPa" and only a short stub of the "full" curve, so it does not give the answer away. The y-axis "water vapour (as pressure), kPa" will puzzle a beginner, but the readouts carry the idea without it.
- Explore: preset 25 / 20 / 15 °C buttons and a slider. Each press moves the dot along the dashed "your air" line, and the "full" curve is drawn down to the current temperature. Water stays at 2.12 kPa, Max falls (3.16, 2.33), and Fullness rises (67%, 91%). At 15 °C the stage marks "dew point ≈ 18 °C", draws drops and labels "extra water leaves as dew", and Water falls to 1.70 = Max. The learner sees the dew point between 20 and 15, which is the bracket they were asked about.
- The why: "warm air allows more, so cooling lowers the ceiling" is on stage as the falling Max readout and the curve. The learner watches it change. The reveal points at it ("the Max readout fell, so Fullness rose"). It is shown, not only told.
- Reveal: it matches what was seen (2.12 kPa held, about 18 °C marked on the curve, 100% full). "Ceiling" is the reveal's word for the readout the stage calls "Max here". That is close enough, but the reveal could use the stage's own label.
- Minor: "extra water leaves as dew" is printed over the pink dot ("extr● water") in steps 3 and 4 and at the reveal.
- Minor: the buttons jump from 20 to 15, over 18. Only the slider can stop near the dew point, and the run never moved the slider to 18.
