## 2. After reading the brief

- doesNotKnow (same average under all three rules): **shown** by the bars converging on the dashed line.
- The objective asks the learner to *explain* why: "each rule changes how much bidders bid, so a higher price paid by one bidder (second-price), a lower price paid by the winner (first-price) and small bids paid by everyone (all-pay) all average the same". On stage, the bid changes are visible for one auction. But the one auction shown contradicts the reveal's "second-price charges a high price, first-price charges less" ($18.9 versus $45.0). The cancelling is told only. Under "Show the why you promised" that is a revise.
