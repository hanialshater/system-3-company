# Learner review: saturated-enzyme
lesson version: vNN
boundary: an adult with school-level maths and science, new to the topic (knows enzymes as "proteins that speed up reactions"; no feel for Michaelis-Menten saturation). Screenshots seen (sheet.png, all 5 frames).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: the stem is dense (defines enzyme, "half-way concentration", half the enzymes busy, then "10 times that") but readable. Options 2 times / 50% / 5% are a real spread. The hook ("How much faster does it get?") does not leak. "Half-way concentration" is a coined phrase for the Michaelis constant; it is used as a unit ("× half-way") on the slider with no worked example.
- The locked stage shows a pale grid of 100 stations and orange substrate dots, with "91 of 100 enzymes busy" only in the picture text (the visible panel shows "?"). On screen nothing leaks, but the grid already looks nearly full, which would leak to a learner who looks at it.
- Where understanding first broke: step 1. The caption says "busy 91 → 95 of 100 ... Speed +5%". 95 over 91 is 4.4% by my arithmetic, not 5%; and step 2: 50 → 67 is +34%, shown as +33%. The numbers are rounded versions of unrounded busy counts, so a careful adult checks the shown figures and finds them off by a point. That erodes trust at exactly the moment of the claim.
- "SPEED (SHARE OF TOP)" is a label never explained before it is used; the learner must infer that busy share is speed and that "top" is all 100 busy.
- What was SHOWN: the pair 10 to 20 (+5%) against 1 to 2 (+33%) side by side is the lesson, and it is on the stage. The brighter "new busy" stations are tiny at phone size; I could see the dark band shift but not count new ones.
- The reveal sentence "the speed levels off at a top rate" is told: only three points were sampled (1 to 2, 2 to 4, 10 to 20); no whole curve is shown, so "levels off" is believed from the pattern 33, 20, 5, not seen. The learner's third step (2 to 4, +20%) is a slider nudge the panel did not ask for.
- Reveal earned: 5% yes; the "extra substrate waits its turn" mechanism is told, though the dots visually crowd, which helps.
- Problems: (1) displayed gains not equal to displayed busy counts; (2) "half-way" unit and "share of top" unexplained; (3) no full curve; (4) new-busy stations too small.
- No FATAL.
