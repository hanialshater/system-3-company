## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: "0" is my real instinct (a proper poll should contain the truth). "about 5" is guessable from 100 - 95 if one thinks for a second, so the trap is mild but fair.
- Explore: one tap draws 100 horizontal intervals around a vertical 50% line; 7 red ones stick out. This is the right picture and reads at a glance: most intervals cross the line, a few do not, and the red ones look like ordinary bars.
- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: "95% interval" is never glossed (a range the pollster reports around their result). I could follow from the picture, but a one-line gloss would help.
- Told-not-shown: "The exact chance that one of these intervals misses is 5.4%". Why 5.4 and not 5 for a "95%" interval? A beginner is left with a small contradiction in the headline number. Either explain in a clause (the usual formula is slightly off for samples this size) or round to "about 5%".

- Layout: dense bars but readable; no clipping; empty top band.
- Fix suggested (minor): gloss "95% interval"; explain or drop 5.4%; require two or three batches.
