# Build note: wire-eight-times

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: the wire side-on (length 76 px per m) over a dashed 1 m x 1 mm reference, plus its end-on cross-section over the dashed 1 mm circle, so "half as wide" is seen as a quarter of the circle's area. Thickness is exaggerated against length and the stage says so. Readouts give the length factor, the area factor and R (R hidden until the prediction).
- Departure from the brief: no "electron traffic" animation in the pipe; the end-on circle carries the area idea more directly.
- Earned reveal: milestones 'length' and 'diameter'. The length slider comes first (1 -> 2 m); the diameter slider starts at its maximum, 1.0 mm, so QA's two presses step it down to 0.5 mm, the question's state (8.0 Ω). The reveal quotes the learner's own length, diameter and the factors they produced.
- The 1.0 Ω reference is illustrative; the muted line and claim c4 give copper's real value for that size (about 0.02 Ω).
- After screenshots: read cleanly on phone; left as built.
