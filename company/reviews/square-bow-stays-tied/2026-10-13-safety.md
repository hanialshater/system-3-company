**Verdict: PASS. Safety class: none (confirmed).** Skip line removed from `company/ops/brief-skips.json`.

## Reasons
- Retying a bow with the second wrap reversed has no harm path, and it gives a bow that is more secure, not less.
- The careless version is a granny bow, which is what most people already tie, so following the lesson badly leaves the learner no worse off.
- The one real risk is untied laces, which trip people, children especially. The lesson would raise that risk if it told learners that double knots are useless and they should stop using them. The current reveal ("Check the direction before reaching for a double knot") keeps the double knot as a valid fallback, so it is acceptable as written.
- Both illustration requests (`square-bow-stays-tied`, `-pair`) show only hands, a shoe and a lace. Nothing unsafe.
