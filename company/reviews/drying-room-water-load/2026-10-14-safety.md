## Reasons
Water released by a whole wet load (250 g per item x count) in a 30 m3 room. The strand note in the safety lens says an indoor-drying lesson that quantifies the water load needs a ventilation line. As written, the reveal ends "do not infer mould or health outcomes from this card". A hurried reader can take that as reassurance that drying a whole load indoors is harmless. It is not: moisture from indoor drying is a recognised cause of damp and mould, and the stage is exactly the picture that makes it concrete. No heater or flame is shown, which is correct.

## Required stage warnings
Required (see changes): one short ventilation line on the stage.

## Changes / conditions
1. Add one stage or reveal line of about 8 to 15 words to the effect "Dry indoors with a window open or a vent on: the water ends up in the room." The builder writes the wording; I re-read it before it ships. Do not approve it in the same pass.
2. Record it as an advice claim in meta.json with a source from a housing, public-health or energy-advice body (fact-checker verifies; I have not verified a source and do not name one).
3. Replace "do not infer mould or health outcomes from this card" with a neutral scope sentence such as "This card counts water only; it does not predict room humidity." Keep the existing "Ventilation, temperature and condensation affect the room".
4. Hero and copy must not show or imply drying on an electric heater, radiator or near a flame (refuse-list candidate in the safety lens).

## Region flags (International team)
Flag for International: indoor-drying norms, climate and air conditioning differ (humid Gulf summers, heated European winters). The ventilation line must not assume a heating season.
