## Part 1: findings from the artifact (before meta.json, earlier reviews, build.md)

**Idea the learner lacks:** a handful of random long links cuts the typical distance between any two people by nearly half (12.9 to 6.8 with 5), and further with more (4.9 with 20).

**Answer visible before predicting.** Mostly no. The question gives the baseline 12.9 and the change (5 links). The hook ("How much closer does everyone get?") and title ("A few shortcuts shrink the world") say distance falls but not by how much; options 12 / 3 / 7 are about magnitude. The correct one is last. "5 links of 200 is 2.5%, so about 12" is a sensible wrong guess and 1 shortcut (12.9 to 10.3) corrects it at once.

**Prediction feel.** Wide and honest; no basis to estimate. The gradient 24 / 9 / 5 / 4 for the A-to-B route and 12.9 / 10.3 / 6.8 / 4.9 for the average gives a visible, earned surprise.

**Where understanding first broke.** At 1 and 5 shortcuts the thick "route A to B" is drawn as one straight chord from a dot next to A to B (see 03-step-2: a vertical line, label "5 steps"). It reads as one direct link yet is labelled 5 steps; the 4 short hops near A are not visible, so I cannot count the steps. Also the shortcut ends clump at the top of the ring (the muted line says this is chance), which looks non-random.

**Told vs shown.** Shown: the route re-routes at each chip, so "some routes get shorter" is visible for one pair. Told: "every pair it helps gets a shorter route". The stage shows one pair and the average, never the many pairs a single link helps. The brief's gap is the size of the effect, which is shown, so a told sentence for the why is acceptable under the decision. Also told with nothing on stage: clustering 0.500 to 0.435 and "a random network this size would sit near 0.04". It is a sentence with no stage counterpart and nothing for the learner to compare it with; as context it is allowed, but it is the least anchored line in the reveal.

**Reveal quotes only reached states?** Yes: 0, 5 and 20 are all required.

**Did the reveal answer the question the learner had?** For a learner who said 12: "Not quite: it was 7 steps" plus the numbers answers "how much". It does not say why 5 links out of 200 matter so much, only that each "joins two far-apart neighbourhoods". "(Watts and Strogatz rewired them)" is lost on a lay learner.

**Verdict: launch with notes.**

**Top remaining finding:** the thick A-to-B route is drawn as a single straight chord labelled "5 steps", so the learner cannot see or count the steps; a direct-looking line contradicts the number. Route: visual-designer / demo-builder.
