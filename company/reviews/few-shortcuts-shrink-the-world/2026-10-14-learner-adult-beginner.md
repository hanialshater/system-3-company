# Learner review: few-shortcuts-shrink-the-world (adult beginner)

date: 2026-10-14 · reviewer: learner-evaluator (borrowed mind: knows people link and a step is one link; no network science) · version reviewed: walkthrough 2026-10-14, phone, first-option path
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, all 5 frames). Hypotheses only.

**Verdict for this version: revise (light).**

## 1. Findings from the artifact (written before meta.json and build.md)

- **Where understanding first breaks.** The prediction is well posed: the question gives the number to beat (12.9) and a concrete change (5 random links). No unknown term in the question. The first foreign idea is the second readout, "Clustering", with a bar and a "Random network 0.04" tick, which sits on the stage from the first frame, undefined, until the reveal. The learner is told to "watch both numbers" but has no idea what the second number is or whether higher is good. It is a second lesson hiding in the readout.
- **Answer visible before predicting?** No. The locked stage already shows 12.9 (restated in the question). Options 12 / 7 / 3: correct is the middle one (index 1) again.
- **Prediction feel.** Likely wide: a beginner has no way to estimate; "5 of 200 links is 2.5%, so about 12" is a sensible wrong answer and the stage corrects it at the first press (1 shortcut: 12.9 to 10.3, already a surprise). Good gradient: 10.3, 6.8, 4.9.
- **Told vs shown.** Shown: numbers drop as the lines appear. Told: "every route that can use it gets shorter" and "each long link bridges two far-apart neighbourhoods". No path is traced. A learner can see lines but not see why one line shortens many routes. Also, in the 5-shortcut frame the shortcut endpoints cluster near the top of the ring, which looks odd for "random"; not explained.
- **Reveal quotes only states reached.** Yes: 0, 5 and 20 (all three chips are required). Cropped in the sheet so the stage at 20 is partly cut off in the reveal frame, but the readouts are visible.
- **Unanswered question.** "Why does the clustering stay near 0.5 while a random network would be 0.04, and why should I care?" The reveal defines clustering and gives the comparison but not the consequence (neighbourhoods stay tight while the world shrinks) beyond one sentence.
- **Claims.** "Watts and Strogatz rewired them" vs the lesson "adds" links: an honest note in the muted line, but a lay learner will not follow the difference.

## 2. Compared with meta.json (read after section 1)

Brief matches: the learner does not know a few random long links halve the distance. The experience delivers the 12.9 to 6.8 half strongly. The clustering half of the objective (0.500 to 0.435) has no prediction attached, so the learner is not surprised by it and may skip it; the objective bundles two ideas.

## 3. After build.md (read only after sections 1 and 2)

build.md confirms the Clustering bar with its 0.04 tick is on from frame one and the reveal defines it only afterward. It confirms seed 1 was not tuned, so the clumped-looking endpoints are chance; a learner may still read them as non-random. Verdict stands: revise (light).
