A beginner told "one third of the area" sees the same length touching. A small footprint inset (top-down rectangle) or a 3/4 view would make 288 → 96 → 48 visible.
- Finding (minor): "μ = 0.40" appears in the stem and on the stage without a gloss. A beginner can ignore it, but it reads as jargon in the first line.
- Finding (minor): the "why" (pressure per bit rises as area falls) is told. Acceptable: the lesson's claim is the invariance, and that is shown.
