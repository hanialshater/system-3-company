# Build note: long-answers-lose-the-product (demo-builder, wave-i-ai, 2026-10-14)

Brief: `company/domains/briefs/ai.neural-language.md` item 59. Status draft, version 1. Claims c1-c5 unverified; c5 (Wu et al. 2016, Murray and Chiang 2018) is a real-systems citation for the fact-checker. The optional Stahlberg and Byrne sentence (brief claim 5) is not in learner text.

- Stage: two token chains. S has 2 boxes at 50%; L has n boxes at 85%. Multiplied boxes fill. Under each chain, a bar (0-100%) with "whole answer: x%", which becomes "per token: x% (whole: y%)" while per-token scoring is on. "◀ more probable" marks the winner once both chains are complete. Message line: once the 10-token case is complete, "Seen: S 25%; L at 10 tokens 19.7%, at n p%."
- Controls in DOM order: +1 token (one token per tap, both chains in step), All tokens, Per token (toggle; enabled once both chains are complete), slider "Tokens in L" (1-10, start 10).
- Milestones: question-case (both complete at L = 10; 25% and 19.7% recorded), crossover-seen (both complete at L <= 8; length and product recorded), per-token-on.
- Departures from the brief: slider 1-10 (brief 1-12), because QA presses arrows from the maximum leftwards (10 -> 9 -> 8 reaches the crossover) and one row of 10 boxes needs no wrap. "All tokens" was added beside the step button so QA's single presses complete the chains. No Reset (the slider and the toggle move freely; the trace keeps the recorded values). The reveal is shortened from the brief draft: "At 8 tokens or fewer, L won." became the learner's own crossover ("At 8 tokens L won with 27.2%, but past 8 it always loses."), and "the length-normalised score you switched on" became "which you switched on". Reveal 82 words, brief draft 77.

## Pre-flight v1
1. **Title and hook.** "Which whole answer is more likely?" / "A model scores a whole answer by multiplying its tokens' chances. Which comes out on top?" The hook names multiplication, which the learner already knows from greedy-word-trap, but no answer and no length.
2. **Options.** "S" / "L" / "They tie": none numbered.
3. **Locked stage.** Both chains with their per-token percentages (the question's own data), empty bars, "whole answer: –", slider at 10 and disabled. No products, no winner badge.
4. **Reveal values.** "25% against 19.7%": (b) milestone question-case, kept in the message trace. "At 8 tokens L won with 27.2%": (b) milestone crossover-seen, in the trace, and on the L bar when the slider is still there. "past 8 it always loses": constant CROSS from the model (c2). "85% against 50%": (b) milestone per-token-on, and the per-token labels while it is on.
5. **QA path.** +1 token (one box each), All tokens (question-case: 25%, 19.7%), Per token (per-token-on), slider 10 -> 9 -> 8 (crossover-seen: 27.2%). All three reached. At the reveal: L at 8 with per token on ("per token: 85% (whole: 27.2%) ◀ more probable"), S "per token: 50% (whole: 25%)", and the trace "Seen: S 25%; L at 10 tokens 19.7%, at 8 27.2%."
6. **Reset.** No Reset control. QA-run reveal text: "Every token of L was more likely than any token of S, yet S won: 25% against 19.7%. Each extra token multiplies the whole by something below 1, so ten confident steps lost to two shaky ones. At 8 tokens L won with 27.2%, but past 8 it always loses. So the single most probable answer tends to be a short one. Scoring per token, which you switched on, put L ahead: 85% against 50%. Real decoders add such a length correction."
