# Learner review: two-springs-end-to-end
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows springs stretch more under more weight and a 1 kg mass is pulled down by gravity; does not know newtons by heart).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 4 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: that the lower spring is pulled by the full weight too. My instinct was "two springs = stronger = 2.5 cm", the classic trap, and the hook ("stiffer or stretchier?") sets it up well.
- Explore shows it: side by side, two springs each labelled "4.91 N each", drop 2.5 cm; end to end, both springs labelled "9.81 N", drop 10 cm. The per-spring pull labels are exactly the evidence I need, and the green drop bar doubles visibly.
- Needed-but-unstated: "9.81 N" for 1 kg is never connected ("1 kg weighs about 9.81 N"). "N: newtons of pull" is in tiny grey text at the bottom of the stage. A beginner can still compare 4.91 vs 9.81 as half vs whole, so the idea survives.
- Readability: force labels and the "drop" label are small on phone; the reveal frame shrinks the diagram so labels are barely legible.
- Told-not-shown: "half as stiff / twice as stiff" is told; consistent with what I saw.
- Layout: reveal fits on phone; no clipping.
