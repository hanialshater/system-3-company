The crosses are scattered at random, which could suggest that which species die is random, or that rows of the forest map to rows of species. "Eventually" and "losses take years" are not shown: the stage is instant.
- Terms before defined: species-area exponent, ∝, "empirical regularity" (reveal).

- Problems: (1) exponent undefined in the question; (2) no mechanism on stage; (3) time is invisible; (4) random crosses.
