# Learner review: news-comes-from-acquaintances (adult beginner)

date: 2026-10-14 · reviewer: learner-evaluator (borrowed mind: knows people have friends and links; not strong triadic closure) · version reviewed: walkthrough 2026-10-14, phone, first-option path (prediction "a close friend", then one tie toggle, two checks, send news)
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, all 6 frames). Hypotheses only.

**Verdict for this version: revise.**

## 1. Findings from the artifact (written before meta.json and build.md)

- **Where understanding first breaks: the question.** "Rule: any two of your close friends are linked." Linked how? By any tie, or by close friendship? The word "linked" in the rule and "link" in "Their link must be..." are the same word with two jobs. A beginner can read the rule as "all your friends know each other" (true here by construction), or "all pairs are close friends", and both readings change the answer. Nothing in the predict frame says "close friend" and "acquaintance" are the two kinds of tie, or that the A–B tie is the only one between the groups (the locked stage shows a "?" pill, which helps). This is the whole puzzle and it is a logic puzzle posed in prose.
- **Answer visible before predicting?** No. The locked stage greys everything out ("Predict first."). Correct option is again the middle one (index 1).
- **What the prediction felt like.** Abstract and almost a coin flip between "a close friend" and "either could be"; the hook ("Your closest friends bring you the most news. Or do they?") is about news, but the prediction is about the rule. The learner predicts about something other than what the hook promised.
- **Explore.** Strong: the stage starts in the learner's guess state, toggling A–B shows red breakage with the caption "Broken: B and C, both close to A, aren't linked", the two check pills show the rule holding in the triangles, and "Send news" shows the dots passing H, B, A, C/D/E. The learner who said "close friend" and toggles sees their own answer break. That is a real prediction-reveal loop.
- **Told vs shown, the weak point.** The reveal's second paragraph, "A–B could only be close if A and B had no other close friends", is told, and it is the general rule. The learner has seen one case. And the hook's real claim, that acquaintances bring you more news than close friends, is never shown: "news crosses A–B" is true here by construction because there is no other route. The caption "News crossed, but a close A–B breaks the rule" shows news also crosses a close tie, so the stage does not demonstrate acquaintances are special for news; only that the bridge has to be one. The hook overpromises on the evidence.
- **Reveal quotes states reached.** Mostly: "the triangles you checked held" is conditional on the learner having tapped a check pill. The sentence "A was close to B and to C" refers to the broken state, which the learner has seen only if they toggled to it (the milestone requires both ties, so they have).
- Not seen in artifact: the first-option walker reached weak by toggling; a learner who said "acquaintance" starts in the correct state and never has a guess broken, only a rule shown holding.

## 2. Compared with meta.json (read after section 1)

Brief says the learner does not know that the only link between two groups cannot be close. The experience gets there for the specific example. The objective's last clause "news from outside the group crosses it" is true but trivial in the toy. The brief assumes "what a link between two people means" is known; the ambiguity above is exactly where that assumption fails.

## 3. After build.md (read only after sections 1 and 2)

build.md corrects a detail of my read: the walkthrough here never photographs the broken (close) state, so I could not see it; the builder says they screenshotted it themselves, which I have not seen. build.md also records that the lesson's QA path used "either could be", while the artifact I saw used "a close friend". Granovetter's claim is deliberately kept out of learner text, which agrees with my finding that the hook promises more than the stage shows; the builder's note says nothing about softening the hook. One screenshot-gap caveat: my verdict on the red state rests on the code and caption, not an image. Verdict stands: revise.
