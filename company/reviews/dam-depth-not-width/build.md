# Build note: dam-depth-not-width

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: side view of one container with a depth ruler (true scale, 6 px per m, 0-20 m) and a gauge dot at the bottom whose reading is the only number on the picture. Width is picked from five named cases (2 cm tube, 0.5 m tank, 5 m pool, 50 m pond, 500 m lake). Widths cannot share an honest horizontal scale, so the stage says "widths not to scale" (claim c4).
- Earned reveal: milestones 'width-changed' (a different width at the question's 10 m depth) and 'depth-changed'. Width slider first on QA's path (5 m pool -> 50 m pond -> 500 m lake at 10 m), then depth (10 -> 14 m). The reveal lists the widths the learner saw at 10 m and their own last depth with its reading.
- The total-force point (the lake bottom carries far more force over far more area) is in the muted line only, to answer the misconception's intuition without a second representation.
- After screenshots: the first draft had a width label under the container that ran off the edge for the lake; removed (the slider's value already names the width).
