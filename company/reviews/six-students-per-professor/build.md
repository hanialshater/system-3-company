- Builder: wave-i-math (demo-builder), 2026-10-14, v1 draft. Brief: company/domains/briefs/math.md #59 (cm-math). Kept from evaluators until they report.
- Stage: P professors (1 to 5) drawn as columns, each with k students below it (k 1 to 8), so S = k × P; "P = n" and "S = n" counts on the left. All three equations are listed under the picture with today's counts substituted ("24 = 24") and lit true or false.
- Departure from the brief (flag for cm-math / interaction review): the brief had a free S slider and a three-way equation toggle. I changed both. (1) Students are set per professor (a "Students per professor" slider), not as a free total, so the QA path (each slider pressed twice) can reach six per professor: 4 → 6. (2) All three equations show at once instead of a toggle, so the reveal's quoted values ("6S = P read 144 = 4") are still on the stage when it opens. Cost: the learner cannot set a total S that is not a multiple of P, and the control label says "per professor", which matches the question sentence once committed. If cm-math wants the free S slider back, the trace problem needs another answer.
- Milestones: `six` (k = 6, the question's case) and `resize` (k = 6 seen at a second number of professors: S = 6P stays true). The reveal has two branches: k = 6 now (quotes current P, S) or k ≠ 6 now (quotes current values; says S = 6P is false, as it should be).
- The explore-phase caption was cut for fit (the slider label already says how many students each professor has).
