# Inclined orbit projection — draft prototype

The founder requested 250 physics lessons, including 3D where useful. This branch contains one first prototype only; 249 remain to be built. The stage rotates a circular orbit in 3D and displays an orthographic projection; its visual is projected into SVG, not rendered with a 3D engine. The circle radius and speed are explicit model assumptions. Claims remain unverified.

Arithmetic spot-check: cos(0°)=1; cos(30°)=0.8660; cos(60°)=0.5. The repository could not be checked out in the author's network environment. Typecheck, build, full company check, walkthrough, phone fit, independent physics fact-check and learner review remain outstanding. No claim of publication readiness.
