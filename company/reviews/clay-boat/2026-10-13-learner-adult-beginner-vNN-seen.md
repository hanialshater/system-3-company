## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. 0.56 L is "same clay, same displacement", 1 L and 2 L are the numeric alternatives. The hook ("how much water must it shove aside to float?") is a fair question. Leak: the locked stage already draws "push 0.56 kg" next to "weight 1 kg" with the caption "1 L of water weighs 1 kg". Anyone who reads the diagram sees push is compared with weight and that litres convert to kilograms, so 1 L is nearly given before the guess is locked.
- Where understanding first broke: only one slider move is needed. After moving to "big cup" it floats, 1.00 L, and Show me why lights up. The reveal then talks about a "small cup" that "could hold back only 0.80 L before water poured in", and flooding. The learner may never have seen the small cup or any flood, since the slider has other positions and the panel does not ask for them. 0.80 L appears nowhere on the stage I saw.
- "Push" as a term (the water's upward push equals the weight of the water moved aside) is shown as an arrow label before it is explained; reveal states it as a rule. Plausible but told. "5.9 cm under" and "2.5 cm" labels have no stated meaning.
- What was SHOWN works: ball pushes 0.56 L (arrow shorter than weight arrow); big cup arrows equal, bowl floats. The shape change is vivid.
- Reveal earned: 1 L, yes. "Clay is heavier than water litre for litre" is shown by 1 kg being 0.56 L (1.8 kg per litre) but not said that way.
- Problems: (1) stage leaks the answer through the push/weight labels; (2) small cup and flooding told not shown; (3) 5.9 cm and 2.5 cm unexplained.
