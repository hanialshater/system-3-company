The "earliest" ring on 13 Dec is there from the first tap, so the discovery is handed to me rather than found by comparing 15:51 with 15:53 and 16:00. A learner who follows the panel's order is told the answer before plotting the alternatives.
4. **Where an idea I lack first appears: "because Earth moves fastest in early January and its tilt adds to the drift".** That is the why of the noon drift, and it is a whole topic compressed into a clause. I accept it; I could not explain it. The lesson's job (noon drifts, so the dark evening comes early) does not depend on it.
5. The effect is small (15:51 vs 15:53). The lesson is honest about that; a beginner may feel "two minutes?" but the 08:06 on 30 Dec makes the pattern feel real.
6. **Fit:** the reveal opens on 13 Jan. The 15:51 and 08:06 rings are labelled; the 11:55 and 12:03 solar-noon values the reveal quotes are not labelled on that stage (they were on earlier frames). Minor.
