**Ruling on c4 (W-581), asked for by the dispatcher:** the builder's objection is right. In deep water w^2 = gk, so the group velocity is dw/dk = c/2 = 3.12 m/s, and a wave train's front and its energy travel at that speed (the energy flux is E c_g). The buoy 100 m away starts to bob at about **32 s**, not 16 s. A dispersive-packet simulation (wj-econ-phys-math-geo/duck.mjs) confirms it: the envelope moves 125 m in 40 s. The stage repeats the error, because Demo.tsx moves the train's front at C. c4 is disputed until the stage, the Seen row, the reveal and the claim say about 32 s.
