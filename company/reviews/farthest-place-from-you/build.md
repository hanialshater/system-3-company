# Build notes: farthest-place-from-you

v1 (2026-10-13), Sonnet build (EXP-003). Wave 6 geography.

- Azimuthal equidistant map centred on a pin (Madrid, Wellington, Quito). Rings every 5,000 km and a learner ring are hidden while locked.
- Correction to the brief: in this projection the antipode is the whole outer edge of the map, so the ring does not shrink on screen. The stage shows the ring's real length (2 pi R sin(d/R)) in a readout; it falls to 95 km at 20,000 km. The lesson says the edge is one point stretched into a circle.
- Milestones: far (ring at 20,000 km, by button or slider) and moved (pin moved). Madrid to Wellington (19,855 km) is a readout and both cities are drawn.
- Title does not state the number: "How far is as far as it gets?".
