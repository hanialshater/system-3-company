## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: partly given away. The hook says "100 / 8 says 12.5 years to double. Compounding has a faster answer.", so option 12.5 is already marked as the wrong one; the real guess is 9 vs 6 and a beginner cannot calculate either. The walkthrough chose 12.5 anyway, so the panel says "You said 12.5 years", which is fine. The question defines compounding in a parenthesis ("each year's growth also grows"), which helps.
- Where understanding first broke: the explore panel asks me to "Set 8% and see where the bars cross the doubled line", but the first reading I got was at 9%, and the slider moves in steps of three. At 8% specifically (the case in the question) I did not stop. The three readout boxes (DOUBLES IN, 72 ÷ RATE, SIMPLE) appear all at once with "72 ÷ RATE 8.0" at 9% before 72 has been explained. Why 72? Nothing says.
- The chart: bars grow and cross a green dashed "200" line, with a green "doubled: 4.4 yrs" tag and a black dashed line for simple interest. At 17% (final screenshot) the whole picture lives in the left sixth of a chart whose x-axis runs to 30 years, and the "doubled: 4.4 yrs" tag is clipped at its right edge ("yrs" touches the border). The dashed line is explained only in the caption below ("Dashed: simple interest, where only the first 100 earns") and is not labelled on the chart.
- The contrast is shown: at every rate the SIMPLE box is longer than DOUBLES IN (e.g. 5.9 vs 4.4 at 17%) and 72 ÷ RATE sits just below the exact doubling time; the learner sees "72 ÷ rate" track "doubles in" closely. That earns the shortcut.
- Reveal earned: mostly. "1.08 multiplied by itself 9 times is 1.999" is a number I could not check and did not see on the stage. The claim "from 5% to 20% it stays within about 0.2 years" matches the values I saw (e.g. 5.0 vs 4.8, 3.8 vs 3.6).
- Reveal gate: reachable (Show me why is lit after moving the slider).
- Problems: (1) the hook gives away that 12.5 is wrong; (2) the explore asks for 8% but the slider's steps skip it; (3) 72 appears unexplained as a magic number in the readouts; (4) at high rates the chart is cramped to the left and the "doubled" tag clips; (5) the dashed simple-interest line is not labelled on the chart.
- No FATAL.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The brief names the misconception as exactly the simple-interest thinking the hook cites (100 / 8 = 12.5), so the hook tells the learner the common belief is wrong before they have committed to it. That weakens the prediction for the lesson's own target; a prediction that does not mention 100 / 8 in the hook might expose the belief better.
- On finding (2): the slider steps I saw (9, 12, 15, 18, 20) may be the driver's coarse jumps rather than the slider's real resolution; 8% may well be reachable, so I treat that as unconfirmed.
- The brief's objective includes the 72 / rate estimate; the stage supports it by showing the number next to the exact answer, but never says why 72.
