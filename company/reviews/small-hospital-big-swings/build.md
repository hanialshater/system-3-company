# Build note: small-hospital-big-swings (demo-builder, 2026-10-12)
- Intent: one seeded year shown as 365 dots, each a day's share of boys, against a red 60% line. A (15 a day) sprays widely over the line; B (45 a day) hugs 50%. The slider runs from 5 to 100 in steps of 5. At other sizes, whole babies make the "more than 60%" count saw-tooth (16 births a day gives more such days than 15), which would mislead.
- Gate: interactions count only after B (45) has been visited.
- After screenshots: I added an "A vs B" readout so the learner does not have to remember the first count, and shortened the title to fit one line.
