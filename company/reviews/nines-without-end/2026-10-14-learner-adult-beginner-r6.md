## 1. From the artifact

- Hook and predict: "Zoom in on the gap and try to find it." The options are now three different beliefs: "A tiny positive gap" / "Exactly zero" / "Zero only at the end, which never comes". The third one is the "shrinks forever" version, separated properly from the first. The locked stage shows 0 to 1, the dot at 0.9 and the red "gap 0.1". Nothing leaks.
- Explore: a new "Name a gap" slider. Moving it to 0.00000001 answers "Your gap 0.00000001: 9 nines get closer than that (gap 0.000000001)". My candidate tiny gap gets beaten, and I chose it. "+ one more 9" and "+ three more 9s" zoom ×10 each, and the red gap stays a full tenth of the view, so the zoom never runs out. "All of them, forever" gives "0.999… = 1, gap 0", lists "gaps you saw: 0.1 … 0.00001", shows the check "3 × 0.333… = 0.999…, and 3 × 1/3 = 1", and says "Every gap you named was beaten by enough nines."
- Where it could still lose a beginner: the "All of them" step is still a button announcing the limit. That is unavoidable, and it now rests on two things the learner did or saw: their own gap beaten, and the 1/3 check. Acceptable.
- Minor: the "beaten" result for a named gap is text only. The number line does not jump to 9 nines to show the dot passing the named gap. Showing it would make it something they see.
- Minor: the "gaps you saw" and "check: 3 × 0.333…" lines are very small on the phone, and the 1/3 check is the most convincing step for this boundary.
- Minor: on this path ("Start over" last) the reveal sits next to a stage reset to 0.9 / gap 0.1, while the reveal says "as the check on the stage showed". The check is no longer on stage.
- Reveal: it quotes the learner's own smallest gap ("0.00000001, by 9 nines") and the 1/3 check. It names "shrinks but never reaches" as the stopped-after-some-nines belief. About 115 words.
- No FATAL.
