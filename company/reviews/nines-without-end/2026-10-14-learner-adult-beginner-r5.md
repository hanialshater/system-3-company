## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: "A tiny positive gap" / "Exactly zero" / "Never zero: always a little short". The two wrong options are the same belief in two wordings. Both say "not zero". As a learner holding that belief, I cannot tell which of the two I am meant to hold. The locked stage shows a number line from 0 to 1 with a blue dot at 0.9 and a red "gap 0.1".
- Explore: "+ one more 9" zooms ×10 (0.9 to 1, dot at 0.99, gap 0.01, "still a full tenth of the view"). "+ three more 9s" gives 0.99999 and gap 0.00001. The picture looks the same each time, which nicely shows the zoom never runs out. Then "All of them, forever" collapses the line to "0.999… = 1, gap 0" and asks "Is any positive number below 0.1, 0.01, 0.001… every one? No. Only 0 is."
- Where my belief survived: that "No." is the whole argument, and the stage gives the answer rather than letting me test it. My mental model is "the gap is some tiny positive number, smaller than anything I can name". It is exactly the thing that answer says doesn't exist, and nothing on stage makes me find out for myself. The button takes me to "= 1" by announcement.
- Reveal: the gaps it lists (0.1 … 0.00001) were on stage. The 1/3 argument (3 × 0.333… = 0.999…, 3 × 1/3 = 1) is the step that actually convinces at this boundary, because I already accept 1/3 = 0.333…. It is told in the last paragraph. The reveal frame shows the stage reset to 0.9 and "gap 0.1" (after Start over) beside "Exactly zero", which jars.
