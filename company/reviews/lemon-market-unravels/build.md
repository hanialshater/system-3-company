# Build note: lemon-market-unravels

builder: demo-builder (sonnet builder, EXP-003 model comparison) · 2026-10-13

- Intent: ten car icons (5 good, 5 bad); cars whose owners' lowest price exceeds the offer turn to dashed outlines.
- Milestone: offer $2,000. The stage starts at $3,000 (all ten for sale, average $2,000) so the learner sees what leaving means. Slider steps $250 from $500.
- A line under the readouts shows when buyers pay more than the average value of cars on sale.
- Readouts hidden while locked. Model labelled (Akerlof 1970, invented numbers).
