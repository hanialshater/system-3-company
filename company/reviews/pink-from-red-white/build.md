# Build note: pink-from-red-white (demo-builder, sonnet build, EXP-003, 2026-10-13)

- Retitled "Two pink flowers: what grows?" (the brief title says red and white give pink).
- Design: two pink parents (R W) and a 100-pot grid. Buttons: Plant 1 seed, Plant 10 more, Fill all 100, Clear pots. Seeds are a fixed seeded sequence (mulberry32 seed 1, untuned); counts for seeds 1 to 3 in artifacts/scratch/w5life/pink.mjs. After 100 seeds: 22 white, 51 pink, 27 red.
- Why 100 and not 51: the first 51 seeds give 20 pink (39%), which would muddy "half"; the full 100 shows 51% pink. The reveal quotes the learner's 100 pots and says the run wobbles around 25 : 50 : 25.
- Gate: milestone "all-100", reached by "Fill all 100" (third button). Pots are empty while locked.
- Legend and model label (made-up flower, R red / W white) sit on the stage.
- Claims all unverified.
