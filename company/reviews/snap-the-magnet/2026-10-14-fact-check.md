# Fact-check: snap-the-magnet (2026-10-14)

Board: the cm-physics round-4 drafts (no fact-check board item; W-581 is the duck objection). Lesson: src/demos/physics/snap-the-magnet/ (status draft, version 1).

Checker: fact-checker (I did not write these claims). Method: I read Demo.tsx on every reachable path before I read the claims: locked state, each control in every order, Reset, the sliders' full range, and the dynamic strings in the stage, Seen line and reveal. Then I re-ran every number in node from my own copies of the lesson logic (scripts in artifacts/fact-check-wave-j/wj-econ-phys-math-geo/; the geography models were run from type-stripped copies of model.ts and solar.ts). Brief check script: artifacts/scratch/cm-physics-r4/check.mjs, re-run in full. The dev server was not used (the dispatcher's instruction). Web: only PyPI, npm, GitHub raw files and public git clones were reachable. Sources opened for this batch: O. Certik, Theoretical Physics (git clone of github.com/certik/theoretical-physics; Maxwell's equations in src/elmag/elmag.rst); the PDG 2026 database (PyPI pdg 2026.0, pdg.sqlite, section S028); ht 1.2.0 (ASHRAE and DIN EN 12524 insulation tables, Churchill-Chu correlation); CoolProp 8.0 (air properties); chemicals 1.5.2 (CRC heat of fusion of water); iapws (IAPWS-06 ice Ih density). Not opened: Halliday-Resnick-Walker, Kundu and Cohen, Griffiths, Incropera. Every verdict that would have rested on them rests on a derivation instead, and the note says so.

Unrecorded claims: I compared every learner-visible sentence (title, hook, question, options, stage labels, Seen line, reveal, recall) with the claims list. None found. The recall question was checked against the claims it rests on (see the notes).

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| c1 | verified | Opened: O. Certik, Theoretical Physics (github.com/certik/theoretical-physics, src/elmag/elmag.rst, Maxwell's equations, 'div B = 0'). Derivation: zero divergence means zero net flux through any closed surface around any piece, so any piece's net pole strength is 0 and an N face comes with an S face. Griffiths was not opened. The stage labels follow the uniform-magnetisation pole model (+sigma A on the face M points to, -sigma A on the other), as in the check script. |
| c2 | unverified | Held: no source on the atomic and domain origin of ferromagnetism could be opened (Griffiths ch. 6 not reachable; Certik's book treats the bar magnet only as a dipole or monopole pair). This cannot be derived from div B = 0. To verify it, give me an openable source on atomic magnetic moments and domains. |
| c3 | verified | Simulation re-run: a coaxial point-dipole chain, two 5-unit halves with a 0.1 gap: force -7.76e4 as cut (attract), +7.76e4 with one half reversed (repel). Stage checked face by face: as cut the break faces are S | N, turned they are S | S, and every compass needle points away from N faces and toward S faces. Path note (not blocking): from 'Snap in the middle' straight to 'Snap each half again', layout('quarters') draws the right half reversed although the learner never turned it. Every piece still shows N + S, but the picture silently flips; build the quarters from the current halves. |
| c4 | verified | Opened: Particle Data Group 2026 database (PyPI package pdg 2026.0, pdg.sqlite, Searches section S028 'Magnetic Monopole Searches'): 199 measurements, almost all upper limits on production cross-sections, fluxes and densities. The only events listed are the CABRERA 1982 single candidate and a CAPLIN 1986 event flagged as controversial; neither was confirmed. 'Nobody has ever found' reads as 'no confirmed detection', which matches. Wording 'No one has ever confirmed a lone north pole' would be exact. |
| c5 | verified | Convention (definition): a compass needle's north end points along B, and outside a magnet B runs from N to S. Checked in Demo.tsx Compass() for every face in all four layouts, and for the middle compass (points east, from the N end toward the S end). |
| c6 | verified | Convention: idealisations, as stated; the arrows are 1 per about 22 px of bar, a picture rather than a count. |

**Result:** no disputes. Held unverified: c2. All other claims verified. The fixes are in the notes; demo-builder owns them (board).
