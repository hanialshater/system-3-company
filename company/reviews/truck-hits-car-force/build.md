# Build note: truck-hits-car-force

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-12 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: a time slider scrubs a head-on crash integrated with RK4 (one spring-damper contact force applied to both vehicles); thick arrows are the two pushes with kN labels, thin arrows the velocities, so 'equal force, unequal change' is on one picture. Force sizes are illustrative and labelled so in claims.
- Earned reveal: unlocks only once the learner has moved into the crash (t > 0, when the forces appear).
- After screenshots: the truck's velocity arrow ran into the force arrows; force arrows moved up and velocity arrows made longer (4 px per m/s) so the car's reversal reads.
