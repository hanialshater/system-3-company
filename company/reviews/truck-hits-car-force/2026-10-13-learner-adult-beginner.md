## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess, and a very strong trap. Everyone "knows" the truck wins; "truck pushes harder" is the answer from everyday life, and "equal" feels absurd. The question wording ("which pushes harder on the other") is clear. Honest.
- Explore: the slider scrubs through the crash and two equal numbers (e.g. 67 kN and 67 kN) with opposing orange arrows sit at the contact point; changing truck mass changes the numbers a little but they stay equal. "Equal at every moment, for every mass" is directly visible. Good.
- Where understanding first broke: the forces change over time (348, 210, 65 kN) which is not what a beginner expects of "a push"; nothing says why the push rises and falls through the crash, so the shape of the numbers looks like noise around the real point (equality).
- Reveal earned? The equality is earned. The second paragraph is not: "the car's velocity reversed while the truck slowed only a little". The thin velocity arrows are on screen, but in the last frame both point right (car arrow longer), so I never saw the car's velocity reverse; I see the car going faster than the truck, not reversing. The claim "ten times as much change of speed" is told, not shown, and the reveal figure is stuck at the 90 ms frame.
- Problems:
  - The "car's velocity reversed" claim contradicts the final screenshot (car arrow still points right at 90 ms), so the picture seems to disagree with the text.
  - Thin velocity arrows are explained only in a small caption; they carry the lesson's second half and are easy to miss.
  - kN is never explained; 348 kN has no scale for an adult (is that a lot?).
  - The truck mass slider goes up from 10,000 kg (and the scale at least to 19,000 kg), so I never see the case of equal or lighter trucks; "whatever the truck's mass" is only tested on heavier ones.
  - The hook "Does it push harder than it gets pushed?" and the answer "equal" may feel like a word game ("push" versus "effect") until the reveal's second paragraph, which comes late.
  - Both vehicles are drawn overlapping at the contact line at 0 ms (no gap), so the "before the crash" state is not shown.
