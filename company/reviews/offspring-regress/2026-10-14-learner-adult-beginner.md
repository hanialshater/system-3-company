# Learner review: offspring-regress
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows parents pass on genes and height is partly environment; has not met heritability).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 5 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: the stem's "40% of the height differences are inherited". I read it, but did not connect it to "only 40% of the 10 cm extra passes on" until the reveal. The pull toward 110 cm is real; the prediction is honest.
- Explore shows it: two dot strips (parents with the kept ones filled, offspring below), a marker moving 100 → 104 → 110 as inheritance goes 0/40/100%. The 0% and 100% ends make 40% sit visibly in between. Good.
- Told-not-shown: why soil and luck don't pass on is told ("seeds do not inherit") and is the core idea; the stage never separates a plant's height into gene part and luck part. Acceptable, but a split bar for one kept plant would show it.
- "response = heritability x selection" introduces two new words in the last line; heritability is never defined on screen (it is "the inherited share").
- Layout: in step 1 the offspring labels "100" and "104" collide into "100104". Reveal fits on phone.

## 2. After reading the brief (version only)

- Version 1, 4 claims shown as checked.

## Fix suggested

Separate the colliding 100/104 labels; gloss "heritability (the inherited share)"; optionally show one kept plant's height as gene part plus luck part.
