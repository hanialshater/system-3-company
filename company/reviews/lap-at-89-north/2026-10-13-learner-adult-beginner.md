## From the artifact
- First needed an idea I didn't have: none at the question. The hook ("Is it always?") pushes me off 40,000, and 700 vs 7,000 is a real guess.
- Not shown: the yellow circle is drawn the same size at the equator, 60°N, 85°N and 89°N, because the map zooms each time.
