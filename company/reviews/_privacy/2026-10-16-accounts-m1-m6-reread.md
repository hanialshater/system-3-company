## M2 ruling

**Question.** Does writing `last_sign_in_at` on session refresh (the `session.update.after` hook), instead of reading the newest session's `updated_at`, keep the column's purpose and retention in EDD-008 §6.1 true?

**Ruling: yes, with two conditions. No change is required before merge.**

Why it holds:
1. **Purpose is the same.** The §6.1 reason for the column is "retention clock (P1.6)". The hook moves it only when the library refreshes a session, which is at most once a day (`updateAge: 86_400`, `auth.ts`), and test 17 shows the refresh moving it. Nothing else reads the column except the export (`progress-store.ts` line 150) and the idle SQL. I grepped every file in `src`, `tools`, `db` and `tests` at 6ff15ba.
2. **No new information is collected.** The date is no finer than the day and equals `auth_session.updated_at`, which the database already holds.
3. **Retention is the same one.** The column lives with the account and goes with it (§7, test 20). The one real change is that a day-resolution "last used" date now outlives the session row, which is purged at sign-out or 30 days after expiry. That is the reason for the change and it is the only way the idle clock can stay true after sign-out; without it an active learner is deleted (my O3). I accept it as proportionate to the purpose.
4. **Why the hook is better than my literal ask.** My formula (latest of `last_sign_in_at` and the newest session `updated_at`) would lose activity when the session row goes. I did not see that. The builder's reading is right. `LAST_ACTIVITY_SQL` still takes the greater of both, so a missed hook write cannot make a learner look idle: harmless redundancy.

Conditions:
- **R-M2a (name the value truthfully; before go-live, G5).** `lastSignInAt` in the export (`account.lastSignInAt`, the `s3c-export-v1` format) and the column name now describe "last sign-in or signed-in use". The inventory (mine, to be written) and the privacy policy must say that, in plain words. The EDD §6.1 row already does. The smallest change that makes the *label* true as well is a rename of the export key to `lastActiveAt`; because migration 001 and the export format have not shipped, it costs a schema diff and one test now and a migration later. I do not require it for the merge. If it is not renamed, the policy text carries the burden.
- **R-M2b (purpose lock; recorded in the inventory now, in a test when someone touches this).** The value is read only by the idle clock and the export. It must never feed personalization, analytics, streaks, reminders or "active user" counts: P3.1 forbids session count and length, and AMD-008 M2 forbids a counter of consecutive periods, and a daily "last used" date is one step from either. Cheap enforcement: extend test 27's file scan so that any read of `last_sign_in_at` or `lastSignInAt` outside `auth.ts`, `progress-store.ts` (export) and `idle-purge.ts` fails. Owner backend-engineer; a hardening, not a gate for the merge.

If either of those cannot be met, the smallest change that keeps §6.1 true is to stop writing the column on refresh and keep `auth_session.updated_at` alone as the carrier, accepting that a learner who signs out loses the clock. I do not recommend it: it brings O3 back.
