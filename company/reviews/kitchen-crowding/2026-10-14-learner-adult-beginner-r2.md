# Learner review (round 2): kitchen-crowding
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult beginner who has worked in a busy kitchen or shop; assumes more workers means proportionally more output.
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 5 frames, phone viewport). Re-review after the kit fix for clipped reveals (W-135); written fresh from this walkthrough.

## 1. From the artifact

- Where understanding first broke: the prediction is not a prediction. The question states the rule: "Each added worker adds 2 fewer than the last (8, then 6, then 4…). How many pizzas an hour with 4 workers?" That is 10 + 8 + 6 + 4 = 28, an addition. My belief ("four cooks make 40") is never at risk; I can only get it wrong by not reading. The walkthrough chose 40, which here means "did not read the question", not "held the misconception".
- Explore: the stacked bar inside a dashed "4 × 10 = 40" outline is a clear picture of the shortfall; "Worker 4 adds 4." "Worker 5 adds 2" and 30. Good visual, but it is a picture of the sum I was given, not a discovery about kitchens.
- Unvisited value: the reveal says "Keep adding and worker 6 adds nothing: the kitchen tops out at 30 an hour." I saw 5 workers at 30; I never saw worker 6 or the flat top. Small, but it is the "tops out" claim, which is the most interesting part.
- "Why" is told: "the oven and the counter stay the same size, so each new worker shares them". The stage never shows the oven or anyone waiting for it; the diminishing numbers are given, not caused by anything visible.
- Phone clipping (the reason for round 2): fixed. Full reveal visible, including the "made-up numbers" caveat and trust link.
- Reveal earned: "28 not 40" yes but trivially; "because the oven is fixed" told.

Fixes (owner: builder):
1. Do not state the decrement in the question. E.g. "One worker makes 10 an hour. The kitchen has one oven. With 4 workers, about how many?" (40 / 28 / 20), then let the stage reveal 8, 6, 4.
2. Ideally make the cause visible: an oven with a fixed number of slots and cooks queueing for it, so the shrinking increments come from something the learner sees.
3. Quote worker 6 / the 30 cap only if the learner reached 6, or let the panel's "keep adding" push to 6.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Misconception: "four workers make four times as much as one". The question text removes it by giving the decrement, so the prediction cannot test it. Objective includes "can stop growing", which the walked path never reaches (it stops at 5 workers, 30).
- The recall asks for the cause (shared machine), which the stage only tells.
- 4 claims verified by others; I cannot vouch for them. Round-1 clipping resolved on this build.

Verdict: revise
