## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: the question no longer states the decrement. "One worker ... makes 10 pizzas an hour. How many ... do 4 workers make?" now tests my "40" belief. But 28 and 20 are both made-up numbers I cannot reason between. The real choice is "40 or less", and the exact pick between 28 and 20 is luck.
- Explore: sliding to 4 workers stacks +10, +8, +6, +4 = 28 inside a dashed "4 × 10 = 40" outline, and "Worker 4 adds 4." The shortfall is a strong picture. "Fill the kitchen" goes to 8 workers: 30 against a dashed 80, "Worker 8 adds nothing". Removing one gives "Worker 7 adds nothing". So I can see the output flatten.
- **Where it loses me (main finding):** why each worker adds less. The stage is a stacked bar of numbers. There is no oven, counter, or queue anywhere, so the shrinking increments are given by the model rather than caused by anything I can see. The cause, which is the lesson's actual idea ("one input is fixed"), is only in the reveal: "The oven and the counter stay the same size". The title says "one oven", but the oven never appears.
- Reveal quotes something I did not see: "You saw worker 6 add nothing". My path went 4 → 8 → 7. I saw workers 7 and 8 add nothing, never worker 6. Worker 5's +2 is an unlabelled sliver in the stack.
