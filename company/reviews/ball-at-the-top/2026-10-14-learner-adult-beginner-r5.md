lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner who knows a thrown ball slows, stops and falls back, reads speed in m/s, and knows gravity pulls down; treats "stopped" as "nothing acting"; doesn't know acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: "Zero" is the natural pick and the three options are honest. The locked stage shows the ball at the top line at 1.4 s with VELOCITY and ACCELERATION both "?". No leak.
- Explore: the walkthrough moved the slider straight to 1.6 s (VELOCITY −1.00 m/s down, ACCELERATION 9.81 m/s² down), then 1.5 s (−0.02 m/s), then back to 1.6 s. **"Show me why" was already lit after the first move.** On this path I never saw an upward velocity: every velocity I saw was zero-ish or downward. The acceleration readout simply printed the answer ("9.81 m/s² down") on the first move.
- Where understanding broke: the idea this boundary needs is "velocity is changing from up to down through the top, and that change is the acceleration". To see it I need a "+" velocity before the top and a "−" after. I got only the "after" half, so I learned the answer from a readout, not from the change.
- Reveal: "Going from 1.4 s to 1.5 s, the velocity arrow went from +0.97 m/s to −0.02 m/s … and flipped." **I never saw +0.97**: at 1.4 s the readout was "?" (locked). The reveal narrates a scrub I didn't do. The definition ("how fast the velocity changes") is told.
- Arrows: the v and a arrows are small and stacked on the ball at the top line. On a phone I read the cards, not the arrows.
