# Build note: two-tugs-right-angle

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: top view of a crate on ice, 22 px per N. 3 N east and 4 N at an adjustable angle drawn tip to tail; the single-force arrow (and its value) appears only after the prediction. A dashed 0-7 N ruler under the crate shows what "just add the numbers" would give, so 5 N is seen falling short of it.
- Departure from the brief: the brief's no-text drag interaction became a 0-180 degree slider (15 degree steps, starting at the question's 90 degrees), because QA presses controls and cannot drag. "Swap order" redraws the 4 N pull first to show the order of tip-to-tail does not matter.
- Earned reveal: milestone 'other-angle' (the 4 N pull turned away from north). QA's two presses reach 120 degrees. The reveal quotes 5.0 N at 90 degrees and the learner's last other angle with its total.
- After screenshots: the 3.6 N and 4 N labels collided at 120 degrees, and the toggle read "3 N first" while drawing 4 N first. Labels are now pushed outward from each triangle side (stacked above the line when collinear); the toggle reads "Swap order".
