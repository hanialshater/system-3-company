## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction leak: "5 N, at an angle" is the only option with a qualifier, and the title ("Surely together that is 7 N?") signals that 7 is wrong. The answer can be picked by reading the options, not by thinking.
- Main finding (told-not-shown): the question is about 90°, but I never saw the 90° result. While locked the stage shows the two arrows tip-to-tail with SINGLE FORCE "?"; my first move turned the slider to 120°, which showed 3.6 N. The reveal then says "At a right angle the single force was 5.0 N, pointing 53° north of east". That number was never on screen in my path. The panel instruction "Turn the 4 N pull" sends the learner away from 90° immediately.
- What is shown works: the orange resultant arrow from the crate to the tip of the chain, with a 0–7 N ruler for length.
- "Swap order" redraws the triangle the other way round with the same 3.6 N, but nothing says that is the point (order doesn't matter). "POINTS 74° from E" is an odd label for direction.
- Layout: the locked stage has no resultant drawn and lots of empty space; ruler ticks are unlabelled except "7 N". In the reveal the diagram shrinks to tiny labels.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Learner field (forces have size and direction; newton as a unit) matches.
- Suggested fix: on unlock, show the 90° resultant (5.0 N, 53° from E) before the learner moves the slider; reword the options so all three are bare numbers or all have a direction; label "Swap order" as "order doesn't change the total" in its feedback; rename POINTS to "Direction".
