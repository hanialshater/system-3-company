# Learner review: a-birthday-like-mine
lesson version: 1
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 6 frames)
boundary: an adult with school-level maths, has heard of the 23-person birthday fact, no probability training.

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction felt real: I picked 23 (same number, I assumed). 183 is the "half of 365" trap, which is the guess I would actually make next.
- Explore is strong: the two bars side by side (yours vs any two) with a dashed 50% line show the gap at 23 (6.1% vs 50.7%) without words. The comparisons / pairs counts under each bar make "you are in only 23 of 253 pairs" visible.
- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: at 183 the bar shows 39.5%. I wanted to know why half the days does not give half the chance. The reveal says "Each comparison hits with chance 1 in 365, so it takes about 253 comparisons". That "so" does not follow for me; 253/365 is 69%, not 50%. The reason (hits overlap; you multiply the chances of missing, 364/365 each time) is not shown or said.
- Told-not-shown: the 253 result is shown; the reason it is 253 rather than 183 is told, weakly.
- Coincidence that confuses: 253 appears as both "pairs among 23 people" and "people to match you". A beginner may think they are the same fact. The reveal's sentence "23 people make 253 pairs" right before "about 253 comparisons" invites that reading. One line saying the match is a coincidence would help.
- Layout: big empty space at the top of the stage once unlocked (frames 2-5). Preset buttons repeat the three options, fine. No clipping seen.
- Fix suggested: in the reveal, replace the "so" sentence with the miss-chance idea, e.g. show "chance nobody matches you = (364/365)^n" falling to 50% at 253, and flag the 253/253 coincidence.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Objective: 253 pairs vs 23 comparisons, so matching you needs about 253 people. The pairs-vs-comparisons half is delivered well by the bar labels. The 'so about 253' step is where the artifact is weakest: the learner is not given why 253 and not 183, and the shared number 253 risks being read as the reason.
