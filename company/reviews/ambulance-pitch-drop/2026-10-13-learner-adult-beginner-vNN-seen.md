## 1. From the artifact

The approaching 754 Hz and the beside-you 704 Hz in the reveal ("Coming toward you, you heard 754 Hz", "Beside you it read 704 Hz") were never displayed during exploration; the start frame showed "?". They are told, not shown, unless the learner thinks to drag back, and nothing asks them to.

"Ring marks every 70th crest" is an unexplained construction: 70 crests per ring with 0.1 s is a neat trick the learner is not shown.
- The 704 Hz beside you ("that sound left a moment earlier") is a hard idea dropped in one clause.
