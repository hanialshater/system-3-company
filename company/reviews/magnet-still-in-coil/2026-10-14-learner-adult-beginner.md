# Learner review: magnet-still-in-coil
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner; knows magnets and coils make electricity (dynamos) and what a meter shows; believes a magnet inside a coil makes current.

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: steady current / zero / current the opposite way. All plausible to a beginner; "steady current" is the honest pick.
- Explore: Push in, Hold still 1 s, Pull out. A 3-second trace shows a downward kick on push-in, a flat line while held, an upward kick on pull-out. The flat line while the magnet sits inside is the lesson, and it is shown.
- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: reading the meter. "METER NOW" reads 0 mV in every frame (the kicks are over by the time I look), and the needle never visibly swings in the screenshots. The kick sizes (-123 / +123 mV) appear only in the reveal text. The trace is tiny and the zero line is a faint dotted grey.
- Told-not-shown: "Faraday's law: voltage = turns × rate of change of flux": "flux" is new jargon for this learner and is not defined or pictured. The core sentence ("responds to a change ... not to the magnetism itself") is fine.
- Layout: phone; reveal fits, no clipping. Large empty area under the trace.
- Suggested fix (minor): show the peak value on the trace ("-123 mV") and keep the needle deflected briefly; enlarge the trace; replace "flux" with "the magnetism through the coil" or picture it.

Verdict: launch
