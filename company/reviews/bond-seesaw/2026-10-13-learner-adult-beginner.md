## 2. After reading the brief

Objective is to say price = payments discounted at the market rate; the artifact shows the result but never shows discounting. Claims: 5 verified.

## Suggested fix
Put the comparison on the stage: a new bond paying $60/year beside this one paying $50/year, and show how much less you'd pay to make the $50 bond as good a deal. Replace "discounted back" with a plain phrase or show the shrinking value of each future $50.
