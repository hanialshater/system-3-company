## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: "25 m" (halving) and "10 m" (a round number) are the natural beginner errors, and I picked 25. But the locked stage already lists the side buttons "25 m, 10 m, 8 m, 7 m, 7.1 m, 7.07 m" under the grid. Why would a lesson offer 7.1 and 7.07 unless the answer lives there? With "just over 7 m" as an option, a learner who glances at the buttons has the answer. The option wording is also the only specific, non-round option, which is a second cue.
- Explore: each side tap draws the square against a dashed 50 m² outline, red when too big and blue when too small. 25 m "runs off the grid" (625 m², 12.5× the target), 10 m gives 100 m², 8 m gives 64, 7 m gives 49 ("too small"), 7.1 m gives 50.41 ("just over"), and 7.07 m gives 49.98 ("just under"). The bracketing between 7 and 8 is visible: whole numbers skip over 50.
- Where it is thinnest: the path is a fixed ladder of buttons, so the learner never chooses a side, only taps the next one. The narrowing is the lesson's, not the learner's. That is acceptable for a beginner.
- Small mismatch: the 7.07 square is blue ("just under") yet visually fills the dashed outline exactly, while 7.1 is red and also looks the same size. Fine, but the colour does the work there, not the picture.
- Reveal: it names the square root and answers my error ("halving 50 does not find the side"). Every number was seen.

Hide the button row until the prediction is locked, or show only 25 m and 10 m and reveal the finer steps as each is tried. The v1 review did not raise this. I can't tell from the artifact whether the button row was visible then.
