## 1. From the artifact

- Explore: a square on a metre grid over a dashed 50 m² outline; red when too big, blue when too small; 10 → 100, 8 → 64, 7 → 49, 7.1 → 50.41, 7.07 → 49.98. Bracketing between 7 and 8 and then closing in is shown, not told. Good.
- Scale problem: the 25 m square (625 m²) is drawn only a little larger than the 10 m one, filling a grid that stops at 10. It is not to scale and it undersells how wrong 25 is (6x the 10 m square's area). The first and most important "you're wrong" picture is the least honest.

It is not a whole number, because 7 × 7 = 49 and 8 × 8 = 64 skip right over 50."
