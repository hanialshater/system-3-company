## Verdict: approve

Both trees sharing one 204-unit height works. At the reveal, in Rotating mode and in Plain mode, the whole tree, the key row, the caption and all four readouts are on screen (stage top 174 px, reveal bottom 788 of 844). Because the stage no longer jumps when you switch trees, the comparison is fair. The plain chain at half pitch still reads as a chain: each key keeps its column and the diagonal is clear.

## Against the four criteria

| Criterion | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Pass | Rule line, then tree, key row, the bold event caption ("Key 7 made 5's side too deep: 6 lifted up"), readouts and controls. The caption is the strongest line on the stage, which is right. |
| Labels ≥ 10 px | Pass | Node keys 12.2 px in explore and 11 px at the reveal (phone), 13 px on desktop. Readout labels (HEIGHT, PLAIN…) 10.9 px. Ghost keys are aria-hidden, at 11 px. |
| Grouping, tap targets ≥ 24 px | Pass | One row of four, each 77×44 px. The active view (Rotating/Plain) is shaded, so the toggle state reads at a glance. |
| Reveal evidence kept | Pass | "3 levels", "chain of 7", "4 rotations" and "2 above 1 and 3" are all visible in the tree or the readouts. The key-3 rotation itself is an earlier event and is not replayed, but its result is on stage. |

## Recommended (not blocking; builder's choice)

- The four readouts repeat each other. HEIGHT and ROTATIONS restate the current tree, and ROTATING says "3 levels, 4 rot." again. One row (Plain | Rotating) would save about 45 px and make the comparison the focus.
- The row mixes two actions (Insert next key, Insert the rest) with a two-way view switch (Plain, Rotating). A slightly wider gap, or a hairline between them, would show the two groups. "Insert next key" wraps onto two lines at 77 px.
- The lift arrow at 6 overlaps the 6–7 edge. Nudge it right.
