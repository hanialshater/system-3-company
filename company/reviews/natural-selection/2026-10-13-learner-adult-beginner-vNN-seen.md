# Learner review: natural-selection
lesson version: vNN
boundary: an adult with school-level maths and science, new to the topic (knows "survival of the fittest" as a slogan; no model of how a 10% edge compounds). Screenshots seen (sheet.png, all 11 frames).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. About 2 is the "10% edge is small, so little change" intuition, 50 is "it just needs time", 99 is the compounding answer. The hook ("No beetle ever changes colour. So how does the whole population change?") half-gives the mechanism (it is about parents and young) but not the number. "Dark ones leave 10% more young" is clear.
- The locked stage shows a grid of pale beetles with one dark and a tiny chart with unreadable axis labels ("% dark", "gen 100" are almost illegible on a phone).
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal. "The start was 1 dark to 99 pale. Every generation that ratio grows 1.1×... 1.1^100 ≈ 14,000×: about 139 dark to 1 pale." The stage never mentions odds or ratios; the learner has watched the share go 1.0, 1.2, 1.3, 3.4 and sees a slow 1.1 percent-ish growth, not a ratio. Why the ratio, not the share, multiplies by 1.1 is asserted. Beginner thinks 1.1^100 ≈ 14,000 is the share and is lost between "14,000×" and "99%".
- What was SHOWN is strong: the beetles on the grid flip dark between gen 13 and 100 and the green curve lifts late (it is flat for a long time). The 1% edge giving only 2.7% at gen 100 is shown and contrasted. The "None changed colour" caption repeats on every frame, so the hook's puzzle is answered by presence, not by argument. The edge buttons are labelled "Edge: +1% / +5% / +10% / +20%" before the learner knows that edge means the 10% more young.
- Inconsistency noticed: after "Start over" the transcript caption says generation 0, but the reveal frame shows generation 100, 99% dark, +10% edge again. The stage silently changes at reveal; harmless but confusing if a learner just reset.
- The 5% edge (57% at gen 100) is shown but never mentioned in the reveal; fine. The final line about peppered moths (1848 to 98% by 1895) is a real-world number never shown; marked "illustration only".
- Reveal earned: the number 99 yes; the 1.1^100 explanation not.
- Problems: (1) odds/ratio explanation not earned; (2) chart axis text unreadable; (3) "edge" term appears on buttons without a pointer to the stem's 10%; (4) stage state at reveal differs from after reset.
