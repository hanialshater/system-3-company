# Safety review: wobbly-table-quarter-turn

lifehacks-safety-reviewer · 2026-10-13 · inputs: brief in `company/domains/briefs/lifehacks.md`, illustration requests in `company/design/illustration-requests/`. Brief stage only: no Demo.tsx, meta.json or walkthrough exists yet, so this is not a launch attestation.

**Verdict: PASS WITH CHANGES. Safety class: none (kept), once the changes are made.** The skip line stays in `company/ops/brief-skips.json` until cm-lifehacks makes the changes and I confirm them.

## Reasons
- The hack (rotate the table in place) is lower risk than the usual fix and involves no tools or heat.
- Careless version 1, hot drinks: people will turn a table with full, hot cups on it, and a rocking table being turned is exactly when a cup tips. Scalds from hot drinks are a common household burn. The hero request asks for "a cup on the table" while two hands turn it. That image teaches the careless version.
- Careless version 2, slopes: claim 4 says the theorem holds up to about 35°. Read loosely, that says a table on a steep slope is "fixed". Balanced (four feet down) is not stable or level; on a slope, things slide off and the table can tip.
- Careless version 3, short leg or broken ground: the brief already shows that turning cannot fix a short leg, and the illustration request already keeps the floor continuous and forbids anyone standing, sitting or leaning on the table. Those are good and stay as they are.

## Required changes
1. **Hero illustration request** (`company/design/illustration-requests/wobbly-table-quarter-turn.md`): remove the cup from the table, and add "no cups, glasses, plates or anything hot on the table while it is turned" to *What it must NOT show*. An empty table top is fine.
2. **Brief, claim 4**: state the 35° figure only as the theorem's mathematical limit, and add one short stage line, e.g. "Four feet down is not the same as level: on a real slope, things slide." Do not put any wording on the card that suggests using a table on a steep slope.
3. **Brief, reveal or stage** (one line, no more): "Clear hot drinks before you turn it." Keep it this short; a longer warning will not be read.
