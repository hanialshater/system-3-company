## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: $160, $220 and $240. The stem gives the greedy result ($160) and says "Whole items only", so $240 is a tempting slip for anyone who pictures cutting, and $160 is the "greedy is best" trap. I picked $160. The locked stage already shows the ratio-first bag: 10 kg · $60 + 20 kg · $100, a hatched "20 kg empty" band, and the 30 kg · $120 ($4/kg) item left outside. "A bag with air in it" is literally on screen, which is a good hook, not a giveaway.
- Explore: tapping 10 kg out leaves "30 kg empty". Tapping 30 kg in fills the bag to "Packed 50/50 kg · $220", and the line "Best whole-item bag so far: $220 (20 + 30 kg)" updates. Taking 20 kg out shows $120. "Items can be cut: on" repacks to 10 + 20 + "20 of 30 kg · $80", $240, with no air. The whole contrast is visible: greedy leaves a gap with whole items, and cutting fills it.
- Reveal: $160, the 20 kg gap, $220 (20 + 30) and $240 (two thirds of the 30 kg item) were all on stage. "Dynamic programming does that without trying all of them" is a told pointer with an unexplained term. That is acceptable as the last line.
