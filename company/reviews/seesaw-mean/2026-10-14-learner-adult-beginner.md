## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction felt real: 2.5 (between the middle two weights) is my instinct; 5.5 (halfway along the plank) is the other natural guess. Both traps are live.
- Explore: a plank with numbered ticks, four blocks, a pivot; it tips right at 2.5, left at 5.5, and sits level (turns green) at 4. The physical intuition does the teaching. Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: none in explore.
- Told-not-shown: "-3, -2, -1, 6 are the weights' distances from the pivot: they add to 0" is told; negative distances are a small new idea for me, and the stage does not draw them. Little arrows or numbers above each block measured from the pivot would show the cancelling.
- Confusing line: "The middle number, 2.5, counts the weights" is hard to parse. I think it means "the median only counts how many weights are on each side". Say that.
- Minor: the "PIVOT AT" tile shows "?" whenever the plank is not level, so I cannot read where my pivot is while trying; I have to read the slider.
- Layout: clean; no clipping; the gate opened on a single slide that happened to land on 4.
- Fix suggested (minor): label each block's signed distance from the pivot as it moves; reword the median line; show the pivot position always.
