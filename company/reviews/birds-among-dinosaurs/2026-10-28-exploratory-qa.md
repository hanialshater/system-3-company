# Exploratory browser QA: birds-among-dinosaurs v1

Date: 2026-10-28. Target: local component harness on 127.0.0.1:4927, source tree at 0279bec2; this is not a built Next.js application, production route, or the protected Playwright contract. Harness bundles the actual DemoCard, Lesson, controls, global stylesheet and lesson component; its DemoHost and Next Link are stubs, and it supplies card metadata directly. Results cannot certify routing, build completeness, accessibility or learning. All reported flows were manually scripted in Playwright/Chromium; one exploratory setup attempt initially failed to connect, and a separate setup was corrected before the recorded runs.

Four browser flows were observed: correct and incorrect prediction on 390×844 and 1280×800. All four had initially disabled stage and commit controls, no initial reveal, reveal disabled until interaction, an observable stage change, enabled reveal after the action, the expected correct/incorrect verdict, no horizontal document overflow, no browser page or console errors, and reload back to predict. The reveal text exceeded 120 characters. A keyboard-only pass reached an option, commit, stage control and reveal using Tab and Space/Enter. The stage control measured 149.2 × 33 px, above the protected contract's 24 px minimum; consider a larger touch target.

At 390×844 the visually inspected wrong-answer screenshot showed the whole stage and reveal in view (stage top 126.3 px, reveal bottom 517.8 px, body bottom 785 px). Supplemental reduced-motion runs used light and dark OS preferences at 390×844 and 360×640. In this standalone skin both preferences render the dark surface. The visually inspected 360×640 dark screenshot showed the stage and reveal within the body (stage top 126.3 px, reveal bottom 503.3 px, body bottom 581 px); no horizontal document overflow.

Finding: the unhighlighted branch leading toward crocodilians and the common root is difficult to distinguish from the dark background. It uses `--line` (#2d2f37 in `.single`) against the dark card's gradient; approximately 1.29:1 against #191b22, below 3:1. The exact gradient varies spatially. Route to the builder and accessibility reviewer for a higher-contrast structural line and independent contrast review. The dinosaur branch remains green in this harness; the SVG's accessible label describes the relationship, but that does not resolve the visual contrast issue.
