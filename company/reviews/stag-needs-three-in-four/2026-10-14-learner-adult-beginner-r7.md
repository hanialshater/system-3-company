A test-wise learner picks 75 without reasoning. Under "No answer before the commit" (labelled values on the locked stage, an option worded more precisely than the others), this is a revise. 3. **The coordination half is thinner.** "both hare: 3 each" gets ticked at 0% and "both stag: 4 each" at 100%. The reveal explains that the ticks mean "each outcome is the best reply to itself". That step relies on the chip's assumption ("one partner as sure of you as you are of them"), which is a hard sentence to parse in 9-point type. The ticks stand for the idea, but I would not have read them as "self-confirming" without the reveal. The hook's question (why both settle for hare) is answered mostly in words.
4. **"At least 75%" vs the tie.** At exactly 75% the stage says "either (tie)", so stag is only strictly better above 75%. "At least" is defensible (stag is not worse), but a sharp learner will notice.
5. **The reveal quotes what I saw.** 2 at 50%, the crossing at 75%, and the 0% / 100% ticks all stay on the chart as dots and ticks after I moved the slider to 90%.

What the prediction felt like: I picked "More than 50%" because stag pays more, which is exactly the wrong intuition the lesson targets. But the locked presets had already whispered "75".

Verdict reasoning: the explore and reveal work, but the commit can be won by reading the preset row and the odd-precision option.
