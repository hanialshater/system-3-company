## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: salt / sugar / iodine. The locked stage labels hexane "no charged ends" and iodine "no charge", water and sugar "± ends". - First idea I lacked: what "charged ends" means and why it matters. Sugar labelled "± ends" next to salt's "+ and - ions" suggests sugar is also charged, which I would take literally.
- Shown well: salt and sugar dots spread in water but sit as grains at the bottom of hexane; iodine turns hexane violet and barely tints water. Clear, colourful contrast.
- Told, not shown: "a liquid dissolves what pulls on it as strongly as its own molecules do" — the pull is never pictured.
- Layout: good; jars and chips are legible. Text readout does not change between steps (only the picture), so a screen-reader user gets nothing from the taps.
- Fix suggested: move the charge labels from the chips to appear after each drop, and add a text line per jar ("dissolved / settled").
