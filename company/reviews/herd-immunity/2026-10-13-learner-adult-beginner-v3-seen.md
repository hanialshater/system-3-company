# Learner review: herd-immunity
lesson version: v3
boundary: an adult with school-level maths and science, new to the topic (knows vaccines protect people; has heard "herd immunity" and "R0" in the news but could not say what R0 does). Screenshots seen (sheet.png, all 10 frames).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. "Nearly all 80" is the "unprotected get sick" fear, "About 20" the middle, "Just a handful" the herd-immunity result. The hook ("Can everyone else's immunity shield them?") leans to yes, so it leaks a little to a learner who has heard the phrase. The question states the setup (400 people, 80% immune, each case infects 4) and R0 is used on the stage and explained there as "each case meets 4 random people". Good.
- The locked stage shows a grid of green dots (immune) and open circles (unprotected), one red. Legend present; nothing leaks.
- Where understanding first broke: step 5, the 70% run: "Over: 2 of 120. It fizzled by luck." beside the readout "AVERAGE, 200 RUNS 21 of 120". At 50% the single run says 168 of 200 against average 146 of 200; at 60% 100 of 160 against 80 of 160. My beginner now holds two numbers per button (this run, average of 200) and does not know which answers "usually how many". The word "luck" arrives with no earlier hint that runs vary. I could not tell whether 70% was safe or not.
- The 75% threshold is first seen in the reveal ("Below the 75% threshold (1 − 1/R0)"). No 75% button exists, and the formula 1 − 1/R0 appears nowhere on the stage; the "4 × 20% = 0.8" readout supports "below 1 means it dies out" but the point where it equals 1 is never shown. The reveal's "a share that grows with distance from the threshold" is told.
- What was SHOWN is good: dots turn red/dashed pink spreading through the grid at 50% and almost none at 80 and 90; "EACH CASE INFECTS 4 × 20% = 0.8" is a clear live readout.
- Mismatch: after Next step the counter reads "2 caught it so far" under the caption "Step 1: 1 new case", presumably including the seed case, but the label is not explained. "Run to end" is greyed afterwards, fine.
- Reveal earned: "just a handful" yes (4 of 80 average, 2 of 80 seen); the threshold language not.
- Problems: (1) single-run vs average confusion; (2) 75% threshold and the formula not shown; (3) "Step 1: 1 new case" vs "2 caught"; (4) the hook leans to the answer.
- No FATAL.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The brief's learner does not know R0 or branching processes; the readout "4 × 20% = 0.8" is a good bridge. But the objective includes "more than 1 - 1/R0 immune", and the 75% threshold appears only in the reveal while the buttons are 50 to 90. Intent says "learner can explain"; experience gives a number to accept.
- The single-run vs average-of-200 readouts are not mentioned in the brief's learner model; they are the part that confused me.
