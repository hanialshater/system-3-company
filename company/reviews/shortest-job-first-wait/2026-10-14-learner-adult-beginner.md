## 1. From the artifact

- Where understanding first broke: the hook, before the question. The hook reads "One order makes everyone wait three times as long." The question then asks whether shortest-first total wait is "the same 34", "about a third of it" or "a little less". The hook has already said "three times", so "about a third" is answerable by matching words. My honest guess ("the same 34", total work is fixed) only survives if I skip the hook.
- Explore: tapping a job to move it earlier is direct, and the timeline with pink "waits N" shading makes the waiting visible. The total drops 34 → 30 → 24 → 17 as I move short jobs forward, which builds the idea one step at a time. "Shortest first" gives 11. Good.
- Reveal: "You tried 5 orders" matches (34, 30, 24, 17, 11 distinct). 0 + 8 + 12 + 14 and 0 + 1 + 3 + 7 are both on the stage I saw. No unvisited values.
- "A job at the front ... is counted once for each of them" is told but the shaded bars show it. "If short jobs keep arriving, a long one can wait forever" is told and never shown; fine as a closing caution.
- Phone: reveal fully visible, trust link visible. No clipping.
- Reveal earned: yes for the mechanism; the answer itself was leaked by the hook.

Fixes (owner: builder): reword the hook so it does not state the ratio (e.g. "The same four jobs, the same printer. Does the order change how long everyone waits?").

- The objective's last part (shortest first can starve a long job) rests on one told sentence; nothing on the stage shows starvation. If it stays in the objective, it is not earned.
