# Learner review (round 2): high-satellite-view
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult beginner who knows satellite TV dishes point at a fixed satellite; assumes a high satellite sees "the whole side" of Earth, i.e. half.
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 6 frames, phone viewport). Re-review after the kit fix for clipped reveals (W-135); written fresh from this walkthrough.

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: geostationary, 35,786 km up; what share of the surface can it see? 50% / about 40% / 25%. Exactly the right trap; I chose 50%.
- Explore: three presets and a height slider. Space station 400 km: 3.0%, a small circle. GPS 20,200 km: 38.0%. Geostationary: 42.4%, the circle nearly fills the visible disc but stops short of the poles. The orthographic globe makes "not quite half" visible: the circle never reaches the globe's edge. Good.
- Unvisited value: the reveal says "From 1,000 km it saw only about 7%." 1,000 km was the locked starting state, shown with "SHARE OF EARTH IN VIEW ?"; after I committed I went straight to Space station (400 km, 3.0%). I never saw 7%. The number I did see for a low satellite, 3.0%, is not mentioned. This is the known "reveal quotes a value the learner never visited" pattern.
- Reveal: 42% and 81° match the stage (81.3° in the picture label). "Exactly half needs an infinite height" is told but follows from watching the circle creep (38% → 42%). Good.
- Phone clipping (the reason for round 2): fixed. Full reveal visible including the dish line and trust link.
- Where understanding first broke: only at "from 1,000 km ... 7%", where I looked back for a number I had not seen.
- Reveal earned: yes for "less than half, and it never reaches half".

Fixes (owner: builder): have the reveal quote the lowest height the learner actually visited (e.g. "From the space station's 400 km it saw 3%"), or show the 1,000 km share after the commit before any preset is tapped.
