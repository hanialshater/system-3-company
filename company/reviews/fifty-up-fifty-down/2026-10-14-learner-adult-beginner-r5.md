# Learner review (re-review r5): fifty-up-fifty-down

## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: 100 / 75 / 125, and 100 is the honest trap. The hook's "Surely you are back where you started?" is a mild nudge toward doubting 100, but it doesn't say which way. The locked stage is a single square, "start 100 / ends 100", at 0%. No leak.
- Explore: one slider move to 50% widens the square (a "rise +50" strip on the right) and cuts a band off the top across the whole new width. Labels: "fall −75", "lost 25" on the top-right corner, "ends 75", and the caption "Up 50%: 150. Down 50% of 150: 75." The red corner sticking out past the dashed start square is the picture of "the fall was bigger than the rise".
- Reveal: every number it quotes (50, 150, 75, 25) is on the stage. I got "same percentage, bigger base, so you end lower" from the caption and the corner before the reveal said it.

- Minor: the area labels don't sit on regions of their own size. "fall −75" is printed on the top-left piece, which by area is 50 (the fall is that piece plus the red corner). A bracket spanning the whole top band for "fall −75" and the whole right strip for "rise +50" would fix it.
