# Learner review: mercator-greenland
lesson version: v?
boundary: an adult who has used maps and a globe and knows the continents; has never studied geography or map projections.

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess and an excellent hook for the misconception: the first (Mercator) map shows Greenland in blue, huge, next to a small green DR Congo, and the question says "drawn about 15 times the size". Most beginners will pick "Greenland, about 15x bigger", having seen it on wall maps all their lives. The hook ("Wall maps make Greenland look enormous") nudges that they are being tested on size, but does not say the answer.
- Where understanding first broke: not on the main idea. The slider "Slide Greenland south" works: at 2° the readout drops from 15.4x to 11.3x, the blue shape visibly shrinks as it moves toward the equator, and tapping "Show Equal Earth map" gives 0.9x. The dashed outline for Greenland's real position helps. I saw that the same land is drawn at very different sizes depending on where it is and on the map.
- A real confusion: the readout is labelled "ON THIS MAP, GREENLAND / CONGO" and the toggle button says "Show Equal Earth map" and then "Show Mercator map", but neither "Mercator" nor "Equal Earth" is defined in the stage (the reveal defines "projection"). In the Equal Earth state the world looks squashed into an oval and Greenland is tiny at the top; a beginner might think that map is the wrong one.
- Also, the learner moves Greenland only 2° in my walk; the stage does not make me slide all the way to the equator, though doing so is what the hook suggests.
- Reveal vs stage: 0.9x / "Congo slightly bigger" matches the Equal Earth readout. 2.17 million vs 2.34 million km² are told, not shown. "Stretches areas by about 1/cos²(latitude): more than ten times at Greenland's latitude" is a formula the stage does not show (though 11.3 to 15.4 gives a feel). "Mercator was made for sailors ... keeps angles true ... no flat map can keep both" is told with no stage support.
- Phone legibility: both maps fit the card; the Equal Earth Greenland at the top edge is small and cramped against the border; the readout and buttons are clear. Reveal clipped at the bottom in the screenshot.
- Reveal earned: yes for "Greenland did not change, the map did". The sailors/angles payoff is told.
- Problems: (1) Mercator and Equal Earth never defined on the stage; (2) angles/sailors/"no map keeps both" told, not shown; (3) the 1/cos² formula is jargon; (4) the slide is short in my walk.
- No FATAL.
