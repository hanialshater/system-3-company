# Build note: light-at-double-distance

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-12 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: oblique view of a square beam; the page is one tile and the light patch at its distance is a d x d grid, with patch and page brightness following 1/d^2.
- Earned reveal: unlocks only once the page has reached 2 m.
- After screenshots: the 'page' label sat on the page edge; moved above it, and the page fill now dims with the lux so the brightness is seen as well as read.
