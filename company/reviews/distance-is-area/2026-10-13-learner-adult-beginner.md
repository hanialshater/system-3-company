# Learner review: distance-is-area
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult who remembers basic school arithmetic and everyday life; has never studied the topic (knows distance = speed x time for constant speed; has not seen speed-time graphs or calculus).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess is possible and each option has a story: 200 m (top speed x time), 100 m (average), 20 m (just the final speed). A beginner can reason "it starts at 0 so not 200". Good. The graph is drawn, but at that point the bars' area reads "?" so nothing leaks.
- Where understanding first broke: step 1/2, "4 strips, each speed × 2.5 s. Bars poke above the line." I understand a strip is speed times a short time, but I am not told why adding strips gives distance, nor why the car's speed within a strip is taken at its right end. Then the caption at step 2, "Triangle: ½ × 10 s × 20 m/s = 100 m", gives the answer by a school-geometry formula; the triangle is shown with 100 m and the learner can just stop caring about the strips.
- The bars' area counter (125 m, 114.3 m, 110 m) falling toward 100 m as strips increase is shown clearly in the screenshot (10 strips: bars poke only slightly above the line, teal triangle sits inside). This does earn "thinner strips get closer to the triangle".
- The reveal quotes "One strip at top speed gave 200 m; … 20 gave 105 m." The explore log never shows me 1 strip's area ("?" at the start) or 20 strips (slider reached 10 in the driver). A learner who did not drag to those would be given numbers they did not see. Also the sentence "the same as the average speed 10 m/s for 10 s" is a new idea (a second route to 100) dropped in at the very end.
- Units: "BARS' AREA 110 m" labels an area as metres; the unit is right for the quantity but a beginner may find "area = metres" jarring, and that is the whole point of the lesson ("distance is area") but is never made explicit until the last line.
- Reveal gate: reachable (Show me why is active after triangle and strips). Reveal earned: mostly yes.
- Problems: (1) why strip areas are distances is asserted, not shown; (2) numbers in reveal (200, 105) not necessarily seen; (3) the triangle's exact formula may let a beginner skip the strip idea; (4) unit of area-as-metres unremarked.
- No FATAL.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The brief's misconception is "speed alone is not enough when it changes"; the options (200 / 100 / 20) actually test top-speed x time, not that. The objective (strips -> area under the line) is what the explore delivers well.
- The brief's boundary assumes straight-line graph reading; my beginner shares that, and the lesson did not stumble there.
- Claims all verified; no false statement seen.
- The gap I found (strip = speed x time reads as distance) is the brief's stated core, so it is worth one explicit visual (e.g. one strip labelled with its metres).
