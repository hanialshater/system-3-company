## 1. From the artifact

The hook ("you might not land where you expect") tells me there is a trap, which mildly leaks that the obvious answer is wrong.
- What was SHOWN: tapping Back moves "you are here" down and marks D as "forward"; tapping "Visit new page E" puts E on top and shows a red "Thrown away: D". That visibly demonstrates the mechanism (the pages above you are discarded) and is the strongest part.
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal. The final answer B is never shown on the stage: the transcript path (Back, Visit E, Start over) leaves the stage reading "You are on D", and nothing in the explore forces the learner to press the last Back. The reveal text then says "the pile is now A, B, E. So Back goes to B": told, not shown. A learner who explores differently from the panel instruction (here the reveal unlocked after "Start over") reaches the reveal without ever seeing the result they predicted. Also in the transcript path E was visited from C, so the pile was E, C, B, A with only D thrown away; the reveal's "everything above B away" describes a state (C and D gone) this path never displayed.
- Terms used before defined: "Forward" is in the reveal ("in case you press Forward") but there is no Forward button on the stage; the stage only shows the word "forward" on a dashed row. "Stack" and "queue" arrive in the reveal's last paragraph. They are defined there, which is fine, but the queue paragraph is a side trip the stage never touched.
- Reveal earned: partly. The "thrown away" idea was earned by the stage; the exact answer B was not demonstrated.
- Problems: (1) the reveal can be unlocked without seeing the final Back; (2) the reveal's state (C and D discarded) differs from what the stage showed along the transcript path; (3) "Forward" unexplained and not on the stage; (4) the hook hints the obvious answer is wrong; (5) queue paragraph is unearned filler.
