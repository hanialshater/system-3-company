## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. Everything needed is defined on the stage: "each case meets 4 people it could infect". The hook "Does 60% immune do anything at all?" is neutral, though the title "Immunity works before the threshold" gives away "yes, it helps". "About 4" is the natural wrong answer; "fewer than 1" is the herd-immunity misreading. R0 is not named in plain words in the question itself, but the stage line covers it.
- Where understanding first broke: nowhere on the idea. The 40-contacts-from-10-cases framing appears only after tapping, and I had to read the caption to see why 40.

Captions read "60% immune: 24 of 40 immune, 16 infected, R = 1.6", then 75%: 10 of 40, R = 1.0, then 85%: R = 0.6. Every number in the reveal (24, 16, 1.6, 4 x 0.4, 75% gives 1) is on the stage. "Newly infected" and "case" are both red dots, distinguished by a black outline; hard at a glance.
- Told, not shown: "R0 x susceptible share, 4 x 0.4" (formula), "herd-immunity threshold = where R drops below 1, not where immunity starts to help". The second is demonstrated by the three taps. The point that 1.6 is still above 1 so cases still grow is not stated; a beginner may take "R = 1.6 at 60% immune" as "immunity did not do much".
- Reveal text is cut at the card edge.
- The trust badge reads "0/4 claims checked".
- Reveal earned: yes.
- Problems: (1) case vs newly infected colours; (2) the 1.6 vs 1 consequence (still growing) left unsaid; (3) title leaks "works"; (4) 0/4 claims badge.
