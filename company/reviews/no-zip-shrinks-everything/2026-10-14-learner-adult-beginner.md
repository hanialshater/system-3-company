## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: 8 three-bit files into strings of 0, 1 or 2 bits. Options: "Yes, with a clever code" (my honest belief, chosen), "No, not enough shorter strings", "Yes, but only slowly". The third is a weak distractor; the second states the mechanism, so a careful reader may pick it by its wording. Still a real choice for me.
- Explore: picking a file and dropping it in a slot is obvious and tactile. The counter "6 of 7 slots used · 2 files waiting" makes the shortage visible before it happens. At the end, "Every slot is taken. 110 has nowhere shorter to go" with 110 in red is the moment, and it is mine: I filled the slots myself.
- Friction: tapping "Pick file X" repeatedly only changes which file is held; nothing visible happens until a slot is tapped. A beginner may tap 7 files and wonder why nothing moved. The "Holding 001: tap a free slot" line does tell me.
- The "0 bits / empty" slot is strange to a beginner: a file compressed into nothing? It is correct and it helps the count (1 + 2 + 4), but one short gloss ("the empty string counts too") would stop the "is that cheating?" thought.
- Reveal: quotes 110 and "all 7 slots", both what I saw. No unvisited values. The general 2ⁿ vs 2ⁿ − 1 line is told, but it is a direct generalisation of the count I just did. "Two files cannot share a slot" answers the question I would have next ("why not double up?").
- Phone clipping: reveal fully visible, including the zip line and trust link.
- Where understanding first broke: the empty slot, briefly. Nothing else.
- Reveal earned: yes.

Fixes (owner: builder, optional): a tiny label on the 0-bit slot like "the empty string"; make "Pick file" taps visibly lift the file.
