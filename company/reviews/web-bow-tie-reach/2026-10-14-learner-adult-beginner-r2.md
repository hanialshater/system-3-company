Simulated learner. This review finds problems; it is not evidence that anyone learned.

## Section 1: from the artifact only

**Idea the learner lacks:** a link works in one direction, so "joined" and "reachable" differ.

**Before predicting.** The stem gives "10 of 12 are joined" and the options are 7 / 10 / 4. The title ("Joined is not the same as reachable") already says the answer is not 10, so a learner can drop option 10 and pick 7 by elimination before any tap. The locked stage shows the arrows, but nothing asks the learner to trace them. The title is a leak of the thesis, not of the number.

**Prediction feel.** I picked 7 by elimination. The first tap (A) shows 7, the same number I picked, so step 1 confirms rather than tests. The real test is the later taps (E gives 10, G gives 3), and those work: the filled picture changes visibly.

**Where understanding first broke.**
1. The stem says "the central group" and the stage has no labels until all three groups are tried (labels appear only after the third milestone). A learner at step 1 to 5 has no name for the groups.
2. In the reveal frame the labels IN, CORE and OUT are cropped off the top of the stage. The frame shows A, D, H, I, E, J, K, L, with B and C half cut and F, G cut out. The text then says "From CORE you can click out to OUT, but nothing leads back to IN". Of the three words, only TENDRIL and DETACHED are visible on that frame, and the text never says those two words in the reach sentences. The reveal text points at labels the reveal picture does not show.
3. The reveal text quotes "From E you reach 10" and "From G you reach 3", but the stage shows only A's reach (dashed rings mark E and G; G is off-frame). The learner did see those, but the picture beside the reveal does not back the numbers. The single filled picture cannot show the "nothing leads back to IN" claim unless the learner notices E is unfilled, and E is the visible dashed ring: that part works.
4. Reveal is about 110 words and uses CORE/IN/OUT/tendril/detached as vocabulary in one go.

**Answer visible before predicting.** Title leak as above. Hook ("How many can you actually click your way to?") fine. Nothing else.

**Reveal quotes only reached states?** Yes: the milestone gate forces core, IN and OUT taps, and numbers are computed from the pages tapped. No W-150 here.

**Told vs shown (2026-10-14 decision).** "Links are one-way" is shown: arrows on the stage, fill stops at the arrow tips. "The bow-tie shape of the web" is shown as a labelled toy, once labels appear, and the real web claim is one claimed sentence. Pass. The weakness is that the labels the reveal leans on are off-frame.

**Did the reveal answer the question the learner had?** Mostly: "why only 7?" is answered by E unfilled and by the filled stretch ending at I. The "why 10 joined but 7 reached" step is in the text, not on the frame.

**Top finding:** reveal text names CORE/IN/OUT but the reveal frame crops those labels off; plus the title gives away the answer's direction.

**Minor:** step 1 confirms the guess instead of testing it; 12 per-page steps in the transcript are the script's, not a burden on a human.

**Verdict for v2 (before checking earlier findings): revise (minor, one visual crop fix). Not a hard block.**
