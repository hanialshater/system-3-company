# Learner review: median-without-sorting
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult beginner who knows what a median is (middle value of a sorted list); has never seen quicksort or a pivot.
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 5 frames, phone viewport)

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: "After each split you: split both sides / keep only the side holding the median / keep the bigger side." The hook has already said "Or throw half away each time", which points straight at option 2. The question is also procedural (what should the method do?) rather than a prediction of an outcome; a beginner picks the option that sounds like the hook.
- Explore: "Split" with 7 as pivot is clear: "7 landed in position 7. Keep the side with position 5." The dashed box over position 5 is a good anchor. 9 and 8 grey out when I keep the left side. "Do the rest" then runs 3 splits at once and ends "5 landed in position 5: the median."
- Where understanding first broke: the payoff. The reveal says I used 17 comparisons and sorting "must ... 18 comparisons here". Saving one comparison out of 18 does not feel like a reason to have a different method; it reads as "same thing". The 18 was never shown on the stage either (I never ran the sort). The convincing numbers (3,465 vs 11,003 on 1,000 cards) are told only.
- And the final row reads "1 2 3 4 5 6 7 9 8": nearly sorted. To my eye the lesson sorted the cards, which is the title's opposite.
- "Do the rest" hides the three later splits, so I did not see which sides were thrown away after the first one ("dropped: 9, 1, 8, 3, 6" is a list, not an experience).
- Phone: reveal fully visible. Badge 70S. Trust link 4/5 claims checked.
- Reveal earned: "the other side can go" yes for the first split; "much cheaper than sorting" no: shown as 17 vs 18 and otherwise told.

Fixes (owner: builder):
1. Show the cost comparison where it is visible: a bigger deal (e.g. 15 or 25 cards) or a side counter "sorting would have done N comparisons" that the learner can run.
2. Reword the hook so it does not state the method.
3. Let "Do the rest" step one split at a time so each discarded side is seen.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Objective: discard the side without the median; 4 splits, 17 comparisons; saving grows with n. The first two are shown; "saving grows" rests on told numbers, and on the stage the saving is 1 comparison.
- The 1,000-card figure (3,465) is disputed by the fact-checker as about 4% high; that number is the only evidence the learner gets for the lesson's main payoff, so it matters twice.
- 4 other claims verified by others; I cannot vouch for them.

Verdict: revise
