## 1. From the artifact (fresh)

- Predict: "From S, the road straight to G costs 10. The other way, S to A to B to G, costs 2 per road. What is the cheapest total cost?" Options 10 / 6 / 3. The question hands me both route costs: 10, and 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 in my head. An adult who can add will answer 6. 10 tempts only a reader who skims, and 3 is not a plausible slip. The prediction tests reading, not the idea.
