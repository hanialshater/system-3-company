## Findings (written from the walkthrough before reading the brief or build.md)

Path walked: chose "about 78 °C"; tapped Pull one out, O to the middle; opened reveal.

1. **The prediction is genuine.** "Same nine atoms, same mass" makes 78 °C the natural answer; 35 °C and −24 °C are spaced so that "a bit lower" and "much lower" are both available.
2. **The pull is a good move.** Pulling one ethanol out of the ring visibly breaks 2 pink dashes ("pull broke 2"); with the O moved to the middle the ring has no dashes and the pull breaks 0. The two readouts side by side are clear.
3. **Where the picture lost me: the molecules do not show nine atoms.** Each ethanol is drawn as a red O, two grey balls and one small white ball; each ether as grey–red–grey with no white at all. Only "Red: O" is in the legend. So I see 4 atoms and 3 atoms, not nine, and the ether appears to have no hydrogen anywhere. "No H sits on oxygen" then reads as "it has no H", which is wrong (it has six). Labelling grey = C (with its H's) and white = H on O, or drawing the H's faintly, would fix it.
4. **"0 dashes, nothing to break" overstates it.** If nothing held ether molecules together, it would not be a liquid at any temperature, yet it is one below −24 °C. A learner who thinks this through will be puzzled, and a learner who does not will leave with "no hydrogen bonds = no attraction". The reveal needs "only a weaker grip" instead of "nothing".
5. The step from my boundary (O pulls electrons) to the hydrogen bond (the H left exposed on O is drawn to a neighbour's O) is never said in words; the dashes do it visually, and I can just about make the link. Not blocking.
6. Reveal fits: 78 °C, −24 °C, 6 dashes, broke 2, 0 dashes are all on the stage. Trust link "0/5 claims checked" (fact-checker's lane).
