## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess; the hook "Does the epidemic stop when it gets there?" leans toward "no" because the headline already says "sails past the line". "R0" and "herd immunity" are used without definition; "needs 67% immune" is taken on faith. The question also says "no one immune", which a beginner may find at odds with "needs 67% immune".
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal's second paragraph, "The threshold is where each case starts infecting fewer than one new person". That is the reproduction-number idea, never drawn on the stage. The first paragraph is fine.
- What was SHOWN: Peak (R0 3) day 19: 65% had it, 30% sick now. Day 100: 94% have had it, 0% sick. R0 2 gives 80% (threshold 50%), R0 4 gives 98% (threshold 75%). The reveal's numbers (65, 30, 94, day 19) all appear. The dot grid with the dashed line makes "overshoot" visible: at day 100 the green passes the dashed region. The R0 buttons do the "higher R0, less left" job.
- Small mismatch: reveal says "the peak comes exactly when it is crossed" yet the peak readout is 65% against 67%; "just about" covers it but a careful learner notices.
- The "sick now" red dots are hard to see in the screenshots I opened; the 30% sick at the peak is read from the caption, not the picture. I did not open the peak screenshot; the day 100 and R0 4 states carry the point.
- The day slider at 98 vs 100 shows nothing different; the learner can drift into a useless step.
- Locked screenshot clips the R0 buttons.
- Reveal earned: yes for "does not stop at the line"; the reason is told.
- Problems: (1) R0 and herd immunity undefined; (2) hook leaks; (3) "fewer than one new person" unearned; (4) 65% vs "exactly".
- No FATAL.
