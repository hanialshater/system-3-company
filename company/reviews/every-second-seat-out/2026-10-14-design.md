## Verdict: revise

The reveal now fits: stage top 154 px, reveal bottom 784 of 844, and the whole circle, the formula lines, the binary and "→ 19 (32 + 9)" are on screen. Putting the binary in the circle's centre is the right call. But the side-by-side layout fails two of the four criteria. The seat labels are under the 10 px floor whenever the learner is working the stage. And at the reveal the circle grows and the row spills past the stage. Fixing either one changes the picture size, so the phone fit has to be measured again. That is why this is a revise and not an approve with changes.

## Required changes (board)

1. **Seat labels ≥ 10 px rendered at 390 px in every phase** (aim for 11). Measure them in explore, not at the reveal. The builder picks the means. Options: a narrower step column (shorter labels such as "Next", "To a power of 2", "To the end"); seat labels at 15 to 16 units (the ring has about 22 units per seat, so check two-digit labels for collisions); or less inner margin.
2. **The picture keeps one width across phases, and nothing leaves the stage at the reveal.** Wrap the svg in a flex child (`flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 0`) so the kit's reveal rule cannot grow it. Then re-measure the reveal fit at 390×844 and update Pre-flight 7 with px: the crop, the label px in explore and at the reveal, and the stage scrollWidth equal to clientWidth.

## Recommended (not blocking)

- In the finished circle, make "survivor: 19" the large line and drop "Left: 1", which says the same thing. Keep the binary at ≥ 11 px.
- On desktop the step-button labels wrap onto two lines at 104 px wide. That is acceptable, but a column of single-line labels would read faster.

## Kit observation (routed to platform-engineer)

`.lesson[data-phase="reveal"] .stage .stage-svg { flex: none }` (src/app/globals.css) makes an svg that is a flex item in a horizontal row take its natural width at the reveal. That is +32 px here and +22 px in a-star-heads-for-goal. A lesson-side wrapper avoids it, but the kit should not grow a picture past the width it had in explore.

## Builder's account, read after the findings

Pre-flight v4 says "Circle fitted 233/250 px (labels 10 px)". That is a reveal-phase number, taken after the circle had grown. In explore the circle is 218 px and the labels are 9.4 px. Crop 0 px matches what I saw. Cutting the rule line is fine, because the question states the rule.
