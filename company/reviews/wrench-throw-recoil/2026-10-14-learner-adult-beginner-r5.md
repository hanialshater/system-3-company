## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: the hook ("nothing to push on") sets up "Not at all" as the honest beginner answer, and I picked it. 10 m/s (a same-speed recoil) is the other natural error. The locked stage shows the astronaut and wrench with "?" readouts. Nothing leaks.
- Layout: in the predict frame the scene is drawn very small (tiny figures, tiny labels). From explore on it is drawn at full size. It looks like the same locked-state sizing issue I have seen in other lessons.
- Explore: "Throw the wrench" moves the astronaut away from a dashed ghost of where they started ("astronaut moved 0.25 m (drawn 10× larger)"). The momentum bar shows −20 | +20 with "Momentum = mass × speed. Total 0". The readouts give 0.25 m/s and 20 kg·m/s. My "Not at all" is contradicted by the picture. Doubling the wrench mass doubles both, and raising the speed to 14 gives 56 and 0.7. Equal and opposite is visible in the bar.
- Where it is thinnest: "momentum = mass × speed" is introduced as a caption, not built up. The learner can check 2 × 10 = 20 and 20 ÷ 80 = 0.25 from on-stage numbers, but the stage never shows the division. It is first written out in the reveal. That is acceptable.
- "Drawn 10× larger" is honest, but it means the picture's distances are not comparable to the wrench's. A beginner may not notice.
- Reveal: it uses the learner's first and last throws and answers the hook ("You do not need anything to push on, only something to push away").
