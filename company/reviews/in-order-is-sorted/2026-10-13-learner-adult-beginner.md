# Learner review: in-order-is-sorted
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (can follow "smaller left, bigger right"; has never walked a tree; does not know recursion).
Screenshots seen: yes

## From the artifact
- First needed an idea I didn't have: "left side, then the node, then right side" as a recursive rule. I read it as "left child, node, right child" at the root only, which gives none of the options cleanly. The locked stage draws the finished tree, so a beginner who reads the bottom row left to right (1, 4, 7, 9) gets a hint; the sorted option also "looks right" without the walk.
- Shown well: wrong taps get specific feedback ("Not yet: 5's left side comes first", "3 comes before its right side"); each visited node gets a numbered badge and drops into the output strip. The final badge numbers 1..7 laid over the tree make "sorted" visible.
- Told, not shown: after one correct tap, "Finish the walk" completes it, so the recursion (go all the way left, come back up) is watched, not done. Why it must come out sorted is reveal text, but it follows from what I saw.
- Layout: on the locked frame the "Start again" button is cut off at the bottom of the stage; on the phone reveal the last line ("unfolds it") is clipped below the card.

## Fix suggested
Require 3 hand taps (1, 3, 4) before "Finish the walk" unlocks so the come-back-up step is done once by the learner; fix the two clipped elements.
