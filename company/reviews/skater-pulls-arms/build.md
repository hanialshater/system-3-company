# Build note: skater-pulls-arms

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-13 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: top view of the skater on a ring of ice with a fixed tick, turning at real speed (1, 1.5 or 3 turns per second). An arms slider with three stops (out, halfway, tucked) sets I = 3.0, 2.0, 1.0 kg·m² (illustrative, labelled). The caption shows the product live ("I x spin = 1.0 x 3.0 = 3.0"), which is the idea: the product never moves.
- Term before definition guard: "moment of inertia" is glossed in the question ("how spread out her mass is around the spin axis") and on the locked stage; "angular momentum" is named only in the reveal, after the learner has watched the product stay at 3.0.
- Earned reveal: milestone 'tucked'. QA path: two presses go out -> halfway (1.5 turns/s) -> tucked (3 turns/s), the question's state. The reveal quotes 3 turns/s and 3.0, and 1.5 turns/s only if halfway was visited.
- Numbers (node): 3.0 / I: 1, 1.5, 3 turns per second; product 3.0 at every stop. Kinetic energy (59.2 -> 177.7 J) left out of the lesson to keep one idea; it is in the brief if a reviewer wants it.
- Departure from the brief: three stops rather than a continuous slider, so the question's state is exact and on the QA path.
- After screenshots: moved the caption chip to the bottom (it overlapped the tick) and the I label to the top right.
