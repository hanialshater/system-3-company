# Build note: twenty-cultures-twenty-plates (demo-builder, wave-i-life, 2026-10-14)

Brief: `company/domains/briefs/life.genetics-evolution.md`, spare-capacity batch item 5. Status draft, version 1. Claims c1-c5 unverified. c4 (Luria and Delbrück 1943) needs a human source check. If it fails, cut the second reveal paragraph.

- Stage: two rows of 20 plate bars (linear to 40; a taller bar is drawn red and broken, with its number printed). Row titles carry the readouts: "Random, while growing: k empty, max m" and "Made by the drug: k empty, lo to hi". For a broken bar a note names its first mutation's division ("128: one mutation at division 13, copied"). Locked: 20 empty plates per row, no titles, no bars.
- Controls in DOM order: "New seed (now s)", then the chips "Random, while growing" and "Made by the drug". Each chip runs its rule on the current seed, fills its row and then stays active (disabled). New seed is disabled until a rule has run. After that it advances the seed and re-runs every row already run.
- Departure from the brief: the brief has two chips plus Run. Here each chip runs its own row, so both rows stay on screen together and the reveal quotes what is drawn. New seed comes first in the DOM, but it is disabled on the QA path, so QA runs seed 1 (the start seed, not chosen). Seed 2 has no big jackpot (8 empty, max 6), and the reveal has a branch for that case ("no big jackpot this time").
- Model: Poisson events per division round (Knuth sampler, mulberry32). Growing seed 1 reproduces the brief's 20 counts exactly. The drug row uses its own mulberry32(seed) stream: seed 1 gives 5 to 15 (the brief said 6 to 15 with a different stream).
- Milestones: "growing", "drug".
