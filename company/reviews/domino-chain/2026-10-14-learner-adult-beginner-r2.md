## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: "The first domino falls, and any falling domino knocks over the next. Which dominoes fall?" Only the first few / All of them / Only the even ones. With both premises stated, "All of them" is close to a reading exercise; "Only the even ones" is not a belief anyone holds. Most learners will be right and feel no tension. For a logic lesson that is partly by design (the conclusion follows from the premises), but the hook's real question, "how could we know the millionth falls?", is sharper than the question asked.
- Explore: "Push domino 1": all 12 fall, "The row could go on forever." "Move domino 6 out of reach": 1 to 5 fall, 6 to 12 stand, with 6 in red. The broken link is the most useful part: it shows the second premise doing the work. Good.
- Reveal: 12 of 12 and the 6-out case both seen. No unvisited values. "Dominoes picture it; they do not prove it" is an honest and important closing line.
- Phone clipping (the reason for round 2): fixed. Full reveal and trust link visible.
- Where understanding first broke: nowhere; the risk is under-challenge, not confusion.
- Reveal earned: yes for "two facts are enough"; the formal statement of induction told.

Fixes (owner: builder, optional): ask about the millionth domino, with a "you would have to check" option ("Can we be sure the millionth falls? Yes, from the two facts / Only by checking / No, too far"), so the misconception is on the table.
