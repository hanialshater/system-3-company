# Build note: flour-on-the-moon

builder: demo-builder · 2026-10-12 · for evaluators to read after their findings

- Design intent: a spring scale (newton scale) and a balance against a 1 kg mass in one row, as the brief asks: the spring changes, the balance stays level and wobbles back to level on each world change.
- Earned reveal: unlocks only after the Moon has been visited (the reveal quotes the Moon reading). The current world's button is disabled so no control is a no-op.
- After screenshots: shrank the spring scale so Jupiter's 24.79 N stretch fits on a phone without clipping the bag.
