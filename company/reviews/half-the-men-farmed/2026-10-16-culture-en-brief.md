## Findings

- F5 / P2 FIX (the main one). The brief's held sentence ("Enslaved people, most of whom worked on farms, were not recorded with occupations, so the true 1850 farm share was higher") must not be cut silently, and it must not stay as drafted. The note found the source: Hacker et al. 2025 (PMC13166968, opened in full): enslaved people were enumerated on a separate schedule with no occupation. The 13.8% (about one in seven) is from Nelson et al. 2025 (PMC12629631). Two parts of the draft are not in any opened source: "most of whom worked on farms" and "the true 1850 farm share was higher" (an inference, direction only). Cut both. The sentence is stronger without them: it says whose lives the record left out and does not guess what they did.
- N2 / P2 FIX. A second omission the brief does not name: "Indians not taxed" are outside the data (PMC12629631 note 1). One clause, sourced, if the word cap allows (it does: the reveal below is about 83 words).
- N4 FIX. The 1850 bar covers free men with a recorded job; 1900 onward covers all men with one. A learner reads "51% down to 2%" as one series. The 1850 readout must say what it covers, and the limit sentence says so. Do not put the 1860 to 1870 inference (note section 3) on the stage.
- P4 / L1 PASS with a condition. The source's term "Slave Inhabitants" appears only in quotation marks as the schedule's name, and the stage uses "enslaved people". The care line (no stage element counts them) holds: "about one in seven Americans" is in the reveal, not on the stage as a bar or icon. If cm-history reads even that as using enslaved people as a statistic, the fallback wording is "left them out entirely".
- F2 PASS. No policy or moral conclusion.
- Title FIX. "Half the men farmed" drops the qualifier "with a recorded job". The stage question has it; the title and hook must too.
- N5 PASS. The prediction is a share, a shape; the lesson is about the record's coverage.
- Women (note section 6 item 5): "(52,043 women)" is ambiguous; it can be read as "few women worked". Say the census recorded jobs for few women, and that this is the record, not the work.
- I1/I2/I5 PASS. No imagery planned.
- R-list: none hit. R5 would be hit if the omission sentence were cut; that is the reason it is mandatory.
