## 1. From the artifact

Example, harm 0.25: 2.0 new cases a day for 0.8 days, so R0 = 1.6"), so I can picture the trade-off. The options are 0.25 / 1 / 4. The hook ("faster but for fewer days") still leans toward "middle", so a learner who games the option shape will guess v = 1, but the misconception (germs end up harmless or deadly) still has a pull. The locked plot has axes only.
- Explore: each harm button plots a point, and the caption gives its rate and duration: 2.0 × 0.8 = 1.6, 4.0 × 0.5 = 2.0, 8.0 × 0.2 = 1.6. After three points a dashed full curve appears. The slider to 8 adds 11.3 × 0.1 = 1.3. The rise-then-fall is on stage, and so are the two factors behind it.

- Minor: "harm v adds to daily removal of carriers" is jargon (removal = recovery or death, never said). "0.1 days" of sickness reads oddly; a toy unit worth one word.
- Minor layout: the plot is still small and the axis labels tiny on phone; empty band under the slider in explore frames.
