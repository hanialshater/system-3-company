## From the artifact
- First needed an idea I didn't have: the question itself. "Harm v removes carriers at v per day, on top of 1 per day recovery, and it spreads 4 x √v new cases per carrier per day" is a formula in prose; I could not turn it into an expectation. I fell back on "middle option", which the hook already suggests ("faster spread but shorter infections"). The prediction is a guess by option shape, not by intuition about germs.
- Shown well: the stage caption is far clearer than the question: "Harm 1: spreads 4.0 cases a day for 0.5 days. R0 = 2.0." Seeing "cases a day" x "days" for each harm makes the trade-off concrete, and the plotted points rise then fall (1.6, 2.0, 1.6, 1.3).
- Told, not shown: the product rule is in the caption numbers, so fine. "Real germs vary and the theory is debated" is a good caveat.
- Layout: the plot is small on phone, axis labels tiny ("harm v (log scale)"); only four points, joined by straight lines, so the peak's shape is coarse. Large empty band below the slider.

## Fix suggested
Rewrite the question in the caption's language ("A deadlier germ infects more people per day but its carriers are sick for fewer days. Harm 0.25: 2 cases/day for 0.8 days ...") or show the caption pattern for one harm before the question; draw the full curve after the three points are plotted.
