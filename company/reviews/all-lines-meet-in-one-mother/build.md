- Intent, as specced: today's 50 women along the bottom; lines drawn upward on a square-root axis (ticks 0, 5, 20, 50, 100, label "√ scale"); each line's x is the mean x of its present-day descendants (no crossings); join dots; a one-time "same mother" label at 1–2 back; at the meeting generation a row of 50 (one filled "common mother", 49 hollow, "+ 49 others"); readout "n back: k lines" and "seed 7004"; after "Run 1,000 families" a 20-bin histogram (0–400) with a solid mean tick and a dashed red "yours" tick, heading "1,000 runs: all met. Mean 96". Controls in DOM order: slider "Generations back" 0–120, "Back 10 generations", "Back until one line" (replays the climb in ~380 ms; instant under reduced motion; disabled at the meeting point), "Run 1,000 families" (disabled after). No reset, no new family.
- Model: haploid Wright–Fisher, N = 50; each generation every distinct line draws floor(rand × 50) from mulberry32(seed). The tree (family) and the counter (meetTime) use identical draws, so seed 7004 gives T = 99 in both. Line identities are tracked to draw joins.
- **Disclosed seed 7004** (claim c1): chosen because its meeting time is near the mean and fits the axis; the 1,000 runs use seeds 7001–8000 as briefed.
- Re-run via transpile-only harness on Demo.tsx, 2026-10-14: seed 7004 lines 50 → 32 (1) → 23 (2) → 9 (12) → 3 (50) → 2 (89) → 1 (99). 1,000 runs: all met, mean 96.03, median 84, min 18, max 371, p10–p90 44–169. Exact expectation (Markov chain, computed in the client by exactExpectation): 96.97. 2N(1 − 1/N) = 98.
- **"About 97", with 98 called an approximation**: the reveal says "Theory for this model expects about 97; the formula 2N(1 − 1/N) = 98 is an approximation" (claim c2, per the CM's edit). The 97 is computed, not typed.
- **Cites same-genes-different-drift in the reveal**: last sentence, "It is the drift from 'Nobody wins, one colour takes over', run backward" (that lesson's title), claim c5 with source life.same-genes-different-drift.c1. Named in text, not linked.
