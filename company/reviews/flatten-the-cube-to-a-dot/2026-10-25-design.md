## Rulings on the three open points

### R1. The probe's "back" FAIL is the probe's problem, not the lesson's

My probe waits 3 s after every press before it fingerprints a card, then goes back through the trail (3 s settle) and forward again. At both 390×844 and 1280×800, cards 1, 2 and 3 are identical in text and picture before leaving and after returning (`probe.out`, `settledBack`: all `same: true`). State is kept. The sequence probe samples about 300 ms after a press, inside the spec'd 700 ms flatten and 2.4 s playback, so it compares a moving frame with a settled one.

- W-2047 stands as filed. Fix it by settling (read until two fingerprints taken about 200 ms apart match, with a ceiling), not by emulating reduced motion: reduced motion would stop testing the path most learners take.
- **Not waived.** Lane M step 4 makes the probe a gate. The gate clears when the fixed probe passes "back" on this lesson, not on my probe's word. W-2047 is with design-engineer, a parked role; W-2052 re-owns it.
- The "four-cards" FAIL at 390 is a hypothetical fourth step (W-601's question) and does not apply to a three-card lesson.

### R2. "Next: Shrink" without flattening is the lesson's problem: D1, required

Path: commit, Next, Next, then Remove an edge and Remove the rest (`phone-skip-flatten.png`). Card 3 shows the flat drawing and `8 − 12 + 5 = 1` that the learner never made. They can shrink it to the dot, the Shrink step gets its tick and trace, "+ the top face = 2" appears (a top face they never took off), and the reveal stays locked on "Try it first". Nothing on the stage says that step 2 is missing; only the trail's missing tick does, at 11 px. That is a dead end at the lesson's payoff, and it breaks the causal chain the lesson exists to show: the 1 is only meaningful if the learner took the face off.

The builder saw this and left it "as in euclid" (build.md); that is not a reason here. In euclid the cards do not depend on each other's results. In this lesson card 3 starts from card 2's result.

**D1.** Until `flat` is true, the Shrink card is inert and says why:
- the picture is card 2's starting cube (`FlattenPicture` at k = 0), the readout `8 − 12 + 6 = 2` with key "faces";
- the change line reads **"Take the top off first"** (4 words; the busiest frame stays at 21 since the change line, strip label and end line are empty then);
- both "Remove an edge" and "Remove the rest" are disabled.

The QA path is unchanged (it presses "Take the top off" before "Next: Shrink"), so no control index moves. Do not gate the kit's Next in this lesson; the format-level answer is W-2059.

The same dead end exists more mildly for a skipped Slice: card 2 does not depend on it, so the stage is honest, but the learner ends with a locked reveal and only the trail to tell them why. That is a format question, not this lesson's: W-2059 (interaction-designer, with a sequence-probe "skip" check).

### R3. The stage is dark under both colour schemes: not the lesson's problem; nothing to fix

The single-lesson page (`.single`) and the feed (`.feed-wrap`) set the dark tokens in either OS scheme (`src/app/globals.css`, "Immersive feed chrome", commit `896905545`, 2026-10-19). My probe read the stage's computed tokens under `colorScheme: light` and `dark`: identical (`--bg #08090b`, `--fg #f7f7f8`, `--warn #f6c453`). The lesson uses theme variables only. With the light values forced onto `.single`, every card reads, and `--warn` (#7d5200) carries the changed numbers and the change line (`phone-lighttokens-card2.png`, `phone-lighttokens-card3.png`). So "it must read in light and dark" (demo-standard) holds for the day the product shows a light surface. Whether it should is a platform and founder question, not a finding against this lesson.

## Lane manager's answer to the org-health audit (P2, warm makers)

Question: lane M ran curator, then interaction-designer, then builder as three cold hand-offs (plus a back-hop to the curator), while PRINCIPLES.md P2 prefers one warm maker per item with independent checks at the end. What would I change for the next lane M item? **A proposal only; no gate changes, and the flow is the founder's (audit G1, F-52 via W-2054).**

What this item shows from the review side:
- The three makers each did their part faithfully, and the defect that matters (D1) fell between them. The spec did not say card 3 depends on card 2. The builder noticed and deferred to the spec ("as in euclid"). Nobody owned the whole stage.
- The pre-build spec check that was supposed to catch it never ran (O4). A hop that exists on paper and not in practice costs a hand-off and buys nothing.
- The useful part of the spec step (the 60 s time budget, the removal order with a corner on press 3, the end-to-end count script) is maker work that one builder can do in its own loop.

Proposal for the next lane M item (agrees with the audit's G1, with three changes from me):
1. **Curator brief as now**, then **one demo-builder session owns spec, mockup and build** in one context. The spec and mockup are committed with the build as artifacts. It may edit a claim's source or wording, which leaves the claim unverified, with a note. No back-hop to the curator.
2. **The maker's own loop covers every reachable path, not only the QA path.** Before handing off, the builder walks the skip path (Next before each card's milestones), the reveal mid-animation, and back navigation after motion settles. It uses `company/reviews/flatten-the-cube-to-a-dot/design-2026-10-25/probe.mjs` as a starting point until the sequence probe has a "skip" check (W-2059). This is what would have caught D1 without an extra role.
3. **Independent checks once, at the end, artifact first:** interaction-designer as checker (interaction review of the spec and the built stage), qa-tester with the sequence probe, fact-checker, learner-evaluator, and, for the first 3, my design review and the pedagogy-critic pairwise. My review is not independent of the lane (I manage it), so it stays a craft review; the pairwise and the learner walk stay the gates (my consultation, restructuring decision).
4. **Twin first.** A lane M item is not dispatched to a builder until its single-card twin is published or queued in the same batch. Otherwise step 7 stalls after the build, as it does here.
5. One revision round by the same builder, from its own build.md.

For the next item (the second of the first 3), I would run steps 1-5 as a trial batch of one only after the founder approves G1. Until then the approved flow runs as written, and I ask the builder in the dispatch to do point 2 inside its own step, which needs no approval.
