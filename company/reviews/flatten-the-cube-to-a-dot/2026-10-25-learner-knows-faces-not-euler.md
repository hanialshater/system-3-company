## Findings (written from the walkthrough and probes before reading build.md or meta.json)

1. **Where understanding first broke: card 2, "Take the top off".** Up to here everything is counting I can do. Then one tap turns the box into a square inside a square. The frames (J) show a lid lifting and the box opening out, but it is over in about 300 ms, there is no replay, and nothing says *why* a box with one face missing may be stretched flat without edges crossing. At the same moment the label under the third number changes from "faces" to "regions" with no word on what a region is. I can follow it because the five regions are shaded and the outside is not, and "top face off: 1 short" plus the little square sitting aside tells me where the missing 1 went. So I follow the cube. What I lack is the idea that this flattening is always possible. The lesson needs that idea and never gives it.
2. **The prediction felt real.** The question hands me 8 − 12 + 6 = 2, so I only have to guess what slicing does. "Less than 2" was my honest guess ("you're cutting a corner away"). Card 1 answers it at once: 10 − 15 + 7 = 2, with "+2 corners, +3 edges, +1 face" in gold. "+2 corners" reconciles only if I notice that the old corner is gone and three new ones appear. The triangle drawing shows this; the caption does not say it. Small, but the stage line and the picture disagree at first glance (I count 3 new corners and the caption says +2).
3. **Card 3 works for me on the cube.** One removal at a time (path B, 12 taps) shows "−1 edge, −1 region" and then "−1 edge, −1 corner", and the row of 1s fills up. I believe the total holds for each removal I watched. The lesson picks each edge for me. I never learn that removing *some* edge (one that would split the drawing in two) would break the rule. So "each edge you removed took a region or a corner with it" is true of what I saw, but not yet something I could do myself on a pyramid.
4. **Whether the reveal answered my question.** The hook asks "Every convex solid too?" The reveal's answer is a sentence: "Any convex solid can be flattened and shrunk the same way, so … always 2." For the cube, the reveal is a fair summary of what I did. For "every solid", it is an assertion resting on the two ideas I lacked in findings 1 and 3: any solid with a face removed flattens, and there is always a safe edge to remove. I leave able to retell the cube argument and trusting the generalisation, not seeing it. For a 60-second card that may be acceptable. Human learners should be asked "would this work on a pyramid?", the question I could not answer.
5. **Paths the walkthrough skips. None is a dead end, two are rough.**
   - "Next: Flatten" and "Next: Shrink" are always enabled (C). Tapping straight through puts me on card 3 with the flat drawing and "8 − 12 + 5 = 1". I never saw the top come off, and the "top face off: 1 short" line is absent. I can finish shrinking (G): Shrink is ticked, "+ the top face = 2" shows, and the reveal stays "Try it first". The panel only repeats "Work through the three cards". What gets me out is that cards 1 and 2 have no tick and their tabs become tappable (I). Going back to Slice, then Flatten, unlocks the reveal. That is recoverable, but the panel never names the missing card.
   - The tabs are disabled until I have reached a card via Next (E). "Slice a corner" works once, then disables. Both are fine.
   - "Remove the rest" is disabled until one edge has been removed (A, D: tapping it first does nothing). There is no hint why. On the QA path, "Remove the rest" right after one removal plays the remaining 11 as an animation. "Show me why" unlocks the moment it is tapped (H: at 150 ms the stage still reads 8 − 10 + 3). So I can open the reveal, which says "down to a single dot", before the dot exists. The animation finishes behind the reveal (H after 5 s: 1 − 0 + 0 = 1, "+ the top face = 2"), so the stage ends where the reveal says it does.
   - Wrong predictions get "Not quite: it was 'still 2'" (options 1 and 3), and the right one gets "Your prediction held." The explanation is the same for all three. For "more than 2" (A) nothing speaks to why someone might think slicing *adds*. That is acceptable here, because card 1's +2/+3/+1 answers either wrong guess.
6. **Text.** Mid-animation and at the end the stage reads "1 corners", "1 regions", and the picture text says "5 corners, 5 edges, 1 regions". This is a small pluralisation slip, visible under the big numbers.

Verdict reason: no finding stops this learner on the cube. The first idea the lesson needs and does not give comes at card 2 (that a solid with one face off can always be drawn flat; the flatten is a sub-second animation with no replay, and "faces" becomes "regions" unexplained). It matters only for the reveal's "every convex solid" step, which this learner takes on trust. These are notes and hypotheses for human learners, not blockers (findings 1, 3, 4). The rough paths in finding 5 recover.

## After reading build.md / meta.json

Verdict and findings above were written before opening either file. Read at HEAD 02f0f4888; the lesson files there are unchanged from the walked build (d5ed24fa4), meta.json version 1.

- **Intent vs experience.** The objective says the learner "can say why corners − edges + faces is 2 for any convex solid", and the brief's knowledgeBoundary says the argument is "shown on the cube". My experience matches the second statement better than the first. I could retell the cube argument. For "any solid" I hold c6 on trust (findings 1, 3, 4). Human-learner question: "would this work on a pyramid?" That is the test of whether the objective is met.
- **Misconception "the 2 is a coincidence of the cube".** Card 1 answers it for one other solid, the sliced cube. Cards 2-3 answer it only by assertion in the reveal.
- **Finding 5, skipping cards.** build.md states it on purpose: "Nothing gates 'Next: Shrink' on flattening … the reveal still needs all 5 milestones". So the behaviour is by design. My objection is narrower: once the learner has skipped, the panel never names which card is missing (path G). The tick marks are the only cue. This is the same class as the earlier 'Try it first' kit note in my memory (company/reviews/the-swap-that-goes-in-circles/2026-10-16-learner-knows-preference-r2.md), so it is kit-owned, not this lesson's.
- **Reveal opening before the dot.** build.md says "once the phase is reveal, the presentation jumps to the committed state, so the reveal always sees the dot". That fits what I saw: H at 150 ms had the reveal unlocked while the stage was mid-play, and the final stage was 1 − 0 + 0 = 1. I did not capture the frame right after the reveal tap, so "jumps" is the builder's account, not my observation.
- **Flatten timing.** build.md: 250 ms lift, then 450 ms stretch. My frames (J) show the lift and the open box within 300 ms. Either way there is no replay, and that is finding 1's note.
- **"1 corners / 1 regions".** Not mentioned in build.md. It is a small text fix for the builder.
