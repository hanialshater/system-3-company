## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess, but weakened by the question. It says the salt "absorbs 25.7 kJ of heat". A learner who takes "absorbs" to mean "takes from the water" can pick "colder" without any chemistry; "the same temperature" is a weak decoy and "11 °C warmer" is the fire-brain slip. The size (11 °C) cannot be worked out by a beginner, since specific heat is not given. I picked 11 °C colder and the lesson said "Your prediction held", which for a guess driven by the word "absorbs" feels earned only by the wording.
- What I saw: tapping "Add a scoop" shows a red arrow "heat out of water", little crystals, and the thermometer going from 20.0 to 17.0 °C; "Add all" gives 9.4 °C with 1 mol in. The thermometer dropping is shown, in steps, and "Empty" restores 20.0. Good.
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal's sum, "25,700 J / (580 g x 4.18 J per g per °C) is about 10.6 °C". The 580 g (500 g water + 80 g salt) and 4.18 J per g per °C (specific heat) never appear on the stage; neither term is explained. The stage shows the results (17.0, 9.4) but not the energy count behind them, so the calculation is told, not shown. Also the stage's 9.4 °C drop (10.6) is described as "about 11 °C", fine.
- A display detail: the "heat out of water" label overlaps the beaker wall in the scoop frames.
- The line "That is how an instant cold pack works" is the payoff, told in one sentence; the cold-pack misconception ("stores cold") is not addressed in the artifact except by "takes 25.7 kJ ... from its surroundings".
- Reveal earned: the direction yes; the 11 °C only by arithmetic I was handed.
- Problems: (1) "absorbs" in the question leaks the direction; (2) 580 g and 4.18 not on stage, specific heat undefined; (3) label overlap.
