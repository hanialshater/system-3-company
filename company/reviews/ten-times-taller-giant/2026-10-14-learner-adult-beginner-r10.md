## Findings (written from the walkthrough before reading build.md)

Path walked: chose "The same"; moved Scale to 3×; tapped "Make it 10×", "Legs: same shape" (turns legs thicker); opened reveal.

1. **The prediction is genuine.** "Same shape, same bone" makes "The same" the honest pick for "bigger is stronger"; 1,000 times catches those who think only of weight.
2. **The numbers carry the argument.** At 3×: weight ×27, bone area ×9, load ×3; at 10×: ×1,000, ×100, ×10, with the load bar in red far above "limit (illustrative)". Thicker legs: bone ×1,000, load ×1, back under the limit.
3. **Where the idea I lack first appears: why ×1,000 and ×100.** The lesson's own gap (cube vs square) is shown only as numbers. A learner who spots 27 = 3³ and 9 = 3² gets it; one who does not is left with "the numbers say so". Neither the stage nor the reveal says "weight goes with size cubed, bone area with size squared". The reveal's "only 100× as big in cross-section" states the number, not the rule.
4. **The toggle is labelled with its state.** "Legs: same shape" is the button that makes legs thicker; after tapping it reads "Legs: thicker". The panel says "try thicker legs", so I had to tap a button that said "same shape".
5. **The caption contradicts the picture after the toggle.** In 04-step-3 and 05-reveal the caption still reads "10×, same shape: weight ×1,000, bone ×100, load ×10" while the figure has thicker legs and the cards say "BONE AREA ×1,000 · LOAD ×1". It is the record of the earlier case, but it reads as a label for what is on screen.
6. At reveal the red 10× load bar is gone (thicker legs are current), so "well past the illustrative limit" is backed by the caption, not the bar. "Where it stops" (big mammals stand more upright) is a good limit.
7. Trust link reads "0/5 claims checked" (fact-checker's lane).

Verdict reason: the outcome (×10 load per cm²) and the fix (thicker legs) are seen; the cube/square rule is shown in numbers but never named, which is the main fix.
