# Learner review (round 2): south-east-north-walk
lesson version: v1
boundary: an adult beginner who knows the classic "walk south, east, north, shoot a bear" riddle answer (North Pole) and nothing more.
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 6 frames, phone viewport). Re-review after the kit fix for clipped reveals (W-135); written fresh from this walkthrough.

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: how many other places do the same walk and return? None / exactly one more / infinitely many. I chose None (the riddle answer). A genuine belief test.
- Explore: preset starts near the South Pole. 1.159 km: "east leg on a 1.00 km circle", 1.00 laps, back at the start. 1.080 km: 2 laps. 1.053 km: 3 laps. Slider to 1.055 km: 2.89 laps, "off by 691 m" with a red dashed miss line. The pattern 1, 2, 3 laps makes "and so on" believable. Good.
- Readability on phone: the plan view is drawn to scale, so the 2- and 3-lap circles are tiny yellow dots at the pole; I cannot see any lapping, only the numbers. The labels inside the plan ("east leg on a 0.33 km circle", "South Pole") are very small text.
- "1.00 km circle" means a circle 1 km round (circumference). A beginner may read it as 1 km across. The reveal says "a circle 1 km round", which helps after the fact.
- The reveal's second idea, "each gives a whole ring of starting points around the pole", is told; every preset start sits at the same spot (straight above the pole), so I never see that any point on the ring works.
- Reveal values: 1.159, 1.080, 1.053 km and the lap counts all seen. No unvisited values.
- Phone clipping (the reason for round 2): fixed. Full reveal and trust link visible. Badge 70S.
- Where understanding first broke: seeing the laps (too small to see); the ring idea told.
- Reveal earned: yes for "infinitely many lap counts"; the ring-of-starts half told.

Fixes (owner: builder, optional): an inset zoom of the pole region so 2 and 3 laps are visible; let the learner drag the start around the ring (or show a faint dashed ring of valid starts) so "a whole ring" is seen; "a circle 1 km round" in the stage label.
