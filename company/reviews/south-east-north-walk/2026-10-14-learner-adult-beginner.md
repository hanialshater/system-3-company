# Learner review: south-east-north-walk
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner (knows walking east follows a circle of latitude, and those circles shrink toward the poles; has never thought about the South Pole case).
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, phone, 6 frames)

## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: that the east leg could be a whole lap of a tiny circle. The stage is meant to show it, and the 1.159 km frame does: the start, a straight south leg, and a yellow 1.00 km circle around the pole. That frame made the idea click.
- Readability: at 1.080 and 1.053 km the "circle" is a dot under the South Pole label; I cannot see 2 or 3 laps. The lap count lives only in the EAST LEG tile. Map labels ("Ice around the South Pole, seen from above. To scale.", "east leg on a 0.50 km circle") are tiny on phone; on the predict frame the whole map is a blank thumbnail.
- The panel says "Try a start that fails" first, but all three preset buttons succeed; only the slider (1.055 km: 2.89 laps, off by 691 m, red dotted gap) shows failure. The failure frame is good; the instruction points to a button that doesn't exist.
- Layout: reveal clipped on phone (W-135), badly. Only "Infinitely many. Start 1.159 km from the South Pole: the south leg leaves you on a circle 1 km round, the..." is visible. The part that answers the question, "Every whole number of laps works, and each gives a whole ring of starting points: infinitely many rings", is cut. The clipped text carries the explanation, so this blocks.
- Also the reveal never tells me why "a ring" (any longitude works), which is half of "infinitely many".
