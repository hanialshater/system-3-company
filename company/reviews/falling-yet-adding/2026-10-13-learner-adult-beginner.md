## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. Hook and question are clear. "About 1,000" is the gut answer ("it is shrinking, so little left"), 9,000 is the surprise. The question is answerable only by guessing or by summing a long list, so it is a pure bet, which is acceptable here. The locked stage shows ten empty circles for "today" and a faint dot grid, with no legend for what a circle or dot is until after Restart ("Each dot below is 100 cases").
- Where understanding first broke: the reveal's "900 + 810 + 729 + ... sums to 900 / 0.1 = 9,000". The formula is not derived on the stage and a beginner cannot see why a never-ending sum stops at 9,000. The picture does carry the point: at day 90 about 90 dots fill the grid (8,999 cases, "9.0 times today's") against the top row of 10 circles for today, so "nine times today" is visible.
- Reveal values vs stage: 8,999 and 9.0 times shown. "At 5% a day it sums to 19,000; at 30% a day, 2,333" are NOT shown. The slider step in the walkthrough (12%) shows no total; the learner is not told to run the days again after moving the slider, so the "slower the fall, the heavier the tail" generalization is told, not shown.
- The "Daily fall" slider seems to reset or do nothing until days are re-run; as a beginner I could think it is broken.
- Terms: "tail" appears first in the reveal. "Toy outbreak" fine.
- Trust badge on the card reads "0/4 claims checked", which a learner would read as the lesson being unchecked.
- Reveal earned: for 9,000 yes (seen); for the slope with fall rate, no.
- Problems: (1) formula unmotivated; (2) 19,000 / 2,333 never seen; (3) slider needs a rerun with no cue; (4) dot legend appears late.
- No FATAL.
