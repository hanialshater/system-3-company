# Learner review: build-a-balanced-mobile
lesson version: v2
boundary: an adult with school-level maths and science, new to the topic (has used a see-saw; has not met turning effect, weight × distance, or centre of mass). Screenshots seen (sheet.png, all 4 frames).

## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. 50 cm is "it balances in the middle", 75 cm the "somewhere between" intuition, 90 cm "close to the lump". The hook lists "the middle, the lump, or elsewhere", which does not leak. The locked stage shows a bare ruler level at 50 with a small arrow for the ruler's own weight, which explains why the bare ruler balances and supports the stem.
- Where understanding first broke: the turn readouts. "LEFT TURN (KG×CM) 0 / RIGHT TURN 5" in step 1 introduces the unit kg×cm and the idea of a "turn" with no definition. It happens to be self-explaining in step 2 (2.5 vs 2.5, level), and a learner can find 75 by watching the tilt flip, so understanding mostly holds. But why LEFT is 0 at a 50 cm pivot (the ruler's own weight sits right over the pivot) needs the tiny arrow, which is hard to see at phone size.
- What was SHOWN is strong: add the lump, the right end drops, slide the pivot, it levels at 75 with 25 cm and 25 cm marked and equal turns 2.5 and 2.5. The 75 answer is found, not told.
- Reveal: "weight × distance" is first named in the reveal, although the numbers support it on the stage. "The balance point is the average position of all the mass, weighted by mass" is told: (0.10×50 + 0.10×100)/0.20 is not shown. "A heavier lump would pull the balance point further toward it" is told, not tried (no lump-weight control).
- Reveal earned: 75 cm yes; the general rule is told but plausible given two equal masses.
- Problems: (1) "turn" and kg×cm unexplained; (2) ruler-weight arrow too small; (3) heavier-lump claim not shown; (4) the slider can be dragged to any pivot, level is found only by closeness, no snap, so "level" might be judged by eye.
- No FATAL.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The brief's learner knows see-saws and that a ruler balances at its middle; the objective (balance at the mass-weighted average, halfway between ruler middle and lump) is found by sliding to 75 and confirmed by 2.5 vs 2.5. Intent and experience match.
- The unknowns torque and centre of mass are named only lightly; "turn" is the lesson's word. Claims verified.

Verdict: launch
