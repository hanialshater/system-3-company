- Explore: the slider and the BEAM readout ("left down", "right down") let me bracket the balance point (right-down at 62.5 cm, left-down at 87.5 cm). The recorded run never shows a "level" state; that may be a driver limit.

The idea that distance times weight decides the turn is never set up by anything I saw; I would nod without following why 25 cm and 25 cm cancel.

- Problems:

  - "BEAM left down" names a side but not whether that is the lump side; with the lump on the right I have to translate each time.
