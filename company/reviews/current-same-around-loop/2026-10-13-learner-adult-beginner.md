## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a genuine guess. "Does the second bulb get the leftovers?" is exactly what an adult believes, and "Less than 0.50 A" is a live option. The option "0 A" is an obvious throwaway. I know what a battery and bulb are, but "ammeter" is only explained by the letter A in a circle and the number; I get that it reads a number.
- Explore: moving the ammeter and the battery slider changes the reading in a way I can follow (6 V -> 0.50 A, 12 V -> 1.00 A). Orange dots on the wire move along the loop, but nothing says what they are.
- Where understanding first broke: "Bulbs take energy, not current." (caption) and then in the reveal "Each identical bulb turned half of the battery's 6 V into light and heat: 3 V each." I do not know what a volt is, so "half of the battery's 6 V" reads as half of some stuff; the difference between "current" and "energy" is exactly what I came to learn, and the caption states it rather than showing it.
- Reveal earned? Partly. I saw the reading stay the same at "bottom wire" and "before bulb 1", but the recorded run never put the ammeter between the bulbs, the exact spot the question asked about. The caption "Same reading at every spot" appears already after my first slide (to the bottom wire), before I had tested more than one spot, so it gives the answer away rather than letting me find it.
- Problems:
  - The reveal says "With the battery at 6.0 V ... 0.50 A" while the screen beside it shows 7.5 V and 0.63 A; the numbers in the text and picture disagree (the run left the battery at 7.5 V).
  - The caption "Same reading at every spot" is a standing statement that is true from the first interaction; it pre-empts the discovery, and it would be shown even if the learner has not tried a second ammeter spot.
  - Volts are used for "energy" ("uses 3.8 V") and the lesson insists current is not used up: the learner must hold three quantities (current, voltage, energy) with no definition of any of them.
  - The orange moving dots are not labelled (charge? current?) so the reveal's "charge has nowhere to pile up or leak away" has no visual hook.
  - No sense of direction or loop: I never see current "going round"; the dots stay near the same positions in the stills.
- FATAL: none found from the artifact.

## 2. After reading the brief

- The brief says the learner does not know the difference between current and energy, and that is the objective; the lesson mainly states that difference in a caption and one reveal paragraph, then relies on "uses 3.8 V" labels the learner cannot interpret.
- The misconception (current is used up) is well chosen and my prediction matched it; the explore evidence (same reading at several spots) counters it, but the between-the-bulbs spot, the one that matters, is not guaranteed by the explore path.

Verdict: revise
