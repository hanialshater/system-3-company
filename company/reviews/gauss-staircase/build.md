# Build note: gauss-staircase (demo-builder, 2026-10-12)

- Intent: a dot staircase 1..n; "Add flipped copy" swings a half-turned copy (it starts rotated 180° exactly on top of the original) into the gap, making an n × (n+1) rectangle.
