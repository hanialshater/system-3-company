# Build note: one-in-a-million-daily (demo-builder, 2026-10-13)
- Intent: a field of 1,000 dots is the city (each dot = population / 1,000). Days light up the events. At 10,000 people nothing happens for months; at 8 million about 8 dots light up every day.
- Gate: 2 milestones, a day at 8 million, then a whole year there (2,946 events, 0 empty days, seed 808 untuned). The population buttons come first so QA reaches 8 million.
- Brief change: buttons (10k / 100k / 1M / 8M) in place of a slider, because a slider's two QA arrow presses would not reach 8 million.
- After screenshots: after a year the stage now says the dots show day 365.
