# Build note: same-genes-different-drift (demo-builder, 2026-10-12)

- Design: five lanes of beetles (Wright-Fisher, haploid, seeded); red is round and green is square, so colour-blind learners can tell them apart. A toggle switches between 20 and 200 beetles per lane.
- Reveal gate: reach generation 60 or later at N = 20, and again at N = 200. Fixed after the first QA run: 'New runs' reset the generation to 0. It now re-rolls in place, and a generation slider replaces the +1 button.
- After screenshots (all): stage SVG capped at 27dvh and the lesson body set to not shrink, because readouts were clipped or text overlapped on the phone; long readout labels shortened so they stay on one line.
