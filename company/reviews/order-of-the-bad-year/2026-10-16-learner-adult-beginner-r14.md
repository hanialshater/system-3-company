1. **Where understanding first broke.** At the reveal's last sentence, mildly: "A withdrawal after the loss comes out of a smaller pot, and that money is not there for the recovery." The withdrawal is $20k either way, so "smaller pot" does not by itself tell me why it hurts. I had to work it out: after the loss, $20k is a quarter of the $80k pot, so a quarter of the money misses the +25% year; in gain-first, the $20k leaves before the loss and so dodges it. The chart shows only year-end balances after the withdrawal (gain first ~$105k, loss first ~$60k at year 1), so the "out of a smaller pot" step (80 → 60) is never on screen.

2. **What the prediction felt like.** A real choice that my prior knowledge pulls the wrong way. From the up-then-down trap I know order does not change a product, so "same either way" is my confident answer, which is what the lesson wants to test.

3. **Whether the reveal answered the question I actually had.** Mostly. My question after step 1 was "why $64k vs $55k?", and the slider to "no money moves" answered the first half by itself: both lines meet at $100k. That control is the best part of the lesson; it isolates the withdrawals as the cause. The second half (why the order then matters) is answered only by the compressed sentence above.

Notes (do not block a first-time learner):
- With no flows both orders end at exactly $100k, the start. Because +25% then −20% happens to multiply to 1, a learner could leave thinking "the gain and loss cancel", which is the opposite of the up-then-down lesson this one builds on. The reveal's "×1.25 then ×0.8 equals ×0.8 then ×1.25" is correct, but nothing says these two returns were chosen to cancel.
- The slider's centre is "no money moves"; the right side presumably adds money in, but nothing invites me to try deposits, which the hook does not mention either.
- The chart's y-scale changes between frames ($50k–$150k shifts position), which makes the two frames harder to compare.

Verdict reason: no finding blocks a first-time learner; the compressed "smaller pot" explanation and the cancelling returns are notes.
