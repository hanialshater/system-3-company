# Learner review: scramble-the-digits
lesson version: v1
boundary: adult beginner; place value and "divisible by" from school; may half-remember the digit-sum trick without knowing why.
Screenshots seen: yes (sheet.png, 6 frames)
Verdict: launch

## 1. From the artifact

- No missing idea at predict. "Sometimes" is the honest beginner answer; "Does the luck hold?" frames it as luck, which is a good tease.
- Explore: tapping two digits swaps them; each time the readouts update: "LEFTOVER ONES 2+7+4+5 = 18" and "÷ 9: 2745 = 9 × 305". Seeing the exact quotient each time is convincing.
- The yellow dot piles under each digit (the "leftover ones") are not explained in words; it took me a moment to see that each pile equals the digit. A one-line label ("left over after the 999s, 99s, 9s") would make the mechanism visible rather than told.
- Told-not-shown: "each digit leaves just itself over. A number's remainder by 9 is its digit sum's" is told, but the "= 999 + 1" labels and dots set it up, so it lands.
- Layout: the stage has a very large empty area below the readouts in every explore frame; the content sits in the top third. The swap instruction alternates between two captions, fine.

## 2. After reading the brief

- Learner field (place value, divisible by 9) matches. Intent delivered.
- Suggested fix (minor): label the dot piles; tighten the stage height.
