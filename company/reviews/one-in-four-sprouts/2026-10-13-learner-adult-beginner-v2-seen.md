## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: a real guess. About 25% is the "1 in 4 per family" confusion, 100% the "four seeds, one in four, so one is white" quota thought, 68% the independent-draws answer. The hook "How likely is a white one among four?" does not leak. The stem says "Two purple plants each carry a hidden white-flower copy", which a rusty adult can follow, though "hidden copy" is jargon-light and unexplained.
- Where understanding first broke: step 1. The caption "Family 1: ww, PP, Pw, wP. 1 white." uses letters ww, PP, Pw, wP that are defined only in step 4's caption ("Each seed gets one copy from each parent: P (purple) or w (white). Two w make a white flower"), which appears after the learner has already clicked New field, with an empty stage. Terms before definition, and the definition arrives in the wrong place. A beginner also cannot tell from the caption why ww is the white one, and why Pw and wP are listed separately.
- The squares: each square on the grid is one family of 4 coloured dots; the dots are small purple blobs with white centres and I could not map which dot in a square is ww. The grid shows 67 white families as outlined squares, but the key to what a square means is not on the stage.
- What was SHOWN works: growing 1, then 10, then all 100 gives running counters (1 of 1, 8 of 11, 67 of 100), and 67 lands next to the learner's 25% guess. The 100% option dies at family 11 (a "ww, PP, PP, ww" family) if the learner notices that some families have none, but "33 with none" only appears at step 3.
- Reveal: "(3/4)^4 ≈ 32%" and "1 − 0.32" are told; not terribly hard for school-level maths, but not shown on the stage. Then "exactly one white is only about 42%" does not match the 49 on the stage (it says "another field would differ"), and "a third get none" matches 33. The 42% never appeared anywhere.
- Reveal earned: the ~68% from the 67, yes; the arithmetic route, moderately.
- Problems: (1) ww/PP/Pw notation used before defined; (2) square-to-family mapping unlabelled; (3) 42% is a number the stage could not produce; (4) "hidden white copy" not explained for newcomers.
- No FATAL.
