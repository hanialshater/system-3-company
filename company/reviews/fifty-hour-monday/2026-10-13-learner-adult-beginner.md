## 1. From the artifact

- Prediction: partly a guess, partly leaked. The hook says "why is it Monday somewhere for much longer than that [24 hours]?", which tells me before I answer that the answer is not 24. "UTC", "UTC+14" and "UTC−12" appear with no explanation of what they mean.
- Where understanding first broke: the stage itself. The picture at Sun 00:00 shows vertical bands over the left half in white and the right half grey, with the two island dots (UTC+14, UTC−12) jammed against the left edge, half cut off. The legend (Sun grey, Mon blue, Tue green, other white) is in small type under the map. I had no idea why the left half is white, nor that the "dots" are islands (only the reveal names Kiritimati and Baker Island; the stage never does).
- What was SHOWN: the two buttons jump straight to the start (Sun 10:00 UTC) and the end (Tue 12:00 UTC), and the readout "MONDAY HAS LASTED 50 h" appears. In the screenshot at the end the whole map is green (Tuesday), with "Monday somewhere? No". I did not see the wave of blue Monday sweeping across the map unless I drag the slider myself; the two buttons give me the endpoints, not the sweep. The "50 h" figure is a total I am told by a counter, not one I derive.
- Reveal vs stage: 50 h, Sun 10:00 UTC and Tue 12:00 UTC match the stage. "Clocks spread over 26 hours, from UTC−12 to UTC+14, so 24 + 26 = 50" is told; the two labelled dots do hint at it, but the sum is not built on the stage. "A date is a wave that takes two days to cross the world" is a good sentence that the stage did not let me watch.
- Terms before definition: UTC, UTC+14, "nominal 15° zones", "equirectangular", and the idea that a date starts at midnight local time. Why UTC+14 and UTC−12 exist (the date line) is never explained.
- Phone legibility: map is small and low-contrast; the pale Sun/Mon/Tue shades are hard to tell apart (Mon blue vs Tue green are both light); legend text tiny. Reveal clipped at the bottom in the screenshot.
- Reveal earned: the number yes, the reason only partly.
- Problems: (1) hook leaks "longer than 24"; (2) UTC and the zone range never defined; (3) wave not visible from the buttons alone; (4) island dots unnamed and cropped at the map edge; (5) pale colours hard to tell apart; (6) 26 h told.
- I did not open an intermediate slider state in the screenshots (only the final-state shots), so I cannot comment on how the blue Monday band looks mid-sweep.
- No FATAL.
