## 1. From the artifact

- No missing idea at predict: "Aa: one copy of each form" is glossed in the question and the legend (filled AA, half-filled Aa, empty aa). "50%" is a believable pull (a hybrid cross gives half hybrids).
- Explore shows the halving clearly: 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 half-filled plants, with the captions "Generation 3: 4 of 32 still hybrid (12.5%). Pure: 14 AA, 14 aa."
- Told-not-shown: the mechanism ("its seed is AA, Aa or aa in a 1 : 2 : 1 split", "a pure plant only makes pure seeds") is only in the reveal. The stage shows field totals, never one hybrid's seeds splitting. A beginner gets the pattern by sight and the why by telling.
- "Shown: the average split" is honest but a beginner may not grasp that a real field with one seed each would wobble.
- Layout: empty aa plants are hollow circles that read as "missing/dead" plants rather than a type; a light fill for aa would help. The reveal stage shows "Start" (all hybrid) because that was the last tap, so the reveal sits next to a picture that contradicts it.
