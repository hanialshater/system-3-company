## Safety boundaries

- Refuse chemical mixing, medical/dosage/diagnosis hacks, electrical work beyond the charter's bulb/breaker boundary, defeated safety devices, and legal/financial recommendations.
- Food, chemicals, heat, electricity, health, children, pets and money retain the safety gate. Arithmetic about costs may compare stipulated quantities; it must not recommend buying, borrowing, investment or legal action.
- Toy models cannot establish safe real-world loads, lifting capacity, furniture fit, drying times, storage conditions, personal memory, concentration or productivity.
- Prepared table: empty tabletop; square/equal legs; suitably gentle continuous floor; all feet touching does not mean level; clear hot drinks before turning. No slope/short-leg/steps guarantee.
- Prepared cord: plain string/cord put away for storage; no powered cable modification or coiled lead in use, and no children or hanging loops.
- Prepared shoe: both bows may loosen; do not imply square bows never fail or that double knots should be abandoned.
- Prepared cloth: no heater/radiator/flame/steam implication; real fabric/care instructions and household conditions bound the model.
- Prepared spacing: study scheduling only; no sleep loss, substances, brain-health framing, medical conditions or personal-retention prediction.
