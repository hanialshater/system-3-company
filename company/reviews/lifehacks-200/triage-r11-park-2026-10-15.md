# Life Hacks r11 triage: lessons parked instead of fixed

owner: cm-lifehacks (Tess) · date: 2026-10-15 · decision: `company/decisions/2026-10-15-lifehacks-r11-fix-plan.md` · board: W-1074, W-1077

**Parked** here means no builder time is spent on the lesson in the r11 fix waves. It stays `review` and holds no publish slot, and it goes first on the list at the next retirement review. Parking is ordering, which is inside my authority. It is not retirement: retiring or merging a lesson happens only at the retirement ritual, on the record. Nothing here deletes a file or changes a status.

The meta `misconception` and `learner.doesNotKnow` of every parked lesson were still rewritten on 2026-10-15. If one is unparked, its boundary is already correct.

**W-1077's other two premise questions, answered.** `undo-then-redo` is parked (section B). `work-back-from-a-deadline` is **kept**, with a new premise: three tasks chained before one deadline. The belief to break is "if each task fits before the deadline on its own, the plan fits". The prediction asks for the latest start of the first task. The options must include deadline − its own duration and deadline − the longest task. Wave 9 of the plan.

**Does each template brief name a belief beginners actually hold?** Often not. 69 Home-template metas said "the two pictured methods are interchangeable", and 69 Study-template metas named the wrong option's label. Both are rewritten now. Each states a belief in words a beginner would use. The plan's per-lesson prediction lines are what make that belief an option on screen.
