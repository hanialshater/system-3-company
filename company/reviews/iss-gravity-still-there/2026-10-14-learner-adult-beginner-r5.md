## 1. From the artifact

- Predict: "0% (none)" is the honest beginner belief, and the hook voices it. Fair options.
- Explore: the to-scale picture does the work. At 400 km the orbit ring almost sits on the Earth's surface and the readout says 88.5%. Further stops (25.0% one radius up, 5.7% GPS, 2.3% geostationary) show the fade only far out. "Gravity is still there" is seen.
- Where it broke: the question I have once I see 88.5% is "then why do they float?". **The answer ("they and the station fall together, round and round") is one told sentence. Nothing on stage falls or moves.** "Floating in orbit is free fall" is one of the two things the brief says I do not know, and it is not earned.
- "Square of distance" is told; 25% at twice the distance from the centre supports it, but the stage does not point that out.
- The gravity bar under the stage is small and unlabelled; the reveal refers to "the gravity bar" I barely noticed.
