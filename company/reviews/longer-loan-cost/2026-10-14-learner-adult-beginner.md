## 1. From the artifact

- Where I first needed an idea I didn't have: the prediction. "$95 a month less" and "how much more overall" pulled me to "about $200" (two extra years of small interest). Good trap.
- Explore is a single slider move, 5 → 7 years. The bar shows "$20,000 borrowed" in grey plus a red interest block, with a dashed box marking the 5-year total. The red block poking out past the dashed box is the extra cost. Tiles: TOTAL INTEREST $4,542, VS 5 YEARS +$1,343. It lands.
- Label collision: "5-year total" (dashed box, top right) sits directly above "interest $4,542" (red, bottom right). I first read it as "5-year total interest $4,542", the wrong way round. The 5-year interest ($3,199) never appears on the stage; it is only in the reveal.
- Told-not-shown: "keeps being charged 0.5%" brings in a monthly rate never shown (6% ÷ 12 is left for me to work out). The mechanism ("more of it stays owed each month") is told; the stage has no owed-balance-over-time picture. Acceptable for one idea.
- Layout: the stage shrinks at reveal but stays readable. No clipping.
