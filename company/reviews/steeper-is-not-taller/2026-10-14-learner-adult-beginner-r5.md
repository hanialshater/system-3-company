## 3. Why revise, and what would move this to launch

The explore is now good and the idea is seen. The verdict is revise only because the prediction is answered before it is made: the locked picture shows B steeper, and the hook signals "the higher one isn't". A learner who commits to A has to ignore what is on screen.

- Top: in the locked state, show only the two end points (or the two ramps' numbers and end heights, not their lines), and draw the lines after lock. Or keep the picture but ask about something the picture does not show.
- Make the hook neutral (e.g. "Two ramps from the same foot. Which is steeper?").
