## 1. From the artifact

- First idea I didn't have: the question uses "points" for two things in one breath: "20 points … groups of 4 points" (data) and "Within 5 points / About 10 points / 25 points or more" (percentage points of score). As a beginner I first read the options as "how many data points apart". That is where understanding first broke. Also "wrong labels": the stage never marks which 4 dots are mislabelled until they turn red in a fold.
- Explore is strong: each fold lights its 4 dots with ticks and crosses, scores 100, 75, 100, 50, 75; HIGHEST − LOWEST climbs to 50 points; mean 80%. The spread is seen, not told.
- Told-not-shown: "in 1,000 shuffles the spread was 25 points or more every time" is told; acceptable as a closing line.
- Layout: large empty band above the chart in every explore frame; in the reveal frame the chart shrinks to tiny labels. The fold buttons wrap (Fold 5 alone on a row) on phone.
- Reveal answers my question ("why do the scores disagree?": which 4 landed in the group).

## 2. After reading the brief

- Learner field assumes "held-out data" is known; with that it reads fine. The "points" clash remains for anyone.
- Suggested fix: say "percentage points" in options and readout (or "Within 5%"), call data "dots"; mark the 4 mislabelled dots faintly on the locked stage or in the legend.
