**R1 · Stage word budget: units after a space, coordinates, formulas (Ruling 2 in `company/decisions/2026-10-16-stage-word-budget-counting.md`).** (a) A unit symbol or standard abbreviation directly after a number counts 0, with or without a space ("60.6 kg", "55 km", "30 °C", "mol/L"). A lone unit symbol, a spelled-out unit ("36 degrees") and a quantity symbol in front of a number ("pH 4", "n = 2") count 1. (b) Coordinates count 0 ("49°N", "49° N"). (c) Chemical formulas and element or tally symbols count 1 each ("H2O2", "H", "O"). The strict reading binds for formulas. **Effect:** in physics, math and geography the lenient total binds. In chemistry, formulas count 1 and spaced units 0. No brief needs a rebuild. matter-reactions #5 stays at 25. The objections are answered in the record, one by one.
