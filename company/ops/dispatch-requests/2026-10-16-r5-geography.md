**R1 · How B1-B3 count geography stages (applies `company/decisions/2026-10-16-stage-word-budget-counting.md`).** Count from the brief's own text. List **every** word the stage may show, from the locked frame to the open reveal, and add them up as if all were visible at once. The total is an upper bound on the busiest frame. Numerals, percentages, scale ratios ("1:50,000") and fused units ("10m") count 0. Each line gives two totals: **strict**, where a spaced unit ("55 km", "400 km²", "7 h") counts 1 and a coordinate token ("49°N", "66.6°N") counts 1, and **lenient**, where both count 0. **The strict total binds** until head-of-product rules (A4). Two kinds of text are **not cuttable** by a strand manager: (a) the projection-and-resolution line ("Equirectangular, Natural Earth 1:110m"), because the charter requires every map claim to name its projection and data resolution, and (b) a claim-bearing scope caption ("Made-up island; 1 square = 5 km"). If a brief is over 25 strict, the strand manager may trim **only** its own control labels, picture labels and readout wording. It edits the brief's Interaction, Locked stage and Key state lines to match and marks each change "r5 trim". It may not change a claim's numbers, the prediction, the options or the reveal draft. If a brief cannot reach 25 that way, it writes "Stage over budget: N (strict)" with the table and stops for that brief. That is for me to rule, or for the learning designer if it needs a re-layout. B1 says yes or no for it under the decision's test, and I sign the declaration. **The list is closed.** A builder who adds an unlisted stage word recounts, and stops if the strict total goes over 25. Line format, one per brief, directly under its Format line: `- Stage word budget: <item> "<text>" = N; … **Total S strict / L lenient.** <closing note>`.
