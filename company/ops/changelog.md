## 2026-10-14: platform-engineer W-287 (+ W-288 change 3) legibility floor for the reveal picture fit
- DemoCard `useRevealFit` (1a879c6) no longer shrinks svg labels below readable size. Per picture, floor = max(0.4, `SVG_TEXT_FLOOR_PX` 10 / smallest meaning-carrying `<text>` px, `SVG_DECOR_FLOOR_PX` 8 / smallest `aria-hidden` label px), measured at natural size; no shrink if that is >= 1; text-free pictures keep 0.4 (W-289 ruling, company/design/system.md). A shrink that frees < 1 px is undone. Skipped outright: `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, svgs that are or sit inside position absolute/fixed, svgs with absolutely positioned siblings laid over them, and svgs holding untagged interactive elements (`[tabindex]`, role button/slider, links, form fields, any `cursor: pointer` element). Probe (review probe adapted to read the app's own fit, build .next-pf2 at :3510, 390x844, 595 measured): lessons fully fitting stage + reveal 398 → 176 (shrunk lessons that fit 261 → 41; the review estimated about 44 at 10 px); lessons shrunk 433 → 179; smallest meaning-carrying svg label after the fit 2.6 px (median 4.9) → 10.0 px, 0 lessons under 10 px (was 370); smallest decorative label 8.0 px; overlay or `none` shrinks 7 → 0; shrunk svgs with pointer-like elements 3 → 0. Contract QA (phone + desktop) on the 29 NCM lessons + 20 random: 98/98. Review: company/engineering/reviews/2026-10-14-W-237-reveal-stage-crop.md.
