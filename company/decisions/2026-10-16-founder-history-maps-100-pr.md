- **A hundred variants can masquerade as a hundred ideas.** **Answer:** the set is called a practice set everywhere; no 100-concept curriculum expansion, published count, learning evidence or unique-idea claim is made. cm-history may reduce, regroup or retire the set.
- **Historical polygons are a reconstruction, not exact sovereignty or identity.** **Answer:** approximate-map captions, all overlapping NAME labels retained, missing coverage preserved, no area ranking, no causal or legitimacy verdicts. NAME is distinguished from SUBJECTO. Mercator is named and is never used to compare areas.

Nominal coordinates, actual coordinates and offset distance are in each map.json and its positions claim. Genuine gaps remain gaps.
