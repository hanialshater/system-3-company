# Founder decision: Book Summaries is its own multi-card experience, with more per book

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Decided by:** founder (in conversation with the chief of staff)
- **Answers:** Books F3 ("what does 'multiple cards' mean?") and part of F4 (depth per book)
- **Supersedes:** the head-of-product reading of F3 in `2026-10-14-books-big-ideas-strategy-review.md` §5 ("several sub-cards inside one lesson about one idea; the summary is the path of lessons") and the strategy's default of 2 lessons per book (books-strategy.md D2).

## What the founder said

> "Ok how many cards is one book? Would we be able to cover ideas?"
>
> "We need more for books, it is different experience probably multiple cards"

This came after the chief of staff explained that the plan was about 6 cards per book (2 lessons × 3 cards) covering each book's 2 most testable ideas.

## Decision

1. A book is a **different experience from a subject lesson**. It is a multi-card format of its own, not single-card lessons strung together.
2. **More per book.** Each book covers more of its ideas than the 2 planned. The design lead and cm-books propose how many, and the shape.

## Constraints that still bind (not changed by this decision)

- Copyright rules (`2026-10-14-books-and-big-ideas.md`): no reproduced text, at most one short attributed line, everything in our own words, and no chapter-by-chapter retelling.
- "What the book argues" and "does it hold up?" stay separate claims, and "holds up?" is required at launch.
- The demo standard's predict, explore, reveal sequence applies to every idea taught. Phone fit and the legibility floor apply to every card.
- No Books lesson publishes until a book source exists (F1) and the legal read is answered (F2).

## Objections carried forward

- **cm-books (books-strategy D1, D2):** review capacity is the bottleneck, so more per book means fewer books, or slower. The proposal must say which.
- **Head of product:** a book retold card by card risks the copyright line and the 60-second budget. The proposal must show where a book experience stops being a summary of the book's text.
- **Principal engineer (EDD-006):** CardSequence allows 2–4 sub-cards and 16 or fewer controls. A longer book format is a kit change and needs a tier-2 review.
