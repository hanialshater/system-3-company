# Decision: a community engineer for external pull requests

owner: principal-engineer · date: 2026-10-14 · status: accepted (founder request)

## Context
External PRs have started to arrive. PR #2 adds 200 Life Hacks lessons from a Codex branch: 688 files and +42,533 lines, with conflicts against our branch. The illustrator delivers by PR too. Until now, nobody owned external PRs.

## Decision
New role `community-engineer` (Femi), in the engineering org on floor 5, reporting to the principal engineer. The process and review matrix are in `company/engineering/community/README.md`. External work gets the same reviews as ours. Merging needs every required review. Publishing stays with the release manager.

## Consulted
Per company/ceo-office/org-change-process.md:
- The founder asked for the role directly, so this consultation decides how it works, not whether it exists.
- Kenji (principal engineer, the role's manager) and Leonie (CEO office) are consulted on placement and handoffs at the first PR evaluation. Their answers will be recorded here.

## Objections
- *A second gatekeeper could slow outside work.* Answer: the role routes work and does not review it. Each review is the same one our own lessons get, and intake has a same-day reply rule.

## Would be wrong if
External PRs wait more than a day without a reply, or merged external work causes more incidents than our own.
