## Decision 1: durable, scalable operations

Leonie fixes how the company operates so that it scales and survives sessions. The rules must be **enforced by mechanisms** (tools, gates, checks that fail), not by the chief of staff remembering them. Today's retro (2026-10-14-retro.md) and the ops review show that rules which only lived in operating-loop.md were broken: the concurrency cap, worktrees, the single board writer, rituals before dispatch, and routing founder asks to their owner.

## Decision 2: a Marketing org

- A new HQ floor for Marketing.
- A **VP of Marketing** who reports **directly to the founder**. This is the first role outside the CEO office to report to the founder directly.
- Leonie designs the org: the VP role, any first reports, scope, decision rights, handoffs with head-of-product, growth-designer, analytics-steward and the category managers, and the HQ floor.

## Constraints that still bind

- Deploys need the founder's approval every time (CLAUDE.md). Marketing cannot ship anything public, or post externally, without it.
- Every factual sentence a learner sees is a claim. Marketing copy that makes factual claims about lessons or learning outcomes is held to the same standard, and to the analytics steward's metric definitions: no learning claims without data, and there is no real-learner data yet.
- No impersonation, no fabricated reviews or testimonials, no external accounts created without the founder.

## Objections carried forward

- **principal-pm-verticals / ops review:** review capacity and the host are the bottlenecks; a new org adds coordination load without adding either.
- **analytics-steward:** nothing is deployed to real learners yet (EXP-002 has not run), so marketing has no evidence of outcomes to cite.
