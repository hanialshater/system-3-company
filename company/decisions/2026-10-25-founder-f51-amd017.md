## What was decided and applied

| Part | Applied |
|---|---|
| (a) tiers N / R / S | Live. R is wider than the draft: a risky route on the lesson or its curriculum concept, a `sensitive` concept, or an incubating domain (no lesson declares `reviewRoute` today). |
| (b) tier-S learner walk sampled at 1 in 5, with the stop rule | Live. `company sample <domain> <slug...> --by=<role>` lists a batch and draws its sample by hash; the drawer is not a builder of the batch. More than 1 fatal in 10 in a sample: that batch and the category's next batch walk every lesson. |
| would_be_wrong_if (audit) | Live, in code: two weekly audits running with more than 1 fatal in 10 in a category switch (b) off for that category. |
| (c) route verdict, (d) sequence-probe record, (e) design and pairwise records | Side by side (warnings in `publish` and `check`) for one cycle, per the amendment's side_by_side line and Article 3.4; enforcement review due 2026-11-08. |
| (f) | No code (not protected). |

Measured on 2026-10-25 (before any batch exists): of 234 lessons in review, 28 are tier S, 205 tier R (incubating domains), 1 tier N; of 721 published, 705 S, 12 R, 4 N. (c)-(e) would stop 206 of the 234 if enforced today. `npm run check`: 810 errors before and after, no new error.
