## Why one category with three strands
- **History is not geography's people-places strand, and not a Big Ideas theme.** Geography owns how maps work and where people live now. Big Ideas owns ideas that stand without a dataset. Neither owns "what the dated record shows". That is the gap the founder named ("history through data and maps").
- **Three strands, because the sources and checks differ.** Population and economy rest on reconstructed series (Maddison, Gapminder, censuses). Maps rest on geometry against historical polygons. Events rest on contemporary tallies (Nightingale, the Bills, EM-DAT) and carry most of the sensitive classes. Fewer strands would put 50-plus concepts under one manager now that the target is 100. More would add idle roles, which is this office's own failure mode.
- **Strand split as proposed, adjusted in one place:** "projections in history" sits in maps-borders, but only as historical maps measured against data. The projection mechanism stays with geography (boundary table).

## Objections (each with what happened to it)
1. **"One new vertical, not three": review is the bottleneck** (principal-pm-verticals, plan-to-2000 §4; carried by the founder decision). *Answer:* History is one category, not a vertical. The founder then raised the target to 100 and lifted W-556 for History. That leaves the capacity objection open: building more categories adds no review capacity. What this record does about it: briefs are written only on opened data, so the fact-checker verifies data claims by re-running one script, not by opening sources. *Status: open*, owned by head-of-product (W-743) and the founder decision's own table.
2. **Overlap with geography, economics, Big Ideas and Books** (founder decision). *Answer:* the charter's boundary table names, for each neighbour, what they own, what History owns and every known collision. The demographic transition, Malthus, germ theory (Snow) and the war-size tail stay with Big Ideas. Books keeps every book's thesis. Geography keeps projections. Economics keeps the mechanisms of real wages and growth, and probability keeps dual axes. Each of the 12 briefs names its nearest lesson and prerequisite. *Status: mitigated*; cm-big-ideas is asked to agree or object (W-746).
3. **Most historical data cannot be opened from this host** (founder decision; fact-checker records). *Answer:* the charter's sourcing rule is binding. A dataset qualifies only if it can be fetched here and is pinned. Reachability was tested on 2026-10-14: raw.githubusercontent.com, the npm registry and PyPI work; ourworldindata.org, rug.nl, CRAN, census.gov, naturalearthdata.com and the GitHub API do not. Every number in the 12 briefs is recomputed from a pinned file. The script caught two of the drafter's own errors before the briefs were written (brief 2's recall answer, brief 11's causes). *What remains:* 30 of 113 concepts have no opened data, and master-branch sources (OWID, Rdatasets, basemaps) will drift, so their pins will fail and need re-pinning after a re-check. *Status: mitigated.*
4. **Independence: briefs written outside the owning role.** This office's scope excludes lesson content, and the caller asked it to draft the curriculum and briefs. *Answer:* every brief says who drafted it. Each has a skip line until cm-history or the strand manager re-checks it (W-742). The drafter does not review that re-check. *Status: open until W-742 closes.*
5. **Sensitive history has no working review route yet** (founder decision; head-of-product). *Answer:* History shares Big Ideas' route (research-lead note, cultural-reviewer `culture-en`, fact-checker) and adds no second route. 5 of the 12 briefs and 34 of the next 88 are in a sensitive class, and they stay held for W-626 (owned by this office) after their re-check. Each sensitive brief carries a care line. *Status: open* (W-626, W-744, W-745).

## Would be wrong if
- Pins fail often enough that the check script is usually red. That would mean pinning by hash is the wrong tool for master-branch sources, and the fix is a vendored snapshot under a licence review.
- The fact-checker cannot verify "data" claims by re-running the script. That would mean the data/derivation/source split does not save review time.
- Learners read History lessons as subject lessons with old numbers. That would mean the "record, not mechanism" boundary is not visible on the stage.
