# Founder decision: new roles, orgs, floors and rooms go through Leonie and the founder; dispatch goes through the owning manager

owner: founder (recorded by chief-of-staff)
date: 2026-10-16
class: governance / org
route: chief-of-staff-ceo-office (Leonie: owns the org design, the POC map and the new-role pipeline) → founder (approves every new role, agent, org, floor and room) → chief-of-staff (operating loop, dispatch)
recorded by: chief-of-staff
status: decided, in force now

## What the founder said
"Make sure you use our org structure. Ok lets add very important rule for you, you never start new agents directly, talke to leone (comany health) to design the org, route to teh right POC, all new agents should be approved by me. Also all new orgs or floors or rooms."
Asked whether this covers new roles only or all dispatches, the founder answered: "1 + yes it can go through you but after asking the manager, your scope is too big so you need to have scalable oversight through others".

## The rule
1. **New roles, agents, orgs, floors and rooms.** The chief of staff never creates them. Each one goes to **Leonie (chief-of-staff-ceo-office)**, who designs it under company/ceo-office/org-change-process.md and routes it to the right point of contact. **The founder approves every one** before it exists (an agent definition, a role file, an org entry in tools/org/roles.mjs, an HQ floor or room). No stand-ins: the chief of staff does not run a missing role as general-purpose.
2. **Dispatch through the manager.** The chief of staff still makes the Agent calls (managers cannot start agents today), but only **after asking the owning manager** of that lane what to run, by whom and in what order. The manager's answer is the brief's source. The chief of staff's job is integration and oversight across managers, not choosing specialists' work itself.
3. **Scalable oversight.** Each lane has one point of contact (the POC map, owned by Leonie). The chief of staff reads managers' reports and the board, not every specialist's work.

## Transition (honest account)
The 8 agents running when this rule was given (behavioral builder, Life Hacks W3 builder, Wave J builder, fact-checker, QA tester, privacy reviewer, growth designer, learner evaluator) were dispatched by the chief of staff without asking their managers first. They finish their briefs; each result goes to its lane's manager before any follow-up is dispatched.
