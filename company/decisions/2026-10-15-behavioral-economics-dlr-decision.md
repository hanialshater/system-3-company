## 2. The slot line: **18 counted lessons, not 40** (recommendation to the founder, F-22)

**Why 40 does not stand.** In the strand variant, 40 counted = 5 Economics lessons re-tagged + 35 new. Under a category, nothing moves out of a neighbour (F-21's own objection), so **every counted lesson must be new**, and 40 means 40 new concepts. The scout found 12 counted concepts plus about 6 reserves: **16-18 collision-free, and 5 of the 12 are close calls** (#7, #8, #9, #10, #12). One lesson per concept is the catalog's rule. Funding 40 slots against 16-18 concepts would either leave 22+ slots idle in the plan or pressure the CM to brief lessons that collide with Economics, Books or Probability, which is exactly what triggers the pause rule (3 duplicates).

**The line, per wave:**

| Tranche | Lessons | When | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (pilot) | **5** (the DLR five) | the next wave whose W-556 gate opens; counted against the N-Q pool; nothing committed in K-M moves | sections 3 and 4 hold; Project Review approves tranche 1 (W-898) |
| 2 (conditional) | **up to 13** | not before Wave N cycle-planning | DLR five passed; held-claim rate ≤ 25%; a Project Review; learner data (commit and 7-day recall) at or above the feed median; and every concept briefed has its neighbour sign-off |
| beyond 18 | **0 funded** | only by a new founder item | a scout pass of fields not yet scouted (bargaining and negotiation anomalies, behavioural finance, savings and health decisions) yields concepts that pass the neighbours' sign-off, so the signed clean count reaches the proposed target. Then head-of-product brings the founder a re-size |

18 is a **ceiling**. If the sign-offs strike close calls, tranche 2 shrinks with them, and the freed slots return to the pool at the next cycle-planning. If fewer than 12 remain, the fold rule applies.

**Where the 18 slots come from** (total stays 2,000): the uncommitted N-Q pool, ~608 → ~590. The targets that pay, chosen because they are saturating, thinning or blocked, and none of them is a neighbour losing a concept to this category:
- **cs −8** (185 → 177; saturating, and its systems and security work already moved to N-Q);
- **geography −5** (95 → 90; blocked on data);
- **life −5** (150 → 145; thinning, new concepts needed).
- **Economics stays at 165** (Selin's 165 → 200 was the strand variant; the category is its own row). **Big Ideas' conditional tranche is not cut** (Selin's −10 assumed Big Ideas would give up decision-bias ideas; under the category it keeps every concept it ranked, so cutting its slots would be a raid by another name).
- New plan-to-2000 row: `behavioral-econ`, target 18, 5 committed (pilot) + 13 conditional, form category, kill rule as section 1.

**What this costs, said plainly.** Set-up does not shrink with the target. Tranche 1 stays **1.83M** (portfolio-manager's measured figure). Tranche 2 at 13 × 122k ≈ **1.59M** (not 4.03M), with no strand-manager org change at this size (see objection 4). Program ≈ 3.42M for 18 lessons, **about 190k per lesson** against the measured 122k. The 40-lesson plan was cheaper per lesson on paper (≈ 147k) only because it assumed 22 lessons we do not have clean ground for. I would rather pay the fixed cost honestly than spread it over duplicates.

**F-22 recommendation (founder's decision):** option (c), "other cuts": **18 slots (pilot 5 + up to 13 conditional) from the N-Q pool; cs −8, geography −5, life −5; Economics and Big Ideas unchanged; total stays 2,000; no W-556 exemption; nothing committed in K-M moves; a re-size to more than 18 only through a new founder item on the scout trigger above.** This changes the F-22 recommendation (35 slots), so I added a note to F-22 in `company/ops/founder-inbox.json`. If the founder keeps 40, the record should say which 22 concepts the 22 extra slots are for, or that they sit unfunded in the pool until the scout trigger fires. I recommend against reserving them.

Every brief names its nearest lesson and links neighbour lessons as prerequisites instead of re-running their stages. **Nothing moves out of a neighbour**; nothing is re-tagged.
