### A1.1 Do signed reserves count toward the 12? **Yes, if every boundary line on the concept is signed and the effect is not contested.** The category continues.

**The rule, as amended (testable):** the fold floor counts a concept, counted or reserve, when (1) every neighbour line on it is `signed` or `signed with changes`, with none open, and (2) its effect is not one the charter can only teach *as contested* (reserve note "contested", or a *mixed or contested* verdict). **Why reserves count.** The fold rule asks one question: is there enough clean ground to justify a category's fixed cost (1.83M for tranche 1, §2)? "Collision-free after the neighbours sign" is what a signature on a reserve's line certifies. Reserve status sets the **order** of briefing (charter "Portfolio rules": not briefed until a counted concept is struck). It does not say the ground is less clean. Leaving out reserves the neighbours have signed would fold a category over a queue position.

They are type (d) only, so every line is held until W-902 and the fact-checker's (d) statement exist. A *did not replicate* verdict sends them to Big Ideas' first refusal (G3). That is ground that can disappear without any neighbour objecting, and a floor should not rest on it.
