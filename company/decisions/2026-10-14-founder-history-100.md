## What the founder said

> "But also add 100 history demos"

## Decision

The History category targets **100 lessons**, to be built now rather than paced across waves K to Q.

## How it runs (operating loop, retro 2026-10-14)

1. The History org lands first: charter, curriculum, 12 briefs and the cm-history role (CEO office, in progress).
2. cm-history extends the curriculum and writes about 100 ranked briefs. Each brief rests on data that has been opened from this host. A brief whose data cannot be opened is not written; it goes on a list for the web-capable fact-check session (W-583).
3. Builds run at most 3 builders at once, each in its own worktree with its own dev server, about 5 lessons per run. Then fact-check, release-build QA and learner review, as for every lesson.
4. Sensitive history (war, colonialism, slavery, genocide, religion, borders) needs the review route (W-626) before it is built. Those briefs wait until the route exists; everything else goes ahead.
5. Spend is tagged `[project:history]` in the token ledger (company/ops/token-budget.md). The 100 is a major project, so it goes on the Project Review agenda as a founder-decided item: the forum records its budget and objections, and does not re-decide the target.
