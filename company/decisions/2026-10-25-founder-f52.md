The proposal sent learning-scientist-personalization's work to personalization-engineer. The evidence work goes to research-scientist instead, which the proposal names for the applied scientist "on request". The reason is the org's two-way independence rule (company/ceo-office/2026-10-16-personalization-org-proposal.md): the maker of a policy must not shape the learner that judges it. This is a choice made in applying the row, and it is open to the founder and head-of-product (objection 6).
