# Founder decision: create a History category, "History through data and maps"

owner: founder (recorded by chief-of-staff)
date: 2026-10-14
status: decided

## What the founder said

After the chief of staff listed possible new categories (astronomy, psychology and the mind, music and sound, language and linguistics, engineering, health and the body, history through data, a second course), the founder replied:

> "I like these, create the orga history through data and maps"

## Decision

1. Create a new category, **History through data and maps**, with its full org: a category manager, strand managers, a charter, a curriculum, an HQ room and first briefs. This follows the pattern of Book Summaries and Big Ideas (`2026-10-14-books-and-big-ideas.md`).
2. The founder liked the other candidates on the list too. They go to the domain scout for assessment. They are not yet created.

## Constraints that still bind

- Every factual sentence a learner sees is a claim with a source. History claims need data that can actually be opened. Fetching from the web is mostly blocked from this host; data bundled in npm or PyPI packages (such as Natural Earth, already in node_modules) is reachable.
- Lessons follow `company/product/demo-standard.md`: predict, explore, reveal; phone fit; labels at least 10 px.
- Sensitive history (war, colonialism, slavery, genocide, borders, religion) needs a review route before build and before publish, as ruled for Big Ideas' society topics (W-626).
- New categories enter as **incubating** and go through Domain Launch Review.
- The W-556 wave gate and review capacity apply. Building more categories does not add review capacity.

## Objections carried forward

- **principal-pm-verticals (plan-to-2000 §4):** "one new vertical, not three", because review is the bottleneck.
- **Overlap:** with geography (its people-places strand, maps and projections), economics (growth and inflation history), Big Ideas (society) and Books (Guns, Germs, and Steel; Sapiens; Why Nations Fail). The charter must set boundaries.
