# Founder decision: First Light, the first public release, as a company-wide initiative

owner: founder (recorded by chief-of-staff)
date: 2026-10-15
status: decided ("I agree with you but with slight disagreement", chief of staff's conversation). Budget pending Project Review 1.

## Decided
1. **Program:** one company-wide initiative with a small core team, run from a temporary war room in HQ (CEO-office floor), not a new floor.
2. **Owner: a new Head of Strategic Initiatives** (founder's change; my proposal was head-of-product with a temporary program manager). The role reports to the founder, owns company-wide initiatives end to end, and runs First Light's cadence. The principal engineer is tech lead. Head-of-product owns product scope inside the initiative. The role is created through company/ceo-office/org-change-process.md. The founder has decided that the role exists; the consultation decides how it fits.
3. **Directs informed** (founder's change): every founder direct gets the kickoff note, a weekly FYI, and the completion announcement.
4. **Code name and dates:** First Light. Go/no-go 2026-11-23, launch 2026-11-30, re-estimated in week 1 by the tech lead and the owner. Ads may slip to release 2.
5. **Scope:** a domain, auth (anonymous-first, keeping history at sign-up), server-side progress, rule-based personalization, mastery-based gamification (no streaks, no guilt), light social, ads (on the terms in company/marketing/2026-10-15-monetization-ads-proposal.md), legal (privacy policy, terms, age gate), and infra.
6. **Surface Value amendment:** yes. One amendment covers rule-based personalization, mastery-based gamification and the ad slot. Drafted by the evaluation-steward and ratified by the founder.
7. **Launch catalog:** published lessons only at launch (revisits W-479 for launch day).
8. **Spend in principle:** Vercel Pro and a domain. The purchase waits for a priced quote, the domain name, and the token and money budget at Project Review 1.

## Correction (founder's change)
My proposal had no token budget and did not involve the portfolio manager. Every initiative proposal from the chief of staff now carries a budget from the portfolio manager (company/ops/token-budget.md) before it reaches the founder. The portfolio manager budgets First Light for Project Review 1.

## Objections
- **Two big migrations at once** (First Light and EDD-003 stage 1). Stage 1 runs only as a 50-lesson pilot off the launch's critical path. Owner: principal-engineer.
- **A new founder direct** adds a seat while 97 roles exist. Its scope must take initiative running away from the chief of staff and head-of-product, not duplicate it. The CEO office checks this in the consultation.
- **The dates are a guess** until the week-1 re-estimate.
