## What the founder said
> "No need to cut we will just do them sequentially" (2026-10-16, on cuts C1–C4 in company/initiatives/first-light/2026-10-16-fast-path-to-release.md)

## Decided
- **Nothing is cut from First Light's charter.** The prediction swap (C1), ad slot (C2), challenges (C3) and rule-based personalization (C4) all stay in scope.
- **They ship one after another**, not all at the launch:
  1. the release milestone: accounts with history kept, server-side progress, legal basics, infra and readiness, the deploy guard, the domain, the launch catalogue, share links and mastery state;
  2. then C1–C4 as follow-on releases, one at a time, each through the same gates and each needing its own founder deploy approval.
- The order of C1–C4 after the milestone and their dates are left to head-of-strategic-initiatives, with the tech lead. The founder's stated aim is to start personalization or ads soon after the milestone (F-43).

## Left to the owners
- head-of-strategic-initiatives: rewrite the plan as a release sequence (milestone, then C1–C4 in order), with a date and a token cost for each step, and fold it into the §4 forecast.
- principal-engineer: re-estimate the milestone without C1–C4 in it, and each follow-on release on its own.
- head-of-product: confirm the charter wording ("at launch" becomes "in the First Light sequence").
