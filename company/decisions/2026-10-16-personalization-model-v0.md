## Decision

The phase A model for the probability + cs pilot is a dependency-free TypeScript module, not a JSON file, as the founder ratified with a change to Q3 (company/personalization/2026-10-16-founder-brief.md §6). The design is company/personalization/2026-10-16-brief-response.md §2. Every curve and weight is a hand-set guess with its reason written beside it. Nothing in `src/` imports it until its home is ruled (W-1919, now "personalization org, pending role approval" per company/decisions/2026-10-16-founder-personalization-org.md). The founder's inspectability ask (founder brief §7, W-1924, W-1925) is met by keeping every number and reason as plain data and exporting `explainNeeds()`, `curveViews()` and `inspect()`.

**Change rule:** every edit to a curve, weight, constraint, signal or persona bumps `MODEL_VERSION` or `PERSONAS_VERSION`, gives the reason in the commit message, and adds one line below. `self-check.mjs` fails if the current version has no line here. There is no curve-editing tool. From model-v0.2.1 on (privacy ruling N6, company/reviews/_privacy/2026-10-16-personalization-model-inputs.md §3), each line's reason says `privacy: no new input` or names the privacy read it needed; `self-check.mjs` fails otherwise.

## model-v0.2: decisions on the simulator's findings (W-1926, 2026-10-16)

The simulator (company/personalization/model/sim/, W-1915) reported five findings about this contract. Each one is answered here. The numbers are UNSCORED: there is no committed criterion (W-1917), and the learner and scorer are builder placeholders that are moving to personalization-eval (W-1927).

1. **Overlap term and slots 2-3 never change what is served. Decision: change the contract.** The composer now composes one card, serves it, and recomputes (`slotsAhead: 1`, `servingMode: "compose-one-recompute"`). `overlap` and `slot` are removed from `ComposerTermName` and `weights`. The alternative was a session of N cards served in full, which would make overlap and slot decay matter, but it delays repair: a wrong prediction on card 1 could not change cards 2..N. It also lowers need on *assumed* coverage by queued cards rather than on the learner's next answer, which is the circularity behind O2. Repeats of one concept are still damped by `novelty.perSimilarRecent`. Re-open this as a new version only if a surface pre-fetches a session (offline mode).
2. **Breadth guardrail cannot move with 2 pilot subjects. Decision: change the guardrail now; do not widen the pilot yet.** `OBJECTIVE.guardrails` now uses the share of completed new cards in the learner's top domain (vs default-order) and distinct concepts completed. Both move with two domains. The breadth *need* is unchanged: `domainConcentration` already moves (crammer .95). Concept-cluster breadth was rejected for now. The curriculum's `subarea` tags cover only 20/77 visible probability lessons, and 90 of the 93 tagged cs lessons share one subarea, so no usable cluster dim exists yet. Graph-derived clusters belong to lesson-dims-owner. Widening to a third domain (math is the candidate: 80 visible lessons, 65% with prerequisites, and 11 pilot prerequisite edges already point into math:) is deferred. It changes the simulated world (persona latent `priorKnowledge`/`interest` per domain, history seeding), which is personalization-eval's (W-1927), and it adds 80 lessons to the ratings. Proposed as a v0.3 candidate once eval has math latents.
3. **Surprise acts only as a readiness term (placeholder strength). Decision: change the contract.** `COMPOSER.surpriseGate` is inactive. Surprise is 0 unless the gate is active AND the lesson's `strengthSource` is a real rating (`cm`, `classifier` or `data`). The placeholder hash has no source, so it can never count, even by accident. Turning the gate on is a version bump after the eval's blind 20% second rating reports agreement. The weight .15 is kept. The ratings plan is company/personalization/2026-10-16-misconception-strength-ratings-plan.md. Consequence: `gam` now equals the old `gam-off-surprise`, and `gam-off-surprise` is a no-op until the gate opens.
4. **The GAM's edge (+.01 to .02 recall, sd .13) reverses when the simulated learner's prerequisite penalty is 0. Decision: no contract change.** This finding is about evidence, not about the composer. It says the simulator's whole case for the GAM over the default order rests on one assumption in the judge: that meeting a concept before its prerequisites hurts recall. Changing curves or weights to win that run back would be tuning against the judge, which the org's independence rule forbids (company/ceo-office/2026-10-16-personalization-org-proposal.md §2). What follows instead: (a) the prerequisite-order effect is the hypothesis phase A has to test, so I make no claim that the GAM is better; (b) the evidence memo is asked for a range for that effect (W-1938; W-1918 sits with research-lead until learning-scientist-personalization is approved); (c) personalization-eval (W-1927, ask filed as W-1937) is asked to make the prerequisite penalty a swept dimension of the W-1917 criterion, so that a pass requires the edge to hold across the evidence range and not only at the base guess; (d) the edge is inside one sd, so the 5-seed quick run supports no ranking.
5. **How the 1-in-3 floor counts moved cards. Decision: confirmed and written into the contract (`antiBubbleFloor.counts`, `knownGap`).** Measured on 30 gam episodes (6 personas × 5 seeds, model-v0.1, scratch script, not committed): over *completed* cards the longest moved run was 2 and no 3-card window lacked a default card, so the floor holds as specified. It counts completed cards only, rebuilt from history. Learner-chosen and pre-personalisation cards count as moved, so 29/30 episodes started on a forced default card (conservative, accepted). Cards left before the reveal do not count, so over *served* cards the longest moved run was 4 (36 moved-but-unrevealed cards). A learner who skips moved cards is therefore not bounded. Closing that needs a session-local served list, which is a new input for privacy-reviewer (filed as W-1939). It is not added here and is open before any phase B serving.

### Objections on model-v0.2

- **personalization-lead (self):** Dropping the overlap term loses a guard against two cards on one concept back to back. **Answer:** under compose-one-recompute the guard was mathematically 0 in every pick. `novelty.perSimilarRecent` (−.2 per similar card in the last 3 completed) is the live guard. If personalization-eval's trace review shows same-concept runs, the fix is a novelty weight change with its own version line.
- **personalization-lead (self):** Redefining a guardrail after seeing a run is a moved goalpost. **Answer:** the old guardrail could not fail with two domains, so it guarded nothing. The new wording is fixed here before W-1917's criterion is written, and personalization-eval owns how it is measured. It is not tuned to any number.
