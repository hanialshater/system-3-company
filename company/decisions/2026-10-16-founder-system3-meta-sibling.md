## What the founder said
"I want you to create a new floor, new VP of system 3 meta - it is meta company with mandate to create whole company for specific use-case. I am seeing a leap from lovable/replit style platforms and probably this is a big idea for AI or a killer app. To keep us focused lets do that after first light. The new VP is running independently and it is a sibling company."

## Decided
- **What:** System 3 Meta, a sibling company (like the Press), run independently by its own VP. Its mandate: create a whole company, of narrow agents on the System 3 architecture, for a specific use case (the step beyond app builders such as Lovable or Replit: from generating an app to generating the organisation that builds and runs it).
- **When:** after First Light. Nothing is designed, staffed or built before then, so the focus stays on the first public release.
- **How:** under the founder's org rule (2026-10-16), Leonie designs the company, the VP role, its floor in HQ and its interfaces with this company and the Press; the founder approves that design before any role, agent, floor or room is created.
- **Not decided:** its name beyond the working title, the first use case, its budget (a Project Review item when it is proposed), where it lives (its own repository, as the Press does in hanialshater/system3, or a new one).
