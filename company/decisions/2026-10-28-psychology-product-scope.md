# Psychology and human learning: product scope

owner: head-of-product
date: 2026-10-28
status: draft direction for implementation

## Founder direction

The founder requested a psychology, human learning and deeper psychology collection with at least 500 demos, then approved proceeding and submitting PRs. The 500 is the target for functioning interactive experiences, not a count of concepts, headings or near-identical parameter variants. It does not by itself authorize a deploy, a new organizational role, or claims of verified learning.

## Product direction

- Begin an incubating Psychology and Human Learning domain; assign an existing category manager as interim coordinating owner, proposed `cm-behavioral` subject to that manager accepting the handoff. Domain scout supplies the domain proposal and initial concept briefs. The new domain is a distinct catalog category; cross-link related lessons rather than renaming them.
- Founder direction to build at least 500 demos takes precedence over the general two-lesson incubation volume limit for the requested draft work. This is a scope decision for creation, not a release approval or a relaxation of quality gates. Build in reviewable bounded batches and keep unreviewed lessons draft.
- Start with two interaction families rather than 500 variations of one mechanism. Pilot requests to the category manager: a memory/source-monitoring task where the actual presented list and a plausible lure can be inspected, and a visual grouping task where the learner changes the distance between items and inspects the resulting scene. Final concept boundaries, detailed briefs and claims are category management's, not a head-of-product assertion that these concepts are novel or ready.
- Every lesson must ask for a prediction, require an interaction that produces visible evidence, reveal the idea against what the learner saw and pose a fresh recall question. The representation and controls must work on phones and with keyboard and assistive technology. Don't expose an answer in the locked stage or title. A self-test is not diagnosis or an assessment of the user's mental health.
- Evidence records distinguish an authored stimulus or model from a psychological finding. Every factual learner-facing statement has a claim record with a source; builders leave those claims unverified. Independent fact checking, pedagogical and accessibility reviews, and browser QA remain required before publication. Do not claim the effect is universal, a clinical result, or the outcome of the user's own test unless it was actually observed.

## Objections and response

- **Existing coverage:** books and Life Hacks already contain retrieval, spacing and chunking lessons, while Big Ideas has a context-dependent gray demonstration. Response: category manager compares objective and interaction against actual lessons before briefing; duplicate objectives are cross-linked, not counted again.
- **False certainty and sensitive topics:** individual responses differ, and evidence may be contested. Response: report stimulus and learner choices faithfully; distinguish theoretical models, illustrative results and empirical findings; route mental health, trauma, attachment, identity and group comparisons for specialist sensitive-content review before publishing.
- **Production risks from a large batch:** bulk generation can create hundreds of labels without 500 meaningful interactions. Response: require independently specified objectives, a mechanism that responds to user action, meaningful distinct cases and verification of counts and routing; sample pedagogy and review all factual claims before publication.
- **Organizational ownership:** creating a new role requires its own founder approval. Response: use an existing category manager or domain-scout; do not add an organizational role without the established process. The proposed interim owner still needs a recorded acceptance.
