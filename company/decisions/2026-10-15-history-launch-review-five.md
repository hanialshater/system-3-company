## Why coal and not children, and why income is out

- **Income is out on the strand cap.** Population-economy gets 2 of 5 slots, and survive-childhood (brief 1, the category's signature misconception) takes one. Income has 11/11 claims verified but no launch verdict on its current version. It stays in the queue as a normal publish candidate. Being out of the five says nothing about its quality.
- **Coal over children, for the second population-economy slot.** Both have launch verdicts. Children has the higher demand estimate (3 against 2). But children shows the same fact as survive-childhood: about 41 in 100 children died before age 5 (children's 1800 readout; survive-childhood's "Deaths before age 5" preset). A launch review of five should test the category's range, and coal adds a different record (energy, shares against totals), a different chart (stacked area) and a different misconception. **Children is the first reserve** for either population-economy slot.
- **Coal's prerequisite** (`history:industrial-co2-share`, britain-made-most-co2-in-1850) is on the sensitive route and not published. Coal's hook states the 1968 fact it needs, so it stands alone. The prerequisite link stays in its curriculum.
