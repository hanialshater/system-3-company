# Founder decision: First Light release 1 keeps the ad slot and richer social (overrules the release-1 scope cut)

owner: founder (recorded by chief-of-staff)
date: 2026-10-15
class: product (founder overrule of a head-of-product scope ruling)
route: head-of-product (amend company/decisions/2026-10-15-first-light-release-1-scope.md Rulings 1 and 2) → principal-engineer (re-estimate W-908 at full scope) → privacy-reviewer (privacy gate §5, P2.3, P4.3) → head-of-strategic-initiatives (dates, dependencies.md, status.json) → evaluation-steward (quality bar "Cut surfaces" rows return) → portfolio-manager (re-base T2 and the stop rule) → vp-marketing (ads F-17..F-20) → chief-of-staff (board, founder inbox)

## What the founder said
The chief of staff explained the scope cut (ad slot to release 2; social reduced to share-a-lesson links; challenges kept with a pre-declared 11-06 cut) and said: "If you want ads or social back in release 1, say so. The cost is a later launch date or lower odds of hitting it." The founder replied: **"Yes i want these back"**.

## Decided
1. **The ad slot is in release 1's scope.** Head-of-product's Ruling 3 rules stay in force as product rules (1 card per 6 lessons is a ceiling; never on lesson pages; teen mode house cards only; the slot ships only if the registered experiment's guardrails hold). Stage 1 is house cards only, as in the ads proposal. Whether to pursue ads at all, and on which terms and accounts, is still F-17 to F-20, now needed on the release-1 timeline.
2. **Social beyond share-a-lesson is in release 1's scope.** Every precondition in Ruling 2's "for release 2" list now applies to release 1: the 5.3 minimums evidenced, a named human moderator and cover **named by the founder**, its own tier-2 EDD, a growth-designer PRD with a committed prediction about learning, and P2.3 (no social for ages 13 to 15) unchanged.
3. **Nothing else changes.** Challenges keep their pre-declared 11-06 cut. The dates (go/no-go 2026-11-23, launch 2026-11-30) stand until head-of-strategic-initiatives re-plans on the new estimate; any date move is theirs to propose and the founder's to accept.
4. **No gate is waived.** The privacy gate, the quality go/no-go (quality team, overrulable only by the founder) and every deploy approval stand as written.

## What would make this record wrong
- The founder later says "these" meant something else. Then this record is corrected, not the scope.
- The founder declines F-17 (ads at all). Then the ad slot leaves scope again, on the founder's word.
- No moderator is named before the privacy gate rules (11-09). Then social beyond share links is absent from the release build, by the gate, not by a new scope decision.
