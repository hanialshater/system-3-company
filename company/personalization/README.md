The founder said the idea is complex and will explain it later (2026-10-13). Until then the team:

- follows the company's anti-Goodhart line: we personalise for recall and understanding, never for time-on-feed, and never on demographics.

## Open questions for the founder's brief
1. What adapts: lesson order, level, variant (context, language), revisit timing, or explanation style?
2. What signal drives it: predictions, explore behaviour, revisit recall, or what the learner says they want?
3. Is it visible and controllable by the learner ("why this lesson next", "show me more like this")?
4. What may we store, and for how long? (privacy reviewer)
5. How will we know it worked? (analytics steward: a committed prediction before any experiment)
