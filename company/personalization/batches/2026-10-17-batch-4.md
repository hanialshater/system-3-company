## Privacy

The reachable-signal oracle must read **only** the input object criterion 2 §I.3 already gives a policy: `now` (per-lesson elapsed time only), `history` (completions, `predictedCorrectly` with null meaning absent, `recalls` cut to 30 days), `dims` (lesson dims and the curriculum graph), `slots`. Every one of these is already ruled in company/reviews/_privacy/2026-10-16-personalization-model-inputs.md (§2 table: completions, recalls standing-conditions; predictedCorrectly conditions P1 to P4; lesson dims and graph not personal; phase A simulated use allowed, A1/A2). **So no privacy-reviewer pre-read is requested by this batch**, and no input list is attached for one.

Limits that keep it inside that ruling (K3 may refuse with a reason; if it does, the arm waits for a privacy read before any score):
- Nothing in model.ts `FORBIDDEN_INPUTS` (persona label, session count or length, dwell and the rest), no `gapDays` (refused, §2.5), no `settings` field.
- **Computed, not carried**: its estimate of the learner is rebuilt from the input object at each call, with no state kept between calls or sessions (N1, §4: no stored per-learner estimate). It may know the world's equations and population-level values; it may not know the persona, any per-persona or per-learner hidden value (prior knowledge, k factor L9, prerequisite knowledge), or anything the learner has not shown through the inputs above.
- No real data of any kind (A2). Everything stays simulated.

If the eval's design needs any input beyond the list above, K3 stops that arm, writes the extra inputs and their purpose in criterion 3, and asks privacy-reviewer through lane 10 (chief of staff routes it). Then and only then is a pre-read list written.
