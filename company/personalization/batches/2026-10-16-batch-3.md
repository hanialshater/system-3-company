## What this batch is for

The founder approved one more offline study (Q3) and review timing as its second candidate (Q4). Batch 3 runs it: sourced evidence first, then a criterion written and frozen **before any policy is scored**, then one scored readout, its reproduction, and my decision by rules written before the data.

It asks two questions, in this order:
1. **Headroom.** Can *any* ordering of new lessons move recall by δ_min in a better-sourced world? An oracle ordering (it knows the hidden state) is the reference. If it cannot, personalised ordering for recall is closed.
2. **Review timing.** Does a candidate that chooses *when* to bring a completed lesson back beat the product's fixed revisit schedule?

Nothing here touches `src/` for learners, stores learner data, or registers an experiment. Phase B stays unregistered (Q1).

- **V5. Built blind to the judge.** The candidate is frozen at a commit, its hash written into criterion 2 before the criterion freezes, and it is never run in the eval harness, or tuned against anything the harness prints, before that freeze.

**Applying the two together.** If pre-commitment 3 closes ordering and V2 or V3 closes review timing, I recommend option A to the founder and stop lane 14's model work.
