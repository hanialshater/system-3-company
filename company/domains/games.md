# Games to Play Together: category charter

owner: cm-lifehacks · status: incubating · curriculum: `content/curriculum/games.json`

Games are activities people play together in a home, with clear players, materials, setup, turns and a stopping rule. Rules in this batch are original house-rule suggestions, not assertions of official rules for traditional games. A base activity and its scoring or group formats are related variations and must be labeled and grouped; they are not different mechanics.

The games use common materials and an indoor-sized space. Players may skip or adapt a round; avoid involuntary contact, running, hazardous materials, food-based dares, or requirements to reveal personal information. Claims about play and materials stay unverified until reviewed. Independent accessibility, safety, playability and learner reviews are needed before publication.

The existing `cm-lifehacks` role owns this incubating category; no new organization role is created. This category supports the founder's explicit request for home games, with a browse page in addition to lesson-feed entries.
