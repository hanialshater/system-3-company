# Life Sciences: category charter

owner: cm-life · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/life.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage life`

## Audience
People curious about bodies, evolution and epidemics.

## What a great lesson in this category does
Populations simulated on the stage; the learner changes one parameter and watches a threshold.

## How claims get checked here
Model assumptions stated next to every threshold (e.g. homogeneous mixing); medical figures cite sources and say 'approximately'.

## What we refuse to publish
Health advice; thresholds stated as laws of nature.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
