# Mathematics: category charter

owner: cm-math · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/math.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage math`

## Audience
Adults who stopped at school maths and suspect they missed the beautiful part.

## What a great lesson in this category does
A picture or manipulation that makes the proof feel unnecessary, then the reveal names the proof.

## How claims get checked here
Derivations. Every numeric claim must be recomputable from the lesson's own state.

## What we refuse to publish
Lessons that are worksheets: procedures with no 'why it works'.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
