# Chemistry: category charter

owner: cm-chemistry · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/chemistry.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage chemistry`

## Audience
People who remember the periodic table poster and nothing else.

## What a great lesson in this category does
Make an invisible scale visible: logs, half-lives, rates.

## How claims get checked here
Quantities with units; approximate everyday values marked 'about' and sourced.

## What we refuse to publish
Memorising names. Every lesson is about a relationship.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.

## Open questions
- Which concepts here are misconceptions-first, and which are wonder-first? (Track in briefs.)
