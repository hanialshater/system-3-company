# Life Hacks scope: the safety lens

author: lifehacks-safety-reviewer (Sana) · written 2026-10-14 · question: if Life Hacks grows to WikiHow / Mawdoo3 breadth, what safety class does each strand default to, what stays out of scope, what one-line warnings are required, and which strands need a safety review on every lesson? · status: **proposal**. It does not change the refuse list in `company/domains/lifehacks.md` yet. The additions in section 5 take effect only when cm-lifehacks puts them in the charter and I confirm the wording there. Strand names follow the category lens (`category.md`), with the extra strands the founder's question names.

Inputs: charter `company/domains/lifehacks.md` (refuse list); curriculum `content/curriculum/lifehacks.json`; briefs `company/domains/briefs/lifehacks.md` and my 2026-10-13 records under `company/reviews/<slug>/`; `company/product/demo-standard.md`; the sibling lenses in this folder; and PR #2 (`origin/codex/lifehacks-200`), read as data: its manifest `content/collections/lifehacks-200.json` and the 25 safety files it adds under `company/reviews/`. **Limit:** I have not run `npm run walkthrough` on any PR #2 lesson. Everything I say about PR #2 is triage from slugs, titles and its own records, not an artifact verdict.

## 1. Safety classes (the four tiers)

| Class | Meaning | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **none** | Nothing on the card can hurt anyone if they copy it carelessly (geometry, folding, scheduling, arithmetic). | No per-lesson review. I spot-check 1 in 5 at launch review, plus any lesson whose stage or hero adds heat, tools, food, chemicals, power, a body, a child or an animal. |
| **warning** | Safe if one known careless version is named. The card carries **one** short required line, recorded in `meta.json` as an advice claim (the way `wobbly-table-quarter-turn` claim 6 is). | The brief names the line. I check it is on the stage, the line is short and the hero does not show the careless version. |
| **review** | The hack touches food, heat, chemicals, electricity, the body, children, pets, vehicles or real money decisions. | A full record `company/reviews/<slug>/<date>-safety.md` **before the brief is claimed** and again at launch, against the walkthrough. |
| **refuse** | We do not teach it. Not even as a debunk with a demonstration (a debunk may say "do not do this" in one line; it may not show how). | On the refuse list. Briefs that need it are closed, not rewritten around it. |

Rule for mixed lessons: the strictest strand a lesson touches sets its class. A "study" lesson that mentions caffeine is a health lesson.

## 2. Strand by strand

"Every lesson" means the strand is review-class by default: a builder cannot propose class none or warning for it, only I can lower one lesson after reading its brief.

| Strand | Default class | Review every lesson? | Out of scope (never taught) | Required line(s), when they apply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Food and cooking** (`kitchen`) | review | **Yes** | Food-safety shortcuts that beat official guidance (thawing on the counter, "sniff test" for meat, reheating rice repeatedly, cooling stock in the pot overnight); home canning, fermenting and curing; raw-milk, raw-egg or raw-flour hacks; deep-fat-frying hacks; water on an oil fire; microwave tricks with metal, sealed containers or eggs in shells; nutrition, diet, weight-loss or "detox" claims; allergen substitutions. | "Use a thermometer: colour does not show doneness." · "Hot oil and water do not mix: never add water to hot fat." · Any temperature or holding time cites a food-safety body (USDA FSIS, UK FSA, or the region's) and is region-flagged for the International team. Scaling a recipe: "Cooking times do not scale with quantity." |
| **Cleaning chemicals** (`cleaning`) | review | **Yes** | Any mixing of products (bleach with ammonia, acids, vinegar, alcohol or other cleaners; "stronger together" recipes); homemade disinfectants; pressure from sealed reactions (baking-soda-and-vinegar in a closed bottle, drain "bombs"); cleaning with petrol, solvents or anything on a hazard label in a way the label does not allow. | "Never mix cleaning products." · "Follow the label and open a window." The only chemistry we show is why one product works on one stain, from the manufacturer's or a safety body's guidance. |
| **Home repair and DIY** (`fix`) | warning | No, but any lesson with a power tool, ladder, height, heavy lifting, gas, structural work, asbestos-age materials or a blade is review. | Gas appliances and pipework; structural changes (load-bearing walls, roof); work at height beyond a step stool; removing or bypassing smoke alarms, CO alarms, guards, RCDs or child locks; lead paint or asbestos disturbance; anything a building code or licence covers. | "Unplug it / switch it off first." · "Wear eye protection" (cutting, drilling, springs, elastic under tension). Lifting: "Get help: this model is not a safe-weight rating." |
| **Electricity** (`energy`) | review | **Yes** | The charter line stands: no electrical work beyond changing a bulb or resetting a breaker. Also out: opening chargers or appliances, repairing cords with tape, extension-cord daisy-chains, overriding fuses, swollen or damaged lithium batteries (charging, puncturing, "reviving"), wet-hands or bathroom-socket hacks, anything mains-voltage on the stage as a thing to touch. Energy-cost **arithmetic** (watts × hours × tariff) is allowed and is the main lesson type. | Arithmetic lessons: "Tariffs and appliance ratings vary: check your own." Cable lessons: "Never coil or cover a cord that is plugged in and in use" when the stage shows a powered cable (coiled extension leads can overheat; manufacturer guidance to be cited). Battery: "A swollen battery goes to a recycling point, not a charger." |
| **Health and body** | review | **Yes** | Medical, dosage, diagnosis or treatment hacks (refuse list). Also: sleep, caffeine and alcohol advice framed as health; first-aid shortcuts (we may link to a recognised first-aid body, not teach CPR in 60 seconds); posture or "safe weight" numbers; supplements; mental-health "hacks"; anything Mawdoo3 files under صحة (health). What remains: mechanism lessons with no instruction to the body (why a fan cools by evaporation) and debunks that say "see a professional". | "This is not medical advice: ask a doctor or pharmacist." (only when the topic is unavoidably health-adjacent; otherwise leave health out of the card). |
| **Beauty and personal care** | review | **Yes** | Anything applied to skin, hair, eyes, mouth or nails from the kitchen or the cleaning cupboard (lemon or baking soda on skin, toothpaste on spots, DIY sunscreen, DIY teeth whitening, at-home peels); hair dye or bleach chemistry; razors and heated tools beyond the manufacturer's use; sun-tanning hacks. In practice this strand is close to empty: what survives is packing and storage (how to fit toiletries, why a pump bottle leaks on a plane). | "Patch test, and follow the product label." I expect to block most briefs here; I recommend the category lens keep it as a sub-area of `storage`/`travel`, not its own strand. |
| **Money** (`shopping`, `resources`) | warning | No for arithmetic; **yes** for anything about credit, debt, investing, tax, insurance, benefits or contracts. | Recommendations (refuse list): what to buy, borrow, invest in or sign; tax or benefit strategies; "free money" schemes; chargeback or refund tricks that rely on misrepresentation; anything that is a scam pattern presented as a hack. Arithmetic about money (unit price, fixed fee vs per-use, compounding shown as a calculation) is fine. | "This is arithmetic, not financial advice." Only on lessons that compute a real-world money decision; not on unit-price comparisons, where it is noise. |
| **Digital and security** (`digital`) | warning | No, but any lesson about passwords, accounts, payments, phishing, device resets or someone else's data is review. | Bypassing locks, paywalls, DRM, parental controls or other people's accounts; "find anyone's" lookup tricks; jailbreaking or rooting; disabling OS security features; anything that would work as a phishing or social-engineering tutorial. Defensive lessons are in scope and valuable (show the file extension, check the real link target, why a password manager beats reuse). | "Only on your own device and accounts." Defensive lessons cite a national cyber-security body (UK NCSC, US CISA) or the platform vendor. |
| **Parenting and children** | review | **Yes** | Children's safety (refuse, as the brief scope frames it): baby sleep, feeding, bathing, car seats, choking, medicine, baby-proofing "hacks" that replace a safety device, toys made from household items for under-3s (choking), water and window safety tricks. What survives: adult-side organisation (packing a bag, scheduling) with no instruction about the child's body or safety. | "Follow your health visitor's / paediatrician's advice." Heroes never show a child in a risky position (on a counter, near water, unbelted). |
| **Pets** | review | **Yes** | Feeding human food (grapes, chocolate, onions, xylitol are toxic to dogs; lilies to cats), home remedies, dosing, grooming with human products, flea or pest treatments, essential oils around pets, training methods that use aversives, leaving animals in cars. What survives: storage and logistics (feeder geometry, lead length) with a vet-sourced line where food is involved. | "Ask a vet before giving any food or treatment." |
| **Cars and transport** | review | **Yes** | Anything done while driving; vehicle electrics, airbags, brakes, tyres beyond checking pressure against the door-sticker value; jump-starting (lead order, battery gas); fuel hacks; defeating seatbelt alarms or any sensor; ice-scraper hot-water tricks on windscreens; legal-road-rule claims (region-dependent: International team). | "Follow your vehicle handbook." · Never show a phone in a driver's hand. |
| **Travel** (`travel`) | warning | No, but any lesson on medicine in luggage, lithium batteries in luggage, visas, customs, border rules or personal safety abroad is review and region-flagged. | Legal advice on visas, customs or declarations; "skiplagging" and fare tricks that breach carrier terms; carrying medicines across borders; safety-in-unknown-places advice. Packing geometry, weight vs volume and time arithmetic are in scope. | Airline rules: "Check your airline's current limits." (cabin weights and battery rules differ by carrier and change). |
| **Gardening and outdoors** (`garden`) | warning | No, but pesticides, herbicides, fungicides, fertiliser mixing, power tools, burning, foraging and anything near children or pets is review. | Homemade pesticides and weedkillers (salt, vinegar, bleach, diesel); foraging or eating wild plants or mushrooms; bonfires and burning waste; chainsaws and strimmers; ponds and open water with children. Watering, light, spacing and soil-moisture mechanisms are in scope. | "Wear gloves and wash your hands after handling soil or compost." (only where soil is handled). |
| **Study and work** (`study`, `time`) | none | No | Health framing (all-nighters, sleep loss, caffeine, "brain boosting", ADHD or medication hacks); academic dishonesty (cheating, ghost-writing, beating plagiarism checks). Confirmed in my `spaced-beats-cram` record. | None needed. |
| **Laundry, storage, organisation** (`laundry`, `storage`) | none | No, but drying on heaters, tumble-drying anything a care label forbids, heavy shelving and tall furniture (tip-over: anchor it), and storage of chemicals or medicines is review. | Drying clothes on electric heaters or near flames; storing cleaning products in food containers; stacking heavy things high where children climb. | Tall furniture: "Anchor it to the wall." Indoor drying lessons (PR #2 has several): "Ventilate: indoor drying adds moisture to the room" only if the stage quantifies the water load. |

**Strands that need a safety review on every lesson:** food and cooking, cleaning chemicals, electricity, health and body, beauty and personal care, parenting and children, pets, cars and transport. Money needs one for every lesson beyond arithmetic. Home repair, digital, travel, garden and laundry/storage need one by trigger, not by default. Study and work need none.

## 3. Out of scope, whatever the strand

These are the four families the founder's question names. They hold across every strand and every format, including debunks.

1. **Medical advice:** diagnosis, treatment, dosage, symptoms, first aid beyond "call emergency services", diet and supplement claims. Mawdoo3 and WikiHow both carry large health sections; we do not match them there.
2. **Legal advice:** what is allowed, what to sign, what to declare, tenancy, employment, tax, insurance claims, road rules as advice. A lesson may say "rules differ: check yours" and nothing more.
3. **Dangerous chemistry:** mixing anything; reactions in closed containers; heating unknown substances; fire, fuel and fireworks; anything a safety data sheet warns about.
4. **Children's safety:** anything that could replace adult supervision, a safety device or professional guidance for a child's sleep, food, bathing, travel or play.

Also out, from the charter's refuse list: electrical work beyond a bulb or breaker; defeating any safety device; financial recommendations.

## 4. Required warnings: how, not just what

- **One line, on the stage, at the moment of risk.** Not a footer, not a modal. My `wobbly-table-quarter-turn` record set the length: "Clear hot drinks before you turn it." Eight words is the target; fifteen is the ceiling. A warning nobody reads is a failure mode in my role file.
- **It is a claim.** Each required line is recorded in `meta.json` as an advice claim with its source (a safety body or the manufacturer), so the fact-checker can check it and I can find it at launch.
- **The hero must not teach the careless version.** No cups on a table being turned, no plugged-in coiled cable, no child on a counter. Illustration requests carry a "must NOT show" line for each.
- **No blanket disclaimer.** A generic "use common sense, we are not responsible" banner on every card trains learners to skip all warnings. Lessons of class none carry no warning at all.
- **Region flags.** Food temperatures, electrical standards (230 V vs 120 V, plug types, RCD rules), airline and battery rules, road rules and pet-toxicity sources are region-dependent. Mawdoo3's audience is Arabic-speaking: the International team needs region notes for Gulf, Levant and North-African standards before any review-class lesson ships in Arabic.

## 5. Proposed additions to the refuse list (for cm-lifehacks to put in the charter)

The current list covers mixing chemicals, medical hacks, electrical work, safety devices and legal/financial advice. At WikiHow breadth it needs:

1. Food-safety shortcuts that contradict a food-safety body (thawing, cooling, reheating, doneness by colour).
2. Anything applied to skin, eyes, hair or mouth that is not used as its label says.
3. Children's sleep, feeding, bathing, car-seat and choking-hazard hacks.
4. Feeding pets human food, and any pet treatment or dosing.
5. Homemade pesticides, weedkillers and disinfectants.
6. Damaged or swollen batteries, and any charging hack.
7. Bypassing someone else's lock, account, paywall or parental control.
8. Hacks done while driving, and defeating vehicle sensors or alarms.

## 6. PR #2 through this lens

- **The 200 lessons sit almost entirely in the low-hazard strands.** 70 home (organisation, folding, packing, measuring), 70 study (planning, files, search), 60 resource arithmetic. That is a reasonable incubation choice, but it means PR #2 is not evidence that we can cover WikiHow breadth safely: the strands that need a review on every lesson (food, chemicals, electricity, health, beauty, children, pets, cars) are nearly absent.

## 7. Dividing the work, and the "50 more agents for verification" idea

- **What parallel agents can do for safety:** run walkthroughs and write per-lesson safety records in the template I use, one lesson each, without seeing the builder's notes first. That scales, and I welcome it for class none and warning lessons.
- **What they cannot do:** issue the verdict. My role holds the block / pass authority, and a builder may never mark its own work safe. So extra agents dispatched for verification must run as `lifehacks-safety-reviewer` instances (`.claude/agents/lifehacks-safety-reviewer.md`) on lessons no instance helped build, and every review-class verdict gets a second reader before launch. The fact-checker checks the claims; that is a separate pass and a separate role.
- **Capacity is the real constraint.** At WikiHow breadth, about half the strands are review-every-lesson. If the portfolio grows there, safety review becomes the bottleneck before building does. I recommend the category lens sequences strands by class: none and warning strands first (study, storage, laundry, digital-defensive, travel packing, money arithmetic), then one review strand at a time (kitchen first, it has the highest demand), each opened only after its first 5 lessons pass, the same rule the charter uses for the domain.
- **Pattern pre-clearance.** For each strand I will write one short pattern note (allowed mechanisms, required line, hero must-not-show list). A brief that stays inside its strand's pattern gets a lighter brief-stage review; one that leaves it gets the full record. This is a proposal; it needs evaluation-steward's agreement because it changes how often I read briefs.

## What I am sure of, and what I am not

Sure: the out-of-scope families, the eight review-every-lesson strands, and that PR #2's safety files are screenings, not attestations. Less sure: the exact required-line wording (each still needs a source at brief stage) and whether beauty and pets are worth having as strands at all; I expect most briefs there to be blocked.
