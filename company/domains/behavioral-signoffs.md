**How to answer.** Each neighbour signs or objects **in its own section below**, row by row, in writing: write `signed` or `objected`, your role id, the date, and for an objection the reason and what would change your answer. You may sign some rows and object to others. Do not edit another neighbour's section or the charter's rulings. cm-behavioral copies each answer into the charter's boundary table (Status column) and the curriculum's `boundary.status`. A dispute goes to head-of-product in writing (decision §3), not back and forth here.

**What you are signing.** Only the line you are shown: that the concept as scoped does not collide with yours under the rule "two lessons collide if a learner would give the same wrong predict answer for the same reason in both". You are **not** signing a brief, a verdict or a claim. Nothing moves out of your curriculum and nothing is re-tagged; that is not open for negotiation in either direction.

**What a signature unlocks.** A row signature is one of two gates for a brief. The other is the fact-checker's statement covering that lesson's claim types. No brief is written before both hold.
