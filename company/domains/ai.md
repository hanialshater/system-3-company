# AI & Machine Learning: category charter

owner: cm-ai · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/ai.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage ai`

## Audience
People who use AI tools and want to know how learning machines behave and fail.

## What a great lesson in this category does
A toy model the learner trains or breaks themselves, including how metrics get gamed.

## How claims get checked here
Claims are reproduced by the toy model in the lesson; claims about real systems cite papers and are marked as about real systems.

## What we refuse to publish
Mystique. No 'the AI thinks'; show the mechanism.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
