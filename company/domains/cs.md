# Computer Science: category charter

owner: cm-cs · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/cs.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage cs`

## Audience
People who use software every day and have never seen an algorithm from the inside.

## What a great lesson in this category does
The learner performs the algorithm by hand on a tiny input; the reveal scales it up.

## How claims get checked here
Derivations and runnable code; complexity claims cite a standard text (Knuth, CLRS).

## What we refuse to publish
Bars moving around. If the learner only watches, it is not a lesson yet.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
