# Probability & Data: category charter

owner: cm-probability · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/probability.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage probability`

## Audience
Anyone who has been surprised by a coincidence or misled by a test result.

## What a great lesson in this category does
Simulate thousands of trials on the stage so the learner's intuition meets the frequency.

## How claims get checked here
Exact computation or simulation in the lesson itself; population figures cite sources.

## What we refuse to publish
Formula reveals with no simulation the learner could have checked.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
