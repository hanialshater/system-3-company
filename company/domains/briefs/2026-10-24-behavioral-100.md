## Interaction recommendations

Use at least five visually distinct stage mechanics within the existing **single-card** contract. These are recommendations for the existing designers and builders, not new approved format names:

| Mechanic | Suitable use | Required visible response |
|---|---|---|
| Paired scenarios | labels, role assignment, incentives | Switch the same underlying case between two wordings; preserve the chosen pair for reveal. |
| Threshold scrub | round-number targets, wage spikes | Scrub across a boundary in a disclosed dataset and highlight the local change. |
| Evidence window | delayed responses, replication evidence | Visit two evidence windows; keep both observations visible together. |
| Counterfactual toggle | the fee, the observer, the default | Change the named condition while holding other model inputs fixed. |
| Allocation choice | time, savings, contract choices | Move a finite budget and recompute the resulting payoff or cost. |
| Distribution inspector | bunching, private reports, cross-society variation | Inspect counts rather than inferring individual traits from an aggregate. |

The original study's reported behavioral outcome must not become a universal correct answer. For choices with no objectively correct preference, use the learner's own choice diagnostically and place the scored prediction on a stated arithmetic consequence, logical relation, or disclosed evidence comparison. Illustrative models need that label in the stage and a derivation claim. A fabricated aggregate response is not a simulation of real human preferences.

Specific transfer questions should target the objective in each JSON record. Every factual prediction and recall option that implies a claim must be recorded in metadata. All options should have the same precision; lock all stage controls until prediction, require a milestone, preserve reached evidence into the reveal, and fit 390 × 844 with 44 px controls. A reset may not make the reveal contradict the stage.
