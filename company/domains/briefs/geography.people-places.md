### Ruling r5 (cm-geography, 2026-10-16)

Applies `company/decisions/2026-10-16-stage-word-budget-counting.md`. The counting rules are in `company/ops/dispatch-requests/2026-10-16-r5-geography.md`, R1, copied here word for word: Count from the brief's own text. List **every** word the stage may show, from the locked frame to the open reveal, and add them up as if all were visible at once. The total is an upper bound on the busiest frame. Numerals, percentages, scale ratios ("1:50,000") and fused units ("10m") count 0. Each line gives two totals: **strict**, where a spaced unit ("55 km", "400 km²", "7 h") counts 1 and a coordinate token ("49°N", "66.6°N") counts 1, and **lenient**, where both count 0. **The strict total binds** until head-of-product rules (A4). Two kinds of text are **not cuttable** by a strand manager: (a) the projection-and-resolution line, and (b) a claim-bearing scope caption ("Made-up island; 1 square = 5 km"). If a brief is over 25 strict, the strand manager may trim **only** its own control labels, picture labels and readout wording, edits the brief's Interaction, Locked stage and Key state lines to match, and marks each change "r5 trim". It may not change a claim's numbers, the prediction, the options or the reveal draft. If a brief cannot reach 25 that way, it writes "Stage over budget: N (strict)" with the table and stops for that brief. **The list is closed.** A builder who adds an unlisted stage word recounts, and stops if the strict total goes over 25.

None of briefs 1-5 has manipulated stimulus (decision point 6).

### 2. density-the-typical-person-lives-at · concept: population-distribution · core · demand 3 [toy]
- Format: Single card (preset buttons on one density picture).
- Stage word budget: stage line "Made-up country. 1 square = 1 km^2." = 3 (km^2 strict +1); legend title "People per km^2" = 2 (strict +1); readout "Land average: 100" = 2; readout "People average: 7,201" = 2; key "1 figure = 10,000 people" = 2; buttons "Average over the people" 4, "Spread everyone evenly" 3, "Back to the real layout" 4 = 11. **Total 24 strict / 22 lenient.** r5 trim: the two readout labels (were "Average over the land: 100 per km^2" = 6 strict and a second readout of 6) and nothing else; before the trim the total was 32 strict / 28 lenient. - Wave rank 7/15 (demand-3 concept whose only lesson, `half-of-humanity`, is still in review).
- Learner knows: population density is people per km^2 / does not know: that a country's average density describes its land, and the density a typical person lives at can be many times higher.
- Misconception: A country's average density tells you how densely its people live.
- Prediction: "A made-up country has 1,000,000 people on 10,000 km^2: an average of 100 people per km^2. Ask every person how many people live in their own square kilometre, and average the answers. About what do you get?" options: A) About 7,000 / B) About 700 / C) About 100 (answer: A). Check: section `lived`: three cities 700,000 people on 70 km^2 (10,000 per km^2), 100 towns 200,000 on 200 km^2 (1,000), countryside 100,000 on 9,730 km^2 (10.3); population-weighted density 7,201 per km^2, 72 times the average; 70% of people on 0.7% of the land.
- Locked stage: a 100 x 100 grid of 1 km^2 squares shaded by density (log legend titled "People per km^2", 10 px numeric labels), the readout "Land average: 100" (r5 trim; was "Average over the land: 100 per km^2"), nothing else.
- Interaction (DOM order): "Average over the people" (adds a strip of 100 figures, one per 10,000 people, with the key "1 figure = 10,000 people" (r5, listed so the count is complete), each standing on its own square's density, and the readout "People average: 7,201" (r5 trim; was "Average over the people"); milestone `q`) / "Spread everyone evenly" (every square 100 per km^2, both readouts 100; milestone `even`) / "Back to the real layout" (milestone `back`; both 7,201 and 100 shown again). Stage line: "Made-up country. 1 square = 1 km^2." (3 words, 4 strict; claim-bearing scope caption, not trimmed).
- Key state: both readouts visible together, "Land average: 100" and "People average: 7,201", with the figure strip mostly standing in the city squares.
- Reveal draft (about 70 words): "The typical person lives at about 7,200 people per km^2, 72 times the average of 100. Seven in ten people live in cities on under 1% of the land; the average spreads them across countryside where few live. Averaged over land, density describes the land. Averaged over people, it describes what a typical person's own square looks like. Spread everyone evenly and the two numbers agree."

- Claims: (1) all figures (derivation from the disclosed made-up table; section `lived`). - Nearest lesson: `half-of-humanity` (where people are on the real map). This one: which density number describes people; the map is a toy because no population grid is local.

### 3. same-blocks-new-zones · concept: areal-units-maup · core · demand 2 [toy]

- Wave rank 11/15 (new concept).

- Misconception: A map of zone averages shows the pattern in the data; where the zone lines go does not change it.
- Prediction: "In a made-up town of 64 blocks, four zones drawn as columns show average walks to the bus stop from 4.5 to 16.4 minutes, a gap of 11.9. Redraw the same blocks as four zones in rows. Check: section `maup` (seed 64): column zones 4.5, 8.5, 12.5, 16.4 (gap 11.9); row zones 10.6, 10.6, 10.3, 10.5 (gap 0.3); quadrants 6.6, 14.6, 6.4, 14.4 (gap 8.2); block values 3.1 to 17.9, mean 10.50.
- Locked stage: the 8 x 8 blocks (about 44 px each at 390 px, values hidden), the four column zones shaded by their average with their averages labelled (numerals), the key "Bus stops" along the west edge and the caption "Average walk, min" (r5, listed so the count is complete).
- Interaction (DOM order): "As rows" (milestone `q`; r5 trim, was "Zones as rows") / "As quadrants" (milestone `quad`; r5 trim) / "As columns" (milestone `back`; r5 trim) / "Show each block" toggle (block values at 10 px or more; milestone `blocks`). Initial: columns, so "As columns" changes the stage only after another zoning: put it third so it is never pressed while active on the QA path. Stage line: "Made-up town. Same 64 blocks every time." (6 words; claim-bearing scope caption, not trimmed).
- Key state: rows selected, the "Gap" list showing 11.9 then 0.3.
- Reveal draft (about 70 words): "As rows, every zone averages about 10.5 minutes: the gap fell from 11.9 to 0.3. The blocks did not change; only the lines did. Walks get longer from west to east, so column zones keep that difference and row zones mix it away. Quadrants give 8.2. A map of zone averages shows the data and the zone lines together, and the lines can hide a pattern or make one."

### 4. two-towns-thirty-minutes · concept: travel-time-distance · intro · demand 2 [toy]

### 5. where-is-the-centre-of-people · concept: population-centre · core · demand 1 [toy]
