## Coverage and overlap decisions

Removed direct reskins of existing capture-recapture (`life/count-marked-fish`), basic Simpson reversal, generic hidden-common-cause confounding, returning-aircraft survivorship and simple randomization/power. Replacements teach synthetic-control pre-period fit, difference-in-differences, matching, instrumental-variable exclusion, regression discontinuity, doubly robust estimation, placebo dates and distinct target estimands.

Some prerequisites recur intentionally while the boundary changes. Maximum likelihood locates a likelihood peak rather than updating a posterior. Measurement error changes an estimated slope rather than merely adding scatter. Range restriction changes which variation is observed, rather than just showing a correlation. A causal comparison must name its identification assumptions. A numeric model is not evidence that real people or institutions behave exactly like that model.

## Objections

- **A hundred lessons might be parameter reskins:** answered by distinct per-lesson model boundaries, the overlap replacements above, and local computations instead of an all-purpose score model.
