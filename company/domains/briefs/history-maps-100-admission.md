# History maps 100 (PR #4): should the 100 lessons be admitted under the current budget?

owner: cm-history · date: 2026-10-16 · for: W-1551 (cm-history, scope and admission), W-1553 (portfolio-manager, F-43 reconciliation)
status: a category manager's view, not a budget decision. portfolio-manager owns the register, and only the founder can top up a project.
inputs: `company/ops/projects.json` (project `history`, budget 2.5M), `node tools/ledger.mjs report` on 2026-10-16 (history 6.5M spent, gate STOP), `company/decisions/2026-10-16-founder-first-light-t1-topup-2.md` (F-43: no History top-up, no new builds dispatched), `company/decisions/2026-10-16-founder-history-maps-100-pr.md`, `company/engineering/community/prs/4.md`, and the W-1561 `reviewRoute` classes now in each `src/demos/history/map-*/meta.json`

**View: no. The 100 should not be admitted under the current budget.** The History line has spent about 6.3 to 6.5M against 2.5M and stands at STOP under F-43. The build cost nothing to the line because the founder's own session wrote it. Admission is a different matter: it is the review chain, and that chain is what we would pay for. The set-level merge gates (W-1557 licence, W-1559 research note, then W-1558 stage A, and W-1556 engineering) are each a single run or a few runs over the whole set. Every lesson then needs its own stage B cultural review, a fact-check of 5 claims, a learner review and a QA record. Under W-1561 all 100 lessons carry colonialism and empire, 28 also carry race, ethnicity and group comparisons, 27 religion and 31 political figures, so none of them is a light review. At the measured 122k to 151k per published lesson all-in (projects.json, the `first-light` and `rnd-knots` bases), the per-lesson chain alone costs several million tokens. That is more than the line's whole original budget, and it would be spent on 100 cases of **one** concept (`history:border-precision-over-time`). Charter-wise this is a practice set, not 100 ideas. Once a learner has seen 5 to 10 cases, each further pair of dots teaches little that is new, while the category still has 96 of 114 concepts unbuilt. My recommendation is as follows. (1) Keep the 100 as drafts. Fund only the set-level gates, and only if portfolio-manager finds a source other than the stopped History line, or the founder tops it up. Merging drafts is not admission. (2) If the founder does fund it, admit about 10 cases, one per regional group of `tools/history-maps/catalog.json`, chosen after stage A so that the cases it flags are left out. Send those 10 through the per-lesson publish chain and hold the other 90 as drafts until recall data on the 10 shows the practice set is worth a second batch. (3) Nothing is dispatched on the per-lesson chain until W-1553 records a funding answer. This view would be wrong if the founder treats the PR as its own funded initiative outside the History line. In that case the cost argument falls away, and only the one-concept-per-idea argument (point 2) still holds.
