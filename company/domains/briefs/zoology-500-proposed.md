# Zoology, animals and dinosaurs — 500 proposed lesson questions

Status: planning inventory, 2026-10-28. This document proposes **500 slots**, including two prototype drafts in PR #32; it does not add 500 implemented lessons. The other 498 are questions, not reviewed lesson briefs. No claim, source, image, question answer, lesson status, or release gate is certified by this list. The ideas need a category overlap audit against current Life Sciences lessons and each other, source-led science checks, individual briefs, learning design, accessibility review, and learner testing before publication. Some questions may be revised or replaced after that audit.

Audience: curious general learners familiar with animals but not with specialist zoological vocabulary. Priority follows strand order only as an initial coverage proposal, not a claim of demand. Suggested batches are 200, 200 and 100 slots, with the two prototypes counted once in the first 200; the existing prototype PR contains only two, so delivery sequencing must avoid duplicate integration and respect the founder's separate-PR request. Do not inflate counts with rewrites or alternate wordings of an existing lesson.

**Format labels are preliminary category-level choices, not interaction specifications.** Single card and simulation run are currently default formats; side-by-side comparison, before/after toggle and storyboard/scrub need the existing design routing. Map explorer is available but its geography kit may not fit species distributions, so map usage needs design review. A relevant visual may be a scientifically reviewed diagram or licensed/created image; generated reconstructions must be clearly labeled as reconstructions, and should not invent known anatomy, location, color or behavior. A representation must show the mechanism or evidence promised, and animal welfare, uncertainty and geological time should be represented carefully. All stage copy must eventually be recorded as unverified claims in each lesson's metadata.

The two prototypes are already in `src/demos/life/birds-among-dinosaurs/` and `src/demos/life/fossil-tracks-and-bones/`. Their appearance here is accounting only, not an endorsement of the unreviewed designs. Several other entries can overlap existing ecology, evolution and life-science batches. The category manager must compare the built catalog and either specify a genuinely distinct payoff or replace the candidate; this list intentionally preserves those review flags rather than asserting novelty.

**Source review:** use primary literature, museum collections and reputable textbooks appropriate to each question. Start with Natural History Museum and Smithsonian paleontology collections, the American Museum of Natural History, OpenStax Biology, and IUCN species assessments where relevant. These are research starting points, not checked support for any listed item; the fact-checker must identify precise citations and confirm species/region/time qualifications. Avoid using images as evidence for an unverified scientific claim.
