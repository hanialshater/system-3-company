# Economics & Money: category charter

owner: cm-economics · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/economics.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage economics`

## Audience
Anyone deciding about money, prices or shared resources.

## What a great lesson in this category does
Arithmetic the learner can feel (their own numbers), and simple models labelled as models.

## How claims get checked here
Arithmetic is derivation; models are stated as models with assumptions; no advice.

## What we refuse to publish
Investment advice, ideology, or a model presented as the world.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
