# Physics: category charter

owner: cm-physics · status: active · curriculum: `content/curriculum/physics.json` · coverage: `npm run company -- coverage physics`

## Audience
People who throw, swing and drop things and never asked why.

## What a great lesson in this category does
A correct simulation with real units; a prediction everyday intuition gets wrong.

## How claims get checked here
Equations with stated idealisations (no air resistance, small angles); units on every number.

## What we refuse to publish
Cartoon physics: motion that looks right and is computed wrong.

## Portfolio rules
- Order by demand (set in the curriculum file), but at least one lesson per cycle goes to a concept nobody has asked for yet, if the category has an exploration slot.
- No more than two lessons on one concept until every demand-3 concept has one.
- Each lesson states its knowledge boundary. Prerequisites in the curriculum file must be taught or explicitly assumed.
