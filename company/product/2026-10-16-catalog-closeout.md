## The rules of the round (product scope, mine to set inside F-46)
1. **One fix round** = one builder run that changes the lesson, followed by its checks (QA by a qa-tester, learner re-walk, fact-check of every claim the fix reset or added). After the checks the lesson is published if `company publish` passes and its hold is lifted by the record that owns it. Otherwise it is retired. No second build.
2. **The fix is the latest learner review's blocking finding, nothing more.** The CM's brief quotes the finding and the review's own Fixes section. New ideas, re-premises and new stages are not a fix round. They are new work after the closeout (F-46 objection 1).
3. **Versions built before F-46 and never walked (path W, 83 lessons).** The current version is walked first. If the walk says launch, there is no build: the lesson goes to its remaining checks. If it says revise, the CM may spend the lesson's one fix round on it, but only if the walk finds a blocking finding the previous review did not already raise. If the finding is the same one the last version was built to fix, the round has already been spent: retire. I chose this because these versions are already the lesson's response to its last verdict. Treating them as fresh would give some lessons two rounds. A CM who disagrees for a named lesson writes it in `## Objections` by 2026-10-18.
4. **No build where none is needed.** Paths C and V need checks or a re-recorded verdict, not a builder.
5. **Gates are unchanged (F-46 item 6).** Holds are lifted only by the record that owns them: Life Hacks rows by a Domain Launch Review row with the lifehacks-safety-reviewer's signature; History by its Domain Launch Review; W-513 by a frame of the current version; W-1078 by the fact-checker; the Books holds by the founder.
6. **New building is paused (F-46 item 5).** The in-flight physics #64-68 build (mail c5b4, /home/user/s3c-wj64) is not in these 258. Its five lessons join the ledger on merge as path W, with the 2026-10-20 deadline.
7. **Retirement (F-46 items 7-8).** No lesson is retired without `company/reviews/<slug>/retirement.md`, written by the CM who retires it. It covers: what the lesson teaches (idea, misconception, predict answer) and its current version; why it was retired, with each blocking finding quoted and its record path and reviewer role; what was tried, each version and fix round with commits; the specific fix it needs, or the open question that comes first; the evidence still reusable (verified claims and sources, valid QA and learner records, assets); the estimated cost to bring it back (tokens, roles) and a suggested priority. head-of-product checks the note before the status changes. Each retired lesson then gets a row in `company/product/retired-lessons.md`: slug, domain, one-line reason, one-line fix, cost, link to the note. I create that file with the first retirement. A retired lesson keeps its files.
