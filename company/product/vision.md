# Vision

owner: head-of-product · last reviewed: 2026-10-12 · confidence: unstarred (no learner evidence yet)

**A minute a day that leaves you able to ask a better question.**

In a year, a learner opens the feed and finds lessons from eight or more subjects. Each one asks
for a guess, gives them a small world to poke and then says why. Ideas they met last week come
back as single questions, at the moment they were about to forget them. When a lesson claims
something, one tap shows what it rests on and who checked it.

## Bets (each could be wrong)

| Bet | Why we believe it | would_be_wrong_if |
|---|---|---|
| A vertical feed is the right *container* for interactive lessons | People already know the gesture; one idea per screen forces focus | Learners swipe past predictions without committing (commit rate < 40%) |
| Predict-before-reveal beats explain-then-show | Testing effect; generation effect; the book's Merge Sort demos | Revisit recall is no better than for a version that explains first (EXP-003, not yet run) |
| Spaced revisits matter more than more lessons | Ebbinghaus; spacing effect | Learners who see revisits recall no better at 3 weeks |
| A company of narrow agents with independent checks makes better lessons than one strong agent | System 3, Chapters 3–5 | Under the same budget, a single agent's lessons tie on QA, fact-check and pairwise review (EXP-001 tests a piece of this) |

## Not the vision
An infinite scroll optimised for time-on-app. A tutor that does the thinking for you. A course
platform. Certificates.

## Horizons
- **Now (Q4 2026):** 8 domains, ~20 lessons, local and preview deploys, the company runs its rituals.
- **Next:** first real-learner sessions; publish only gated lessons to production; revisit analytics.
- **Later:** learner-chosen paths through the curriculum map (prerequisites become a route); lessons that compose from reusable "experiences" (the store's idea, applied to teaching).
