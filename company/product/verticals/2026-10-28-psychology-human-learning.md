# Psychology and human learning: 500-demo vertical

Status: proposed delivery plan, 2026-10-28. This is a capacity map, not 500 written briefs or completed demos. Founder direction: `company/decisions/2026-10-28-founder-psychology-learning.md` (W-2055).

## Learner and promise

Curious adult learners explore what perception, memory, learning, emotion, identity and relationships can reveal about human experience. Each approximately 60-second lesson starts from a concrete question, elicits a prediction, allows a meaningful action, and reveals what the learner saw, with a fresh recall question. Make room for deep and competing psychological theories as well as foundational cognitive science. Present theoretical models as models, and show where evidence is mixed or the inference is limited.

## Boundaries and safeguards

- Cross-link existing Behavioral Economics lessons about choices, framing and biases; reserve new lessons for a distinct psychological objective. Audit titles, objectives, stages and recalls, not merely slugs.
- Cross-link Life Sciences for physiology, AI for computational analogies, Life Hacks for task-specific applications, Books for authored arguments, and Probability for research methods. A mental model is not evidence about actual people.
- Psychological effects can vary by context, population, method and replication. Separate learner observations, illustrative or fictional model outcomes, and empirical claims. Never imply a deterministic personal result from a group finding.
- Sensitive subjects—including trauma, attachment, disorders, identity and cultural comparisons—need specialist review and appropriate routing. Interactive tasks are educational experiences, not diagnosis, therapy, or a measurement of the user's ability or mental health. Avoid collecting sensitive responses.
- Every learner-facing factual claim needs its own unverified claim record with an inspectable source and independent fact-check; do not assign a replication verdict without evidence. Include study populations, methods and limitations when they matter. Respect existing review and publication holds.

## Delivery and acceptance

1. Principal PM and domain scout: compare with the actual catalog, develop a checkable domain proposal and route ownership to an existing manager; do not invent a new role. Head of Product makes the domain decision. Founder has requested the vertical and minimum count, not a specific org change.
2. Produce a source-backed inventory with one unique objective, prediction, interaction, expected visible result, recall, claim sources and overlap decision per proposed lesson. A title or parameter variant alone is not a demo.
3. Build a perception and a human-learning pilot end-to-end. Independently review claims, pedagogy, accessibility and both desktop and phone interactions before expanding the design.
4. Build remaining lessons in bounded batches, checking registry and catalog integration, source provenance, behavior, accessibility and meaningful differences. Keep incomplete or unreviewed lessons draft. Separate automated test coverage from independent content review and observed human learning.
5. Completion requires at least 500 actual reachable demos, correct count and no unresolved duplicate objectives, plus recorded test and review results; report the number and status at each stage.
