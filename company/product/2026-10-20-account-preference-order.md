## Decision on the exact proposed wording

**Accept AMD-016's proposed saved-choice exception and cold-start trade-off in principle, subject to the concrete conditions below.** The specific founder ask establishes the product intent to save voluntary choices to the account and use them in the real ordinary feed/default branch. Product supports that work proceeding now. The former preview-only choice is superseded; persistence and ordinary-feed integration should not remain a demo-only feature merely because the first prototype was scoped that way.

The text is appropriately narrow: account-bound, explicit Save and enable, All-feed new lessons only, edit/off/clear, anonymous/null/off/error baseline, default subject-chip ordering, mutually exclusive with reachable-v3, and no behavioral/engagement/ad/fitted term. It does not authorize anonymous preference persistence, automatic onboarding import, inferred learner categories, ability estimates, a learning-effect claim or deployment. The five experience labels describe desired content and challenge describes preference; neither labels a person as clever, advanced, struggling or a particular learner type.

Product accepts this exact cold-start cost: a person can deliberately change the first minute from their saved tastes even though the company has no evidence that those changes improve understanding, recall or completion. Explicitly chosen interests are a sufficient reason to offer bounded taste matching, not a substitute for learning evidence. Baseline remains a first-class choice; Skip, Off and Reset must genuinely restore it rather than leave an unlabelled residual ranking. No settings failure should silently fall back to previously enabled choices.

This is one conditional Board-seat acceptance. AMD-016 remains proposed until the actual required founder confirmation/Board dispositions are recorded and its protected wording is applied through Article3. Do not mark ratified from this document, treat my earlier routing as ratification, or enable the candidate by reusing AMD-015's flag. No general GAM, Bayesian, default-on or future-input approval follows.

## Product conditions that define acceptance

1. **Consent and persistence are understandable.** Loading a page, selecting a draft or signing in must not save or enable anything. The person can explicitly Save and enable, edit existing choices, turn matching off and clear preferences. An existing/null row starts off. Reset means choices cleared and off; failed Save/Off/Reset is visibly unsaved and retryable, without a false cross-device-success claim. Account A's draft, reasons or order must not appear for account B or signed out. Existing account age/consent gates and history-import choice remain separate. Account and onboarding copy must name preferences among stored data and explain account persistence, export and deletion truthfully.
2. **The actual ordinary feed changes, conservatively.** Use the same `/` feed and real lesson interactions. Subject chips retain their existing filter/default order. Signed-out/no-row/null/empty/off/reset/read-error states use the canonical baseline card for card. A resolved enabled account can see the bounded candidate before its first completion. Do not reshuffle a prediction already being answered when settings arrive, a tab regains focus or another device changes choices. Make an intentional feed restart or next unseen segment boundary explicit. Pagination must represent one complete deterministic ordering, without mixing baseline and candidate pages or duplicating/skipping identities.
3. **Due recalls have priority in the enabled path.** Accept due-first ordering for the new saved-choice path: when due recalls are known before starting, they precede its new lessons; preference points must never demote them. When they arrive late, preserve the current active interaction and place due material before subsequent unseen new lessons, rather than interrupting a prediction. This does not authorize changing recall correctness or inventing mastery from taste. Current off/signed-out baseline interleaves recalls; keeping that unchanged avoids silently broadening this feature's behavioral delta. That existing discrepancy with the protected due-first statement remains explicit inherited debt, not a Product waiver or a claim that the old code complies. AMD-016's final admission must name both tested states and retain the steward's no-weakening position; no new candidate may make due handling worse.
4. **Visibility and dependency limits precede taste.** Existing visibility, publication labels and active holds win. Unknown profiles do not gain visibility through ranking. Preserve published-before-review status partition and every fixed slot; do not allow a five-card boundary swap to put an in-review item ahead of a published item while claiming the baseline partition remains. Refuse an adjacent swap that reverses a declared prerequisite relation. Do not describe an unimplemented eligibility engine or concept mastery as already present. Existing applicable prerequisite restrictions stay upstream, not an extra score that a large taste contribution can defeat.
5. **Reasons and uncertainty stay honest.** Every actually moved new lesson gets a short, true reason based on the person's saved subject/experience/challenge choices and the implemented comparison. A fixed/default lesson is not labelled a personal recommendation merely because preferences are enabled. “Based on your saved choices” is suitable; “you learn best this way,” “best for you,” “improves your recall,” “Bayesian estimate” or “learned your interests” is unsupported. Hand-set ordinal match points are not measured learning, probability or calibrated quality. Technical rubric/provenance detail belongs in synthetic HQ; any learner-facing editorial explanation must be readable, attributed as provisional and not raw reviewer shorthand mistaken for a fact-checked lesson claim.
6. **Provisional editorial use is bounded and separately admitted.** Product accepts the uncertainty of using current provisional observations for modest taste ordering only within AMD-016's fixed contract: subject40, selected known experience mean0–40, challenge20 exact/10 adjacent, five-card baseline groups, adjacent advantage at least5, displacement at most2, stable ties, local subject-run bound, unchanged per-group subject counts. Five core quality dimensions must meet the specified known-score/confidence/evidence guard; requested experience dimensions must be known. Unknown, stale, disputed, weak or insufficiently supported profiles remain exact-position barriers. These guards cannot certify factual truth, accessibility or pedagogical effectiveness. Missing accessibility stays unknown, and ordinary independent lesson gates remain binding.
7. **A later round is possible without hidden policy drift.** Append new dated observations/adjudications, preserve originals and disagreement, regenerate profiles, and obtain independent provenance, exact-dataset Product and engineering acceptance before use. An exporter is not an admission authority. No parked walkthrough is resumed by this decision; no source refresh turns an unknown into a reviewed observation. Changing weights, rubric, movement bounds, evidence guard or permitted inputs returns to the amendment/owner process, rather than claiming that “evaluate more later” authorized automatic policy changes.

The status-partition condition above makes explicit the proposal's statement that it does not remove published-first ordering. If the implementation intentionally permits that crossing, it is a substantive different product choice and must return for a precise amendment/cost disposition; it is not accepted by this record.
