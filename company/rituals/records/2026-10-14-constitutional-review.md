## 3. Proposed amendments (prepared, not ratified)

- **AMD-003 (not drafted; awaits commissioning): fixed-base protected-path check, plus path-scoped amendments.** Covers G5 (a, b, c) and the CI half of G1. Surface: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `.claude/hooks/protect-constitution.mjs`. What it removes: a PR or push that touches protected paths can no longer ride on an unrelated ratified AMD. Applying an amendment then needs an accurate `surface:` list. Prediction: zero false passes on a planted set (a red push followed by a clean push; a new branch with a protected edit; an AMD-002 trailer on a `tools/company.mjs` edit), and zero false fails on the AMD-002 application commit replayed. Rollback: revert, under its own trailer.
- **AMD-004 (outline; the steward drafts the file if the board agrees): state the rule 7 reading in `company/reviews/README.md`.** The proposed text to add under rule 7: *"For builders the bar is the role: no builder verifies a claim in a lesson it built. For checkers the bar is the instance and its context: a checker instance that drafted a claim's wording, or read that drafting or its evidence before checking, does not verify it. A fresh instance may, if it reads the artifact and the claim's text and support first and writes its findings before it reads the source fields or the trail. Adopted checker wording is labelled `example wording, owner decides` in the dispute."* What it removes: the absolute reading of "author" for checkers. Who is judged: fact-checkers, and the steward authored the ruling, so **neither the fact-checker nor the steward may be the deciding vote**. Seat: fact-checker. Side-by-side: for one wave, flag every verify by a fresh instance of checker-drafted wording, and have a second fresh instance re-check a sample of 5. Any disagreement is studied. Pair it with a mechanical aid in ordinary tooling: the claim records a `draftedBy` review-pass id, and `check` warns when `checkedBy`'s pass equals it.
- **Contract amendment for reveal-fit (future, J4):** this is not proposed now. The trigger is W-242 and W-349 being stable for one wave.

2. *What does it remove?* See above. G2(a) removes nothing from the gate, but it removes the gate's control over what learners see. That has to be said in the override.
3. *Side by side?* Planned for AMD-003 (planted cases) and AMD-004 (one wave, sample re-check). Not run.
4. *Rollback?* All of them are reverts under their own trailer. G2(b) is one environment variable.
5. *Who is affected but not in the room?* **Learners**, by G1 and G2. Today they can see 238+ lessons with unverified claims. The "In review" tag does not tell them which claims are unverified (Article 6.3 asks that a learner can see what a lesson rests on; whether the tag meets that is for head-of-product).
