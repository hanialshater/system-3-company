## What went well (keep doing)
- Independent checks found real defects in every lesson, including the operator's own (mercator 15× vs 10.7×).
- Parallel worktree builders with separate build dirs had zero merge QA failures.
- Batched sonnet checks cut checking cost by about 3×.

## What hurt (with evidence)
- The reveal gate was weak everywhere (18/18 reviews). Builders were never told the key state matters.
- The same kit bug was worked around four times (slider tap target).
- Subagents could not write handoffs or board items (their briefs forbade shared files), so the operator had to transcribe every report.
- Parallel agents overwrote each other's scratch scripts (fact-checker and learner evaluator).
- The walkthrough driver pushed sliders past their maximum, so evaluators saw no movement.
