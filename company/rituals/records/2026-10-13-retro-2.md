## Numbers
- **Lessons:** 50 added (geography w3 and w4, probability w3, chemistry w4, ai w4) and about 60 revised (physics, probability, life, the fatals, claim fixes). 0 merge QA failures.
- **Fact-checks:** passes 4 and 5 covered 80 lessons: 3 disputed, all fixed the same evening. Claims are 96% verified.

## What went well
- **Revisions are cheap and they work.** A Sonnet reviser fixed 11 life lessons for about 20k tokens each. The fatals were fixed within an hour of being found.
- **The holds file caught 3 cases** the gate would have shipped: lru-eviction-order, which a sentence on its own stage contradicts; friends-have-more-friends, which has a false verified claim; and 8 launches written without screenshots.
- **The event log made HQ possible** with no new process: the snapshot backfills it.

## What hurt (with evidence)
1. **Usage limit (3 times today).** The biggest throughput loss of the day: about 7 h and then about 1 h with nothing running.
2. **Evidence quality varies by reviewer.** The geography reviewer's image reads failed for 21 lessons and verdicts were written anyway. One launch (eratosthenes-shadow) was published on that basis and then unpublished. Mitigation already in the brief: no "launch" without screenshots seen.
3. **Two publishes went wrong, both by me.** friends-have-more-friends went out because a hold was lifted in the same command as a merge that failed. eratosthenes-shadow was published before the reviewer reported its caveat. Both were reverted within minutes.
4. **`pgrep -f`/`pkill -f` killed my own shell again,** despite the checklist rule. The rule existed but wasn't followed, so it needs a mechanism.
5. **A resumed builder found its worktree deleted.** I clean worktrees only for finished agents, but a stopped agent's worktree with no commits can still disappear. No work was lost.

- Starting more than 3 builders and 3 reviewers at once while the budget is unknown.
