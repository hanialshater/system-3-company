# Correction of Error: 2026-10-13: session usage limit stopped 8 of 9 builders mid-wave

owner: chief-of-staff · reviewers: evaluation-steward, principal-engineer

## What happened
At about 02:40 UTC, nine Opus builders (wave 2 cs/geography, wave 3 for six domains, wave 2
probability) were running in parallel, while the chief of staff ran a 168-lesson build and
walkthrough. The account hit its session usage limit (HTTP 429, resets 03:30 UTC). Eight builders
stopped partway with uncommitted work in their worktrees; only wave 2 probability finished. The
work resumed at 05:21 UTC.

## Impact
No lost lessons (partial work is on disk in 8 worktrees), about 2.5 hours idle, no learner impact.

## Five whys
1. Why did builders stop? The account's session limit was exhausted.
2. Why was it exhausted? About 10 heavy agents (~200k tokens each) ran at once, on top of the operator's own work.
3. Why that many? The operator sized waves by CPU (4 cores) and briefs available, not by token budget.
4. Why was the token budget not a constraint? There is no budget on record. The OP asked the founder for one; none was set, so the operator assumed none.
5. Why did nothing warn before the wall? The waves log records tokens after a wave, not a running total against a limit.

## What allowed it
No capacity plan for model usage; concurrency was an unwritten judgement call.

## Actions (each changes a mechanism)
| Action | Mechanism changed | Owner | Board id |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cap concurrent Opus builders at 4; checkers on sonnet, at most 4 at once | chief-of-staff checklist step 3 | chief-of-staff | — |
| Builders commit after every 2 lessons, so a stop loses at most one lesson | builder brief | chief-of-staff | — |
| Resume stopped agents with SendMessage (context intact) before re-dispatching | operating-loop | chief-of-staff | — |
| Record cumulative tokens per wave in waves.json, and show them in `npm run metrics` | waves.json fields | analytics-steward | — |
| Ask the founder for a usage budget, and plan waves against it | OP section 7 | head-of-product | — |

## Pattern update
Candidate: "capacity is a budget, not a CPU count". Unstarred, one case.
