## Reusable lessons for the pattern librarian (W-514)

Candidates, each from at least two of today's pairs. They are hypotheses from simulated reading, not evidence that anyone learned.

1. **Draw the base, not only the result** (pairs 1, 2). When an idea is "the second change acts on a different amount", the winning stage draws both amounts (the \$8 chunk, the lost corner). Stages that show only totals plus a formula move the mechanism into the reveal's prose. Fits as a known_failure or extension of `numbers-from-the-stage`.
2. **The reveal's picture must survive the QA path** (pairs 1, 5). Reset (twenty-then-ten-off) and wall taps (wave-from-the-corner) destroy the state the reveal cites. Seen lines rescue the first; nothing rescues the second. Extension of `earn-the-reveal`.
3. **Ask for the surprising number, not the obvious direction or definition** (pairs 2, 3). "Below/at/above" and "which method checks fewer?" let a learner be right without the insight. Extends the 2026-10-12 `predict-before-reveal` rule that the commitment must be able to be wrong: it must be wrong *for the misconception's reason*.
4. **Shrink is not fit** (pairs 4, 6). Two lessons pass "on screen at 390x844" by scaling the stage until its labels are a few pixels high, at predict (penalty-kick-mix) or at the reveal (who-pays-same-filter). Proposed known_failure for the phone-fit rule in company/decisions/2026-10-14-reveal-fits-with-its-picture.md.
5. **Related lessons are often a sequence, not substitutes** (pairs 4, 5, 6). Where the newer lesson presupposes the older (A* on BFS, mixed equilibrium on dominant strategy), "which is better" is the wrong question. The pairwise output is an order. Proposed note for `pairwise-not-scores`.
6. **A duplicate built on a generic template loses to a bespoke lesson on the same concept** (pairs 1, 2, 3, all three Life Hacks lessons). The common cause is the shared template, not three builders. Fix it once (W-518) before judging the other 13 duplicates one by one.

Calibration note on my own failure modes: in pairs 2 and 6 I preferred the lesson with the longer reveal in one case (fifty-up-fifty-down) and the shorter in the other (supply-demand loses on length). The reason in each case is what the text points at, not its length. No pair was decided by resemblance to Distill or 3Blue1Brown.
