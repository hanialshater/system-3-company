I read no builder notes (`build.md`) for any lesson here.
- **No new walkthroughs, no build directories** (disk at 83%). Every lesson here already had a walkthrough. **Three are stale:** the walkthroughs for cluster-stops-the-cascade (recorded 16:10, v4), enemy-of-my-enemy (16:12, v3) and metal-feels-colder (16:22, v1) predate the r9 revise (Demo.tsx edited 16:34, 17:00 and 16:36; meta now v5, v4, v2). So §2 judges the versions learners flagged, not the fixes. Sign-off on the fixed versions waits for the r10 walkthroughs that W-640 will record; §2 lists what to check there.
- **The sample walkthroughs are fresh** (all recorded 18:51 to 18:57, after their last Demo.tsx edit).
- **Measurements here are by eye from the sheets** (a 390 px phone frame drawn at about 310 to 390 px). Where I give pixel numbers they are estimates. Where something needs a number, the ask names a probe.

## Top findings (in order of how many learners they touch)

1. **Kit stage layout (every lesson): the stage is a scroll box whose content is vertically centred** (`.stage { flex: 1; overflow: auto; justify-content: safe center }` in `src/app/globals.css`). This causes two visible defects:
   - **Predict hides controls inside a nested scroll.** With the question and options in the panel, the stage is short, so its last control row is cut at the stage's bottom edge, with no scroll cue: each-element-pops-once (List row cut, W-567), same-grey-in-shadow (Remove the shadow / Reset cut), cluster-stops-the-cascade v4 (legend cut). In explore, wheel-needs-four-colours v4 scrolls its own picture top and caption off inside the stage (frames 10 to 14), so its clash caption and the hand-colouring buttons are never on screen together (W-567 / W-642). The learner would have to discover a scroll area inside a scrolling page.
   - **The picture jumps.** When the prediction locks in, the stage grows and the centred content drops 40 to 65 px (euclid-three-steps, metal-feels-colder, secret-in-the-counts, two-locks-wait-forever), and it shifts again whenever a step changes the content height (two-locks, one-crowded-bucket). On short pictures this leaves a large empty band: metal-feels-colder's explore stage is about 470 px tall around a 120 px picture.
   - This is a kit fix, not ~900 lesson fixes. Design direction: **anchor stage content at the top** (`flex-start`) so nothing moves when the panel changes, and make the predict stage show every control row, or show a visible "more below" edge. The reveal fit (W-135/W-237/EDD-005) depends on this CSS, so the platform engineer must measure before and after with `tools/reveal-fit.mjs` on a sample. It is not a change for me to make.
2. **The reveal shows the state of the last control pressed, and on the QA path that control often empties the evidence.** formats.md already says "No reset that empties it". Four of the fifteen lessons I looked at break it at the reveal: secret-in-the-counts (Reset is last: Output "–", counts "–", while the reveal quotes "'is' was followed by 'ready' three times"); two-locks-wait-forever (the reveal's first paragraph describes the jam, but the stage shows "Both finished", with only the text line "Jam: P holds A, Q holds B" left); which-sea-gets-the-rain (Reset is last, so the 20% / 80% colouring that the reveal's last sentence rests on is gone; only the Seen line has it); wheel-number-pulls-guess (Hide wheel is last: the picture shows "median 35" next to the slider's "Wheel lands on 65", and nothing on screen says "no wheel"; that label is only in the aria text). The Seen lines save the numbers but not the picture. **Rule I will propose for formats.md:** Reset and hide/undo toggles never come last in DOM order, or the lesson restores the picture the reveal quotes when the reveal opens (the `revealCard` idea, generalised). A pattern-librarian check fits here.
3. **`CardSequence` (EDD-006) reference: the format's main promise holds on the artifact.** At 390×844 the reveal opens with the trail (three ticks plus traces "6 primes · 30031 · 59×509") and card 3's whole table on screen. The reveal (62 words) quotes only the traces and the reveal card, and fits with no crop by eye. Its one real design defect is finding 1: the trail floats in the middle of the stage, 80 to 100 px below the stage's top edge on cards 1 and 2, and it drops about 65 px between predict and explore, so the stepper does not read as the card's header. Details and asks in §1.
4. **Domain pastel used as text in two physics lessons.** tug-of-war-scale (`.labT { fill: var(--domain) }`, the "tension 100 N" labels, 10.5 px) and metal-feels-colder (`.readNow { fill: var(--domain) }`, the "skin 21 °C" readout the reveal quotes). system.md: `--domain` is 1.1 to 2.4:1 as light-mode text and must be mixed (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--domain) 45%, #000)`). These are the very numbers the learner reads. tug-of-war's label is also under the 11 px authoring target.
5. **Pictures that argue against their own reveal.** hydrogen-per-litre draws energy per litre on an **unlabelled log axis** (1 kJ / 100 kJ / 10 MJ / 1 GJ). The hydrogen-gas bar looks about a quarter of petrol's, while the lesson's point is "about 2,900 times less". A learner who reads bar length the usual way sees about 4×. winning-bid-overpays marks winners over $100 red and under $100 green, by colour only (system.md known gap: colour-blind pairs).
