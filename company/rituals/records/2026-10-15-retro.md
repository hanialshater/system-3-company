2. **I gave the founder wrong numbers twice:** "656 published" (it was 635) in the digest, and "changed-only QA saves time" (PE: not on this deploy).
3. **I merged claim verdicts by hand** in the fixes-2 conflict (a293a45). My per-claim rule was sound, but the PE called hand-merged verdicts the real semantic risk.
4. **The allow-list I gave the founder was incomplete.** Bare hosts redirect to www hosts, which stay blocked, so 6 of 10 sites still fail (W-939).
5. **Founder decision records lacked class/route lines** until dispatch-check blocked builds (fixed today). Ritual debt built up again: the retro and WBR were overdue, which blocked dispatch.
6. **Builders hit each other's dev ports** (W-851). One reviewer's `--help` habit and autostash pulls carried other agents' uncommitted edits through.
7. **A background command hung on `npm run check`** and left a decision record uncommitted until I noticed.
