## What went well
- **Gates held.** Nothing unverified published. publish-ready refused patience-wins-the-split on a held claim, and the holds file kept who-pays and penalty-kick back after the head-of-product ruling.
- **Fact-checking without the web worked.** Checkers reran numbers from copies of the lesson logic, found 9 real disputes (the ammonia K at 450 °C, the 8+ run bin, the epidemic peak) and held whatever they couldn't open. They didn't guess.
- **Reviews found the systemic problem.** The demo crit, then r9 batch D, then the interaction designer traced the Life Hacks failures to one engine bug (the mode toggle greys out the mode in effect) before anyone fixed 200 lessons one by one.
- **Strategy questions went to owners and came back with objections.** The Books and Big Ideas leads both pushed back on volume; the head of product kept the founder's targets and let the leads set the pace.

## What hurt (with evidence)
1. **I ran the company at 2 to 3 times its concurrency limit.** operating-loop.md says about 8 agents and 3 builders; I ran up to 20, with 9 builders. The load average was 25 to 60 on 4 CPUs. Dev pages took 1 to 12 minutes, every builder needed raised timeouts or a private tool copy, and official QA could not run until the load fell to 5 at 18:00.
2. **Builders shared the main tree, against the loop.** One half-written Demo.tsx (nearest-town-first-detours) broke the dev server for every agent for 10 to 20 minutes, at least twice.
3. **The board collided at every merge.** The rule is that only the chief of staff writes the board, but every agent did: W-477..480, W-600, W-609 and W-666 were duplicated and renumbered by hand, and at least 3 updates were overwritten (portfolio-manager).
4. **Nine rituals fell overdue, the WBR among them** (36 h, against a 24 h limit). I dispatched new work instead of running them.
5. **I routed founder asks around their owners.** History went to the CEO office and the domain scout without the principal PM for verticals. The founder had to ask why.
6. **The disk ran out several times** (stale `.next-*` folders). I freed 11 GB of caches; deleting the old worktrees was blocked as destructive and they remain.
7. **Process incidents:** the walkthrough loops died with their parent shell; the founder-decision record I wrote broke `check` (no owner line); one commit message said 4 publishes when there were 3.

## What we will stop doing
- Treating "spin many agents" as permission to pass the cap. The cap exists because more agents made everything slower today, not faster.
- Recording founder decisions without routing them to the owner first.
