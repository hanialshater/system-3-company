## Questions the board asks of each
1. Does this change who judges the proposer's own work? (If so, the proposer cannot be the deciding vote.)
2. What does it remove? Where is the selection record's "removes" column?
3. Was old vs new run side by side? Were the disagreements studied, not just the agreement rate?
4. What is the rollback?
5. Who does this affect who is not in the room (learners, a category left out)?
